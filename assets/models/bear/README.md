# Bear models

This folder holds the three bear models that the user gave, and the files that this project made
from them. The app does not serve them.

## bear-tpose.glb

`bear-tpose.glb` is a mesh with no skeleton, with the arms in a T-pose: out to the sides and a
little down, free of the trunk. The user gave it on 2026-10-23 and said: "also use the new bear
model [...] note that this is a T-pose". The file is the file of the user, byte for byte. Since
2026-10-26, the rig of this project makes the studio body of the bear conductor from its copy with
rebuilt arms, `bear-arms.glb` (below, `tools/mesh-rigs/bear.json`): `pnpm rig-mesh bear` writes
the rigged file `bear-tpose.rig.glb`, and `pnpm studio-body bear` writes the module
`src/models/studio/bear.ts` from it.

## bear-arms.glb and bear-weights.json

`bear-arms.glb` is `bear-tpose.glb` with new quad faces on each arm from the shoulder line to the
wrist. The user said on 2026-10-25: "the bear's arm has unnatural facets underneath". The long
thin faces of the source arm folded into shards under the bent elbow. `tools/blender/bear_arms.py`
replays the Blender session that made both files, byte for byte. The faces
of the paws, the trunk, the legs, and the head are the faces of the user. The new arm surface
lies on the old one, with the arm length of the user. Only the inner side of each upper arm is a
little flatter, so that it stays out of the round belly. The flatten comes after the bake, so that
each texel takes the fur of its own place on the source arm. On 2026-10-26 the user said: "there's
black area under the bear's armpit". The flatten before the bake had moved the tube out of the
reach of the bake rays, and the bake wrote black texels on 65 faces under the armpits.
On 2026-10-28 the user said: "the bear has a crease around the wrist". The wrist cut stood at a
right angle to the paw, so the band from the last ring to the cut loop was thin on the palm side,
and its sharp edges showed as a notch on the top of each wrist. The cut now stands parallel to the
last ring, and Smooth Vertices and a Shrinkwrap even the band of each wrist
(`cut_planes()` and `step_smooth_wrist()` of `tools/blender/bear_arms.py`), as on the polar bear.

The edit: the shoulder ball of the source (welded vertex 583 on the right, 460 on the left) has
half of its weight on the upper arm. The turns of the arm sink it 2 to 3 cm below the surface
around it, and a sharp V dent shows at the top back of the arm root. The edit moves the shoulder
ball and 4 (right) or 5 (left) vertices around it out along their normals, at most 0.0125 units
(2.06 cm in the app). It also splits the longest edge across each shoulder joint (26 cm on the
right, 17 cm on the left) at a new vertex on the round curve between its ends, which adds 4
triangles. No other vertex, UV, or texture changes. `tools/blender/arm_root.py` with
`tools/arm-roots/bear.json` gives the edit, and a run of it gives the same bytes.
