# Fox model

This folder holds the source mesh of the fox, a copy with rebuilt arms and its weights, and the
rigged copy. The red fox and the tan fox share the rig. The app does not serve these files. It
loads the module `src/models/studio/fox.ts`, which this chain writes:

```text
fox.glb                            source mesh
   │  tools/blender/fox_arms.py    rebuilt arms and painted weights
   v
fox-arms.glb + fox-weights.json    `source` and `paintedWeights` of tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json
   │  pnpm rig-mesh fox
   v
fox.rig.glb                        rigged mesh
   │  pnpm studio-body fox
   v
src/models/studio/fox.ts           studio body of the app
```

`docs/agents/rigging.md` gives the steps of the chain.

Facts of the mesh:

- A weld at 1e-5 gives 1,559 vertices in one connected part. The mesh has 5 open edges and 2
  edges with more than two faces.
- The box is x -0.550 to 0.549, y -0.952 to 0.951, and z -0.545 to 0.536. Thus the fox is 1.903
  units high from the soles to the tips of the ears.
- The fox faces +z, with its left side at +x. These are the axes of the studio.
- The fox stands in an A-pose: the arms go down and out at about 48° below the horizontal.
- The eyes are brown and black faces of the head surface, with no shells. The tail hangs down
  behind the legs to 0.01 units above the soles.

## fox-arms.glb and fox-weights.json

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| `fox-arms.glb` | 1,173,608 bytes, SHA-256 `e975e65114c6591cb4327d3d82b72a71f81a3c2b17d7d2967026aac3d65bbb44` |
| Mesh | 2,046 vertices after a weld at 1e-5, 4,085 triangles: on each arm a tube of quad rings of 16 vertices, joined to the socket and the paw with Bridge Edge Loops, with Beautify Faces and Collapse Edges on the bridge triangles |
| Material | One material with one base color JPEG: the atlas of the source, and a strip of 512 pixels on top with the fur color of the source arms, baked onto the new faces. The side walls and the flat inner sides take one fur color of the trunk. No metallic-roughness texture |
| `fox-weights.json` | 459,512 bytes, SHA-256 `ccf5e085477dc8f27f8bdd3d2777ecfab8605e9a5800285dcdb375a684d2b2bf` |
| Weights | The automatic weights of Blender with the Weight Paint fixes of the script, on the studio bones and the arm segments and helpers (`armSegments` of the config), for `paintedWeights` of the config |

The inner side of the upper arm joins the trunk down to the armpit. Thus the script follows the
route of `cat_arms.py`: a cut along the seam where the arm meets the trunk, a side wall of the
trunk under the arm, and a tube with a flat inner side on the wall. The arms are 1.21 times longer
from the socket, because a rig with arm segments does not stretch the arms.

The faces of the paws, the trunk, the legs, and the head are the faces of the source, with two
exceptions. The short edges of the thin faces at the cut loops collapse. Smooth Vertices smooths
18 vertices of the small notches at the back of the neck, below the ruff
(`step_smooth_notches()`). A run gives the same bytes:

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup -P tools/blender/fox_arms.py
shasum -a 256 assets/models/fox/*.glb assets/models/fox/fox-weights.json
```

The rig gives the faces near each shoulder joint and each elbow joint the normals of the skin with
no crease angle (`rootNormals` of the config). The seated bind bends the elbows, and a crease
angle splits the normals of each inner elbow into a visible notch.
