# Fox model

This folder holds the fox mesh that the user gave. The app does not serve it.

## fox.glb

`fox.glb` is a mesh with no skeleton. The user gave it on 2026-10-14 with the words "now replace
the fox by @~/Downloads/fox.glb". The file is the file of the user, byte for byte. The rig of this
project makes a studio body from it, as for the rabbit mesh (route 3 of `AGENTS.md`,
`tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json`): `pnpm rig-mesh fox` writes the rigged file `fox.rig.glb`, and
`pnpm studio-body fox` writes the module `src/models/studio/fox.ts` from it. The app does not
serve the two GLB files.

The user also chose "1.08x, rabbit reach" on 2026-10-14: the upper arm and the forearm get
longer, so that the reach is 0.377 m, as the rabbits. The rig measured the factor 1.0782
(`armFactor` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json`). The rig adds a second tail bone, so that the seated
tail goes back through the open back of the chair.

These facts of the mesh were measured on 2026-10-14
(`docs/research/2026-10-14-fox-mesh/facts.txt` and `sections-y.txt`):

- A weld at 1e-5 gives 1,559 vertices in one connected part. The mesh has 5 open edges and 2
  edges with more than two faces.
- The box is x -0.550 to 0.549, y -0.952 to 0.951, and z -0.545 to 0.536. Thus the fox is 1.903
  units high from the soles to the tips of the ears.
- The fox faces +z, with its left side at +x. These are the axes of the studio.
- The fox stands in an A-pose: the arms go down and out at about 48° below the horizontal.
- It has orange fur, a white chest, muzzle, and tail tip, and black lower legs, lower arms, paws,
  and ear backs. The nose is black. The eyes are brown and black faces of the head surface, with
  no shells.
- The tail hangs down behind the legs from the lower back to 0.01 units above the soles.
