# Rabbit models

This folder holds two models of the rabbit that the user gave. The app does not serve them.

## rabbit.glb

`rabbit.glb` is the source of the body of the rabbit of 2026-10-10 (design 4.4). `pnpm rig-body rabbit` makes
the packed body `src/models/rigged/rabbit.ts` from it. The app does not serve this file. Since
2026-10-11, the app has no rigged body, and no species uses the packed body.

To check the file:


The rabbit faces +z in glTF, and +y is up. It is 1.2 m high from the soles to the tips of the
ears. `tools/glb-bodies/rabbit.json` gives the scale, the map from the joints of the file to the
bones of the player, the bones that the file does not have, and the color rules.

## rabbit-4.glb

`rabbit-4.glb` is a mesh with no skeleton. The user gave it on 2026-10-13 with the words
"replace the rabbit by using the model at ~/Downloads/rabbit-4.glb. Note that you need to rig it
yourself." The file is the file of the user, byte for byte. The rig of this project makes a
studio body from it (route 3 of `AGENTS.md`, `docs/decisions/2026-10-13-mesh-rabbit.md`).

The user also chose "Longer arms, 28 cm violin" on 2026-10-13. The rig makes the upper arm and
the forearm about 1.25 times longer, so that the reach is 0.377 m, as the studio rabbit of
2026-10-13. The violin stays 0.28 m. This changes the mesh at the arms only.

These facts of the mesh were measured on 2026-10-13:

- A weld at 1e-5 gives 1,536 vertices in one connected part. The mesh has 4 open edges and 15
  edges with more than two faces, at the whiskers.
- The box is x -0.464 to 0.463, y -0.951 to 0.953, and z -0.372 to 0.367. Thus the rabbit is
  1.903 units high from the soles to the tips of the ears.
- The rabbit faces +z, with its left side at +x. These are the axes of the studio.
- The rabbit stands, with the arms about 35° out from the body and down, and the paws open.
- The eyes are dark faces of the head surface, with no shells. The whiskers are thin faces that
  join the muzzle. The tail is a ball.
