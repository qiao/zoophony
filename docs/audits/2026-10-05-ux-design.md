# Audit: end-to-end UX and design

Date: 2026-10-05 (the test run ended after midnight). Auditor: an agent in the role of a senior
product designer and UX auditor. The auditor did not build the UI.

## Scope and method

- Code: `main` at `6bdf960` for the tests. The later commits up to `d06e1cf` change only CI,
  the smoke test, and one test attribute of the canvas. The file and line numbers below are
  for `d06e1cf`.
- Build: `pnpm build`, then `pnpm vite preview` on port 4401 from a separate worktree.
- Browsers: Playwright 1.63 headless Chromium with `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu
  --ignore-gpu-blocklist`, and Google Chrome 154 (channel `chrome`) for a second run of the
  frame and play checks. No run used `--autoplay-policy`. A Playwright click is a user gesture.
- Sizes: 1440 × 900, 1920 × 1080, 1280 × 720, a phone of 390 × 844 with touch and device
  scale 3, and a tablet of 820 × 1180 with touch.
- Network: no throttle, 50 Mbit/s with 20 ms, 4 Mbit/s with 150 ms, and a slow 3G profile
  (0.4 Mbit/s with 400 ms), with the CDP network throttle.
- Camera: one screenshot in the middle of each of the 182 shots of the six demos at
  1440 × 900, from the shot plan of `prepareShots()` with the seed of the app.
- Sound: an `AnalyserNode` on each channel of the node that connects to the destination. The
  script read the RMS level, the peak, and the largest step between two samples every 40 ms
  to 100 ms.
- Files: two demo files of `demos/src/` as user files, a piano-only file and a drums-only file
  from `src/midi/test-smf.ts`, a file with no notes, a text file, and a file of 5 MB.

Severity: **P0** broken, **P1** clearly unpolished, **P2** polish, **P3** nice to have. Size:
**small** (less than a day), **medium** (one to three days), **large** (more).

## Summary

**Verdict: a good foundation that needs polish.** The look of the default view matches the
reference. The animals are charming in close shots. The controls are complete, accessible, and
stable. The sound starts on the downbeat, and the frame rate holds 60 fps. The first 10
seconds after a click on Play are the weakest part: the camera cuts from the beautiful wide
stage to a dark close shot of the back of the hall and the bear. Many close shots also hide
their subject behind front-row heads, stands, or the piano case. The load states and the phone
sheet need feedback.

| Severity | Count |
|---|---:|
| P0 | 0 |
| P1 | 7 |
| P2 | 19 |
| P3 | 12 |

**Top 10 fixes**, in order of value for the cost:

1. **FI1 (P1)**: start each piece on the wide stage, and show the bear later.
2. **SC1 (P1)**: reject close shots in which heads, stands, or the piano case hide the subject.
3. **LD1 (P1)**: give the large Play button a visible "waiting" state during the load.
4. **PH1 (P1)**: close the phone sheet after a demo selection.
5. **LD2 (P1)**: show a load state at once on a slow network, before the first preset arrives.
6. **FI2 (P1)**: tell a first-time visitor what the app is and that a MIDI file can play.
7. **CT1 (P1)**: add tooltips with the key to the icon buttons.
8. **SC5 (P2)**: remove the shadow acne in close shots.
9. **SC3 and SC4 (P2)**: fewer identical conductor shots, and show the soloist of a concerto.
10. **SD1 and SD2 (P2)**: match the loudness of the demos, and make Mute silent at once.

The first 10 seconds of the default demo today:

```text
 click      count-in (1.6 s)        downbeat              first cut (10.0 s)
   │◄────────────────────────────►│◄───────────────────────────►│
   │ wide stage → HARD CUT to a   │ bear close shot continues:   │ flute section
   │ dark close shot of the bear  │ black stand over the body,   │ (crowded)
   │ (no orchestra in the frame)  │ black piano at the edge      │
```

## Findings

### First impression

#### FI1. The play starts on a dark close shot of the conductor (P1)

- **Seen:** A click on Play cuts at once from the wide, lit stage to a close shot of the bear
  from the orchestra side (`first-shot-conductor-1440x900.webp`, `first-play-400x250.gif`). The
  background is the empty dark back of the hall. The black desk of the conductor stand covers
  the body and the left hand. The black piano case fills the right edge. The light comes from
  71° above the bear, so the face is dark brown on dark gray. The shot holds 5.5 s (Beethoven 8)
  to 10.4 s (K. 315). Each of the six demos starts this way, and each demo switch cuts from this
  shot to the same shot of the next demo (`demo-switch-conductor-1440x900.webp`).
- **Why it matters:** The first click is the moment of delight. The visitor loses the
  orchestra that the first screen promised, and sees no animal play for up to 10 s.
- **Cause:** `src/shots/prepare.ts:959-975` makes the conductor the opening shot from
  `-Infinity` (design 5.6, "Cut points", choice D of the camera report). The conductor shot
  looks from the orchestra to the audience (design 5.6, "Jump cuts").
- **Fix:** Hold the first-screen view through the count-in, then a slow push-in on the wide
  view to the first cut. Show the conductor later, over the shoulder from the audience side,
  with players in the frame. For the dark face, add a weak fill light from the camera side in
  the conductor shot, or raise the fill of the bear only. Change design 5.6 ("The first shot
  shows the conductor") and the table of kinds. Size: medium.

#### FI2. A first-time visitor does not learn what the app is or can do (P1)

- **Seen:** The page shows the piece title, a gold Play button, and the bar
  (`first-screen-1440x900.webp`). The name "Zoophony" is only in the browser tab. Nothing says
  that a MIDI file can play, or that more pieces exist. The demo list and the file button are
  in a closed drawer behind an icon with no label.
- **Why it matters:** The main feature of the product is "animals play your MIDI file". A
  visitor who plays the demo and leaves never finds it.
- **Fix:** Below the Play button, add one line of dim text, for example "An orchestra of
  animals plays any MIDI file", and a small text button "Choose a piece" that opens the drawer
  at the demo list. Hide both after the first play. Change design 2.2, 2.8 (components), and
  the prototype. Size: small.

#### FI3. The large Play button covers the middle of the orchestra (P2)

- **Seen:** At 1440 × 900, the 72 px button sits on the cellos and violas, next to the bear.
  On the phone, it covers about a third of the stage (`phone-first-screen-390x844.webp`).
- **Why it matters:** The first screen hides the animals that make the product special.
- **Fix:** Move the button and the load text below the front edge of the stage (about 72% of
  the height on a desktop and 70% on a phone), or keep the center and make the button fade to
  60% opacity while the pointer is away. Change design 2.8 ("First screen"). Size: small.

### Stage and camera

#### SC1. Close shots hide the subject behind heads, stands, and the piano case (P1)

- **Seen:** In the 182 shot frames, many close shots have a large foreground item over the
  subject, or a head cut at the frame edge.
  - Boulanger harp solo: two rabbit heads in the front cover the harp and the deer
    (`shot-boulanger-harp-solo-1440x900.webp`). The same composition repeats at 188 s and 244 s.
  - Boulanger piano (celesta) solo: the black case fills about 60% of the frame. The hands and
    the keys do not show (`shot-boulanger-piano-solo-1440x900.webp`). Bach "piano" shots show
    the same case.
  - K. 315 flute solo: the music stand crosses the flute and the hands of the soloist, and a red
    fox head fills the lower left (`shot-k315-flute-solo-1440x900.webp`).
  - Trumpet, tuba, horn, and oboe solos of Boulanger: half heads at the bottom edge, and the
    subject at the side edge (`shots-boulanger-1-20.webp`).
- **Why it matters:** A concert film shows the player who plays. A hidden subject makes the
  cut look random and the film look unedited.
- **Cause:** `src/shots/clearance.ts` tests one sight line from 0.3 m outside the target, and
  skips all boxes within 0.4 m of the target (design 5.6, "Clearance"). A head or a stand in
  front of the face or the hands passes this test.
- **Fix:** Test 5 sight lines per camera: to the head, to each hand, to the instrument center,
  and to the target. Reject a camera when more than 1 of the 5 crosses a box. Add a penalty
  for a box of another player in the lower 15% of the frame. For the pianist, put the camera
  on the audience side of the keyboard. Change design 5.6 ("Clearance"). Size: medium.

#### SC3. The conductor shots repeat and all look the same (P2)

- **Seen:** The plans have 4 to 12 conductor shots per demo. Bach has 12 of 35 shots (34%).
  All use the same angle from the orchestra side (`shots-bach-all.webp`).
- **Why it matters:** The same dark frame every 20 s to 40 s makes the film monotonous.
- **Fix:** Limit the conductor shots to about 15% of a plan. Add a second conductor framing:
  over the shoulder from the audience side, with the section of the next cue behind the bear.
  Change design 5.6 (kinds and candidate scores). Size: medium.

#### SC4. The camera misses the soloist of a concerto and the first voice (P2)

- **Seen:** In K. 315 (a flute concerto), the first solo shot of the flute comes at 93.9 s.
  In the Ode to Joy, the baritone sings from 1.5 s, and the first shot of a singer comes at
  18 s.
- **Why it matters:** The soloist is the story of these pieces.
- **Fix:** Give a bonus to the solo of a seat that carries an exposed line (one loud seat over
  quiet accompaniment) and to the first entry after the count-in. Change design 5.6 ("Choice
  of the next shot"). Size: small.

#### SC5. Shadow acne shows as a cross-hatch pattern in close shots (P2)

- **Seen:** A lossless capture of the cello solo shows a regular cross-hatch on the cello
  body, the white fur, and the chair (`shadow-acne-crop-3x.webp`). It moves with the camera.
- **Why it matters:** The flat-shaded faces are the style of the app. Noise on them looks like
  a defect.
- **Cause:** `src/scene/lights.ts:191-192` sets `bias = -0.0005` and `normalBias = 0.01`. The
  light comes in at a steep angle on vertical faces.
- **Fix:** Raise `normalBias` to about 0.02 to 0.03 and check the contact shadows under the
  feet. Record the values in design 4.5 ("Light and render"). Size: small.

#### SC6. A drag in a close shot orbits around the close target (P2)

- **Seen:** A drag during the conductor shot changes to Free mode and turns around a point at
  the head of the bear, 4 m away (`free-camera-after-drag-1440x900.webp`). The wheel then goes
  near the head (`free-camera-zoom-in-limit-1440x900.webp`).
- **Why it matters:** A user who drags wants to look at the stage. A turn around a close point
  feels like a lost camera.
- **Cause:** `src/scene/camera.ts:106-109` keeps the target of the last shot (design 2.5).
- **Fix:** At a change from a close shot to Free mode, move the orbit target to the stage
  center over 0.4 s, and keep the camera position. Change design 2.5 ("Free"). Size: small.

#### SC8. The default view leaves large empty areas on portrait screens (P3)

- **Seen:** On the phone, the stage is about 300 px wide, and the animals are about 10 px tall
  (`phone-first-screen-390x844.webp`). On the tablet, the lower third of the screen is empty
  (`tablet-first-screen-820x1180.webp`).
- **Fix:** In portrait, move the stage down to the middle of the free area and zoom to keep
  0.3 S of margin. Change design 4.5 ("Camera"). Size: small.

#### SC9. The music stands are black slabs in close shots (P3)

- **Seen:** From the audience side, each stand shows its black back. In close shots, two to
  four stands are the largest shapes in the frame (for example `shots-boulanger-1-20.webp`).
- **Fix:** Use a dark warm gray (`#2e2722`) with a lighter edge for the desk, so that it
  reads as an object and does not cut the image. Change design 4.5 (scene colors). Size: small.

### Animals and motion

#### AM1. The conductor beat reads as a hand at the ear (P2)

- **Seen:** From the front, the baton hand stays next to the head, and the baton is short. The
  beat is a small motion of the wrist (`first-shot-conductor-1440x900.webp`,
  `first-play-400x250.gif`). The bear of `animals.webp` holds the baton out in front, with the
  arms wide.
- **Why it matters:** The conductor is on screen in 15% to 34% of the shots. The count-in is
  the first motion that a visitor sees.
- **Fix:** Put beat 1 at the height of the chest, in front of the desk, and widen the beat
  points by about 1.5. Make the baton about 30% longer. Change design 5.4 ("Conductor in
  detail"). Size: medium.

#### AM2. Nothing marks the end of a piece (P3)

- **Seen:** At the song end, the last wide shot holds, the players lower their instruments,
  and the bar shows "Play" (`song-end-1440x900.webp`).
- **Fix:** The bear turns to the audience and bows. The bar shows a replay icon and the next
  demo. Change design 5.4 and 2.8. Size: medium.

#### AM3. The concerto soloist sits in the section row (P3)

- **Seen:** In K. 315, the solo flute sits in the woodwind row behind a stand.
- **Fix:** For a concerto demo, seat the soloist at the front left of the conductor, as the
  piano of a concerto (design 4.2, 4.5). Size: large.

### Controls

#### CT1. The icon buttons have no tooltip and no visible label (P1)

- **Seen:** No element of the page has a `title` or a tooltip. The bar has six icon buttons
  (Play, Mute, Automatic camera, Reset view, Full screen, drawer). The camera icon and the
  target icon of "Reset view" are not standard icons. The seat rows have "M" and "S".
- **Why it matters:** A sighted mouse user cannot learn what the camera and the target icons
  do, or that the key C exists.
- **Cause:** `createButton()` in `src/ui/dom.ts:33` sets only `aria-label`.
- **Fix:** Show a tooltip on hover and on keyboard focus after 400 ms, with the label and the
  key: "Automatic camera (C)", "Play (Space)". Add the tooltip to design 2.8 (components).
  Size: small.

#### CT2. The camera mode changes with no message (P2)

- **Seen:** A drag or a wheel turns off the automatic camera. Only the color of the camera icon
  changes from gold to white.
- **Why it matters:** A user who drags once loses the film and does not know how to get it
  back.
- **Fix:** Show a short note above the bar for 3 s: "Automatic camera off. Press C to turn it
  on." Use the style of the messages with an accent bar in place of `--danger`. Change design
  2.5 and 2.8. Size: small.

#### CT3. Small interaction details (P3)

- **Seen:** The pointer stays visible while the bar hides during play. The hover background of
  a button changes with no transition, and a pressed button has no `:active` state. The focus
  ring of the seek bar is a square box around the full input.
- **Fix:** Hide the pointer over the canvas when the bar hides. Add 120 ms transitions and an
  `:active` state to `src/ui/ui.css:56-58`. Give the focus ring the button radius. Size: small.

### Drawer

#### DR1. The seat rows show MIDI data and do not name the animals (P2)

- **Seen:** Each row shows "Port 0, channel 12, programs 40 and 45"
  (`drawer-seats-solo-1440x900.webp`). The only link to the stage is a dot of 12 px.
- **Why it matters:** Most users think of "the rabbits" or "the violins". Few users know what "channel 12" is.
- **Fix:** Make the second line "5 rabbits" (the count and the species), and move the source
  line to a third, smaller line or a tooltip. Change design 2.8 ("Seat texts"). Size: small.

#### DR2. The seat "Piano" is a celesta or a harpsichord (P2)

- **Seen:** Boulanger plays a celesta and Bach a harpsichord (design 8.2, expected sections),
  but the seat list says "Piano" (`src/app/seat-text.ts:12`).
- **Fix:** Name the seat after its keyboard program: "Celesta", "Harpsichord", "Piano". Keep
  the piano model. Change design 2.8 ("Seat texts"). Size: small.

#### DR3. The drawer opens and closes with no motion and has no close button (P2)

- **Seen:** The drawer and the phone sheet appear and disappear in one frame
  (`src/ui/drawer.ts:72-74`). Only the bar button and Escape close it.
- **Fix:** Slide the drawer 16 px and fade it in 200 ms (no motion with reduced motion). Add a
  close button to the top of the drawer. Change design 2.8. Size: small.

#### DR4. The settings do not explain their values (P3)

- **Seen:** "Section size: Auto, One per part, Full" and "Quality: Auto, High, Medium, Low"
  have no help. "Auto" does not show the current tier.
- **Fix:** Add one dim line under each control, and show "Auto (High)". Size: small.

- **Cause:** `src/ui/file-picker.ts:38`.
- **Fix:** Hide the hint for `(pointer: coarse)`. Size: small.

#### PH3. The "Playback" group has no labels (P2)

- **Seen:** The group shows a speed menu, a slider, a target icon, and a frame icon in one row

### Loading and errors

#### LD1. A click on the large Play button during the load gives no feedback (P1)

- **Seen:** With a slow load, a click on the large button leaves its icon and its label at
  "Play" (`load-click-during-load-1440x900.webp`). Only the small bar button changes to
  "Pause". A second click on the large button does nothing.
- **Why it matters:** The user clicks again, waits, and thinks that the app is broken.
- **Cause:** `onFirstPlay` ignores a click while the piece waits (`src/app/app.ts:293-297`),
  and `FirstScreen.update()` has no waiting state (`src/ui/first-screen.ts:85-98`).
- **Fix:** Add a waiting state: the ring turns, the icon changes to a pause icon, the load text
  says "Starts when the instruments are ready", and a second click cancels. Change design 2.2
  and 2.8.

```typescript
export interface FirstScreenView {
  readonly shown: boolean;
  readonly loading: boolean;
  /** True after a click on Play during the load: the piece starts when the samples are ready. */
  readonly waiting: boolean;
  readonly loadShare: number;
  readonly loadText: string;
}
```

Size: small.

#### LD2. A slow network shows long periods with no load state (P1)

- **Seen:** On the slow 3G profile, the page is only the background gradient for 5.7 s. Then
  the bar and a Play button show over an empty background, with no title and "0:00 / 0:00",
  for 2.5 s (`load-slow-3g-6s-1440x900.webp`). Then the stage shows, and the ring stays empty
  with no text for 15 s, until the first preset decodes. At 50 Mbit/s, the full load takes
  2.2 s, and these gaps do not show.
- **Cause:** `loadText()` gives '' before the first decoded file
  (`src/app/play-control.ts:99-104`). The progress counts files. It does not count bytes.
- **Fix:** Put a static title and "Loading" text in `index.html`. Show "Loading instruments:
  0 of 17" when the list of presets is known. Let the ring follow the bytes, and turn it slowly
  before the first byte arrives. Change design 2.8 ("Load progress"). Size: medium.

#### LE3. A file with no sequence name shows its name twice (P2)

- **Seen:** A dropped "drums-only.mid" shows "drums-only" and below it "drums-only.mid"
  (`file-drums-only-1440x900.webp`).
- **Cause:** `fileTitle()` in `src/app/file-rules.ts:83-89`.
- **Fix:** With no sequence name, show "Your MIDI file" or nothing on the second line. Change
  design 8.2 ("Texts"). Size: small.

#### LE4. The failure texts repeat "try again" (P3)

- **Seen:** "The instruments did not load. Check the connection, then try again." stands next to
  the button "Try again" (`phone-load-failure-390x844.webp`).
- **Fix:** "The instruments did not load. Check the connection." Change design 2.8 ("Message
  texts"). Size: small.

#### LE5. The page with no WebGL2 has no name and no picture (P3)

- **Seen:** The full-page message is correct, but it is the only content of the page.
- **Fix:** Add the name "Zoophony" and one line about the app above the message. Size: small.

### Sound

#### SD1. The demos differ by about 4 dB in level (P2)

- **Seen:** The mean RMS over five windows of 6 s at 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, and 85% of each demo:

  | Demo | Mean RMS | 90th percentile | Peak |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|
  | Bach | -21.6 dB | -18.7 dB | 0.64 |
  | K. 315 | -22.1 dB | -19.1 dB | 0.56 |
  | Boulanger | -22.3 dB | -16.7 dB | 0.79 |
  | Eine kleine Nachtmusik | -22.7 dB | -19.8 dB | 0.47 |
  | Beethoven 8 | -25.2 dB | -21.0 dB | 0.82 |
  | Ode to Joy | -25.8 dB | -20.1 dB | 0.49 |

  A switch from Boulanger to Eine kleine Nachtmusik went from about -23 dB to -13 dB at the
  forte opening.
- **Why it matters:** The choir finale sounds the quietest. A user changes the volume after a
  demo switch.
- **Cause:** `DEMO_LOUDNESS` in `src/audio/demo-loudness.ts:14-21`.
- **Fix:** Measure each demo again on the full output chain with a gated, K-weighted loudness
  (ITU-R BS.1770), and keep all demos within 1 LU. Record the values in design 6.8. Size:
  small. This RMS is not a loudness measure, so the step is also under "Unverified".

#### SD2. Mute fades over about 0.8 s, and unmute is instant (P2)

- **Seen:** After a click on Mute, the level went from -23 dB to -45 dB in about 0.8 s. After
  Unmute, the full level came back in the first 40 ms.
- **Why it matters:** A user expects silence at once from a mute button.
- **Cause:** Mute and the volume slider set the master gain of the synth in the worklet
  (`src/audio/engine.ts:250-252`). The hall reverb of the output chain comes after it, so its
  tail rings on (design 6.8).
- **Fix:** Apply the mute to a gain node after the hall, with a ramp of 20 ms. Keep the volume
  slider where it is. Change design 6.8 ("Output chain"). Size: small.

### Copy

#### CP1. The page has a fixed title and no metadata (P2)

- **Seen:** The tab always shows "Zoophony". `index.html` has no description, no favicon (the
  site answers `/favicon.ico` with 404), and no preview data for a shared `#demo=` link.
- **Fix:** Set `document.title` to "{title}, {composer} · Zoophony". Add a description, an SVG
  favicon, and Open Graph data with a screenshot of the default view. Size: small.

#### CP2. The demo titles are not consistent (P3)

- **Seen:** The list mixes "mvt 1" and "first movement" in the title, and gives the Köchel
  number only for K. 315 (design 8.2, texts).
- **Fix:** One pattern, for example "Eine kleine Nachtmusik, K. 525: I". Size: small.

#### CP3. The speed menu uses the letter x (P3)

- **Seen:** "1.0x". **Fix:** "1×". Size: small.

#### CP4. The site ships the development pages (P3)

- **Seen:** `dist/` holds `spike.html`, `gallery.html`, and `views.html` (`vite.config.ts:24-28`).
- **Fix:** Build them only for development, or link them from nowhere and add `noindex`.
  Size: small.

### Visual design

#### VD1. The title is hard to read over light animals (P2)

- **Seen:** In close shots, the title and the dim composer line sit over white fur. The
  composer line is almost unreadable (`title-over-light-fur-crop.webp`).
- **Cause:** `.title` has only `text-shadow: 0 1px 2px #000` (`src/ui/ui.css:84`).
- **Fix:** Add a soft dark gradient behind the top left corner, or hide the title with the bar
  during play. Change design 2.8 (components). Size: small.

### Performance

#### PF1. A demo switch and a section size change stop the frames for 133 to 150 ms (P2)

- **Seen:** One frame of 133 to 150 ms in both Chromium and Chrome, at each demo switch and
  each change of "Section size". All other windows had a steady 60 fps (p99 16.8 ms), also
  over cuts.
- **Cause:** `#showPiece()` (`src/app/app.ts:654-677`) and `#setSectionSize()`
  (`src/app/app.ts:420-441`) build the layout, the pose plan, and the shot plan in one task.
- **Fix:** Cover the build with a short fade to the background (about 200 ms), so that the
  stop does not show as a hitch. Size: small.

## What works well

Keep these when you fix the findings.

- The default view matches `orchestra.webp`: the stage, the beam, the warm light, the steps,
  and the layout (`first-screen-1440x900.webp`).
- The animals are charming and readable in close shots. The cello and choir shots are good
  examples (`works-cello-close-1440x900.webp`, `cello-close-360x225.gif`,
  `works-choir-1440x900.webp`).
- The UI follows the tokens and the layout of design 2.8 and the prototype. The bar, the
