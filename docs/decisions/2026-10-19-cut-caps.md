# Decisions of the normals of the cut caps

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, the user said: "the arms for hippo and elephant seem twisted and deformed". After two
hippo rounds, the side view of the hippo still showed a dark slot at the right armpit and light
slivers at the right shoulder. The round `cut-caps` chose with no questions. The report
[`2026-10-19-cut-caps.md`](../research/2026-10-19-cut-caps.md) gives the measures. The user can
reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.
