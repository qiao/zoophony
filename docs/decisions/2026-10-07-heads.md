# Decisions of the heads

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-07, the heads agent made the heads of the species look like the heads of
`reference/mockup.png`: gaps 2, 5, 7, and 8 of the review `reviews/2026-10-07-mockup-match.md`.
The agent decided the details below. The user can reverse each one. Then update the section that
holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The eyes follow `mockup.png`: one form for each species (an almond, an oval, or a bead) at 0.08 to 0.135 H high, in place of the cute eyes of 0.18 × 0.21 H. The user chose "cute" on 2026-10-06, but did not choose the eye size, and the goal of the user is the mockup (manager decision of 2026-10-07) | 4.3, 4.5 |
| Each eye has one white light at its upper outer side, 0.34 of the eye height. The mockup eyes show one small light. The second light of the cute eyes made the small eyes look busy | 4.5 |
| The cat, the foxes, and the wolf have amber eyes with a black pupil, as the eyes of `mockup.png`. The iris colors are lighter than the mockup eyes, because the eyes are small and the key light comes from behind | 4.5 |
| The cat (1.08 H), the wolf (1.12 H), the foxes (1.2 H), and the dog (1.22 H) get narrower heads, as the heads of `mockup.png` (1.0, 1.1, and 1.2 H). The head height stays 1.0 H (user choice of 2026-10-07). A width factor scales all head rings, so the cheeks keep their form, and the trunk grid takes the narrower jaw ring through `headRings()` with no change of `trunk.ts` | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The long muzzle (the foxes, the wolf, the deer, and the badger) reaches 0.43 H in front of the face, in place of 0.25 H, and narrows to a thin tip. The mouth of the winds keeps its place under the muzzle, so the flute and the clarinet holds do not change | 4.5 |
| The blunt muzzle of the dog reaches 0.43 H in front of the face (0.3 H before), so that it is about 0.6 H in front of the eye, as the beagles of `mockup.png` | 4.5 |
