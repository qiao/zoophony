# Decisions of the beater of the badger bass drummer

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, the user said: "the badger is not holding the drumstick properly". The paws stood
at the chest, and the drum hid the beater. The manager chose the size of the drum and of the
beater, and the stance of the round. The report
[`2026-10-27-badger-beater.md`](../research/2026-10-27-badger-beater.md) gives the measures and
the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| Manager: the bass drum and its beater take the scale of the badger: its head top (1.038 m) over the mean height of an adult (1.683 m, CDC 2015 to 2018), 0.617 (`BADGER_SCALE`). | 4.6 |
| Manager: the drum is a concert drum of 36 by 16 inches at that scale: 0.56 m wide and 0.25 m deep (0.70 m and 0.32 m before). The legs of its stand scale with it. | 4.6 |
| Manager: the beater is a concert beater of 419 mm with a head of 69.8 mm (Vic Firth BD3) at that scale: 25.8 cm long, with a head of 4.3 cm (43.5 cm and 11 cm before). The middle of the paw holds it one third up from the butt, so 9.5 cm of shaft lies behind the grip (`malletButt`). | 4.6, 5.3 |
