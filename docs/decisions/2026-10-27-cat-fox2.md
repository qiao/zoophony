# Decisions of the cat arm length, the cat rest grip, and the right flute paw

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, the round `sixth-cat-fox2` fixed three issues of the user: "the cat's arm has dent
and is longer than expected", "the cat is not "holding" the viola in rest mode", and "the fox
doesn't seem to be holding the instrument properly". The report
[`2026-10-27-cat-fox2.md`](../research/2026-10-27-cat-fox2.md) gives the measures and the images.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "the fox doesn't seem to be holding the instrument properly". The right tan fox paw holds the flute as the right hand of a flutist: behind it and a little below, pointed up past its back, with the fingers curled over the top (`gripShiftR`, `aimSide`, `poleR` of `FOX_FLUTE`). | 5.3 |
| A share of `WindHold.aimSide` below 0 turns the paw toward the opposite side direction across the tube (`aimSide()` of `pose/winds.ts`). With the side aim to the front, the flute lay in the right palm as in a left hand, and the paw turned 180° in the moves. | 5.3 |
| The right flute elbow points down, out, and a little forward, (1, -1.4, -0.3): out to the side, the paw swing was 45° (30°), and a little back, the right upper arm went 1.5 cm into the body in the moves (0.6 cm). | 5.3 |
| `pnpm wind-grip` gives the right flute paw its place behind the flute and a little below (`ROLES` of `tools/wind-grip.ts`). The place in front was the place of a left hand. | 9.3 |
| The left flute paw keeps its grip. No left elbow that grasps brought its move twist to 70° or less: (-1, -1.4, -0.3) gave 77° with the tips 0.8 cm off the flute. | 5.3 |
