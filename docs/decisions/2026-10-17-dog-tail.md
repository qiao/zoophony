# Decisions of the tail of the dog cellist

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

After the round `dog-arms`, the user said: "the dogs' tails look weird". The hanging tail was a wide
wedge behind the chair, about as thick as a thigh. From the variants of the round `dog-tail`, the
user chose "V1, thin and hanging (Recommended)". The report
[`2026-10-17-dog-tail.md`](../research/2026-10-17-dog-tail.md) gives the measures. The user can
reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: the seated tail of the dog hangs down behind the seat between the back posts on the curve of the tail of `2026-10-17-dog-arms.md`, and it is thin (`studio.tailCurl` of `tools/mesh-rigs/dog.json`, in place of `tailBind`). After the root, the offset of each tail vertex from the line shrinks (`thin`, a new rule of `tail_curl()`), because the tail of the source is a wedge 0.18 units high at its root. `2026-10-28-tails.md` gives the factor | 4.4 |
| Each tail vertex moves by the smoothstep of its tail share from 0.3 to 0.8 (`blend`). The side view of the haunch is 0.52 cm above the seat top, and the chair of the app is 0.263 m deep (0.255 m before). With the blend from 0.1 to 0.7, the root moved back, and the chair was 0.277 m deep. With 0.5 to 0.95, the haunch was 1.15 cm above the seat | 4.4 |
| A thinner tail by the weights alone (radius 0.05) kept the thick root on the hips and lowered the seat of the app from 26.5 cm to 21.4 cm, so the tail is thin by its form | 4.4 |
| In the app, the tail goes 0.0 cm into the seat, the back posts, the rail, the body, and the cello, and its lowest point is 14.5 cm above the floor. No tail face folds, and the tail sways | 4.4, 5.1 |
