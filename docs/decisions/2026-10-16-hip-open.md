# Decisions of the seated posture of the red fox and the rabbit

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-16, the user said these things about the seated red fox and rabbit, in this order:

1. At 13:11, with a crop of the side view of the red fox: "the angle between the torso and the
   upper leg is too unnatural".
2. After the images of a trunk that reclines toward the chair back by 10°, 20°, and 30°, the user
   first chose 20°, and then said: "actually, +10%, but the chair can be taller, and then the
   angle can be more natural, right?"
3. After the images of a seat that tilts forward under thighs that slope down: "essentially, the
   animals' back should be straight (or correct me if that's not the case for professional
   instrument players while sitting). But the chair can be taller so that the angle is more
   natural".
4. After the images of an upright trunk on a tilted seat: "seat should not be tilted".
5. After the images of three level seats, the user chose the variant L15: thighs 15° down on a
   short level seat.
6. After the images of L15: "there's an obvious fold/gap between the trunk and the thigh, can that
   be made less obvious?"

The manager of the session and the agents of the branch `hip-open` decided the other rows. The
report [`2026-10-16-hip-open.md`](../research/2026-10-16-hip-open.md) gives the measures, the
probes, and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds
it, and remove its row.
