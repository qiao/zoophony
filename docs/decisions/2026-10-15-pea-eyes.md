# Decisions of the pea eyes of the studio bodies

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-15 the user asked for the pea eyes of the parametric bodies on all animals. The manager
of the session wrote the brief, and the agent of the branch `pea-eyes` decided the rows below. The
manager accepted the size, the direction, the place, and the triangle rule after the mid-report,
and asked for the move of the bear eyes and the colors of the old eyes. The report
[`2026-10-16-pea-eyes.md`](../research/2026-10-16-pea-eyes.md) gives the measures, the probes,
and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The four studio bodies (the bear, the polar bear, the rabbit, and the fox mesh of both foxes) have the pea eyes of the parametric bodies: the oval, the finish, the light, and the blink of `addEye()` of `models/eyes.ts`. The parametric species keep their eyes, byte for byte. | 4.4, 4.5 |
| A pea eye has 1.0 times the size of the oval of `addEye()` in the head height of the head that shows (`StudioEyes.headHeight`): 0.313 m on the bear, 0.361 m on the polar bear, 0.323 m on the rabbit, and 0.386 m on the foxes, whose rig grows the head by 1.2. The sizes 0.8 and 1.2 were rendered beside the cat. At 1.0 the eye fills about the same part of the head as the eye of the cat, and the manager chose it. | 4.5 |
| `pnpm studio-body` drops the eyes of the mesh: the gloss shells of the head out of the middle plane (`eyeVertices()`). The rig GLB files keep their gloss fans as the marks of the eyes, so the export finds the eyes of all four bodies with one rule. | 4.4 |
| Each eye bone starts 0.01 H of the eyes into the fur of the head, at the nearest point to the middle of the old eye (`eyeOnHead()`, `eyeMiddle()`), as on a parametric head. The final bone lies 0.003 H to 0.031 H from the middle of the old eye, so the rule of the brief for a bone more than 0.1 H away never applies. | 4.4 |
| Each eye faces out from the middle of the box of the head that shows (`StudioEyes.directions`). The brief asked for the normal of the head surface. The mean normal of the faces within half the eye height faces 39° up on the bear. Along it, the muzzle hid the inner half of each eye in the front view, and the light floated 0.024 H in front of the oval. The other bodies turn by 3° to 16°. | 4.4 |
| Each eye moves out along its direction, in steps of 0.001 H, until each corner of the oval more than a quarter of its depth in front of its middle lies on the outer side of the nearest face of the head (`eyePush()`). The rim stays in the head, as on a parametric eye. The bear eyes move 0.046 H, the polar bear eyes 0.009 H, the left fox eye 0.010 H, and the rabbit eyes and the right fox eye 0. A rule for the rim too needed 0.082 H on the bear and floated the edge of the bead 0.107 H out of the face. | 4.4 |
| After the palette of `pnpm rig-mesh`, the faces of each old eye take the color with the largest area in the ring of faces that share a corner with them (`recolor_old_eyes()`). The old eye is the dark faces and each face whose middle lies within the radius of their edge, seen along the normal of the eye, and within half that radius of its plane (`reach` 1 of `eyeShell`). This removes the cream white of the almond eyes of the foxes and the brown lid shading under the right fox eye. A majority of the direct neighbors brought the brown into the right eye. The palette and each other face keep their colors. | 4.4 |
| A part of the mesh of a studio player on an eye bone of the player follows the eye bone of the studio (`studioRenderMesh()`), because the scene scales only the eye bones of the studio in a blink. | 4.7, 5.1 |
| The budget of 3,200 triangles of a studio body counts the faces of its GLB file. The pea eyes add their 144 triangles, as the eyes of a parametric body count in its budget of 2,600. The app body of the rabbit has 3,299 triangles, the foxes 3,255, the polar bear 2,232, and the bear 2,392, against 3,176, 3,135, 2,608, and 2,768 on `main`. The scene of `perf-60.mid` with 60 players has 175,315 triangles of 200,000, against 174,249 on `main`. | 4.5 |
| The closed body of the sweeps of the rabbit and the foxes has 4 open loops (`MESH_BODY_LOOPS` of `pose/test-sweep-roles.ts`), because the export drops the eye fans. | 9.3 |
