# Decisions of the cleanup of the tests and the design

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-08, the user asked which tests and which design rules bring more harm than good, and
approved their removal or relaxation. The agent of branch `cleanup-tests-design` decided the
details below. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The limit of the shot plan in `shots/shots-speed.test.ts` is 11, 2 times the ratio 5.5 of 2026-10-08 on the development Mac (14.4 ms for the Boulanger demo). The bodies of 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 block more lines of sight: one Boulanger plan tests 2,317 segments in place of 1,149, and 1,661 boxes in place of 419. Two speed changes with the same plans took the plan from 16.9 ms back to 14.4 ms. The limit is about 29 ms on that Mac, above the 20 ms of the target, so a measure on that Mac checks the target. This row replaces the row of the limit 6.4 of `2026-10-07-entry-shots.md` | 9.7 |
| The layout tests pin no stage side. Each stage of a demo, of the choir test file, and of `perf-60.mid` can grow by at most 3% over its side of 2026-10-08, and the stage of the reference composition by 3% over the side of its rule (`STAGE_GROWTH` of `models/layout-scene.test.ts`). The default view frames the whole stage, so a larger stage shows each player smaller, and a new body moved a side by less than 1%. The near corner of `perf-60.mid` can be 0.25 of the side (was 0.21, with the value 0.205). This row replaces the row of the stage sides of `2026-10-05-animal-views.md` and the row of the stage of `perf-60.mid` of `2026-10-05-animal-views-round-3.md` | 4.5 |
| No test pins a hash of the skeleton of a species. A deliberate change of the proportions changed the hash each time, and the agent copied the new hash from the diff, so the test found no fault. The tests of the behavior (the arm joints, the clearance, the bow, and the hidden heads) check the skeletons. This row replaces the row of `skeleton-snapshot.test.ts` of `2026-10-07-proportions.md` | 4.7, 9.3 |
| The seat arc of 0.95 m is the least arc of a seated player in the layout. It sets no limit on the width of a body: the arc between two neighbors grows until their footprints keep 0.05 m apart. The test of the width of each body in the bind pose goes, and the layout test that keeps all footprints of each layout apart checks the risk of review of step 3, F12. A layout with the seat arc alone makes that test fail. This row replaces the row of the large-flap ear at 32° of `2026-10-04-design-session.md`, and the clauses on the seat arc in the rows of the large-flap ear of `2026-10-07-heads.md` and of the polar bear and the hippo of `2026-10-07-proportions.md`. Their values stay | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The shares of N4 (60% of the section and solo cameras at 30° or lower, 15% above 40°) are a rule of the planner. A test checks them on the layouts of the demos spread 3 times, where no player blocks a camera and each camera stays at 30° or lower. The plans of the demos keep only the limit of 50°. Seed 1 gave 63.5% and 14.1%, and seeds 2 and 3 failed, so each new body moved the result across the limits, and the dog and the fox tail were tuned to keep it. This row replaces the clauses on the shares of N4 in the rows of the tail of the red fox and the head center of the dog of `2026-10-07-proportions.md` | 5.6 |
| The tests of the forms of the parametric body go: `trunk.test.ts`, `legs.test.ts`, `snouts.test.ts`, `biped.test.ts`, `head.test.ts`, `arms.test.ts`, and `eyes.test.ts`, and the tests of the neck band of the bodies in `shading.test.ts`. They pinned the sizes, the rings, the color borders, and the skin weights of a look that the user rejected, and each change of the look edited them. The 7 species that still use the parametric body lose this protection, as the user accepted. The tests of the sides and of the mouth move to `species.test.ts` for each body first. The unit tests of `shadeMesh()` on plain parts stay in `shading.test.ts`, because each body uses the module and a unit test goes beside its module. This row replaces the row of the tests that pin a skeleton value of `2026-10-07-proportions.md` | 9.3 |
