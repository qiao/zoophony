# Decisions of the seat glow

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-21 the user asked for a way to show which seat of the tab "Seats" plays. The user chose
the dot glow: "The fur-color dot gets a soft ring in its own color while the seat plays. The ring
comes on fast and fades over about 0.3 s, so short notes do not flicker. With reduced motion, the
ring is on or off with no fade. It costs no layout space." The agent of the branch `seat-glow`
decided these rows. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its
row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "soft ring in its own color". A ring of 2 px with a gap of 2 px and a soft shadow of 6 px. The level sets its opacity and a scale from 0.8 to 1 (`.dot::after` in `ui/ui.css`). | 2.8 |
| The notes of all players of a seat that overlap or touch become one span with the level of its loudest note. A binary search finds the span at the song time, so each frame costs about 0.25 µs for 12 seats (`seatSpans()`, `seatLevels()`). | 2.8 |
| A note gives the level 0.5 at the velocity 0 and 1 at the velocity 127, so a soft seat still shows a clear ring (`GLOW_FLOOR`). | 2.8 |
| The fade of 0.3 s is in song time, as the meter of the demo row. At the speed 0.5, it takes 0.6 s (`GLOW_FADE_SEC`). | 2.8 |
| A dark fur mixes with `--text` until its ring keeps 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) on the hover row over white fur. Only the red fox, the bear, the beaver, the monkey, and the penguin change (`ringColor()`). | 2.8 |
| With reduced motion, the ring is on at the level 0.05 or more, so it stays on for nearly all of the fade (`GLOW_ON` in `ui/seat-list.ts`). | 2.8 |
