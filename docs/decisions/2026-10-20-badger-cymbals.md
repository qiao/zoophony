# Decisions of the cymbals of the badger mesh

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

Round `mesh-badger` left the cymbals open: the short arms of the badger mesh held the cymbals of
the app (0.40 m) 11 cm into the body, and missed each crash by up to 84 mm. The user rule is to
keep a mesh and fit the props. The user slept, so the agent of the round `badger-cymbals` took
each choice by the rules of the brief. The report
[`2026-10-20-badger-cymbals.md`](../research/2026-10-20-badger-cymbals.md) gives the measures. The
user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| A species can play smaller hand cymbals (`SpeciesParams.cymbalScale`), and a hold can turn both plates (`PERCUSSION_PLACES.plateTurn`). No species sets them. | 4.6, 5.3 |
| Hand cymbals fail on the badger: with the smallest real size, 14 inches (0.356 m), each of 16 crash places in reach puts the plates 7.6 cm or more into the body, and misses each crash by 44 mm or more. A tilt of 20° to 70° makes both worse. | 4.6, 5.3 |
| So the badger plays a suspended cymbal (`SpeciesParams.cymbalForm`): one cymbal 0.40 m wide (about 16 inches), level on a stand 0.42 m in front, with its rim 0.5 m high (`SUSPENDED_CYMBAL`). | 4.6 |
| The two yarn mallets take the grip of the snare sticks, and the paws strike in turn 1.3 cm in from the edge, opposite each other, as three of four sources give (`SUSPENDED_CYMBAL_HOLD`). 20° toward the player put the upper arms 2.6 cm into the body and folded them. | 5.3 |
| The homes of the mallets move out to 0.6 H: no play fold, upper arms 2.3 cm in the body (`BADGER_UPPER_ARMS` accepts 2.5 cm). | 5.3 |
| The agent accepted the paw crops while the user slept (`arms-*.webp`), and set the grips of the cymbalist to the play median (`ACCEPTED_GRIPS`). | 4.4, 9.8 |
