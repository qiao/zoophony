# Decisions of the wolf and the mouse rests

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, after the clarinet rest on the right knee, the user said: "for the wolf, in rest
mode, the instrument should be vertical". The round `sixth-wolf-vertical` changed the rest of the
clarinet. The report [`2026-10-27-wolf-mouse-rests.md`](../research/2026-10-27-wolf-mouse-rests.md)
gives the measures. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its
row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "for the wolf, in rest mode, the instrument should be vertical". The clarinet stands upright in front of the belly between the knees, the bell at the height of the knee tops, 4 cm right of the middle and 22 cm in front of the seat, and both paws keep their grips of the play (`rest`, `restShift` of `WOLF_CLARINET`). | 5.2 |
| The bell rests on nothing. The belly reaches the seat front, the right knee top put the left forearm 7 cm to 12 cm into the belly with each left elbow, and a bell nearer to the belly put it 1.8 cm to 3.6 cm in. | 5.2 |
