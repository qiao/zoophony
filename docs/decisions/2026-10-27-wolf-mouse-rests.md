# Decisions of the wolf and the mouse rests

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, after the clarinet rest on the right knee, the user said: "for the wolf, in rest
mode, the instrument should be vertical", and "for the mouse, in rest mode, their two hands are
overlapping". The round `sixth-wolf-vertical` changed the rest of the clarinet and of the oboe.
The report [`2026-10-27-wolf-mouse-rests.md`](../research/2026-10-27-wolf-mouse-rests.md) gives
the measures. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "for the wolf, in rest mode, the instrument should be vertical". The clarinet stands upright in front of the belly, the bell between the knees at their height, and both paws keep their grips of the play (`rest`, `restShift` of `WOLF_CLARINET`). | 5.2 |
| The bell rests on nothing. The belly reaches the seat front, the right knee top put the left forearm 7 cm to 12 cm into the belly with each left elbow, and a bell nearer to the belly put it 1.8 cm to 3.6 cm in. | 5.2 |
| The user: "for the mouse, in rest mode, their two hands are overlapping". Both paws keep their grips of the play at rest, with a gap of 5.7 cm, so that no paw slides along the oboe in a move (`MOUSE_OBOE` has no rest grips). | 5.2 |
| The rest oboe stands 0.25 H below the mouth (0.45 H before). Lower, an arm went 1.3 cm to 1.9 cm into the body (`restShift`). | 5.2 |
| At rest, the left elbow points out, down, and forward, and the right elbow out and down, so that the left paw turns 131° and the right paw 113° on its forearm, against 143° and 121° before (`restPoleL`, `restPoleR`). | 5.3 |
