# Decisions of the arms, the cello size, and the tail of the dog

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

After the snapshot of the dog cellist of 2026-10-17, the user said: "look at the snapshots, the dogs
arms are way too long and unnatural", and then: "the tail seem unnatural too". The manager showed
the variants of the first phase of the round `dog-arms`, and the user chose:

1. Arms: "1.35 + 1/2 cello (Recommended)". The option said: "Arm 0.92 of the trunk, as the fox
   (0.91). The bow arm stays bent near the body. The paw reaches the whole fingerboard and 60% of
   the bow hair. The cello is a real 1/2 size, 14% shorter."
2. Tail: "T3, hang down (Recommended)". The option said: "The tail hangs down behind the seat
   between the back posts, with a soft bend. Clean from the back, with no contact."

Before, the user chose "Cello reach (Recommended)", with the option text "Long enough for the whole
fingerboard and the whole bow, as a real cellist (about 1.4x at the fox head height)", and the fit
went to the arm factor 1.75 with no new question (`2026-10-17-mesh-dog.md`). The report
[`2026-10-17-dog-arms.md`](../research/2026-10-17-dog-arms.md) gives the measures, the probes, and
the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove
its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user, choice 1: the dog plays a 1/2 cello and its bow, 0.86 of the cello of the app (`SpeciesParams.celloScale`). A real 1/2 cello is 0.87 of the total length of a 4/4 cello (section 2.7 of `docs/reference/string-technique.md`). The end pin of the smaller cello is longer, so that the top of its body stays against the chest (`endPinLength()`). The bow scales with the cello, as the violin bow scales with `violinScale` | 4.6 |
| The user, choice 1: the arm factor of the dog rig is 1.35 (`tools/mesh-rigs/dog.json`). The reach from the shoulder joint to the middle of the palm is 0.451 m, and the arm is 0.92 of the trunk from the hips joint to the shoulder joints: the red fox has 0.91, the cat 1.15, and the rabbit 1.25. The factor 1.75 of `2026-10-17-mesh-dog.md` gave 0.571 m and 1.16, the largest stretch of a mesh arm of the app (the red fox 1.21, the cat 1.15, the rabbit 1.63). A real adult has an arm of 0.332 of the height to the wrist and a trunk of 0.288 (Drillis and Contini, in Winter, fig. 4.1) | 4.4, 4.6 |
| The user, choice 2: the seated tail hangs down behind the seat between the back posts of the chair, with a soft bend: the root 50° and the tip 80.5° below the level (`studio.tailBind`). Its lowest point is 14.0 cm above the floor, and in the app it goes into no back post, no seat, and no body. The tail of `2026-10-17-mesh-dog.md` went back over the seat 10° below the level and stuck out 31.2 cm behind the seat. The hanging tail sways, as a tail that does not lie on the seat | 4.4 |
| The hanging tail takes the vertices behind the plane z = -0.09 units (`weights.tail.behind`, -0.06 before), so that the bottom of the thick root follows the hips, and the sit counts the root shares 0.8 to 0.98 as the haunch (`sit.root`) and keeps the seat top 0.2665 units (`sit.top`). With the rule of the old tail, the side view of the haunch was 3.30 cm above the seat top, and 9 faces of the root turned over. Now the side view is 0.39 cm above it, and no face turns over after the crease (`rig-mesh-check-1.log`, `sit-grid.txt`) | 4.4 |
| The hold of the short arms (`DOG_HOLD`): the trunk leans 3° forward, the right elbow points out and down at the frog, (1, -1.2, -0.3), pizzicato plucks over the fingerboard (`pluckPlace` -0.3), and the rest bow moves 0.3 H out in the lift. With the hold of the arm factor 1.75, 92% of the play samples folded, and the right forearm went 5.0 cm into the cello. With this hold, 11.0% and 4.1 cm (`fit-all-1.txt`). A lean of 6° put the cello 2.2 cm into the body | 5.3 |
| The refit of the short arms (`DOG_HOLD`): no hair within 0.12 m of the frog (`frogHair`, the new rule of each hold, 0 on the others), string planes 6° apart (12° before), the left paw tilted 55° (60°), the tip elbow out and down, (1, -0.5, -0.5), and pizzicato at `pluckPlace` -0.6 with the elbow forward. The bow paw reaches 0.120 m to 0.445 m of 0.479 m, 78% of the hair between the free ends (`fit-final.txt`) | 5.3 |
