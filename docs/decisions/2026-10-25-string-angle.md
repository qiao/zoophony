# Decisions of the angle of the violin and the viola

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

The manager asked to hold the violins and the viola near 45° to the left of the front, as a real
player, with no arm in the body or the instrument: first with the technique of a real player,
then with a smaller viola, and with no longer arms. The report
[`2026-10-25-string-angle.md`](../research/2026-10-25-string-angle.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.
