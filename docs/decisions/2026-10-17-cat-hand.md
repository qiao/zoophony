# Decisions of the left arm of the cat violist in high positions

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

The round `left-hand` left one point open
([`2026-10-17-left-hand.md`](2026-10-17-left-hand.md)): the violas of `mozart-k191` end in the
fifth position, where the left forearm of the cat went 2.7 cm into the viola near the wrist, also
while the arms lowered after the last note. The manager gave the round `cat-hand` these goals: the
left forearm and the left upper arm of the cat at most 0.8 cm into the viola in each sample set,
the other depths at most `main` + 0.5 cm, the folds at most `main` + 1 point, `stopRange` as on
`main`, and only the poses of the cat change. The agent of the branch decided the rows. The report
[`2026-10-17-cat-hand.md`](../research/2026-10-17-cat-hand.md) gives the measures and the probes.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.
