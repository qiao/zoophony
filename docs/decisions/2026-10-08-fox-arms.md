# Decisions of the fox arms

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-08, the agent of branch `fox-arms` decided the details below. The work puts the
improved route B fox of [`2026-10-08-fox-b-model.md`](../research/2026-10-08-fox-b-model.md)
into the app, gives the red fox the full bow, gives the paws to the hand bones, and moves the
sheet moment of the views page to the middle of the bow. The user can reverse each decision.
Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The paw of a rigged body is the part of the forearm and the hand past the wrist, moved to the rest skeleton by the skin of the arm bones. The finger block is its middle, and the mouth is 0.12 H below and 0.03 H behind the tip of the nose. This row replaces the paw row of `2026-10-08-fox-b-app.md` | 4.4, 4.7 |
| The right palm of a rigged body follows the bow stick: in the rest skeleton, the stick runs along z at the height of the grip above the middle of the paw, and keeps 0.02 H (`GRIP_GAP`) from the forearm and the hand. The grip is the point of the stick nearest to them. A palm 0.1 H under the farthest point of the paw left the stick 0.042 H from the slim paw of the improved fox, and a gap at the grip alone put the frog end of the stick 0.013 H into it | 4.4 |
| The left palm stays 0.1 H (`PALM_INSET`) under the farthest point of the paw on the palm side. A left palm on the rule of the stick came 0.004 m under the strings (limit 0.005 m) | 4.4 |
| A bow arm that does not reach the full hair with the hold alone gets the tip help (`TipHelp` in `pose/bowed-rig.ts`): toward the tip, the right shoulder bone turns up to 30° further forward, and the bow slants up to 20° further with the frog to the right shoulder. The help grows evenly over the free hair from 0 at the frog, and fades out in pizzicato. `bowedRig()` scales the largest help in 10 even steps and keeps the first step with which the arm reaches the full hair. This row replaces the row of the bow range of 0.285 m of `2026-10-08-fox-b-app.md` | 5.3 |
| A turn or a lean of the torso does not help the bow arm of the violin, because the violin rests on the chest and moves with the shoulder | 5.3 |
| The search of the bow range keeps the elbow direction of the frog and one roll of the hand. With the elbow direction of the pose and three rolls, the rabbit and the cat also needed a help of 3°, and their poses would change. Thus the arm at the end of the range can use up to 98% of its length in the pose | 5.3 |
| Each paw of the route B fox follows only its hand bone: the part mask of `rig.py` allows only the hand bone for the paw parts. The end of the forearm bends at the wrist. A test pose turns both hands by 40° | 4.4 |
