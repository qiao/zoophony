# Decisions of the fox arms

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-08, the agent of branch `fox-arms` decided the details below. The work puts the
improved route B fox of [`2026-10-08-fox-b-model.md`](../research/2026-10-08-fox-b-model.md)
into the app, gives the red fox the full bow, gives the paws to the hand bones, and moves the
sheet moment of the views page to the middle of the bow. The user can reverse each decision.
Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| A turn or a lean of the torso does not help the bow arm of the violin, because the violin rests on the chest and moves with the shoulder | 5.3 |
| The search of the bow range keeps the elbow direction of the frog and one roll of the hand. With the elbow direction of the pose and three rolls, the rabbit and the cat also needed a help of 3°, and their poses would change. Thus the arm at the end of the range can use up to 98% of its length in the pose | 5.3 |
| The sheet pose of the violin moves it 0.25 H to the left and 0.05 H up from the hold of the stage, turns it 25° toward the front, and tilts the scroll up 15°. Near the frog, 0.1 s into the stroke, the bow paw of the red fox came 0.007 H from its head.
