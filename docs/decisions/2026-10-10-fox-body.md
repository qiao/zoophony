# Decisions of the fox body

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-10, the user set the goal: "make the fox model look like the reference image", and
permitted a change of the rules and the tests where the goal needs it. The agent of the fox body
decided these rows. The report [`2026-10-10-fox-body.md`](../research/2026-10-10-fox-body.md)
gives the measures and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section
that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The red fox and the tan fox have a body of their own (`SpeciesParams.body: 'fox'`, `models/fox/`) in place of the parametric biped. Code builds it on the bones of 4.7, so the pose code drives it, and it takes the stance of each seat. The other species keep the parametric body | 4.3, 4.4 |
| The tan fox takes the fox body with its own colors, because design 4.3 gives both foxes the same form. `fox-views.png` shows only the red fox | 4.3 |
| The foxes have the skeleton of `fox-views.png` (`FOX_PROPORTIONS`): `H` 0.3 m, the head center 2.33 H and the shoulder joints 1.43 H above the seat. The default skeleton put the head center lower, the shoulders lower, and the head larger than the sheet | 4.3 |
| The red fox has the colors of `fox-views.png`, from samples of the sheet page: fur `#e8773c`, white `#ecd8c4`, and dark brown `#5a3826`. This row replaces the colors of 2026-10-06 of the red fox of `2026-10-09-body-revert.md`. The tan fox keeps its colors | 4.5 |
| The fox body paints its own white and dark regions: the face mask, the bib, the inner ears, and the tail tip in the belly color, and the gloves, the socks, the paws, the ear tips, and the ear backs in the accent color. The accent parts of 4.5 do not change the fox body | 4.4, 4.5 |
| The fox has the eyes of the sheet: a white almond, a brown iris, a pupil, a light, and a dark lid line, all on the eye bone. The other species keep the black eyes | 4.5 |
| The seated tail of the fox lies on the seat and hangs over its right side, as in the front view of the sheet. The back view of the sheet shows it behind the chair, and the front view wins. The brush stays out of the back post of the chair | 4.4 |
| The upper chest of the fox is about 0.03 m less deep than the sheet, and the torso leans forward 0.08 H at the neck. With the depth of the sheet, the left arm of the tan fox went 0.057 H into its chest while it played the flute, and the flute test failed | 4.4, 5.3 |
| The standing fox has the standing base 1.05 H and a standing thigh of 0.55 H. With the legs of the seated fox, the solo flute of K. 315 hid 3% and 5% of the head of a first violin | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The views page has the key `matte` (`?sheet=fox&matte`), and `pnpm sheet-check` compares the silhouette with the matte of the sheet in `tools/sheet-mattes/` | 7.1 |
