# Decisions of the oboe hands

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-25, the user asked if the animals hold the wind instruments in the opposite direction.
The hand order of each wind is correct, but the two paws of the mouse lay together on the lower
half of the oboe, 17.4 cm and 22.3 cm from the reed. The manager asked to move the left paw up to
the upper joint. The report [`2026-10-25-oboe-hands.md`](../research/2026-10-25-oboe-hands.md)
gives the measures. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its
row.
