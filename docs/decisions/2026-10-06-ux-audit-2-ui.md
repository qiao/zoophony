# Decisions of the UI findings of the second UX audit

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-06, the agent of the UI findings of the second UX audit
([`audits/2026-10-06-ux-design-2.md`](../audits/2026-10-06-ux-design-2.md)) decided the details
below. The user can reverse each one. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| While the first screen shows, a message sits 12 px above the load ring of the play button, beside the open drawer it moves left with the first screen, and above the open sheet of a phone it keeps its place above the bar (N12) | 2.8 |
| The sliders: a track of 4 px in the new token `--track`, an accent fill and an accent thumb of 14 px, a thumb 1.2 times larger on a hover, a dim thumb on a disabled seek bar, and the speed menu with the icon "collapse" turned down (N13) | 2.8 |
| On a screen below 800 px high with an aspect of at least 7:5, the first screen stands at the right of the stage, 24 px from it and from the window edge, a message stands above the bar at its left, and beside the open drawer the first screen fills the gap with no intro, or hides in a gap below 100 px (N14, FI3) | 2.8 |
| Before the file of the first piece arrives, the bar hides its times and its seek bar, and keeps their places (N15) | 2.8 |
| The turning 15% of the load ring stays until the bytes pass 15%, in place of until the first byte (N16) | 2.8 |
| The speed menu and the volume slider have the tooltips "Speed" and "Volume", with no key (N17) | 2.8 |
| A seat with one player takes a singular name, one singer of the choir is "Voice", the soloist that does not sit at a keyboard is "{name} soloist" (the audit proposed "Solo flute", and the solo button would read "Solo Solo flute"), and the numbers count the seats of a section with no soloist (N20) | 2.8 |
| After 10 s of load, the load text adds the megabytes, "({arrived} of {sum} MB)", with megabytes of 1,000,000 bytes and one decimal, counted from the start of the load of the samples (N22) | 2.8 |
| At the song end, the play button of the bar shows "Play again" with the icon "replay" (a circle arrow). The bar shows no next demo, because that is a user-visible change for the user to decide (AM2) | 2.8 |
