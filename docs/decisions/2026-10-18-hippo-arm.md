# Decisions of the twisted bow arm of the hippo

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-18, the user said: "the hippo's arm is twisted and deformed while playing", with an image
of the right arm of a contrabass hippo from its right side. The user had asked for no questions on
the hippo ("use your recommended settings instead of asking me questions"), so the manager chose.
The report [`2026-10-18-hippo-arm.md`](../research/2026-10-18-hippo-arm.md) gives the measures. The
user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| A bowed hold gives the far side of the stick for the roll of the bow hand (`BowedHold.farSideR`). Each other hold keeps 180°, and the poses of the 17 other species do not change (`pose-hash.txt`). | 5.3 |
| The hippo moves the far side to 30°, in the middle of the elbow angles that its play never takes: the jumps of the paw above 90° fall from 847 to 20 (`HIPPO_HOLD`). | 5.3 |
| Accepted: on the E and A strings, the paw goes more than 1.5 cm into the belly in 4.6% of the play samples, and the forearm in 12% (6.4% before) (`depth-count-far.txt`). | 5.3, 9.3 |
| Rejected: other right elbows (up and back folds 18 to 21 faces in each sample, against 12), a turned bow grip, an elbow that turns with the string plane, and a start roll. Each one left the far elbows or the jumps. | 5.3 |
| Open: the bow slant of -10° puts fewer elbows on the far side (672 against 3,709), but it turns the paw 54° on the forearm (20° now). | 5.3 |
