# Decisions of the arm skin of the hippo and the elephant

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, after four arm rounds, the user said the first quote below. The round `arm-skin`
measured the twist and the collapse of the arm skin, tried three remedies, and the user chose two.
The report [`2026-10-19-arm-skin.md`](../research/2026-10-19-arm-skin.md) gives the measures. The
user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "the hippo and elephants' arms are still twisted and deformed when playing. Take screenshots and see for yourself." A fold count does not see this, so `pnpm arm-shape` measures the twist, the flat share, and the spikes of each arm. | 4.4 |
| The user chose "B + C": a twist bone of each upper arm, and new holds with lower elbows. Two refit rounds turn them on for the hippo and the elephant. | 4.4, 5.3 |
| The twist bone takes a share of the roll of the upper arm from the chest, and the weight of the upper arm moves to it from 0.1 to 0.9 of the arm (`StudioBody.upperTwist`, `UPPER_TWIST_SPAN`). | 4.4, 4.7 |
| The share comes from `upperTwist.share` of the config of the mesh rig. The rig solves the shapes of `raise` with the twist bone, so a refit fits them to the skin of the app. | 4.4 |
| Rejected: the dual quaternion blend (A). It kept the girth, but it left the twist, bulged the hippo shoulder to 1.28, and needed a shader change. | 4.4 |
| Open: the hippo hold C (right elbow `[1,-1.5,-0.5]`) puts the right forearm 8.2 cm into the body. Its refit round scans the pole first. | 5.3, 9.3 |
| Open: each variant folds most samples until the refit, because the shapes and the rigid weights fit the old holds and the old weights. | 4.4 |
