# Decisions of the bent pea eyes

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-20 the user sent a close view of the rabbit head and wrote: "the eyes are popping out.
Also, the bear's eyes are too far from the head". The agent of the round decided the rows below.
The report [`2026-10-20-pea-eyes.md`](../research/2026-10-20-pea-eyes.md) gives the measures,
the probes, and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and
remove its row.

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Section  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| The user: "the eyes are popping out". Each pea eye of a studio body bends along its direction to a fit of the head surface (`eyeBend()`, `StudioEyes.bends`). The rims lie 0.009 H to 0.016 H out of the fur, against 0.012 H to 0.072 H on `main`.                                                                                                                                                                          | 4.4, 4.5 |
| Each eye keeps its direction, out from the middle of the head or level (`eyeLevel`), so the look and the outline along the direction stay. The bend takes the slope between the direction and the surface, up to 24°.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | 4.4      |
| A bent eye has its middle on the head surface (`eyeAt` of `studioBody()`). At 0.01 H under it, as on a parametric head, the facets between the corners of the oval covered a third of the front, and the eyes looked torn.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | 4.4      |
| The light of a bent eye lies on the front of the oval, 0.035 H up the oval (`addBentLight()`), so it stays on an eye that faces up or to the side. It is a disc 0.04 H wide and 0.008 H deep, flat on the oval there: the old ball did not turn with the eye and stood up to 0.017 H out of a side eye (user: "the white dot of the eye is still protruding too much"). The parametric eyes keep their light, byte for byte. | 4.5      |
