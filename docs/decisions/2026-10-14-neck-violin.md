# Decisions of the neck of the red fox, the violins at the neck, and the seat place

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the user looked at the app after the merge of the branch `fox-rabbit-form` and
said: "fox neck too long", "fox and rabbit violin too small and not touching their neck", and
"fox and rabbit are sitting on the edge of the chair. they should move inside a little bit". The
manager of the session and the agent of the branch `neck-violin` decided the other rows. The
report [`2026-10-14-neck-violin.md`](../research/2026-10-14-neck-violin.md) gives the measures and
the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.
