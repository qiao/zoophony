# Decisions of the seated cat and the standing bow

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

After the images of the merged cat mesh of 2026-10-16, the user chose these options of the
manager, in this order:

1. Fur color: "Gray on stage (Recommended)". The option said: "Make the fur and the belly a little
   cooler, so that the cat looks gray under the stage light, as on your sheet and as the old cat
   looked."
2. Seated tail: "Curl on the seat (Recommended)". The option said: "The tail lies on the seat and
   curls around beside the haunch, as on a sitting cat. Nothing touches the floor behind the
   chair."
3. Standing bow: "Hang down, as the cat (Recommended)". The option said: "The bow hangs from the
   right paw with the tip down. It stops crossing the face in the front view." This is for the
   standing fox and rabbit soloists.

After the mid-report of the branch `cat-seat`, the user chose "T1, curl left (Recommended)" for
the seated tail and "Half step (Recommended)" for the gray of the fur. The agent of the branch
decided the other rows. The report [`2026-10-16-cat-seat.md`](../research/2026-10-16-cat-seat.md)
gives the measures, the probes, and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update
the section that holds it, and remove its row.
