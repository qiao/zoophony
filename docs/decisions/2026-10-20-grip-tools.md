# Decisions of the retro of round elephant-grip

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

The agent of round `elephant-grip` spent most of 95 minutes on waits. The manager gave the tool
round `grip-tools` with four items. The user slept, so the agent made each choice below. The user
can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| With no `--demo`, a role that no demo of `FIT_DEMOS` has takes its one demo with the most notes of the role, and not all its demos (`roleNotes()`, `chooseDemos()` of `tools/hold-fit.ts`). The conductor counts the beats. `pnpm hold-fit timpanist` took 4:22 on four demos and 1:47 on `brahms-2`. | 9.8 |
| `pnpm hold-fit --base` with no `--demo` gives that one demo to both children by name, because the hold-fit of an older commit takes all demos of such a role. | 9.8 |
