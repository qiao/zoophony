# Decisions of the front key light

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, the user looked at the default view and at two wide views from the front right,
where the shadows fell toward the camera, and said: "make the key light go from front to back,
instead of back to front, and let the shadows react accordginly". The manager then planned a front
key, beams from rim lights behind the stage, and a low fill. Measures:
[`2026-10-27-key-front.md`](../research/2026-10-27-key-front.md). The user can reverse each
decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "make the key light go from front to back". The key light stands 2.45 S from the stage center, 35° left of the front of the stage axis, 60° high (`KEY_PLACE`). The shadows fall to the back right, beside the players in both views. | 4.5 |
| The side lights give 0.5 lux and the back-row lights 1.5 lux (`SIDE_LIGHT`, `BACK_ROW_LIGHT`), because the key light now lights the fronts and reaches the back rows. | 4.5 |
| Each visible beam comes from a rim light: a spotlight with no shadow at `RIM_PLACE`, 2.45 S behind the stage and 66° high, 5 lux (`RIM_LIGHT`). It aims at the foot of its beam and follows a solo (`aimRimLights()`). | 2.4, 4.5 |
| The rim light is a diffuse term of the vertex shaders (`addRimLight()`): as a spotlight of three.js, or as a term for each pixel, it took the GPU p50 of `perf-60.mid` from 7.93 ms to 8.22 ms. The lit floor goes from 115 to 110. | 4.5, 9.7 |
| One rim light and one beam on the stage axis (`RIM_PLACE.count` 1), as in each reference image. The variants with no beam and with three beams are in the research report. | 4.5 |
| The user, 2026-10-18: "why is the beam not vertical?" A rim light stands at the x of its beam, so each beam stands upright in the frontal view. The tip and the foot of the haze stay (`BEAM_LOOK`). | 4.5 |
| The room haze grows toward the rim light, and the hall floor shows its gloss (`ROOM_HAZE`, `HALL_FLOOR`), because haze scatters most light forward. | 4.5 |
| The soft key of a close shot turns 35° toward the key light, and the kicker stands behind the subject on the side of the nearer stage light: the rim lights or the key light (`shotRig()`). | 4.5, 5.6 |
| The hot spot of the key light on the players and the props has 0.3 of its radii on the stage (`LIGHT_POOL.playerSpot`): the rabbit shot clipped 15% of its white fur, and now 5.3% (2.4% on `main`). | 4.5 |
| The skirt and the lip keep their baked light (`SIDE_GLOW`): the front key light moves the skirt only from a luma of 35 to 37. | 4.5 |
