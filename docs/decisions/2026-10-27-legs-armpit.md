each section.

deer's upper arm are missing pieces (have dent)". The round `fifth-legs` worked on the two faults

can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row. The rows of the leg flips,
the knee smoothing, and the calf rings went to [`2026-10-27-polar-legs-rebuild.md`](2026-10-27-polar-legs-rebuild.md).
