# Decisions of the look of the front key light

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

The round `sixth-key-look` fixed three faults of the front key light of
[`2026-10-27-key-front.md`](2026-10-27-key-front.md) (user, 2026-10-27: "make the key light go
from front to back"): the light fur that clipped in close shots, the darker lit floor, and the
darker back of the conductor. Measures:
[`2026-10-27-key-look.md`](../research/2026-10-27-key-look.md). The user can reverse each
decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The key light gives 5.8 lux and aims at the stage center (`KEY_LIGHT`). With 6.5 lux and the boost of 2.1 on the podium, the rabbit close shot clipped 3.1% of its light fur (2.3% with the key light from behind). The back row gets 0.7 of the key light of the first row. | 4.5 |
| The hot spot of the key light has a boost of 2.5 on the podium (`LIGHT_POOL.spotBoost`). With 0.85, the lit floor beside the podium had a luma of 110 and 106 and the back of the conductor 36, against 118, 131, and 43 with the key light from behind. | 4.5 |
| A solo keeps the hot spot boost of 0.85 (`SOLO_LIGHT.boost`), because the soloed players stand at the center of its hot spot, and the key light from the front lights their white fur. | 2.4, 4.5 |
