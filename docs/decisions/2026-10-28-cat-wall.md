# Decisions of the side walls under the cat arms

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-28, the round `sixth-cat-wall` worked on two faults of the image of the user of
2026-10-27, when the user said "the cat's arm has lumps": a light patch with a crease under the
left arm, and a dark crease at the front of the left arm root. The report
[`2026-10-28-cat-wall.md`](../research/2026-10-28-cat-wall.md) gives the measures and the images.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.
