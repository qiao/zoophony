# Decisions of the straight baton

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-13, the user asked: "why is the conductor's baton bent? should it be straight
instead?". The manager answered that the bend copies the hooked tips of the batons of
`bear-views.png`, and that the two newest reference images show a straight baton. Then the user
said: "do it". The user can reverse the decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "why is the conductor's baton bent? should it be straight instead?", and then "do it". The baton is a straight stick that tapers from the handle to the tip, as the batons of `orchestra-latest.png` and `orchestra-side.png` and as a real baton. Its length, its radii, its handle, and the tip target of the motion do not change. The tip target is at the end of the stick again. The bent tip ended 1.7 cm from it. This row replaces the bend row of `2026-10-10-bear-body.md` | 4.6 |
