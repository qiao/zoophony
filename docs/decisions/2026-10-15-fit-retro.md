# Decisions of the retro of the fix waves

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-15, after the flute rest of the tan fox (`cce64562`), the manager of the session chose
the tool items of the retro of the fix waves of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15. The agent of the branch
`retro-fit` decided the rows below. The report
[`2026-10-15-fit-retro.md`](../research/2026-10-15-fit-retro.md) gives the times and the break
commands. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove
its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| `pnpm hold-fit` has no hill climb (`--climb`). The grid of `--grid` covers the search, and a climb on few demos can overfit. The manager decided it. | 9.3 |
| Each probe JSON that a `--base` compare reads holds the field `version`: `pnpm body-probe`, `pnpm fold-probe`, `pnpm hold-fit`, `pnpm pose-depth`, `pnpm shot-trace`, and `tools/rig-form.ts`. A text with no field has the version 1, the format before the field, so a compare with `main` of a tool that keeps its format goes on. Two versions stop the compare with both versions in the message, after the two probes. | 9.3 |
| The JSON of `pnpm fold-probe` and of `pnpm hold-fit` takes the version 2 on this branch. Until the merge, `--base main` of these tools stops with the message of the versions. The JSON of `pnpm shot-trace` keeps the version 1, because the mark `chosen` is in its text lines. | 9.3 |
| The form tables of `pnpm rig-mesh --check` run `tools/rig-form.ts` of the working tree in the probe tree of `main`, in place of the script of `main`. The script imports only the code of the app and the demo patches, so both tables have the same measures. A script that imports a function that `main` does not have fails, and the tables then have no values of `main`. | 9.3 |
| The hold anchors of a player are the head center and the head height H of `BodyBones`, the nose of the studio body, and the box of the corners of the bind mesh on the bones of the instrument, all of the seated player. A point moves by its distance, a box by the larger move of its two corners, and H by its change. The mark `MOVED` starts above 1 mm. | 9.3 |
| `--roles` of `pnpm rig-mesh --check` also limits the reach of `pnpm hold-reach` and the players of the form tables to its roles. A role that the animal does not have stops the tool before the first rig. | 9.3 |
| The turn probe of `pnpm fold-probe --turns <face>` takes the upper arm of the side of the middle of the face in the bind pose: the left side at x < 0. The turn of the upper arm in the axes of the chest is the turn of its skin matrix with the turn of the skin matrix of the chest taken off. The mean turn is the sum of the quaternions with the sign nearest to the first, made a unit quaternion. The bins of the angle from the mean are 10° wide. | 4.4 |
| The split of the folds of a sample set is the split of the sample with the most folded faces, the first such sample, by the end of the name of the main bone: `_L`, `_R`, and the middle. | 4.4 |
| `CutTrace.chosen` is the index in `candidates` of the candidate whose try found the camera, and `null` for a hold, a conductor fill, or a wide fill. The plans of all demos keep their JSON, byte for byte. | 5.6 |
| The rest place of a free paw (`WindHold.restFreeL`) in `pnpm hold-reach` and `pnpm hold-fit` is the middle of the palm at its place on the thigh, with the chest in its bind pose, as for the grips. Its reach share is its distance from the shoulder joint over the arm to the middle of the palm. The mark starts above 1. A standing player has no share, because its arm hangs. | 4.6 |
| The instrument part of `pnpm hold-fit` (`HELD_PART`) holds the bones `instrument` and `slide`, and only its pair with the body counts. The column gives the pair nearest to its limit over all sample sets. The roles with the part hold a violin, a viola, or a wind instrument. | 9.3 |
| The gap of a free paw in `pnpm hold-fit` is measured in the rest set of each seated player only, because the paw of a standing player hangs. The column gives the largest of the smallest gaps of the players. The reach share comes from the player on the chair. | 9.3 |
| `pnpm test:paused --base` copies `PAUSED_PATHS` of the commit: the source, the tools, the demos, the design and the decision files, and the config files. A test file that fails with no failed test, such as a file that does not load, counts as one failed entry. The tool exits with 1 when a test fails on the working tree alone. | 9.3 |
| After a stop, the promise of `runChildStatus()` of `tools/ref-tree.ts` never settles, so the caller starts no work, and the stop ends the tool with 130. | 9.3 |
