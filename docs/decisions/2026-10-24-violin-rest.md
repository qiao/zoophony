# Decisions of the violin rest of the rabbit and the cat

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-24, the user said: "the cat and the rabbit are not holding the violin right when in rest
mode" and "the violinists like rabbit and cat should use similar poses and motion as the fox". The
main session decided the rows below. The report
[`2026-10-24-violin-rest.md`](../research/2026-10-24-violin-rest.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| A bowed hold can give the turn of the instrument in the middle of the lift and of the lowering (`liftTurn`). The instrument turns from the rest to this turn, then to the playing turn. | 5.2 |
| The rabbit violin rests upright on the top of the left knee with the top plate to the front, as the violin of the red fox. In the middle of the moves, it turns upright in front of the chest, and both elbows bend. This replaces the rest top row of `2026-10-24-rabbit-hold.md`. | 5.2, 5.3 |
| The cat viola rests upright on the front of the left knee. In the middle of the moves, it turns upright in front of the chest, and both elbows bend. This replaces the lift row and the open belly row of `2026-10-24-bow-holds.md`. | 5.2, 5.3 |
| The red fox keeps its rest and its direct turn. Its paw twist passes 180° once in each move (`OPEN` in the gate, as before). | 9.3 |
| Open: the cat left paw swings 98° at rest (87° before, limit 70), and the rabbit right wrist stretches 0.37 in the play, as before. | 9.3 |
