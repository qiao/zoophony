# Decisions of the cat arm and the tan fox flute hold

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, the round `sixth-cat-fox` fixed two issues of the user: "the cat's arm has dent and
is longer than expected", and "the fox doesn't seem to be holding the instrument properly". The
report [`2026-10-27-cat-fox.md`](../research/2026-10-27-cat-fox.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "the cat's arm has dent". The bind upper arm of the cat points 38° from the hanging arm of the mesh (53° before), as on the deer, and the faces within 0.1 units of each shoulder joint take the skin normals (`studio.bindArms`, `rootNormals` of `tools/mesh-rigs/cat.json`). | 4.4 |
| The rest bow elbow of the cat points a little further out, (0.8, -1, 0.3): with the near bind, the right upper arm went 1.7 cm and the forearm 0.2 cm into the belly at rest (0.9 cm, 0.0 cm now) (`CAT_HOLD.restPoleR`). | 5.2 |
| The cat bow via moves the grip 0.45 H out, 0.35 H up, and 0.1 H forward in the middle of a move (0.05 H): with the viola nearer to the hip, the bow went 0.7 cm into the viola in a lowering (`restBow.via.reach`). With 0.4 H forward, the bow arm went straight (2026-10-26). | 5.2 |
| The user: "the fox doesn't seem to be holding the instrument properly". A wind paw that leaves its grip at rest takes the aim and the roll of its grip by the weight of the play, so the left flute paw faces the flute in the play (`poseSide()` of `pose/winds.ts`). | 5.3 |
| The left tan fox paw holds the flute from its front and a little below, points half up across it, and curls 115° over the top (`aimSide`, `gripShiftL` of `FOX_FLUTE`): it grasps in each play sample (0%). | 5.3 |
| `pnpm wind-grip` gives the left flute paw its place in front of the flute and a little below (`ROLES` of `tools/wind-grip.ts`). | 9.3 |
