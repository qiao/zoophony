# Decisions of the arm fixes of the hippo, the pig, and the bear

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-25, the user said: "the rhino's arm is bent unnaturally" (the image shows the bow arm
of the hippo bassist), "the pig has unnatural seam between the trunk and the arm. also there's a
black area under the armpit", and "the bear's arm has unnatural facets underneath". The manager
worked alone on `main`. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove
its row.
