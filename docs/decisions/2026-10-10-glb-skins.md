# Decisions of the skins of the rigged bodies

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-10, after the rigged rabbit and bear of [`2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md`](2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md)
and [`2026-10-10-bear-glb.md`](2026-10-10-bear-glb.md), the user asked: "make the bear and rabbit
look more like the reference image and the model. you can remove triangles / decimate if needed",
then "why are the screenshots look so different from the glb files that I shared before". The
user then chose the route with a texture and the colors of the reference images. The agent of the
skins decided the other rows. The report [`2026-10-10-glb-skins.md`](../research/2026-10-10-glb-skins.md)
gives the measures. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it,
and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: the rabbit and the bear look like their GLB models and like `rabbit-views.png` and `bear-views.png`. This replaces "with no comparison against `rabbit-views.png`" of `2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md`. The meshes keep the forms and the limb lengths of the files | 1, 4.4 |
| The user: each rigged body shows the texture of its GLB file. `pnpm rig-body` bakes the base color texture onto a new UV layout of the collapsed mesh with Cycles, into a WebP skin of 1024 × 1024 beside the module. This replaces "There are no texture files" of design 4.4, and the regions of the nearest palette colors of `2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md` and `2026-10-10-bear-glb.md` | 4.4, 4.5 |
| The user: the skins take the colors of the reference images. The fur and the accent of the palette of each config move to the colors of the species in Oklab, with the same change of the lightness, the same ratio of the chroma, and the same turn of the hue. Each color of the texture takes the move of its nearest palette color, and the move is weaker far from the palette. Each palette color lands within 2 / 255 of its target. Thus the gray rabbit of the GLB file becomes white with pink inner ears, and the light chest of the bear stays light. The dark parts keep the colors of the texture | 4.4, 4.5 |
| The islands of the face get two times the texels of the other islands, because the eyes, the nose, and the mouth are the smallest details of the texture | 4.4 |
| A plate on the surface of the head in front of each eye follows its eye bone, so that the eye blinks. The plate is 1.35 times the eye that the dense texture samples measure, and it carries the eye of the texture. The two eyes are mirror images, because the measured eyes of each file differ by 3 mm at most. The body under a plate bakes from a copy of the texture with the fur color on 1.3 times the eye, so that a closed eye shows fur. This replaces the code eye plates of `2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md` | 4.4 |
| The corner (0, 1) of each skin is white. Each corner with no skin (the instrument, the props, and the parametric bodies) points at it, so that its vertex color stays its color. One material and one draw call for each player stay | 4.4, 4.5 |
| Each species with a skin has one material: a copy of the shared material, with the skin as the color map on `uv1` (`scene/player-materials.ts`). The material stays hidden until its texture loads, and a failed load shows the body white | 4.5 |
| The size check counts the assets of the chunks of the first download, so that it counts the skins. The first download grows to 449,040 B after gzip | 7.2 |
| The bear gets the round tail of the side and the back views of `bear-views.png`, because its GLB file has no tail: a ball of 80 triangles with a radius of 6.5 cm in the units of the file, on the bone `tail1` | 4.4 |
| The collapse budget goes to 2,400 triangles for the rabbit and 2,380 for the bear, so that the plates (120 triangles) and the tail of the bear (80 triangles) keep each body under 2,600 | 4.4, 4.5 |
| The test of the eye plates checks that each plate corner stays within 1.5 eye radii of its eye bone, and that the eyes are mirror images. The old limit of 0.15 H was tuned on the code plates | 9.3 |
