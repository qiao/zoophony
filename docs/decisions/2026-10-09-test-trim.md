# Decisions of the test trim

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-09, the user approved three changes that make the wall time of `pnpm test` shorter.
Each behavior check stays. First, remove the code that has no user, and its tests: the meerkat
species, which has had no seat since 2026-10-06, and the tip help of a short bow arm, which no
body needs after the body revert of 2026-10-09. Second, sample the three slow sweeps less often.
Third, split the three slow test files into smaller files, because Vitest runs the tests of one
file in one worker. The agent of the test trim decided the details in the rows below. The user
can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The meerkat leaves `SpeciesId`, `SPECIES`, the colors, and the seat text. Its ear, snout, tail, and face pattern types stay, because other species use them: the hippo has `small` ears, the mouse the `pointed` snout, and the panda the `eye-patches`. The test of the hand tools builds the badger, the percussion player, in place of the meerkat. The test of `limitReach()` builds the polar bear, the pianist | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The `girth` of `SpeciesParams` goes with the meerkat, the only species with a value other than 1.0. The width of the torso and the thickness of the limbs are now the same for all species, in `H`. Each player model stays the same to the bit (the same SHA-256 of the meshes, the skeletons, and the body points of each species with each instrument). The rows of older files that give the bear and the polar bear the girth 1.0 still hold: that is the one width of all species | 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 |
| The tip help of a short bow arm leaves `pose/bowed.ts` and `pose/bowed-rig.ts`, with its test. Before the removal, a print of `bowedRig()` gave the tip help 0 for each bowed player of each demo at each section size, of the four chamber test files, and of the three bowed sheets of the views page. Only made-up players had a tip help, such as a rabbit with a cello, or the dog with its cello on a high stool. The search of the bow range now poses the shoulder one time, because the shoulder turn no longer changes with the place of the bow. `hairShare()` goes too, because only the tip help used it. This row replaces the tip help row of `2026-10-08-fox-arms.md` and the row of `2026-10-09-body-revert.md` that kept the tip help | 5.3 |
| The camera tests of the shot frames sample the song time every 0.2 s (0.05 s before): the clearance of the camera, the angle to the front of the subject, and the beam. The clearance test needs more than 250 samples of close shots in each scene (1000 before), because the chamber test files give 692 to 764 samples at the new step. A fault that moved the camera 0.8 × sin(π × blend) of the way to the target in each blend failed the clearance test of K. 525 at 114.80 s (0.78 m from the podium, the rule is 1.0 m). The step of 0.05 s also failed Beethoven 8 at 105.50 s, by 0.008 m, so the new step can miss a short and shallow fault | 5.6, 9.3 |
| The test of the conductor clearance samples every 0.25 s of a full play of each demo (0.1 s before), and every 0.05 s of the wall time of the lift, the bow, and the lowering (0.02 s before). Its own constant `ARM_STEP` gives the second step, in place of a fifth of the first step. The test keeps the bear 0.02 m from the stand, and the left forearm and hand 0.02 m from the head, the right hand, the baton, and in the playing pose the body. A desk 0.8 m above the podium (0.7 m in the code) failed the test of the arms in each of the six demos, in the bow at the song end. A left cue that can point 20° to the right of the upper body, with no least turn to the left, failed the test of the play in five demos. The step of 0.1 s failed the same five demos. This row replaces the row of the test of `2026-10-06-conductor-left-arm.md` | 5.4, 9.3 |
| The play clearance test checks each half second of the default demo, as before, and the lift every 0.1 s (0.05 s before). While the players play, the forearm, the hand, and the lower half of the upper arm keep at least 0.01 H from the torso and the head, and do not enter the thighs. A fault that turned the right elbow of each wind player inward in the middle of its lift failed the test of each of the eight winds, at 0.3 s to 0.5 s of the lift. The lift samples are about a tenth of the time of the test, so the new step saves little. This row replaces the row of the test of `2026-10-09-body-revert.md` | 5.2, 5.3, 9.3 |
| The play clearance test has three files, one for each family of instruments: `pose/play-clearance-strings.test.ts`, `pose/play-clearance-woodwinds.test.ts`, and `pose/play-clearance-brass.test.ts`. `describePlayClearance()` of `pose/test-play-clearance.ts` registers the test for a list of instruments. The test of the horn in the bind pose goes into the brass file. Each file builds the default demo again, in about 0.35 s | 9.3 |
| The tests of the shot frames have seven files: one for each of the six demos of `SHOT_DEMOS` (`shots/shot-frames-<demo>.test.ts`) and one for the four chamber test files (`shots/shot-frames-chamber.test.ts`). `describeShotFrames()` of `shots/test-shot-frames.ts` registers the tests for a list of scenes. The test of the K. 315 soloist runs only for the scene of K. 315. Each demo scene is built one time, as before, so the split adds only the import of the modules to each file | 9.3 |
| The test of the conductor clearance has six files, one for each demo: `pose/conductor-clearance-<demo>.test.ts`. `describeConductorClearance()` of `pose/test-conductor-clearance.ts` registers the two tests of one demo, and fails at the collection for a demo with no conductor. `demoSceneOf()` of `pose/test-scene.ts` builds the scene of one demo, also for the default demo of the play clearance test | 9.3 |
