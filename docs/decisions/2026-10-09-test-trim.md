# Decisions of the test trim

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-09, the user approved three changes that make the wall time of `pnpm test` shorter.
Each behavior check stays. First, remove the code that has no user, and its tests. These are the
meerkat species, which has had no seat since 2026-10-06, and the tip help of a short bow arm. No
body needs the tip help after the body revert of 2026-10-09. Second, sample the three slow
sweeps less often. Third, split the three slow test files into smaller files, because Vitest
runs the tests of one file in one worker. The agent of the test trim decided the details in the
rows below. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The meerkat leaves `SpeciesId`, `SPECIES`, the colors, and the seat text. Its ear, snout, tail, and face pattern types stay, because other species use them. For example, the hippo has `small` ears, the mouse the `pointed` snout, and the panda the `eye-patches`. The test of the hand tools builds the badger, the percussion player, in place of the meerkat. The test of `limitReach()` builds the polar bear, the pianist | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The `girth` of `SpeciesParams` goes with the meerkat, the only species with a value other than 1.0. The width of the torso and the thickness of the limbs are now the same for all species, in `H`. Each player model stays the same to the bit: the SHA-256 of the meshes, the skeletons, and the body points of each species with each instrument does not change. The rows of older files that give the bear and the polar bear the girth 1.0 still hold. That is the one width of all species | 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 |
| The tip help of a short bow arm leaves `pose/bowed.ts` and `pose/bowed-rig.ts`, with its test. Before the removal, a print of `bowedRig()` gave a tip help of 0 to each real bowed player. The print read each demo at each section size, the four chamber test files, and the three bowed sheets of the views page. Only made-up players had a tip help, such as a rabbit with a cello, or the dog with its cello on a high stool. The search of the bow range now poses the shoulder one time, because the shoulder turn no longer changes with the place of the bow. `hairShare()` goes too, because only the tip help used it. This row replaces the tip help row of `2026-10-08-fox-arms.md` and the row of `2026-10-09-body-revert.md` that kept the tip help | 5.3 |
| The camera tests of the shot frames sample the song time every 0.2 s (0.05 s before): the clearance of the camera, the angle to the front of the subject, and the beam. The clearance test needs more than 250 samples of close shots in each scene (1000 before). The chamber test files give 692 to 764 samples at the new step. As a fault, each blend moved the camera 0.8 × sin(π × blend) of the way to the target. The clearance test of K. 525 then failed at 114.80 s: the camera was 0.78 m from the podium, and the rule is 1.0 m. The step of 0.05 s also failed Beethoven 8 at 105.50 s, by 0.008 m. Thus the new step can miss a short and shallow fault | 5.6, 9.3 |
| The test of the conductor clearance samples a full play of each demo every 0.25 s (0.1 s before). It samples the lift, the bow, and the lowering every 0.05 s of the wall time (0.02 s before). Its own constant `ARM_STEP` gives the second step, in place of a fifth of the first step. The test keeps the bear 0.02 m from the stand. It keeps the left forearm and hand 0.02 m from the head, the right hand, and the baton, and in the playing pose from the body. As a fault, a desk 0.8 m above the podium (0.7 m in the code) failed the test of the arms in each demo, in the bow at the song end. As a second fault, the left cue could point 20° to the right of the upper body, with no least turn to the left. The test of the play then failed in five demos, as with the step of 0.1 s. This row replaces the row of the test of `2026-10-06-conductor-left-arm.md` | 5.4, 9.3 |
| The play clearance test checks each half second of the default demo, as before, and the lift every 0.1 s (0.05 s before). While the players play, the forearm, the hand, and the lower half of the upper arm keep at least 0.01 H from the torso and the head. They do not enter the thighs. As a fault, the right elbow of each wind player turned inward in the middle of its lift. The test of each of the eight winds then failed, at 0.3 s to 0.5 s of the lift. The lift samples are about a tenth of the time of the test, so the new step saves little. This row replaces the row of the test of `2026-10-09-body-revert.md` | 5.2, 5.3, 9.3 |
| The play clearance test has three files, one for each family of instruments: `pose/play-clearance-strings.test.ts`, `pose/play-clearance-woodwinds.test.ts`, and `pose/play-clearance-brass.test.ts`. `describePlayClearance()` of `pose/test-play-clearance.ts` registers the test for a list of instruments. The test of the horn in the bind pose goes into the brass file. Each file builds the default demo again, in about 0.35 s | 9.3 |
| The tests of the shot frames of the demos have three shard files, `shots/shot-frames-1.test.ts` to `shots/shot-frames-3.test.ts`, on the request of the manager. Shard `k` checks each demo of `SHOT_DEMOS` whose index `i` gives `i % 3 === k - 1`, so a new demo joins a shard with no new file. `shardOf()` of `src/test-shards.ts` picks the demos, and `describeShotFrameShard()` of `shots/test-shot-frames.ts` builds them and registers their tests. With 3 shards, the default demo shares its file with the Bach demo, the two Beethoven demos share a file, and the two Mozart demos share a file. The four chamber test files keep their own file, `shots/shot-frames-chamber.test.ts`. The test of the K. 315 soloist runs only in the shard of K. 315. `src/test-shards.test.ts` fails when a demo is in no shard or a shard has no file | 9.3 |
| The test of the conductor clearance has six files, one for each demo: `pose/conductor-clearance-<demo>.test.ts`. `describeConductorClearance()` of `pose/test-conductor-clearance.ts` registers the two tests of one demo. It fails at the collection for a demo with no conductor. `demoSceneOf()` of `pose/test-scene.ts` builds the scene of one demo, also for the default demo of the play clearance test | 9.3 |
| After the three changes, `pnpm test` takes about 27 s of wall time in place of 37 s to 46 s, at a load average of about 20 on the development Mac. The longest test file takes about 12 s. The heavy files end after about 14 s, and the imports of about 175 small test files fill the rest of the wall time. Thus a further split of the heavy files saves no time | 9.3 |
