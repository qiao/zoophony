# Decisions of the test pause

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the user said: "we have too many tests right now. disable the unnecessary ones for
now. especially because we're now constantly iterating on the design / look&feel of the animal
models. also, disable the frame test for now. we can do a sweep of performance improvements
later." The manager of the session and the agent of the branch `test-pause` decided the other
rows. The report [`2026-10-14-test-pause.md`](../research/2026-10-14-test-pause.md) gives the
times, the counts, and the group of each test file. The user can reverse each decision. Then
update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: pause the tests that the work on the look of the animals makes red, and the frame test. The manager: pause, and delete no test. `PAUSED_TESTS` of `vite.config.ts` lists the paused test files in the Vitest project `paused`. The project `unit` of `pnpm test` skips them, and `pnpm test:paused` runs them for information. CI, `tools/check-commits.sh`, and `tools/merge-branch.sh` run `pnpm test`, so they skip them too. The typecheck and the lint still read them, and each test helper stays | 9.3 |
| The rule of the choice: a test stays when it guards a rule that the look of an animal does not change, or a crash. These are the rules of `midi/`, `score/`, the audio engine, the block scheduler, `ui/`, the app state, the URL, the transport, and the camera, "Stateless poses" and "One score" of `AGENTS.md`, the size budget of 7.2, and the tools and the docs paths. A test pauses when it checks the look or the geometry of a body or a seat, the fit of a pose to a body, the clearance of a body, the layout from the footprints of the bodies, or a pose of a sheet page or of the gallery. A test file goes into the group of its main checks. When the agent was not sure, the test stayed | 9.3 |
| The list has four groups and 36 files. The clearance of the bodies: `pose/*-clearance-*.test.ts` (the sweep shards of the conductor, the pianist, and the violinist, and the play clearance of the families), `pose/violinist-standing-clearance.test.ts`, and `pose/rest-clearance.test.ts`. The fit of a pose to a body: `pose/bowed`, `pose/bow-hold`, `pose/hands`, `pose/winds`, `pose/percussion`, `pose/choir`, `pose/seated-feet`, `models/player`, `gallery/sheet-poses`, and `gallery/view-poses`. The look of the bodies and the seats: `models/species`, `models/budget`, `models/proportions`, `models/legs`, `models/props`, `models/face-patterns`, and `models/penguin/penguin-body`. The layout from the footprints: `models/layout-scene`, `scene/hidden-heads`, and `shots/choir-theme`, the check of the choir shots of the Ode to Joy, which the fox mesh moved out of `shots/prepare` ([`2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md`](2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md)) | 9.3 |
| Some paused files also hold rules that the look does not change: the seeded lift and lowering in `pose/bowed` and `pose/winds`, the kettle choice of `percussionTracks()` in `pose/percussion`, the jaw and the sway of a singer in `pose/choir`, and the order of the seats and the places of a chamber group in `models/layout-scene`. The kept files `pose/pose-at`, `pose/rest`, `pose/strikes`, `pose/choir-notes`, `pose/wind-notes`, `models/cast`, and `models/layout` cover the same rules in part | 9.3 |
| Some kept files also hold a check of a look or a fit: the rest angles of the arms in `pose/conductor`, the standing legs and their folds in `models/studio-rig`, the desk of the conductor stand at the shoulders of the bear in `models/stage`, the bench and the keys in `tools/studio-body`, and the paws below the shoulders in `models/studio-body`. They stay, because most of their checks guard a mechanism: the cues, the retarget of a studio body, the stage, and the export. The shard tests of the shot frames (`shots/test-shot-frames.ts`) check "the subject in the frame" and "the subject behind other items" against the footprints of the real bodies. They stay too, because the model rounds of 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-14 did not edit them | 9.3 |
| The kept test of the bodies is `pose/pose-at.test.ts`: `perf-60.mid` casts 18 species and the bear conductor, so it builds each of the 19 species and poses each player alone and in the scene in seven states. `tools/body-probe.test.ts` ("probes each species on each support and the conductor") builds each species on each support, with no check of finite numbers. `models/rig.test.ts` keeps the bones of 4.7, and `scene/player.test.ts` keeps the skin of a studio body. No kept test checks that each species on each support poses with finite numbers. The manager: no new test of this kind now, because the user asked for fewer tests | 9.3 |
| The speed tests of `pnpm test:perf` stay as they are. They check no look, and CI runs them | 9.3 |
| To bring a test back, remove its file or its group from `PAUSED_TESTS`, make it pass with `pnpm test <path>`, and update this file. A model round does not fix a paused test. When a change needs a paused check, run it with `pnpm test:paused <path>`. A new test of the look, the fit, or the clearance of a body goes into the list. The shard files of a new role sweep match the first pattern of the list, so they pause too | 9.3 |
| The tools `pnpm pose-depth`, `pnpm fold-probe`, `pnpm close-views`, `pnpm body-probe`, and `pnpm hold-reach` stay. A step of a model route that ran one of them before each report now runs it when the change needs it, for example `pnpm pose-depth <role>` after a change of a hold | 9.3 |
| The user: the frame test pauses until the sweep of the performance work. The rule "Frame time" of `AGENTS.md` pauses: a change of `src/scene/`, of a shader, or of the post effects does not run `pnpm frame` before its report. The tool, its tests, and the frame target stay. This row replaces the row of [`2026-10-13-frame-time.md`](2026-10-13-frame-time.md) for each change of the scene. When the sweep starts, restore that rule: each change of `src/scene/`, of a shader, or of the post effects runs `pnpm frame` before its report, and gives the table in the report or the commit message. CI has no GPU, so the check stays local | 9.7 |
