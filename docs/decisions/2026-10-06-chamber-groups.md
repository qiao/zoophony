# Decisions of the chamber groups

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-06, the user decided finding N9 of the second UX audit
([`audits/2026-10-06-ux-design-2.md`](../audits/2026-10-06-ux-design-2.md)): a score with at most
5 players has no conductor and no podium, and its players sit in a small arc at the stage center
and face each other a little (4.2, 4.5, 5.4, 5.6). The agent of this finding decided the details
below. The user can reverse each one. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The rule counts all players of the seats with players, also the singers. A score with no players keeps the conductor, so that the stage is not empty | 4.2 |
| Three forms: one player alone, a pianist with one partner (a recital), and an arc for the other groups of 2 to 5 players | 4.2 |
| A player alone and the partner of a pianist stand when their instrument stands as a soloist (4.5), and have no music stand. The players of an arc sit, also the soloist of 3.8. The seat name of the soloist (2.8) does not change | 4.2, 4.5 |
| The arc: one row centered on 0°, with a radius of at least 2.4 m (the radius of row 0), and at most 60° on each side with the room of the end players. The players face the center of the arc. No spread and no wedge pass | 4.5 |
| The order of the arc from the left: a keyboard (turned along the arc, as a keyboard of the orchestra), then the order of the rows, with the desks of a string seat together. The wind quintet test file thus has the flute, the oboe, the horn, the clarinet, and the bassoon | 4.5 |
| The recital: the case center of the piano is at the middle of the group. The partner stands 0.45 m from it toward the tail, in front of the curved side, 0.05 m from the piano, facing the audience and turned 25° to the pianist | 4.5 |
| A player alone faces the audience (180°). A pianist alone keeps the facing of a piano soloist, so the audience sees the right side of the pianist and the open lid | 4.5 |
| The stage: the rule of 4.5 from the middle of the group in place of the podium center, with the minimum of 5 m, and the middle of the group at the stage center. The podium center of the layout is the center of the arc, 2.4 m in front of the middle of a solo or a recital group | 4.5 |
| The leader of the count-in: the principal player of the first seat of the first section of the list violins, piano, flutes, oboes, clarinets, trumpets, horns, else of the first seat | 5.4 |
| The count-in keeps its times. The leader leans back 4° in the first half of the preparatory beat (a breath in), leans forward to 6° along a quarter cosine onto the downbeat, and comes back up in 0.4 s. The nod scales with the playing pose of the leader | 5.4 |
| The spine takes the nod. The head of a pianist, a harpist, a percussion player, or a singer nods by the same angle. A violinist, a violist, and a wind player keep the head still, because the chin holds the violin and the mouth the mouthpiece. A cellist or a bassist does not lean: its head lifts with the breath and drops to level on the downbeat, so it keeps 0.03 m from the neck of the instrument | 5.4 |
| The idle looks: the players look at the head of the leader, the leader at the middle of the heads of the others, and a player alone ahead to the audience | 5.1 |
| No conductor shot with no conductor, and a wide shot in place of the conductor shot that fills the time. With fewer than 3 seats with players, also with a conductor: no conductor shot, no high shot, and no section shot | 5.6 |
| The two-shot of a group of fewer than 3 seats: a section shot of all players on the stage, with the score of a section shot of all seats, and a sphere of each player plus 0.5 m (0.75 m for a section shot) | 5.6 |
| With one seat, rules 1 and 2 of the choice do not stop a solo shot of the seat after itself. The jump cut rule and the rule of no repeat of a framing keep the cameras 20° apart | 5.6 |
