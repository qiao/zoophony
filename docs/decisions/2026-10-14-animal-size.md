# Decisions of the size of the red fox and the rabbit

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the user saw the red violin fox in the app and said: "the fox is too small. make it
1.3x the size. also make the rabbit 1.2x the size". The manager of the session and the agent of
the branch `animal-size` decided the other rows. The report
[`2026-10-14-animal-size.md`](../research/2026-10-14-animal-size.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its
row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: the red fox is 1.3 times and the rabbit 1.2 times the size of the morning of 2026-10-14. The manager: a linear scale of the whole body, so each length of the fox grows by 1.3 and each length of the rabbit by 1.2. The arm factors stay, so the proportions of the bodies stay. The GLB files of `pnpm rig-mesh` keep their bytes, because they hold the units of the studio, and only the scale of the app changes | 4.4 |
| The manager: a depth of the sweep that keeps its value in head heights passes, also when it is over the contact limit of 1.5 cm, which stays in meters. These depths are over the limit and keep their value in H (`pnpm hold-fit violinist --demo all` and `pnpm hold-fit fox-violinist --demo all` with the fixed sweep of `9fd0100d`, `hold-fit-*.txt` of the research folder): the play of the red fox, the right forearm 2.4 cm into the body at 162.55 s of `bach-bwv1050` (1.9 cm on `main`, 0.076 H against 0.074 H), and the left forearm of the rabbit 1.6 cm into the violin in the lift, the rest, and the lowering (1.4 cm, 0.052 H against 0.050 H), and its left paw 1.6 cm into the violin in the play (1.3 cm). They stay open until the manager decides. This round fits no hold for them | 5.3, 9.3 |
| The red fox folds in 3.4% of the play samples (3.3% on `main`, `pnpm fold-probe fox-violinist`). The rabbit folds in 6.1% of the play samples (3.9% on `main`, 1,082 of 17,788 samples with the fit of the elbow at the frog). The cause is the free hair at each end of the bow, 0.03 m (`HAIR_END` of `pose/bowed.ts`): it does not grow with the bow, so the strokes of the larger rabbit come nearer to the frog, and the bow arm rises more. A probe with 0.036 m, 1.2 times the old length, gave 3.8% and a bow paw 1.4 cm into the body (`fold-probe-violinist-hair-end-0.036.txt`). The free hair is a rule of the pure core for each bowed player, so this round keeps it. Open: a free hair in head heights or in parts of the bow, with its review | 5.3 |
| `size` of `SPECIES` stays 1 for the rabbit and the red fox. A studio body takes its height from its module, and `size` scales only the parametric body. The rule of 4.2 and 4.3 (the strings have species of the size 1.05 or less) reads `size`, so the cast does not change. The paused test `scene/hidden-heads.test.ts`, which checks that a large species hides no head, passes on `main` and on the branch (`pnpm test:paused src/scene/hidden-heads.test.ts`) | 4.2, 4.3 |
| Changed test: the check of the neighbor shots in `shots/prepare.test.ts` accepts a conductor shot after a conductor shot with another field of view. Design 5.6 (rule 6) lets the conductor fill the time with its other framing when no subject has a clear camera. The larger bodies change the plan of the Boulanger demo from its shot 2, and at 133.8 s the plan takes this path. A break of the alternation of the two framings fails the check | 5.6, 9.3 |
| `pnpm body-probe --base main` names the bodies of the rabbit and the red fox on each support and their two players. The other 85 bodies and 21 players stay. 729 of 1,492 pose sets change: each set of the rabbits and the red foxes, the conductor in each demo, and players of the other sections by their places on the larger stages, their looks, and their cues, with two sets of a tan fox in the Beethoven 9 and the Boulanger demos | 4.2, 5.4 |
| The first download grows by 853 B after gzip (`pnpm size --base main`): the digits of the new scale in the two modules of the bodies | 7.2 |
| The meters in the rows of [`2026-10-13-mesh-rabbit.md`](2026-10-13-mesh-rabbit.md) and [`2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md`](2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md), and in their research reports, are at the sizes before this file, unless the row names this file. The units of the rigs, the angles, and the shares stay | 4.4 |
