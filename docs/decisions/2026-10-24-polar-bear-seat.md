# Decisions of the size and the seat of the polar bear pianist

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-24, the user looked at the T-pose polar bear pianist and said: "the polar bear is too
tall and sit too far away from the piano" and "the polar bear's arms are too stretched". The round
asked no questions and decided the rows below. The report
[`2026-10-24-polar-bear-seat.md`](../research/2026-10-24-polar-bear-seat.md) gives the measures and
the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| "Too tall": the polar bear is 0.85 times its size of 2026-10-24 (1.63455 m for each unit). In the play, its seated head top is 1.42 m (1.67 m before), and its shoulder joints 1.05 m. The side view of `polar-bear-views.png` gives 1.40 m and about 1.0 m against the keys of 0.70 m. | 1, 4.4 |
| "Sit too far away": the keys stand 1.55 H (0.56 m) in front of the bench middle, 1.68 H (0.70 m) before. The mean play elbow is 104° to 108° (152° before, at the reach limit). The forearms go 4.3 cm into the belly (3.1 cm before, limit 9 cm). | 4.6, 5.3 |
| Conflict: design 1 said "about 1.8 m high", from the label "180 cm" of `polar-bear-views.png`. That label ends above the head top, and it puts the keys at 0.79 m. The newest words of the user win, so the size comes from the keys of the app. | 1 |
