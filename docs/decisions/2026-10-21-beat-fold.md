# Decisions of the beat fold

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-21, the user wrote: "the animals are wiggling too frequently while playing
/Users/xueqiaoxu/Downloads/Disney_Themes_-_Whole_New_World.mid". The file notates a ballad at 241
BPM in 4/4, so the sway changed its side each 0.25 s. The agent asked no question, so each choice
is a row. The report [`2026-10-21-beat-fold.md`](../research/2026-10-21-beat-fold.md) gives the
measures and the sources. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user, quote above. A bar of 4/x gets 2 conducted beats from a beat tempo of 168 and 1 beat from 336, the limit that 2/x and 3/x already had (`barPattern()` of `score/beats.ts`). No source gives the tempo of the change from 4 to 2. | 5.4 |
| A bar of 9/8 or 12/8 gets 1 or 2 conducted beats from a dotted tempo of 168, with the same limit. No demo has such a bar. | 5.4 |
| A bar of a `generic` pattern, such as 7/8, keeps one beat for each beat unit, because a fold needs an uneven grouping that the file does not give. | 5.4 |
| The sway follows the felt pulse: the conducted beat up to a median of 120 BPM, the preferred tempo, else 2 or 4 beats (`PULSE_TEMPO`, `markPulses()` of `score/beats.ts`). | 5.1 |
| The fold of the pulse is one for the whole song, in note values, so a ritardando slows the sway also where the conducted pattern changes. A fold for each bar doubled the sway speed in the ritardando of the file. | 5.1 |
| A bar whose length is no multiple of the pulse, such as 3/4 with a pulse of a half note, has one pulse at its downbeat. | 5.1 |
| The singer sway keeps 2 conducted beats a side (`CHOIR_MOTION.swayBeats`), because it stays below the limit in each demo (66 for each minute in `beethoven-9`). | 5.3 |
| `pnpm beat-rates` fails a file with more than 2 s of a sway side above `--limit` (120 by default) or of a conducted beat above 168, with a slack of 3% for the seeded timing offsets (`tools/beat-rates.ts`). | 5.1, 5.4 |
