# Decisions of the bow arm of the string players

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-17, the user wrote: "Do a deep research on how professional players move their
hands/fingers (both left and right) and adjust the the movement's accordingly". Then: "make sure to
document the professional movements so that they can be referenced later". The round
`string-hands` wrote the reference [`string-technique.md`](../reference/string-technique.md) and the
motion spec of the report [`2026-10-17-string-hands.md`](../research/2026-10-17-string-hands.md).
The round `left-hand` did the left hand. The manager gave the round `bow-arm` the bow arm of the
spec (B1 to B8) and two defaults of its open questions. The agent of the branch decided the other
rows. The report [`2026-10-17-bow-arm.md`](../research/2026-10-17-bow-arm.md) gives the measures,
the probes, and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds
it, and remove its row.

| In a fast run across two neighbor strings, the right elbow keeps the mean string: in a fast run whose strings take two values at most one string apart, `crossStrings()` gives the mean of the two strings to the strokes next to a change of the string, in a part of the run that changes the string at least twice in a row. The first rule gave it to each stroke of the run, and the second violins of the default demo held the elbow between two strings from the first note, while the right forearm of the red fox went 2.9 cm into the body in the lift (1.2 cm on `main`). In the lift and the lowering, the rule fades in over the second half of the move. `crossPole()` turns the elbow toward the elbow of the bow on that mean string. The bow still turns the full angle to each string, and the hand holds the bow, so the forearm and the bend of the wrist make the rest of the crossing | 5.3 |
| A repeated pitch takes a new bow, also in a run of short notes, because the file plays it as a new note and a slur would not let it sound again. A run of more than four notes in one beat splits into slurs of nearly equal size, such as 3 and 3 for 6 notes. A note that starts up to 0.03 s before a beat belongs to that beat. With no beats, as on the sheet pages, each note takes its own bow | 5.3 |
| The bow of a slur moves at one speed until its last note and slows to a stop in its last note, so the bow does not stop between the notes. A separate note keeps the stroke of `main`: it starts at its full speed and slows to a stop at its end. The length of a slur follows the rule of a bow with the time of the whole slur | 5.3 |
| The length of a bow is multiplied by `0.7 + 0.3 ×` the gesture size (`BOW_MOTION.length.loudness`), the value of the spec (B5) | 5.3 |

The string length and the place of the contact point of the model (1/16 of the string from the bridge) come from the proportions of the violin model, because each bowed model is the violin model at its scale | 5.3 |
