# Decisions of the fox feet

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-08, the agent of branch `fox-feet` decided the details below. The work puts the feet
of the seated red fox on the floor, and adds a test of the feet of each seated body. The report
[`2026-10-08-fox-feet.md`](../research/2026-10-08-fox-feet.md) gives the measures and the images.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The feet of a seated rigged body rest on the floor, as the feet of the other species. `fox-views.png` lets the feet hang 0.07 m to 0.09 m above the floor, under the front of the chair. Design 4.5 (chair) puts the feet on the floor, and the foxes of `orchestra-latest.png` have their feet on the floor. `orchestra-latest.png` wins where it differs from the other references | 1, 4.4, 4.5 |
| The model and the GLB do not change. Feet on the floor in the model add about 70 rows of model pixels under the legs in the front view of the sheet, where the matte has background. An estimate from `fox-matte.png` gives 13,000 px in the front view and 8,500 px in the side view, and an IoU of about 0.90 in each view. The gates (front at least 0.94, side at least 0.93) then fail. The GLB stays the record of the sheet | 4.4 |
| `buildRigged()` stretches the lower legs (`feetToFloor()` of `models/rigged-body.ts`). A corner of a lower leg is a corner whose main bone is its shin or its foot. The top of the stretch is the lowest corner of the rest of the body (0.303 m for the red fox, the bottom of the hips). Under the top, each lower leg scales along y, until its lowest corner is on the floor: 1.31 for the right leg and 1.41 for the left leg of the red fox. Thus no other corner is in the stretch, and the mesh has no crack | 4.4 |
| The ankle joints (`footL`, `footR`) move by the stretch of their legs. The knee joints are above the top and stay. The pose code does not turn the legs, so the stretch holds in each pose | 4.4, 4.7 |
| A seated foot rests on its rest when the lowest corner of its shin and its foot is at most 0.02 m above the floor, or above the foot ring of the high stool, and not below it (1e-6 m for the rounding of the skin). `pose/seated-feet.test.ts` checks each species on the seat of each instrument of its section, in the rest pose at four wall times. Before the fix, only the red fox failed | 4.5, 9.3 |
