each section.

wrote: "also the bear's eyes are still too far from the face". The agent of the round decided the
rows below. The report [`2026-10-20-bear-eyes.md`](../research/2026-10-20-bear-eyes.md) gives the
measures, the probes, and the images. The rows of the eye places of the studio bear went with
its body (`2026-10-22-mesh-bear.md`). The user can reverse each decision. Then update its
section, and remove its row.
