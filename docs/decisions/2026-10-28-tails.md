# Decisions of the seated tails of the cat and the dog

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-28, the user said: "the cat's tail has malformed meshes" and "the dog's tail is
unnatural". The report [`2026-10-28-tails.md`](../research/2026-10-28-tails.md) gives the
measures and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove
its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The curl of a seated tail measures each tail vertex against the middle line of the source tail (`tail_line()`), a curve through the middle of ten slices. The line of the two tail bones had a corner at the bend, so the tail folded and went flat there. | 4.4 |
