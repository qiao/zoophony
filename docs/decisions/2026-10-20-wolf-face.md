# Decisions of the head size and the face of the wolf

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-20, the user said: "make the wolf's head the same size as the fox. reduce its body if
needed", and "the wolf look sad now. make it look happier". The report
[`2026-10-20-wolf-face.md`](../research/2026-10-20-wolf-face.md) gives the measures. The user can
reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user, quotes above: the kept head of the wolf takes the factor 1.235 on each axis around the top of the blend zone (`proportions.head` of `tools/mesh-rigs/wolf.json`). The mean of its skull height and width is 0.464 m, and the red fox has 0.465 m. | 1, 4.4 |
| The mean factor of the skull (1.2095) left the mean 9.8 mm short, because the chin lies in the blend zone and grows less. The factor 1.235 meets the limit of 5 mm. | 4.4 |
| The manager chose: the standing skull top stays at 1.620 m, so the body below the neck takes 0.9422 on each axis. The width and the depth to the height stay as in round `wolf-shape`. The places of the config take the map of both factors (`size_config.py`). | 1, 4.4 |
| The arms keep the arm factor 1.178 and take the body factor, so the reach is 0.4787 m (`app.reach`). | 4.4, 4.6 |
| `H` grows with the head (0.281 m to 0.370 m), and the clarinet grips moved out of reach. The mouthpiece goes 0.4 H back in place of 0.3 H, and the right shoulder bone turns 35° in place of 30° (`WOLF_CLARINET`, `fit3.txt`). | 5.3 |
