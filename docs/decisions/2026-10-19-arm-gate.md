# Decisions of the arm gate

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, five arm rounds met their own measure and shipped or nearly shipped a twisted arm.
The rounds `arm-gate` and `gate2` made one gate with one table of limits. The reports
[`2026-10-19-arm-gate.md`](../research/2026-10-19-arm-gate.md) and
[`2026-10-19-arm-gate-2.md`](../research/2026-10-19-arm-gate-2.md) give the measures and the
validation. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "how to prevent future sessions from twisting / deforming the arms". One gate reads all arm measures of each arm against one table of limits (`pnpm arm-check`, `ARM_LIMITS` of `tools/arm-check.ts`). | 4.4 |
| The gate reads the folds, the skin twist of the upper arm and the forearm, the flat share of five rings, the twist, swing, jumps, and grip turn of the paw, and the stretch of the forearm and paw faces. | 4.4 |
| The limits come from the good arms of `a3755083`: folds p95 8 faces, twist 55°, forearm twist 80°, flat p5 0.55, paw twist 170°, swing 70° (anatomy), jumps 0.5%, stretch 0.35 upper arms, grip turn 45°. | 4.4 |
| The paw twist from the bind keeps 170°: the bind paw is no neutral wrist (good violin left paws 133° to 156°, phase 2 136°, and 0° from its rest), so no anatomy limit separates them. | 4.4 |
| Manager: a turned paw must fail with no base. The grip turn is the median play twist of a paw from its accepted grip (`ACCEPTED_GRIPS`, the play medians of `eefece23`), with half of the 90° of anatomy. A new grip updates its entry. | 4.4 |
| The forearm twist has its own limit, because a turned paw twists the forearm skin of a good arm (rabbit 77° in the lift). | 4.4 |
| A value fails when it goes over its limit and the base does not, or when it gets worse than the base by more than its margin, also within the limit. It is open when the base is over too and it is not worse (`verdictOf()`). | 9.8 |
| The value of the gate is the worst sample set. The folds read the p95 count, because the good red fox folds 4 to 6 faces in each rest sample. | 4.4 |
| The paw jumps count the play and the lift: the baton paw of the bear conductor spins about 200° in the first 0.2 s of the lift and snaps back 71° in 0.01 s. | 4.4 |
| Manager: the stretch counts from the rest sample of the same body, in upper arm lengths, because a long face of the bind (mouse right forearm, 0.60 in each sample) is no deformation. | 4.4 |
| The merge runs `pnpm arm-check --all --base main` for a change of `src/pose/`, `src/models/`, or `tools/blender/` (`tools/merge-branch.sh`). It runs all roles, because the run takes about 3 minutes with the base in the cache. `--no-arm-check` skips it. | 9.8 |
| The gate imports only the exports of the trees since 2026-10-18, so it runs on an old commit as a base. | 9.8 |
