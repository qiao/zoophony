# Decisions of the arm gate

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, five arm rounds in two days met their own measure and shipped or nearly shipped a
twisted arm. The user asked: "how to prevent future sessions from twisting / deforming the arms,
which was a common issue that happened a few times". The round `arm-gate` made one gate with one
table of limits. The report [`2026-10-19-arm-gate.md`](../research/2026-10-19-arm-gate.md) gives
the measures and the validation. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "how to prevent future sessions from twisting / deforming the arms". One gate reads all arm measures of each arm against one table of limits (`pnpm arm-check`, `ARM_LIMITS` of `tools/arm-check.ts`). | 4.4 |
| The gate reads the folds of the arm faces, the skin twist of the upper arm and of the forearm, the flat share of five rings with a wrist ring, the twist, swing, and jumps of the paw on its forearm, and the stretch of the forearm and paw faces. | 4.4 |
| The limits come from the arms that look good on `a3755083`: folds p95 8 faces, twist 55°, forearm twist 80°, flat p5 0.55, paw twist 170°, paw swing 70° (anatomy), paw jumps 0.5%, stretch 0.35 upper arms. | 4.4 |
| The paw twist from the bind takes 170° of the good arms in place of the 90° of anatomy: the bind paw is no neutral wrist (rabbit left paw 165°). The base compare catches a turned paw (margin 30°). | 4.4 |
| The forearm twist has its own limit, because a turned paw twists the forearm skin of a good arm (rabbit 77° in the lift), and the upper arm keeps the limit of a twist band. | 4.4 |
| A value fails when it goes over its limit and the base does not, or when it gets worse than the base by more than its margin, also within the limit. It is open when the base is over too and it is not worse (`verdictOf()`). | 9.8 |
| The value of the gate is the worst of the rest, play, and lift sets. The folds read the p95 count in place of a share, because the good red fox folds 4 to 6 faces in each rest sample. | 4.4 |
| The paw jumps count only the play, because the lift turns the paw of the bear conductor by about 100° in its first 0.1 s steps. | 4.4 |
| The stretch is the largest edge growth of the forearm and paw faces in upper arm lengths, because short thin faces of good arms grow 4 to 5 times. | 4.4 |
| The merge runs `pnpm arm-check --all --base main` for a change of `src/pose/`, `src/models/`, or `tools/blender/` (`tools/merge-branch.sh`). It runs all roles, because the run takes about 3 minutes with the base in the cache. `--no-arm-check` skips it. | 9.8 |
| The gate imports only the exports of the trees since 2026-10-18, so it runs on an old commit as a base. A tree with no label of the fold visit gets its labels from the plan of the demo. | 9.8 |
| `--views` renders the worst play sample after 5 s, a strike nearest the middle of the demo, and the middle of the longest rest of the seat. Each frame gets one image of 2x crops of the arm box of the working tree (`tools/arm-views.ts`). | 9.8 |
| Each round that changes a hold, a rig, a pose, an instrument size, or a seat runs the gate with `--views` before its report (`docs/agents/model-work.md`). | 9.8 |
