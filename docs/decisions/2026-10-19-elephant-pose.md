# Decisions of the pose of the elephant timpanist

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, after the refit of the elephant arms, the manager compared the front view of the
timpanist with the reference images: the upper arms pointed out, and each paw bent down to hold
its mallet straight down. The round `elephant-pose` showed three variants, and the user chose
V1. The report [`2026-10-19-elephant-pose.md`](../research/2026-10-19-elephant-pose.md) gives
the measures. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "the hippo and elephants' arms are still twisted and deformed when playing. Take screenshots and see for yourself." The timpanist takes the pose of the reference images: arms forward and down, paws above the near rims, and mallet heads up between strikes (`PERCUSSION_HOLDS.timpani`). | 5.3 |
| The user chose "V1, reference": large kettles, and strikes about 0.25 m in from the near rim (`PercussionHold.rim`). A real timpanist strikes about 3 inches in, but a paw above the rim and a mallet of 0.3 m reach 0.25 m in. | 4.6, 5.3 |
| The kettle heads have their real size of 32 to 23 inches (`TIMPANI.radii`): the largest is 1.36 H wide, and the reference kettles are 1.38 to 1.40 H by the head of the elephant. | 4.6 |
| The heads stand 0.76 m high (`TIMPANI.headHeight`), in the reference range of 0.71 to 0.78 m by the height of the elephant. At 0.84 m, the forearms stayed level. At 0.76 m, they point 18° to 23° down. | 4.6 |
| Each note plays on one of the two front kettles, split at the middle key of the part (`PercussionHold.pair`). The outer kettles stand 0.95 m out (`TIMPANI.side`): at the arc, the third kettle cut 8.8 cm into the body. | 4.6, 5.3 |
| The mallets stay over the kettles in the rest pose (`PercussionHold.ready`), because the hanging left paw went 3.7 cm into the front left kettle. | 5.2 |
| Phase 3: at rest, the top of each grip points along the forearm, so the paw does not bend on it. The three shapes of phase 2 keep the play folds at 0.3%. | 4.4, 5.3 |
| Phase 4: rings at the middle, the elbow, and the wrist of the right forearm gave no visible gain, so the round kept none. Open: the right shoulder ring stays flat (0.70 of the bind), and the right paw bends about 50° at a strike. | 4.4, 9.3 |
