# Decisions of the shorter arms with the full violins, and the rest of the red fox

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the user looked at the app and said: "change the rest pose of the fox to be like
this", with a screenshot of two cat viola players at rest, and "also, reduce the arm length of the
fox and rabbit without changing the violin size". The manager of the session and the agent of the
branch `arms-rest` decided the other rows. The report
[`2026-10-14-arms-rest.md`](../research/2026-10-14-arms-rest.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its
row.
