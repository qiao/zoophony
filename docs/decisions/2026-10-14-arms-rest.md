# Decisions of the shorter arms with the full violins, and the rest of the red fox

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the user looked at the app and said: "change the rest pose of the fox to be like
this", with a screenshot of two cat viola players at rest, and "also, reduce the arm length of the
fox and rabbit without changing the violin size". The manager of the session and the agent of the
branch `arms-rest` decided the other rows. The report
[`2026-10-14-arms-rest.md`](../research/2026-10-14-arms-rest.md) gives the measures and the
images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its
row.

| The user: "reduce the arm length of the fox and rabbit without changing the violin size". The manager rendered three arm factors in one lineup (`arm-lineup.webp` and `arm-frog.webp` of the research folder): the factors before the full violins (1.0782 for the red fox and 1.445 for the rabbit), the middle (1.21 and 1.63), and the factors of the full violins (1.35 and 1.81). The manager chose the middle one, as for each open amount. The arms reach 0.545 m (red fox) and 0.506 m (rabbit) from the shoulder joint to the middle of the palm, 0.604 m and 0.557 m before (`armFactor` and `app.reach` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json` and `rabbit.json`). The paws keep their size. The tan fox shares the rig of the red fox, so its arms are shorter too. This replaces the arm row of [`2026-10-14-neck-violin.md`](2026-10-14-neck-violin.md) | 4.4, 4.6 |
