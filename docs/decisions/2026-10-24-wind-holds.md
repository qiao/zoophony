# Decisions of the wind holds of the wolf, the mouse, and the tan fox

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-24, the user said: "fix how the wolf, tan fox, and mouse are holding the instrument, in
both rest and play modes. validate through screenshots". The main session decided the rows below
with no questions. The report [`2026-10-24-wind-holds.md`](../research/2026-10-24-wind-holds.md)
gives the measures and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section,
and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The wolf clarinet hangs 20° from the vertical (30° before, 45° in the source), with the mouthpiece 0.18 H below the mouth (0.12 H before). At 30°, the arms still reached far forward. The mouse oboe keeps 30°: at 20°, its right arm folded in play. | 4.6, 5.3 |
| A wind paw on its grip can turn toward the side across the tube (`WindHold.aimSide`, 0 by default). The wolf and the mouse take 0.5: the lower paw of the wolf stuck out in front of the tube when seen from the side. | 5.3 |
| At rest, the wolf holds the clarinet upright, the bell at seat height about 0.2 m in front of the knees, with the elbows down. The source puts the bell on the knee or the seat. Nearer places failed the arm gate or the depths. | 5.2, 5.3 |
| At rest, the mouse holds the oboe nearly upright in front of the body (15° bell forward, 27° to the side before), with the elbows further down. Nearer places flattened the left upper arm or folded the right arm (arm gate). | 5.2, 5.3 |
| At rest, the tan fox flute stands on the right leg near the knee, leaned about 25° out and forward, the foot joint 2.6 cm above the leg (4.3 cm before).
