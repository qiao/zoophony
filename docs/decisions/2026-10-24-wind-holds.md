The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

both rest and play modes. validate through screenshots". The main session decided the rows below
with no questions. The report [`2026-10-24-wind-holds.md`](../research/2026-10-24-wind-holds.md)

and remove its row.
