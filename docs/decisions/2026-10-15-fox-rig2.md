# Decisions of the second round of the fox rig

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-14, the manager of the session listed the faults on `main`, and the user said: "fix
them after this round". This round fixes two faults of the fox rig, which the red foxes and the
tan foxes share: the dark band at the back of the neck, and the folds of the front of the right
shoulder of the red fox. The manager and the agent of the branch `fox-rig2` decided the rows.
The report [`2026-10-15-fox-rig2.md`](../research/2026-10-15-fox-rig2.md) gives the measures and
the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove
its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The manager: a clean neck line of the foxes from the back and the sides. The fox rig smooths the back of the neck and the bottom of the back of the head before the weights (`smooth` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json`, the box of `fox-smooth-config.json` of the research folder of 2026-10-14). The smoothing grew the stages of four demos by 0.2% to 4.6% because the fur spikes at the back of the head set the back of the box of the head, from which the export measures the head center of the holds. With no spikes, the head center moved 1.71 cm forward, the violin, which the holds place from the head center, moved with it, and the violin box came nearer to the stands and the neighbors in the arcs of the violins. With the head center of `main` and the smoothed mesh, the stages and the places of each player stay as on `main`, and with the moved head center and the mesh of `main`, the four stages grow (`layout-dump.ts`). The shoulder boxes of the footprints also change by up to 10.6 cm, because the main bones of corners at the base of the neck change, but they move no stage. The moved violin also lost the nut: the left paw reached 0.08 of the fingerboard, against 0.00 on `main`. Thus the rig writes the move of the head center back to the head before the smoothing into the file (`head_shift()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`), and the export adds it (`headShiftOf()` of `tools/studio-body.ts`), as it keeps the head before the growth of the head factor. The head center, the holds, the stages, the poses, the folds, and the depths of both foxes stay as on `main` | 4.4 |
