# Decisions of the seats, the legs, and the rest pose of the red fox and the rabbit

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-16, the user looked at the app and said: "reduce the height of the chairs (both chair
leg and back) so that the fox and the rabbit's feet can touch the ground", "let the fox and rabbit
sit in more.", "fix the pose of the rabbit and fox.", and "The fox's legs are too bent". The
manager of the session and the agent of the branch `seat-rest` decided the other rows. The probes
are in [`docs/research/2026-10-16-seat-rest/`](../research/2026-10-16-seat-rest/). The user can
reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "reduce the height of the chairs (both chair leg and back) so that the fox and the rabbit's feet can touch the ground". The entries of the rabbit and the red fox in `ANIMALS` of `tools/studio-body.ts` have no `seat`, so the export drops each seated body until its lowest point, a sole, is on the floor, and the chair fits the legs. The seats are 0.136 m (rabbit) and 0.167 m (red fox), 0.36 m before, with the slanted shins and the flat soles of the next row (`pnpm rig-mesh <animal> --check`). The legs keep the bind pose in each pose (`writeBody()` of `pose/pose-at.ts`), so the soles stay on the floor in each pose set. This reverses the user decision of 2026-10-14 ("it's ok for the animals to have their feet not touching the floor while sitting"), and replaces the row of the hanging feet of [`2026-10-14-fox-rabbit-form.md`](2026-10-14-fox-rabbit-form.md) | 4.4, 4.5 |
| The soles of the seated rabbit and red fox stand flat on the floor: the foot and the toes keep the turn of the source (`studio.legTurns` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json` and `rabbit.json`, from `leg_turns.py` of the research folder). The lowest point of the heel of each foot is 0.0 cm to 0.1 cm above the floor, and of the toes 0.4 cm (`seat-probe.ts`). This replaces the row of the relaxed feet of [`2026-10-14-fox-rabbit-form.md`](2026-10-14-fox-rabbit-form.md) | 4.4 |
| The user asked for a lower chair back. Each chair back stands 0.45 m above its seat (`CHAIR.back` of `models/props.ts`, 0.5 m before), and the top of the back falls with the seat. The brief of the manager gave the variants 0.5 m, 0.4 m, and 0.3 m, with 0.4 m as the default. The tail of the red fox passes the back posts up to 0.336 m above the seat, and the top rail of 0.1 m starts 0.3 m above the seat with a back of 0.4 m, so the rail went 3.6 cm into the tail (`seat-probe.ts`). With 0.3 m it went 13.6 cm into the tail. Tail binds 7° to 30° below the level kept the top of the tail at the posts at 33 cm to 34 cm and put its underside up to 3 cm into the seat (`tail-variants.txt`). So the default is 0.45 m, the lowest back of 0.05 m steps whose rail clears the tail, by 1.4 cm. The backs from 0.5 m to 0.4 m give the same stage sides, and 0.3 m grows four stages by 0.5% to 2.6% (`stage-sides-backs.txt`) | 4.5 |
