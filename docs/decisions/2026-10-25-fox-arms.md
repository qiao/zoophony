# Decisions of the rebuilt arms of the foxes and the flute grip

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

twist ("add loop edges and retopology and additional bones if needed to address them"). The manager
asked to rebuild the arms of the fox mesh, which serves the red fox violinist and the tan fox
flutist, to try the palm grip of the flute again, and to move the rest flute higher in the paw.

[`2026-10-25-fox-arms.md`](../research/2026-10-25-fox-arms.md) gives the measures and the images.
