# Decisions of the rest, the runs, and the butt ends of the percussion

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-25, the manager gave the round `percussion-rest`: the sticks at rest, the alternation
of the hands in xylophone runs, the butt ends behind the paws, and the exit of the xylophone
shafts. The goal of the user is that each player holds and plays its instrument as a real player,
with no twist or clip fault from any side. The report
[`2026-10-25-percussion-rest.md`](../research/2026-10-25-percussion-rest.md) gives the measures.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The timpani mallets have 6 cm of shaft behind the grip, and the triangle beater 7 cm (`malletButt`), so that 1.8 cm to 2.3 cm sticks out behind the paw, as a real player holds 13 mm to 25 mm. With 4 cm, the butt stayed in the paw. | 4.6 |
| At rest, the snare and the suspended cymbal paws hold the sticks in the rest position of a real snare drummer (Alfred, PAS): level and across the body in front of the waist, a little above the near rim, the palms down (`PercussionHold.set`). The sticks no longer stand up beside the shoulders. | 5.2 |
| The two sticks of the set position cross at two heights: the right stick rises 6° to 7° more than the left (`MalletSet.rise`), so that the shafts do not touch. | 5.2 |
| The wrists of the cymbalist rise 6 cm at rest, so that the yarn heads stay about 2 cm above the plate. No source gives this rest. The bongos with sticks take the positions of the snare drum. | 5.2 |
| A fast xylophone strike takes the other paw also in a run, as a real player alternates in a scale (Vic Firth, Peters), and passes the last bar of the other paw by up to 3.5 cm (`PercussionHold.pass`). A wider leap takes a double stroke. | 5.3 |
| The reach rule counts from the shoulders of the side move (`HandRules.shift`): the spine lean moves them up to 5.5 cm. The hands change in 35% of the fast pairs of the test score (`main` 3%) and 74% of the pirates part (`main` 14%). | 5.3 |
| The right head lifts with twice the pitch of the left head between strikes (`PercussionHold.pitchR` 2), so the heads pass at two heights. At one height, the mallets touched in 7% of the samples. A lower right head bent the right wrist over the margin of the gate. | 5.3 |
| The side lean of the spine is 12° (`SideMove.spine`), in place of 10°, so that both paws reach more bars of a run. At 14°, the left paw swing reached 70°, the limit of the arm gate. | 5.3 |
| The mallets keep the depths of `main`, the left nearer than the right. With the set-up of Green (the left farther), the left paw missed a third of its strikes. | 5.3 |
