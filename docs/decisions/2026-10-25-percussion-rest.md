# Decisions of the rest, the runs, and the butt ends of the percussion

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-25, the manager gave the round `percussion-rest`: the sticks at rest, the alternation
of the hands in xylophone runs, the butt ends behind the paws, and the exit of the xylophone
shafts. The goal of the user is that each player holds and plays its instrument as a real player,
with no twist or clip fault from any side. The report
[`2026-10-25-percussion-rest.md`](../research/2026-10-25-percussion-rest.md) gives the measures.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The timpani mallets and the bass drum beater have 6 cm of shaft behind the grip, and the triangle beater 7 cm (`malletButt`), so that 1.8 cm to 2.3 cm sticks out behind the paw, as a real player holds 13 mm to 25 mm. With 4 cm, the butt stayed in the paw. | 4.6 |
| At rest, the snare and the suspended cymbal paws hold the sticks in the rest position of a real snare drummer (Alfred, PAS): level and across the body in front of the waist, a little above the near rim, the palms down (`PercussionHold.set`). The sticks no longer stand up beside the shoulders. | 5.2 |
| The two sticks of the set position cross at two heights: the right stick rises 6° to 7° more than the left (`MalletSet.rise`), so that the shafts do not touch. | 5.2 |
| The wrists of the cymbalist rise 6 cm at rest, so that the yarn heads stay about 2 cm above the plate. No source gives this rest. The bongos with sticks take the positions of the snare drum. | 5.2 |
