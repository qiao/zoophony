# Decisions of the redder red fox

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-19, the user said: "make the red fox's color more red, like the reference image's". The
agent of the round `fox-red` made three variants and chose by the rules of its brief. The report
[`2026-10-19-fox-red.md`](../research/2026-10-19-fox-red.md) gives the measures. The user can
reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "make the red fox's color more red, like the reference image's". The rig moves the orange fur of the red fox in Oklch to the red-orange of the reference foxes (`colors.shift` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json`). | 4.4, 4.5 |
| Of the half step, the full step, and the step past the reference, the full step is nearest to the reference in the play frame (lightness 0.655 against 0.651, hue 43.1° against 43.4°). It reads as red-orange, with no brown and no pink, so it stays. | 4.5 |
| Each fur face takes the move by the smoothstep of its chroma and of its lightness (`shift_colors()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`). Thus the white belly and muzzle, the black legs, the ear backs, the eyes, and the nose keep their colors. | 4.4 |
| A moved color outside the sRGB gamut keeps its lightness and its hue, and takes less chroma (`gamut_chroma()`). A clip of each channel turned the hue by up to 12°. The chroma of the fur stays 0.014 below the reference. | 4.4 |
| The tan palette maps the texture colors before the shift (`COLOR_1` of the GLB file, `FOX_TEXTURE_COLORS` of `tools/studio-body.ts`), so the tan fox keeps each byte of its colors. | 4.4 |
| `SPECIES_COLORS['fox-red'].fur` is the old fur color after the same shift, `#bd4a00`, for the seat dot of the second violins (`models/colors.ts`). | 4.5 |
