# Decisions of the unwind of the left forearm in the violin carry

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-27, the round `fifth-strings3` worked on two faults of the lift and the lowering of the
rabbit, the red fox, and the cat: the left forearm skin unwinds near 180° in each move, and the
carried instrument went up to 2.1 cm into the body near the jaw. The report
[`2026-10-27-violin-unwind.md`](../research/2026-10-27-violin-unwind.md) gives the measures. The
user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.
