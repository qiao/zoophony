# Decisions of the heads L3 task

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-07, the user wrote that the polygons of the animals do not look good, and sent
`lowpoly-animals.png`, `orchestra-latest.png`, and `orchestra-side.png` (design 1). After the
look track (`2026-10-07-look-track.md`), the fox, the wolf, and the cat still had round heads of
12 sides with small even facets, and the reference heads show 6 to 8 large face planes. Stage L3
of the plan [`research/2026-10-07-animal-anatomy-plan.md`](../research/2026-10-07-animal-anatomy-plan.md)
(decision D6) gives the heads large planes. The agent of this task decided the rows below with
close views of each head, crops of the references, and the default view during play. The user
can reverse each row. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.
