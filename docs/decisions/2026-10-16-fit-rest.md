# Decisions of the rest mode and the lap rest of pnpm hold-fit

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-16, two retros asked for one tool change. The rest fit of the round `seat-rest` took
about 40 minutes, with 12 runs of `pnpm hold-fit` and a probe by hand for the violin and the bow.
The hold refit of the round `hip-open` took about 45 minutes, with 70 s to 150 s for each variant
and a separate run of `rest-probe.ts` for the lap distance. The manager of the session gave the
goals, and the agent of the branch `fit-rest` decided the rows below. Each time and each value
comes from the command in its row, on a commit of the branch. The branch changes no source file,
so its source is the source of `main` at `8017b327`. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The rest mode of `pnpm hold-fit` is the option `--sets <set>[,<set>]...`, with the names of `FIT_SETS`: `play`, `lift`, `rest`, and `lower`. It names the sets that the run keeps, so `--sets lift,rest,lower` is the rest mode, and a fit of the lift alone needs no other option. A name that is not a set stops the tool before the first run. The folds have no lowering, and the table gives `-` for a fold set that the run skips. `probeFolds()` of `tools/fold-probe.ts` takes the sets to count, and the sweep filters the sample sets of its role. One variant of `fox-violinist` on `FIT_DEMOS` took 68.7 s with all sets and 5.2 s with `--sets lift,rest,lower` (`pnpm hold-fit fox-violinist`, and with `--sets lift,rest,lower`), with the same deepest point of each kept set. The `violinist` took 5.4 s and the `flutist` 3.8 s | 9.3 |
| The lap rest columns come from `rest-probe.ts` of [`2026-10-16-seat-rest/`](../research/2026-10-16-seat-rest/), as the pure function `lapRest()` of `tools/lap-rest.ts`. The lower end of the instrument is each corner of the `instrument` bone less than 2 cm above its lowest corner. Its gap is the distance to the nearest triangle of the skin of the thighs and the hips (by the main bone of each triangle). The front of the knees is the front of the skin of the thighs. The ends of the bow are its two corners that are farthest apart, and the frog is the end nearer to the right paw | 9.3 |
| The row gives the deepest corner of the lower end in the body in parentheses after the gap, with no mark: the depth comes from `closedDepth()` in the obstacle `body` of the sweep of the role, and a corner in the body gives the gap 0. The column of the instrument in the body already marks the deepest point of the whole instrument over its limit | 9.3 |
| The marks `OVER` of the gap of the lower end follow the goal of the lap rest of [`2026-10-16-seat-rest.md`](2026-10-16-seat-rest.md): a gap above 2 cm. The marks of the bow of a violin role follow the goals of the upright rest of [`2026-10-16-bow-rest.md`](2026-10-16-bow-rest.md), in place of the rise of the bow tip and its reach past the knees | 9.3 |
| The lap rest takes its own rest samples: each seated body of the role in each demo of the run, in the rest before the first play, at the four wall times of `rest-probe.ts` (`REST_WALLS`: 10 s, 13.7 s, 21.1 s, and 29.3 s). The rest set of the sweep has one sample at 10 s, and the idle motion moved the bow tip of the rabbit 0.8 cm past the knees between these times (`docs/research/2026-10-16-hip-open/rest-probe-branch.txt`). A standing soloist has no lap and does not count | 9.3 |
| The skin of the lap of the flute stays the thighs and the hips of the probe. A violin role takes the top of the lap of [`2026-10-16-bow-rest.md`](2026-10-16-bow-rest.md). With the shins too, each largest value of the three roles was the same on `FIT_DEMOS` and on all demos of the flutist (`probeLaps()` with `shinL` and `shinR` in `LAP_BONES`) | 9.3 |
| The values of `main` on `FIT_DEMOS` match `rest-probe-branch.txt` of the round `hip-open` within 0.1 cm (`pnpm hold-fit <role> --sets lift,rest,lower`). The red fox: gap 1.0 cm, rise -1.8 cm, past the knees -16.5 cm (the probe: 1.0 cm, -1.8 cm, -16.5 cm). The rabbit: 1.5 cm, -1.7 cm, -0.6 cm (the probe: 1.5 cm, -1.7 cm, -0.6 cm). No corner of the lower end is in the body | 9.3 |
| A violin or a viola has a lap rest with the bow columns. Of the winds, only a hold of `LEG_RESTS` has the gap column: the flute of the tan fox, which stands upright on the right leg ([`2026-10-15-flute-rest.md`](2026-10-15-flute-rest.md)). The key of a hold is the root of `--set`, `flute@fox-tan`, because the rest belongs to the hold of the species. On `main`, the lower end of the flute is 3.7 cm from the skin of the thighs and the hips, with the mark `OVER` (`pnpm hold-fit flutist --sets lift,rest,lower`) | 9.3 |
| A change of `--set` with the root `<instrument>@<species>` copies each object and each list of its path below the hold of the species in memory before the change (`copyPath()` of `tools/hold-sets.ts`). The copy is shallow at each step, so the other values of a copied object stay shared, and the undo puts the shared objects back. On `main` of 2026-10-16, the rabbit and the red fox shared the list of the bow tip in the rest, and the red fox takes the pole of the left elbow of the chin hold of the violin and the viola, so `violin@fox-red.restBow.tip.0` and `violin@fox-red.poleL.1` also changed these holds. A change with the root of an instrument, such as `violin.restBow.lap.along`, still changes the shared object, and its text names the other paths | 9.3 |
| A `--base` compare keeps each side on its own tool (commit `94c6ace9`, `withProbeTree()` of `tools/ref-tree.ts`). Before this branch merges, the tool of `main` does not know `--sets`, so `pnpm hold-fit fox-violinist --sets lift,rest,lower --base main` stops with the message of that tool, "The option "--sets" is not known.". The branch changes no source file, so `--base HEAD` on the branch gives the table of `--base main` after the merge: the row of the commit and the rows of the working tree with the rest columns, in 15 s with one variant | 9.3 |
| The JSON of `--json` takes the format version 4 for the rest samples of the folds, 5 for the lap rest, and 6 for the upright rest of [`2026-10-16-bow-rest.md`](2026-10-16-bow-rest.md), so a compare with a commit of an older tool stops in `formatNote()` of `tools/ref-tree.ts`. Since [`2026-10-16-retro-arm.md`](2026-10-16-retro-arm.md), the version 7 compares with the version 6, because it only adds fields (`FIT_SINCE`) | 9.3 |
