# Decisions of the dual quaternion bind

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-22, the user found dents and flaps at the arm roots of many animals. The agent of the
round `dq-bind` decided the rows while the user slept. The report
[`2026-10-22-dq-bind.md`](../research/2026-10-22-dq-bind.md) gives the measures. The user can
reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The user: "why there are dents everywhere?", "or is the dent due to twisting?", then "Yes, test the bind". The rule `bind.blend` "dq" of a rig config binds the arm roots with dual quaternion skinning (`bind_skin()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`). | 4.4 |
| The flag ships on no species: on the rabbit, the notch vertices keep their radius, but their depth grows in the bind (5.50 to 5.85 cm) and the crops show no change. | 4.4 |
| The round stops after five rabbit variants by the rule of the brief, and tests no dog, deer, or panda. | 4.4 |
| The blend takes only arm root vertices (an upper arm weight, no lower arm weight). All vertices dropped the seat 3 cm, and all arm vertices folded the left forearm in more lifts. | 4.4 |
| The recline of the trunk and the corrective shapes keep the linear blend, as the app and the standing export use it. | 4.4 |
| The code keeps the flag and `bind.ramp` for a later test. The rabbit flag needs `raise.1.below` 0.3, and with no ramp the standing body misses the source by 2.3 mm (limit 1 mm). | 4.4 |
