# Decisions of the front of the cat arm root

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-28, the round `sixth-cat-root` worked on the dark crease at the front of the left arm
root and on face 1594 at the front of the left armpit, in the image of the user of 2026-10-27,
when the user said "the cat's arm has lumps". The report
[`2026-10-28-cat-root.md`](../research/2026-10-28-cat-root.md) gives the measures and the images.
The user can reverse each decision. Then update its section, and remove its row.
