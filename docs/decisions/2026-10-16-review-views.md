# Decisions of the round review-views

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-16 at about 23:10, the user asked the manager to review the poses of the fox, the
rabbit, and the cat from different angles and in different states, "including rest and play, and
the transition in between". The close views showed the playing pose and the rest pose only. The
manager asked for moments of the lift and of the lowering in the gallery and in
`pnpm close-views`, and for a review image set. The agent of the branch `review-views` decided
these rows.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The gallery query `lift=<share>` shows each animal at that share of its lift from the rest pose to the playing pose, and `lower=<share>` at that share of its lowering after a pause (`galleryMove()` of `src/gallery/items.ts`). A share below 0 or above 1 goes to the nearest end. `lift` comes before `lower`, and both come before `rest`. A value that is not a number, or no value, gives no move | 7.1 |
| The share is a part of the time of the move after its seeded delay. The delay is a time with no motion, so a share of the whole time with the delay puts the early shares near the rest pose. At the share 0.33, the weight of the playing pose of the rabbit is 0.162 with the rule, and 0.055 with the delay in the share (`docs/research/2026-10-16-review-views/transition-probe.txt`) | 5.2, 7.1 |
| The pose comes from `poseAt()` with a transport of the app (`moveTransport()`). The lift plays from a change before the wall time by the delay and the share of the duration, from the rest pose, with no count-in limit. The scale of a short count-in (`liftScale()`) changes only the times of a lift, so the poses of the shares stay. The lowering pauses a play that lifted each animal long before, and starts after the hold of the pause (`REST_TIMES.hold`). The tool copies no motion rule | 5.2, 7.1 |
| Each animal of the page gets its own transport, because each animal has its own seeded delay and duration, and one transport gives each animal another share. The gallery splits its plan into one plan for each animal (`aloneOf()` of `src/gallery/main.ts`). The plan of one animal alone gives the pose of the full plan with the same transport, within 3.4e-6° | 7.1 |
| The pose at `lift=0` and at `lower=1` equals the rest pose, and the pose at `lift=1` and at `lower=0` equals the playing pose at the time of the slider. The largest bone difference was 3.4e-6°, with no difference of the offsets, over each species item of the gallery, seated and standing, at the song times 0 and 0.55, and the conductor (`transition-probe.txt` of `docs/research/2026-10-16-review-views/`). Two equal poses give 3.0e-6° in the same probe, so the poses are equal. The probe takes the angle of the normalized quaternions, because an angle of the float32 quaternions as they are gave 0.04° for two equal poses | 7.1 |
| The moments of the mode `species` of `pnpm close-views` are `play`, `rest`, `lift<p>`, and `lower<p>`, with a whole percent `p` from 0 to 100 and no leading zero (`speciesMoment()`). The names are also the folder names of the mode `batch`, such as `species-lift33/`, so they hold no dot and no colon. The slider stays at the time 0 of the default test notes, as for `play`. `--check` stops on an unknown name before the build, with the list of the names | 7.1 |
| The roles `violinist`, `fox-violinist`, `violist`, and `flutist` always get the rows `lift50` and `lower50` after the rest pose (`halfMoves()`), with no flag. The slider stays at the time of the first moment of the role, the frog or the long note, so that the lift goes from the rest pose to that moment. These roles hold the animals of the review of the user, and each round of these roles then shows the moves. The rows add 8 cells to each of the four grids. The conductor and the pianist keep their rows | 7.1 |
| `--zoom` of the mode `batch` can come more than one time. Each zoom other than 1 gives the moments again into `species-<moment>-zoom<n>/`, and the zoom 1 keeps `species-<moment>/`. The folder takes the number of the zoom, so `2.5` and `2.50` come two times and stop the tool. The zoom aims at the middle of the box of the body, and the round added no aim at the paws: at the zoom 2.5, the cells of a seated player show the lap, the paws in the rest, and the bow paw in the play | 7.1 |
| The review set of the round is not in the repository. One batch made it: `pnpm close-views batch scratch/review --species fox-red,fox-tan,rabbit,cat --moments play,rest,lift33,lift66,lower33,lower66 --views front,front35,left,side,back,above35 --zoom 1 --zoom 2.5 --role violinist --role fox-violinist --role violist --role flutist`. The standing soloists come from the row `stand` of the role views, because the mode `species` has no standing moment. It wrote 396 cells and 16 grids in 259 s with the build (`/usr/bin/time -p`), at a 1-minute load average of 46 to 50. A script in `scratch/` of the worktree made one WebP grid of each species from the cells, with the six moments, the play and the rest at the zoom 2.5, and the stand row. Each grid was below 300 kB | 9.8 |
