# Decisions of the choir shots

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-06, the Ode to Joy demo became the choir theme and the coda (row 1 of
[`2026-10-06-joyful-ode.md`](2026-10-06-joyful-ode.md)). The choir sings in 82% of the time,
but the shot plan had no close shot of the choir. The project manager asked for the choir as a
proper subject of the shots. The choir shots agent decided the details below. The user can
reverse each one. Then update the section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| A head of another player in a choir shot with its top under the chest of each singer on the screen covers no face and no folder. The rules of a near head and of the middle of the frame skip it, and the rule of a large head still applies. The chest is 1.0 `H` below the middle of the head. With no such rule, the heads refused each camera of the choir section below 50°, and the plan had no choir section shot. The feet of the singers in place of their chests still refused each camera below 50° | 5.6 |
| All singers of a choir seat are subject players of a choir shot. The eighth singer of the demo stands outside the sphere of 2.6 m, and its head refused cameras of the section | 5.6 |
| An active choir seat sings the tune when its top voice has the key of the top line, or that key one or more octaves lower, in half the window or more. It then carries the lead. The flutes and the violins double the sopranos an octave higher, so the top line alone gave the choir 0% to 34% of each window. The rule is for a choir seat only, because the voices sing the words | 5.6 |
| A choir seat that sings the tune adds a part shot: a section shot of the players of its top voice, with the group `choir` and the score of a section shot of the seat alone. The sphere of a part holds the place of one more singer (0.6 m) on each side. With the section margin, the heads of the contrabass players refused each camera of the sopranos below 50° within 60° of their front | 5.6 |
| No lift of the elevation for a choir shot, as the piano has. With the two rules above, the choir section has clear cameras from 26° and the sopranos from 18°, in front of them | 5.6 |
| No extra bonus of the choir in a tutti. With the lead of the tune, the plan of the demo has 3 close shots of the choir: the sopranos at 8.6 s, the full choir at 48.6 s, and the principal singer at 127.7 s | 5.6 |
| The test of the first voice at the first cut is back, with the test file `score/test-first-voice.ts`: an oboe alone from 1 s, and four string seats that enter at the first cut. With the rule, the oboe has the first close shot for the seeds 1 to 5. Without it, the strings have it. This replaces the last sentence of the shot row of `2026-10-06-joyful-ode.md` | 5.6 |
