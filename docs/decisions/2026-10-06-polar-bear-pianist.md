# Decisions of the polar bear pianist

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-06, the user asked for a polar bear as the pianist, in place of the meerkat. The agent
of the polar bear decided the details below. The user can reverse each one. Then update the
section that holds it, and remove its row.

| Decision | Section |
|---|---|
| The polar bear has the size 1.1, the size of the deer of the harp. At 1.0, it was as tall as a rabbit, and its small ears made it look smaller. The size rule of the piano changes from 1.0 or less to 1.1 or less. In the default view and the three other wide views, the polar bear hides 0% of the head of each other animal at the sizes 1.0, 1.1, and 1.15, in each demo with each section size, and in the piano concerto, the piano solo, and the duo test files. The case of the celesta of Boulanger hides 8.9% of the head of a horn pig at the azimuth 0°, against 3.2% with the meerkat, because the bench moves with the size of the pianist | 4.2, 4.3 |
| The polar bear has the girth 1.0, as the bear. The round torso already reads as a heavy body | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The polar bear gets two new part types: `low-round` ears (discs of 0.24 H, low on the sides of the head) and a `roman` muzzle (0.27 H in front of the face, with its top in line with the forehead). With the round ears and the short muzzle of the bear, it looked like a white teddy bear | 4.3, 4.5 |
| The fur is `#efeae0` with a belly of `#f6f2ea`, and the accent color `#1a1715` colors only the nose. The default nose color `#2a1d15` is dark brown | 4.5 |
| The celesta, the harpsichord, the clavinet, and the organs follow the piano: the polar bear plays them. They are seats of the piano section (3.8), and the section map of 4.3 gives one species to a section. They also play the model of the grand piano (4.6) | 4.3 |
| The gallery shows each species of a seat and the bear of the conductor, so the meerkat leaves the gallery | 7.1 |
| The views page keeps the models of `animals.webp`, which has no polar bear | 7.1 |
