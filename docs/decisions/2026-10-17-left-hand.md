# Decisions of the left hand of the string players

The Section column names a section of the design. [`design.md`](../design.md) gives the file of
each section.

On 2026-10-17, the user wrote: "currently, the violin, viola, and cello players are moving their
left hand up and down too much, whereas in reality many notes do not need to move hand, just need
to move fingers. Do a deep research on how professional players move their hands/fingers (both
left and right) and adjust the the movement's accordingly". Then: "make sure to document the
professional movements so that they can be referenced later". The round `string-hands` wrote the
reference [`string-technique.md`](../reference/string-technique.md) and the motion spec of the
report [`2026-10-17-string-hands.md`](../research/2026-10-17-string-hands.md). The manager gave the
round `left-hand` the left hand of the spec (L1 to L5) and the defaults of its open questions. The
agent of the branch decided the other rows. The report
[`2026-10-17-left-hand.md`](../research/2026-10-17-left-hand.md) gives the measures, the probes,
and the images. The user can reverse each decision. Then update the section that holds it, and
remove its row.
