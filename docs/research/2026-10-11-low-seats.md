# Low seats: the chairs at 0.75 head heights

Date: 2026-10-11. Branch: `low-seats`, from `main` at `585140c1`. The decision file
[`2026-10-11-low-seats.md`](../decisions/2026-10-11-low-seats.md) gives the decisions. The report
[`2026-10-11-short-legs.md`](2026-10-11-short-legs.md) gives the measures of the reference images.

## 1. The request

After the short legs, the seated rabbits, foxes, and wolves still looked almost the same in the
default views. The manager offered to "lower all chairs from 0.92 to about 0.75 head heights, so
that the seated legs are clearly shorter; it lowers the sheet match a little", and the user said
"do it".

## 2. The change

The default `seatHeight` of the skeleton is 0.75 H (0.92 H before). It is the height of the seat of
the chair above the floor, and of the seat of the high stool above its foot ring. The knee stays
0.1 H above the seat, so the shin gets shorter, and the feet stay flat on the floor.

| Seat | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Chair of the rabbit and the foxes (H 0.380 m) | 0.350 m | 0.285 m |
| Chair of the cat, the wolf, the beaver, and the monkey (H 0.361 m) | 0.332 m | 0.271 m |
| Chair of the dog and the pig (H 0.399 m) | 0.367 m | 0.299 m |
| Chair of the mouse (H 0.285 m) | 0.262 m | 0.214 m |
| Chair of the panda (H 0.456 m) | 0.420 m | 0.342 m |
| Chair of the rhino (H 0.513 m) | 0.472 m | 0.385 m |
| Foot ring of the high stool of the hippo (seat 0.70 m) | 0.228 m | 0.315 m |
| Stool of the harp, piano bench | 0.35 m, 0.45 m | the same |

```text
 seated leg, in H above the floor (each chair species)

   before                         now
   1.24 -- knee top               1.07 -- knee top
   1.02 -- knee center            0.85 -- knee center
   0.92 -- seat                   0.75 -- seat
   0.25 -- top of the foot        0.25 -- top of the foot
   0    -- floor                  0    -- floor
   visible shin: 0.55 H           visible shin: 0.38 H
```

The visible shin, from the bottom of the round knee to the top of the foot, is 31% shorter. The
harp stool and the piano bench keep their heights, because the strings and the keys need their
players at that height (the decision file of the short legs gives the faults).

**The seat height.** The user asked for about 0.75 H. Seats of 0.72 H, 0.75 H, and 0.78 H each
failed the same two tests of the cello. With 0.78 H, a lean of 9° alone was enough. The branch
keeps the 0.75 H of the user.

| Seat | Faults of the full test suite before the pose fits |
|---|---|
| 0.72 H | the bow stick 12.9 mm into the right forearm of the dog, the head of the dog 0.023 m from the neck of the cello |
| 0.75 H | the same, 6.0 mm and 0.026 m, and the right upper arm of the pig 0.031 H in the trunk |
| 0.78 H | the same, 1.9 mm and 0.029 m |

**The pose fits.** Each value fixes a failed behavior test:

| Value | Before | Now | Fault before |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean of the cellist in play | 10° | 8° | The head of the dog came 0.026 m to the neck of the cello (limit 0.03 m). |
| Forward turn of the right shoulder of the cellist | 40° | 45° | The bow stick went 6.0 mm into the right forearm of the dog (limit 1 mm). |
| Right elbow of the horn in play | (0.5, -1, -0.5) | (0.6, -1, -0.5) | The right upper arm of the pig went 0.031 H into the trunk (limit 0.02 H). |

The trials for the cello at 0.75 H: a lean of 8°, 7°, or 6° alone cleared the neck, but the stick
stayed 1.9 mm to 5.2 mm in the forearm. A shorter end pin (0.15 m or 0.12 m) moved the cello
down along its axis, and the stick went 16 mm to 21 mm into the forearm. A higher right elbow,
(1, 1.8, -0.3), and a turn of 45° each cleared both faults with the lean of 8°. The branch takes
the turn, one angle.

`pnpm body-probe --compare` against `main`: the bodies on the chair (19 species) and on the high
stool (18 species) change. Each body on the stool, on the bench, and standing is the same. Of the
players, the 12 chair players and the hippo of the high stool change. The conductor, the penguin of
the choir, the standing players, the harp player, and the pianist are the same. The bear and the
penguin on a chair change, but the cast never gives them a chair.

## 4. Before and after

`scripts/low-seats/compare.py` makes four images of the seated animals in
[`2026-10-11-low-seats/`](2026-10-11-low-seats/). In each image, the top row is `main` and the
bottom row is the branch:

- `play-front-rows.png`: the default demo during play, in the default view (`pnpm snapshot`): the
  rabbits, the foxes, the cats, and the dogs of the front rows.
- `species-row-1.png` and `species-row-2.png`: the close views of the gallery models of the
  species sheet (`pnpm snapshot`), each on its chair.
- `views-rabbit-side.png`: the side view of the rabbit page of the views page
  (`pnpm sheet-check rabbit`).

To make them again: run `pnpm snapshot` and `pnpm sheet-check fox <dir>/fox` and
`pnpm sheet-check rabbit <dir>/rabbit` on `main` and on the branch, then run
`uv run --no-project --with pillow python -I docs/research/scripts/low-seats/compare.py`
with the two snapshot folders, the two sheet check folders, and the out folder. The images of this
report came from `docs/snapshots/2026-10-11-73d2a012` (the code of `main`) and
`docs/snapshots/2026-10-11-548f3dc8` (the code of the branch).

In the default view, the seated animals sit lower, their knees are near the seat, and their
lower legs are short blocks above the feet. In the side view of the rabbit, the knee is at the
height of the seat, and the shin is short.
