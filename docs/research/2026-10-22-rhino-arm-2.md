[`2026-10-22-rhino-arm-2.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-rhino-arm-2.md). The probes and their

## 1. The question

The user said: "the panda and rhino's arms also got deformed", then, after the round patch of
`rhino-arm` (`b22a786c`), "the rhino's left arm is now completed deformed", and after its revert,
"the rhino's arm is still deeformed". The goal: both arms read as round, clean arms at full size
in the app, from the stage camera and from each view.

## 3. The edit

`rhino_tubes.py` writes `assets/models/rhino/rhino-tubes.glb` (the file of the user stays byte
for byte). For each side, it keeps the 136 faces of the paw beyond the wrist plane, removes the 207
other faces with a corner in the free arm, fills the side of the trunk (33 rim vertices, y 0.19 to
0.68 units) with 193 and 209 faces, and builds a tube of 23 rings of 14 vertices from the wrist to
a dome inside the shoulder. Each ring is an ellipse that spans the front and the back of the
source arm and touches its outer side. The fill smooths the distance from the middle of the trunk
with a bilaplacian, because a fill of the points sank 5 cm into the trunk under the shoulder.

The rig (`tools/mesh-rigs/rhino.json`) has no cut, `weights.armIslands`, the new rule
`weights.armOwn` (each vertex of the free arm keeps only its arm weights), and `weights.armBelow`
[0.6, 0.7]. `headFloor` 0.668 of `ANIMALS` keeps H at 0.420 m (0.442 m without it).

The down area grows, because the fill under each shoulder faces down, as the underside of a
shoulder does. These faces have no arm weight and the arm does not move them. On `afff590f`, the
inner side of the arm was there, and its faces were arm faces, which the probe leaves out. The
dragged down area grows on the left and falls on the right. The dome and the inner side of each
tube lie inside the trunk, so the sweep gives the upper arms 12.6 cm in the body. No view shows
them.
