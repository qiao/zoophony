# The rebuilt arms of the mouse and the wolf, and the wind rests

Date 2026-10-25. Decisions:
[`2026-10-25-wind-arms.md`](../decisions/2026-10-25-wind-arms.md). The probe outputs and the images
are in [`2026-10-25-wind-arms/`](2026-10-25-wind-arms/). "Main" is `main` at 7da10e89. Each command
runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user saw dark slits and shards on the right arm of the mouse ("the mouse's right arm is
regressed"), and allowed loop edges, retopology, and extra bones for the arm and wrist twist on
2026-10-24. The manager asked to rebuild the arms of the mouse oboist and the wolf clarinetist with
the method of the rabbit ([`rig-blender.md`](../agents/rig-blender.md)), to fix their holds and the
rest of the tan fox flute, and to go on until each image passes.

## 2. The route

```text
 mouse.glb / wolf.glb (user)
   --> Merge by Distance --> Bisect at the shoulder line and the wrist --> Delete Faces
   --> Collapse Edges on thin faces at the cuts and the mouse armpits  (new)
   --> quad tube --> Shrinkwrap --> Bridge Edge Loops --> Beautify --> Collapse Edges (new)
   --> UV, bake, normals --> arms x 1.4 (mouse) or x 1.178 (wolf) --> <s>-arms.glb
   --> skeleton + segments + helpers --> automatic weights --> paint fixes --> <s>-weights.json
                                   (live Blender, MCP; tools/blender/wind_arms.py replays it)
   --> pnpm rig-mesh <s> (armFactor 1, paintedWeights, paintedLower) --> pnpm studio-body <s>
```

A headless run of `tools/blender/wind_arms.py <animal>` gives both files byte for byte. The wolf
config scales its source (`proportions`, `soften`), so the session works in the units of the
source, and the weights file holds its points in the units of the config.

| Item | Mouse | Wolf |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulder cut | 0.6 of the upper arm, square to it: higher, no closed loop around the arm | 0.3, tilted 20° |
| Joint gaps | 0.0085 units | elbow 0.016, wrist 0.0085 |
| Paint fixes | trunk side, elbows, front of the armpits | those of the rabbit |
| Faces that turn over in the bind, after the relax | 15 before the armpit collapse, 0 after | 0 |

The thin faces of a Bisect, the bridge, and the mouse armpit folded in each pose, so the replay
collapses their short edges (`step_clean_cut()`, `step_clean_bridge()`, `step_clean_pit()`). With
the elbow gap 0.0085, 9 faces of the left wolf elbow folded in the play, with 0.016, 4. A wrist gap
of 0.016 opened the right wolf paw on the clarinet (axis 1.0 cm to 1.5 cm from its middle).
