# The arm form of the rabbit and the pig against the red fox

Date 2026-10-21. Round `arm-form`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-21-arm-form.md`](../decisions/2026-10-21-arm-form.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-21-arm-form/`](2026-10-21-arm-form/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user said: "the rabbit and pig's arms are still very deformed. take screenshot for youself.
note that the fox's arm is looking great." The pig showed upper arms like crumpled paper, and the
bow upper arm of the rabbit showed as a flat wide plank. The arm gate gave both PASS, and the fold
probe gave 0% of the play, so the round needed a measure of the look, with the red fox as the
target.

## 2. The measures

`arm-look.ts` (phase 1, now `pnpm arm-look`) poses each body over the play and compares each face
of the upper arm and the shoulder with the same face of the mesh of the user. The rig script gives
the corners of the source face of each face of the module (`rig_mesh_dump.patch`, now
`--corners`). The crease share is the share of the edges whose fold angle changes by more than 40°
from the long-armed source. The section is the narrow axis of the middle ring of the upper arm
over its wide axis.

## 3. The causes

**Pig.** The mesh has no upper arm of its own. A cut across the middle of the upper arm is 0.24
units wide, the trunk and the arm in one loop, and the arm is a separate loop only from the elbow
down (`source-rings.txt`, the sections of phase 1). The faces there are long: the mean edge is
0.37 of the upper arm (0.22 on the fox), and bone heat gives a zone of the trunk 7.2 squared upper
arm lengths of mixed weight (4.7 on the fox, `bind-facts.txt`). Each turn of the arm crumples that
wall (`images/pig-flagged-faces-L.webp`). No arm stretch, split plane, weight rule, ring, cut,
twist bone, or elbow pole brought the left arm below 14% (`variants.txt`, `look/`). With the arm
factor 1, the images still crumpled.

**Rabbit.** Its skin deforms as the fox skin does. The upper arm of the mesh is flat (0.45 to 0.48
at its middle, 0.65 to 0.68 on the fox), the stretch 1.63 makes it long, and the bow elbow pointed
down, so the broad side faced the front. The dark wedge on the chest is the shadow of the bow arm.

## 4. The result

The user chose a remodel of the pig and the round upper arm with the bow elbow of the fox for the
rabbit. `pig_arms.py`, run in Blender through MCP, cuts each arm below the elbow, removes the 93 to
95 faces of the bulge, fills the wall with a fan, and builds a tube of two rings of 10 edges up to
a dome around the shoulder joint, at 3,162 triangles (budget 3,200). The rig takes the free arm and
a twist bone of 0.4. `rabbit_arms.py` moves 8 and 9 vertices of each upper arm toward the axis.
A move of the arm run of the cut folded the quad at the top of the right cut in each play sample,
and a new shape for it twisted the arm (twist p95 104), so the arm run keeps its places.

`pnpm arm-check --all --base main` gives no `FAIL`. `pnpm body-probe --base main` changes only the
5 bodies of the rabbit and of the pig and their 2 players. `pnpm pose-depth` grows the paws of the
rabbit in the violin from 2.1 cm to 2.4 cm, and the upper arms of the pig in the body from at most 0.5 cm
to 4.2 cm in each set, where the dome lies inside the shoulder (`round/pose-depth-*.txt`).

`pnpm size --base main` gives 996 B less in the first download (`round/size.txt`). The crop
pairs of `pnpm arm-check --views` (`round/pairs/`, the base on the left) show the arms of the pig
as tubes, with a thin dark line where each tube enters the shoulder. On mozart-k525 alone, the
move twist max of the bow arm of the rabbit grows from 107° to 128° (limit 176°).

The lineups `phase2-pig-lineup.webp` and `phase2-rabbit-lineup.webp` show `main`, the round, and
the fox at a note and at the rest, from the front and at ±35°, and `phase2-*-crops.webp` the 2x
crops of the arms. Over all mesh roles of `main`, the oboe mouse, the elephant, the beaver, and the
rhino are past a limit of `pnpm arm-look` (`arm-look-all-main.txt`).

## 5. Round arm-form-2

The user asked to fix the line at the shoulders of the pig. The outputs are in `round2/`: the
lineups and 2x crops from the front, the back, each side, and ±35°, and the 3x crops of the pig
shoulders from each side.

**Pig.** The cause was the fill of the wall: the saw rim of the hole and the fan from its middle
showed as a star of dark spikes at the top of each shoulder. `pig_arms.py` now smooths the fan, the rim,
and two rings of the trunk around it in 12 rounds, and the right dome stands 1.2 radii above the
shoulder joint, because a small dark notch showed there at the back (`pig-variants.txt`). The dark skin pixels of the shoulders in the
side views fall from 27,623 to 7,257 in 12 boxes (`shoulder_dark.py`, `round2/shoulder-dark.txt`,
before the higher right dome).

**Rabbit.** The cut closes the inner side of each upper arm with a flat cap, whose arm run sets
the width of the arm. The left arm now also moves its inner side and its arm run. A move of the right arm run
folded the caps of the body of the right cut in each rest sample (`round2/rabbit-variants.txt`).
