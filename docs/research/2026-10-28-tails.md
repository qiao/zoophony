# The seated tails of the cat and the dog

Date 2026-10-28. Decisions: [`2026-10-28-tails.md`](../decisions/2026-10-28-tails.md). The rig
logs and the images are in [`2026-10-28-tails/`](2026-10-28-tails/). Each command runs from the
root of the tree. "Main" is `1aea48ff1`.

## 1. The question

The user gave two close images of the stage and said: "the cat's tail has malformed meshes" and
"the dog's tail is unnatural". On `main`, the curled tail of the cat was a thick club with sharp
lumps that stuck out past the left side of the seat. The hanging tail of the dog was a flat blade
with a sharp back edge, and from the back right its two edges showed as two spikes between the
back posts of the chair.

## 2. The source and the rig

In Blender, the source tail of each mesh is a smooth curved tube: the cat tail hangs down and
bends back at its end, and the dog tail is a curved wedge. `tail_curl()` of
`tools/blender/rig_mesh.py` moved each tail vertex from its place along the line of the two tail
bones (the root, the bend, and the tip) to the same place along the curve of `studio.tailCurl`.

- The line of the bones has a sharp corner at the bend, about 70° on the cat. The vertices near
  the bend took the frame of one bone or the other, and the vertices outside the corner all went
  to the point of the bend. So the tail folded and went flat near the bend.
- The bone line lies near the back of the tube, away from its middle, so the offsets of the front
  vertices were large, and the curl swelled them into the club.
- The curve of the cat curl bends with a radius of 0.07 to 0.15 units, and the tube has a radius
  of about 0.03 units, so the curve alone does not fold the tube.
- The dog rule `thin` shrank the offset of each vertex to 0.4 within 0.08 units of the root. The
  section kept the shape of the wedge, so the tail stayed a blade, and the outline had a step
  where the shrink started.

## 3. The fix

`tail_line()` fits a Catmull-Rom curve through the middle of ten slices of the vertices only on
the tail bones, and the curl measures each vertex against that curve. `taubin()` smooths the
curled tail by the rounds of `studio.tailCurl.smooth`: a half step to the middle of the
neighbors, then 0.53 of the step back, so the tube keeps its width.
