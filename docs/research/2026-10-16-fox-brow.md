Date: 2026-10-16. Branch `fox-brow`, from `main` 9079ee84. The decisions are in

command runs from the root of the repository.

## The mark

The user sent a screenshot of the red fox from the front and said: "The fox has a black mark
above the eyes". The mark is one small black wedge above the inner corner of the eye at the right
of the image, which is the left eye of the fox.

`brow-faces.ts` reads a rig GLB file and lists each dark face near each old eye. A face is dark
when its Oklab lightness is below 0.45, or when its Oklab distance from the color of the gloss fan
of the eye is below 0.25. The probe measures in radii of the fan, seen along the normal of the
fan. On the fox rig of `main` (`brow-faces-fox-main.log`):

| Eye | Face | Color | Across | In, up | Along the normal | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L | 2048 | `#211006`, the eye color | 1.67 radii | 0.91, 1.40 | 0.09 radii | the mark |
| R | 691, 690, 673, 674 | `#302c29`, `#211d1b` | 2.57 to 2.97 radii | 0.48 to 1.12, -2.52 to -2.74 | 0.58 to 1.21 radii | the mouth |

Face 2048 is the end of the black upper lid line of the almond eye of the texture. The old eye
rule of 2026-10-15 holds each face within 1 radius (`reach` of `eyeShell`), so the face stayed
black. No other face near the eyes has a lightness below 0.6 (`brow-faces-fox-main-0.6.log`), so
the brown lid shading above the right eye is not dark. The rabbit has no dark face within 3
radii of its eyes (`brow-faces-rabbit-main.log`, `brow-faces-rabbit-main-0.6.log`).

`fox-eye-faces.webp` draws the faces near each eye of the fox, seen along the normal of the fan,
with the inner corner of each eye at the left: `main` above the branch. The fan is green, the
circles of 1 and 2 radii are blue, and the dark faces of the probe have a red outline. The command:

```bash
node docs/research/2026-10-16-fox-brow/brow-faces.ts fox <main rig> main.json 3 0.45
node docs/research/2026-10-16-fox-brow/brow-faces.ts fox '' branch.json 3 0.45
uv run --no-project --with pillow python -I docs/research/2026-10-16-fox-brow/draw.py \
  fox-eye-faces.webp 'fox main'=main.json 'fox branch'=branch.json
```

## The rule

After the old eyes, `pnpm rig-mesh` gives each patch of dark faces near an old eye the color with
the largest area in the ring of faces around the patch (`dark_faces_near()` and
`recolor_old_eyes()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`). A face counts when the dark rule of the
texture marks it (`face_colors()`), no old eye holds it, and its middle lies within `darkReach`
radii of the middle of the eye and within half a radius of the plane of the eye. `darkReach` is 2
on both configs. The mark lies at 1.67 radii, and the mouth at 2.57 radii and more.

The rig of the fox gives face 2048 the fur color `#f18a30`, which covers 88% of the area of its
ring (`rig-mesh-fox.log`). The rule changes no other face of the fox and no face of the rabbit:
the check of the rabbit gives a rig with the bytes of the rig file of `main`
(`rig-mesh-check-rabbit.log`, sha256 92ba1e6e). On the branch, the probe finds no dark face within
2 radii of an eye of the fox (`brow-faces-fox-branch.log`, `brow-faces-fox-branch-0.6.log`).

`faces-main-branch.webp` shows the red fox and the tan fox from the front and from 35°, `main`
above the branch, with the camera of `docs/research/2026-10-16-pea-eyes/faces.ts`:

```bash
node docs/research/2026-10-16-pea-eyes/faces.ts <dir> main=<dist of main> branch=<dist> \
  -- fox-red,fox-tan front,quarter
uv run --no-project --with pillow python -I docs/research/2026-10-16-pea-eyes/grid.py <dir> \
  faces-main-branch.webp 4 <the eight images>
```

The tan fox shares the rig and maps each face color to its tan palette, so its mark goes too.
