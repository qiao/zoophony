# The deer mesh of the user as the harp player

Date 2026-10-21. Round `mesh-deer`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-21-mesh-deer.md`](../decisions/2026-10-21-mesh-deer.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-21-mesh-deer/`](2026-10-21-mesh-deer/): the mesh probes at the top, the scans of
the harp in `harp/`, and the checks of the app in `app/`. Each command runs from the root of the
tree, and the base is `a80d80bf`.

## 1. The question

The user gave a textured deer mesh with no skeleton (`assets/models/deer/deer.glb`) and asked for a
seated deer with its hip fully on the seat, at about the size of the parametric deer. The user then
chose the stool of the harp. The measures that decide are the size rule of the user, the gap under
the haunch, the reach of the strings, the clearance of the arms, and the arm gate.

## 2. The measures

**Mesh.** One part of 1,573 vertices and 3,140 triangles (`facts.txt`). The deer stands upright on
short legs (crotch at y 0.245 u), with short arms, dark hooves, large ears out at the sides, antlers
from the skull top (0.852 u) to 1 u, and a puff tail (`sections-*.txt`, `measures.txt`,
`arm-axis.txt`, `paw.txt`, `free-arm.txt`, `source-views.webp`). `make_config.py` writes the rig
config.

**Size.** No reference image shows a deer, so the mesh takes the skull top of the parametric deer
(`landmark-probe.ts`, `landmark-probe.txt`, run on the base):

| Measure | `main` (parametric) | round |
|---|---|---|
| Standing skull top (below the antlers) | 1.191 m | 1.191 m (0.8523 u at 1.3974 m/u) |
| Top of the antlers, standing | 1.344 m | 1.397 m |
| Seated skull top | 1.311 m | 1.125 m |
| H | 0.418 m | 0.297 m (`headCeiling` 0.87 u) |
| Seat | 0.35 m (stool) | 0.206 m (stool that fits the legs) |
| Arm, shoulder joint to the middle of the palm | about 0.6 m | 0.312 m, 0.382 m with the factor 1.25 |

The standing skull top is within 0% of `main`, inside the 3% of the brief. The seated deer is lower,
because its legs are shorter than the legs of the parametric deer, and its head is smaller
(`app/lineup-base-views.webp`). With the antlers in the head measures, H was 0.481 m.

**Seat.** `pnpm rig-mesh deer --check` (`app/check-final.log`). The front edge of the rig chair is
at 0.025 u, before the shins (0.0325 u). Sit variants of `pnpm rig-mesh deer --check --set`
(`sit-variants.log`), as the side view of the underside behind the hip joints above the seat top:

| Sit rule | Before the sit | After the sit | Final bind |
|---|---|---|---|
| panda (`most` 0.03) | 9.6 cm | 6.0 cm | 5.4 cm |
| `most` 0.09 | 9.6 cm | 1.6 cm | 1.8 cm |
| `most` 0.12 | 9.6 cm | 1.5 cm | 1.6 cm |
| dog (`most` 0.25, `top`), chosen | 8.1 cm | 1.6 cm | 1.0 cm |

The puff tail stays 0.0658 u (9.2 cm) above the seat top, and the soles are on the floor (leg L
and R 0.000 m). `app/seat-crops.webp` gives 3x crops of the seat from the sides and the back: the
haunch rests on the seat of the stool.

**Arms and harp.** The real harpist sits so that the hands reach the middle of the strings, with the
elbows raised and the wrists a little low, and the harp rests on the right shoulder and between the
knees (musicmark.org.uk, "A Common Approach", harp, area B, B1). A forum of harpists gives the
target as "elbows out, forearms parallel to the floor" (harpcolumn.com, "sitting at the balancing
point of the harp"). The concert harp of the app was too far and too high for the arms of the
mesh: 0% of the 350 note targets of `boulanger-matin` within 5 mm. Scans of `pnpm pose-depth
harpist --gaps --set` (`harp/`):

| Arm factor | Best harp found | Targets within 5 mm | Forearms in body | Upper arms in body |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | scale 0.65, upright, turned 10°, 0.45 m | 99.1% | 8.2 cm | 3.6 cm |
| 1.15 | scale 0.65, upright, turned 30°, 0.40 m | 100% | 3.9 cm | 3.1 cm |
| 1.25 | scale 0.65, upright, turned 30°, 0.45 m | 100% | 2.2 cm | 2.3 cm |

With the factor 1, each place that reached the strings put the left forearm through the chest,
because the strings stand near the chest (`harp/depths-arms1.txt`, `harp/targets-arms1.txt`). A
tilt back of the harp put the upper strings into the chest (`harp/depths-arms125-a.txt`). The
manager chose the factor 1.25, the largest factor of the other meshes with no choice of the user.

**Hold.** With the fingers on the line from the shoulder, the left paw bent 86° to 91° on its
forearm (paw swing, limit 70°). The left fingers up along the strings and the left elbow down give
65°. The aim moved the wrist away, so the left palm lies 0.1 m above the middle of each string,
and the harp stands at 0.44 m (`harp/arm-aims.txt`). A left palm on the thigh at rest folded the
left shoulder (11 faces, limit 8) and flattened the upper arm (0.39, limit 0.55), so both hands
rest on a string.

**Colors and face.** `dark_probe.py` (`dark-probe.txt`): the fur has Oklab L 0.571, hue 59°, and
chroma 0.092 against 0.609, 61°, and 0.085 of `SPECIES_COLORS.deer`, so the config has no tone
rule. The dark limit 0.0093 makes 168 faces dark: the hands, the hooves, the eyes, and the nose.
The smile line of the texture shows (`app/head-views.webp`).

**Balance.** The deer sits, so its support is its soles and its haunch on the seat (`balance.ts`,
`balance.txt`):

| Sample | Behind the middle of the soles | Inside the support of the soles and the haunch |
|---|---|---|
| rest | 12.4 cm | 1.5 cm |
| note, median | 9.6 cm | 4.2 cm |

## 3. The result

**Gates.** `pnpm pose-depth harpist` (`app/pose-depth.txt`): each pair is within its limit, with
three accepted depths of 3.5 cm, 3 cm, and 2.5 cm (the right forearm on the soundbox, the upper
arms on the sides, the left forearm at the side of the chest). Each note target is within 3.4 mm,
and the head keeps 5.7 cm from the harp (`app/pose-depth-gaps.txt`). `pnpm arm-check harpist
--base a80d80bf` (`app/arm-check.txt`):

| Arm | Folds p95 | Twist p95 | Forearm twist p95 | Flat p5 | Paw twist p95 | Paw swing p95 | Jumps | Stretch p95 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L | 4 | 24 | 23 | 0.71 | 70 | 55 | 0.0% | 0.11 | PASS |
| R | 7 | 40 | 42 | 0.73 | 104 | 29 | 0.0% | 0.07 | PASS |

`app/arms-pluck.webp` and `app/arms-rest.webp` give the 2x crops: the parametric deer of the base
at the left and the mesh at the right.

**Other bodies.** `app/body-probe.txt`: only the deer bodies and the harp player change. The poses
of the other players of `boulanger-matin` change, because the smaller body and harp move the
layout. `app/shots.txt`: the 5 shot tests that fail also fail on the base. `app/size.txt`:
+25,577 B, 762,135 B in all.

**Images.** `pnpm close-views batch <dir> --species deer,dog,wolf,polar-bear --role harpist
--lineup --camera default --camera violins --base a80d80bf` (`app/harpist-base-views.webp`,
`app/species-play-base-views.webp`, `app/species-rest-base-views.webp`,
`app/lineup-base-views.webp`, `app/play-base-views.webp`).
