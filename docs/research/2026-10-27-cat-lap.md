## 1. The question

The oval belly round left four faults: a crease where the bottom of the oval meets the thighs
(a turn of 34° between the cuts of `pnpm trunk-form`), four faces at the right clavicle and the
neck that the shoulder turn of 36° folds, the bow upper arm 2.3 cm in the body in the play and
1.9 cm in the lowering (limit 1.5 cm), and the right forearm 0.4 cm in the upper belly. The
reference `cat-views.png` shows the belly going into the thighs as one curve.
