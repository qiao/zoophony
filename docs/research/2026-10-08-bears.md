## 2. Method

- **Units.** H is the head height of the skeleton. Hv is the visible head from the chin to the
  crown: 0.854 H for each bear sculpt (the crown 0.5 H above the head center, the chin 0.354 H
  below it). A value of a reference is in Hv.
- **The sheet.** The polar bear values come from crops of `polar-bear-views.png` with a grid of
  10 px (the method of the proportions report, section 2.2). The bear and the panda values come
  from `orchestra-latest.png` and `orchestra-side.png`, with an error of about 15%.
- **Our bodies.** A script builds each body with `buildBiped()` and groups the corners by their
  main bone. For a sculpt, the chin is 0.354 H below the head center, because the thick neck has
  no clear chin line.
- **Colors.** A sample is the mean of a box of 9 × 9 px. The views page has its studio light, and
  the default view has the stage light (design 4.5, 7.1).
- **Images.** Each side-by-side image shows the references, the Blender preview of the sculpt in
  its bind pose, the views page (`/views.html?page=bears`), and the default view after 6 s of the
  Boulanger demo at 1440 × 900.

```text
 reference crops --> measures in Hv --> SPECIES[...].proportions (skeleton)
                                     \-> tools/blender/<species>.py (forms in H)
                                                  | pnpm sculpt <species>
                                                  v
                                   src/models/sculpts/<species>.ts
                                                  |
                     Blender preview <------------+------------> views page, default view
```

## 4. What does not match, and why

### 4.1 The polar bear

- **The keys are low.** The sheet has the keys 0.38 m above a bench of 0.43 m. Our keys are 0.25 m
  above the bench of 0.45 m (design 4.6). Thus the forearms go down to the keys, and the bear looks
  more bent. The piano and its bench are outside this stage.
- **The front view of the views page.** The lid of the piano opens toward the camera and hides the
  head. The sheet has its lid open to the other side in the front view, and to the same side as
  ours in the side view. The Blender preview shows the front of the head.
- **Narrower knees and a shorter haunch.** The seat-arc test keeps the body on a chair within
  0.95 m. The sheet knees (1.85 Hv apart) would make it 1.00 m wide.
- **A narrower belly than the sheet at the forearms.** A belly of 2.0 H put the forearms of the
  rest pose 0.06 H to 0.22 H into the trunk. The round seat of the back keeps the pear of the back
  view.

### 4.2 The bear

The scenes show the bear with a baton in each paw. The conductor pose (design 5.4) has one baton
and the left hand for the cues, so this stage keeps the pose.

### 4.3 The panda

The report of the proportions found the heights of the panda within 10% of ours, so it keeps the
default skeleton. The crown is 2.66 Hv above the seat against 2.4 Hv: 11%, inside the error of
15% of the scenes. A lower head center moves the mouthpiece of the trombone, and the holds of the
trombone pass now.

## 6. Notes for the later stages

- **Sculpt and layout.** `chairForward()` reads the mesh, so a sculpt with a deeper back moves
  the body forward on its chair, and the rows in front of the risers grow. Check
  `layout-scene.test.ts` after each change of the back of a sculpt (`back.ts` in the scratch
  tools measured the backmost point at the chair back).
- **Rest pose.** The arms of the rest pose read the trunk mesh. A wide belly needs room for the
  forearms between the belly and the thighs: run `pose/rest-clearance.test.ts` and
  `pose/hands.test.ts` after each change of the torso.
- **Neck band.** The neck band of a sculpt must lie in a fold, so that its light reaches the floor
  of 0.6 (`models/shading.test.ts`). A thick open neck gave 0.73 to 0.78.
- **Budget.** The cuts of the color borders of the panda doubled the triangles of the trunk (500
  after the decimate, 1,240 after the cuts).
- **The bench stance.** A species of the piano section gets the bench legs from
  `sculptStances()`. Another seat of its own needs a new stance there.
- **The views page.** `/views.html?page=bears` shows the bears. A page of other species can use
  the same `VIEW_PAGES` table.
