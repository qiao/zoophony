## 1. The question

The user said "the cat's arm has lumps", with an image of the viola cat in the play from its left
front. The target is an arm with an even, round form from the shoulder to
the paw, as on `cat-views.png`. The measure: `probes/arm-rings.ts` cuts the left arm at a right
angle to the upper arm and to the forearm, at steps of 5% of each bone, and at seven planes
through the elbow joint (`e0.00` to `e1.00`). Each cut gives the area radius
`r = sqrt(area / pi)` of the closed loop around the bone line. Near the bent elbow, some cuts
give no closed loop (a dash or a value under 2 cm).
