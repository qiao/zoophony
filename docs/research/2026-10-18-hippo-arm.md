# The twisted bow arm of the hippo

Date 2026-10-18. Round `hippo-arm`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-18-hippo-arm.md`](../decisions/2026-10-18-hippo-arm.md). The probes and their outputs are in
[`2026-10-18-hippo-arm/`](2026-10-18-hippo-arm/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user said: "the hippo's arm is twisted and deformed while playing". The image shows the right
arm (the bow arm) from the right side. The round finds the cause of the twist, and removes it with
no change to the other players. The measures are the jumps of the roll of the bow hand, the twist
of the paw on the forearm, the folds, and the depths of the right arm.

## 2. The measures

The cause has two parts:

- **The paw jumps.** `rollToward()` of `pose/bowed.ts` rolls the hand around the stick so that its
  back points to the elbow, within -120° to 100°. At the far side of the stick (180°), the roll
  changes from one end of the range to the other end. The hippo elbow lies 25° to 35° off the
  backward line of the stick. On the E and A strings, it lies on the far side (74% to 87% of their
  samples in `brahms-2`), and on the D and G strings never. So a change of the string turned the
  paw by about 200° in 0.25 s (`paw-twist.ts --detail --state`).
- **The shoulder folds.** The right side folds about 12 faces in each sample, also in the rest
  pose. The hippo rig has no corrective shape. The upper arm turns 96° around its axis in the play
  (the dog cellist: 65°) (`arm-twist.ts`, `fold-probe-main.txt`).

## 3. The result

The hold of the hippo puts the far side at 30°, in the middle of that empty band. The jumps fall
to 20, as the 25 of the dog. In the frames of the solo shot of `brahms-2` at 101.5 s to 102 s, the
paw of `main` curls and pinches at its end, and the new paw stays straight on the forearm.

The cost: on the E and A strings, the roll stays at its low end, and the paw goes more than
1.5 cm into the belly in 4.6% of the play samples (`pnpm pose-depth bassist --base main`:
deepest 4.4 cm against 0.5 cm). The folds of the shoulder stay. They need the corrective shapes
of the open item in `TODO.md`.
