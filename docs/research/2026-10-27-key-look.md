# Look of the front key light

Date 2026-10-27. Round `sixth-key-look`, on `main` at `8ecacf43`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-27-key-look.md`](../decisions/2026-10-27-key-look.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-27-key-look/`](2026-10-27-key-look/), and `commands.txt` gives each command. Each
command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The round `sixth-key-front` moved the key light to the front left (user, 2026-10-27: "make the
key light go from front to back"). It brought three faults (`TODO.md`, step 8): the light fur
clipped in close shots, and the lit floor and the back of the conductor were darker than with the
key light from behind. The goals: in each close shot and wide view, at most the clip of the key
light from behind, and at least its floor (118 and 131) and conductor (43). The direction of the
key light, its height, and the one beam stay.

`clip.py` gives the part of the light fur pixels (saturation below 0.4, luma above 120) with a
channel at 255. `pnpm look-lab` gives the floor and the conductor in the camera `orchestra`.

## 2. The causes

`pnpm look-variants` with one light off (`variants/ablate.md`):

| Variant | Lit floor L/R | Back of the conductor |
|---|---|---|
| `main` | 110/106 | 36 |
| no key light | 47/46 | 8 |
| no rim light | 100/96 | 32 |
| no direct gloss of the stage | 107/103 | 35 |
| no hot spot | 87/85 | 22 |
| no side lights and back-row lights | 107/103 | 29 |

- **Floor.** The key light gives most of the light of the floor. Its gloss now falls to the
  back, away from the camera, so the gloss gives the floor only 3 levels. Behind the stage, the
  key light gave the floor 9 lux at 66° and its gloss toward the camera. In front, it gives
  6.5 lux at 60°, and the rim light gives no gloss.
- **Conductor.** The key light gives almost all the light of the back of the conductor. With the
  key light behind, the side lights of 2.7 lux and their hot spot lit it. They now give 0.5 lux.
- **Close shots.** `rig.txt` gives the camera of each close shot of Boulanger: 2° to 49° from
  the key light. Of the 120 close shots of three demos, 97 stand within 60° of the key light
  (`gap.txt`). The camera thus sees the lit side of the subject. The soft key turns toward the
  stage key light and adds light to that side, and the close shot adds 0.15 stop.

The shade share (`shadeShare()`) is the part of the subject that the camera sees in the shade of
the key light, as the phase of a lit sphere: (1 - cos α) / 2, for the angle α at the subject
between the camera and the key light. It is 0.06 to 0.26 in the five shots (`rig.txt`). The soft
key, its follow-spot, and the extra exposure take it.

The boost of the hot spot also adds clip in the close shots. The rabbit of seat 0 gets no more
key light, but the captured environment sees the brighter floor and lights each player more. The
key light of 5.8 lux removes this clip, and it brings the white fur of the wide views nearer to
the reference.

## 5. The frame time and the frozen shots

`pnpm frame-compare main 9c8196cb --runs 2` measures `perf-60.mid` (`frame/table.md`,
`item*-run*.txt`). `9c8196cb` has the code of the round. `pnpm frame` measures the tree one more
time (`frame/frame.txt`):

| Item | Main thread p50/p95 | GPU p50/p95 | Main thread p50 of each run |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main` | 3.85/4.92 ms | 7.75/7.91 ms | 3.93, 3.77 ms |
| round, `frame-compare` | 4.12/5.21 ms | 7.78/7.96 ms | 4.30, 3.93 ms |
| round, `pnpm frame` | 3.76/4.81 ms | 7.76/7.95 ms | 3.76 ms |

The GPU time stays. The first run of the round started after a load average of 30 on the
machine, and `pnpm frame` gives 3.76 ms, so the main thread also stays. The code adds one shade
share in each frame and changes no shader.

`pnpm frozen --base main` (`frozen.txt`, `images/frozen.webp`): each of the four shots changes,
because the key light, the hot spot, and the close-shot light change. The part of the pixels
above 16 levels: `play` 7.4%, `conductor` 5.4%, `perf-wide` 7.0%, and `perf-section` 12.4%, the
close shot of the pianist, where the shade share removes most of the soft key.
