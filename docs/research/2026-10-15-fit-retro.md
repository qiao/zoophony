# The tools of the retro of the fix waves (2026-10-15)

The fix waves of 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15 lost about 2 hours in hand probes and waits. The agents
of the fox rig round and of the flute rest round named the causes, and the manager chose ten items
against them, with no hill climb in `pnpm hold-fit`. This report gives the tools, their times, and
the break commands of their new functions. The logs and the probes are in
[`2026-10-15-fit-retro/`](2026-10-15-fit-retro/).

| 30 min | Each variant of `--check` ran both fox roles on 14 demos, and the arm turn of a fold took two scratch probes | `--roles`, `--step`, `--demo`, and `--faces` of `--check`, and `pnpm fold-probe --turns` |

| 5 min | A worktree of `main` to find the new failures of the paused tests | `pnpm test:paused --base` |

## The format of a probe JSON

Since `94c6ace9`, each side of a `--base` compare runs its own tool. A branch that changes the JSON
of a probe thus gave false differences. Each probe JSON now holds its format version, and the
compare stops on two versions:

```text
 tool --base <ref>
   |-- probe tree of <ref> (tools of <ref>) --> base.json  { version: 1, ... }
   |-- working tree (tools of the branch) ----> tree.json  { version: 2, ... }
   v
 formatVersion(base) == formatVersion(tree) ?  no --> stop: "The JSON of tools/<tool> on <ref>
   | yes                                               has the format version 1, and the
   v                                                   working tree has 2. ..."
 compare
```

A text with no field has the version 1, the format before the field, so a compare with `main` of
a tool with no change of its format goes on. `pnpm hold-fit flutist --base main` stops after
27.5 s with this message (`hold-fit-flutist-base-main.txt`), and `--base HEAD` gives the face
tables of both trees in 27.6 s (`hold-fit-flutist-base-head.txt`). The form tables of
`pnpm rig-mesh --check` run the `tools/rig-form.ts` of the working tree on the source of `main`,
because the script imports only the code of the app.

## The new measures

```typescript
/** The folded faces of one posed body by the side of their main studio bone. */
export interface SideSplit {
  readonly L: number; // main bone ends with _L
  readonly R: number; // main bone ends with _R
  readonly other: number; // chest, neck, and the other bones of the middle
}

export interface CutTrace {
  // ...
  /** The index in `candidates` of the candidate that found the camera, or null. */
  chosen: number | null;
}

/** The reach of an arm to the rest place of a paw that leaves its grip (restFreeL). */
export interface FreeRestReach {
  readonly side: 'L' | 'R';
  readonly kind: 'lap' | 'hang';
  readonly arm: number; // shoulder joint to the middle of the palm, along the joints
  readonly palm: Vec3 | null; // the middle of the palm at its lap place
  readonly distance: number | null; // shoulder joint to that palm, chest in the bind pose
}
```

The rest of the tan fox flutist folds in 10 of 10 samples, with 12 faces in the worst sample: 3 on
the left, 6 on the right, and 3 in the middle (`fold-probe-flutist-base-head.txt`). One variant
of `pnpm hold-fit flutist` gives 0.0 cm of flute in the body, a gap of 1.0 cm from the free left
paw to the body in the rest, and a reach share of 0.995 for its rest place
(`hold-fit-flutist-after.txt`). `pnpm hold-reach fox-tan --instrument flute` gave the left rest
grip as a miss of 1.3 cm before. It now gives the rest place at 0.543 m from the shoulder joint,
0.995 of the arm of 0.545 m.

The turn probe of face 389 of the red fox violinist at a step of 1 s finds the fold in 89 of 4902
samples. The mean turn of the right upper arm in these samples is 109.0° from its bind pose, with
the arm out 0.86 and down 0.50. Each sample within 30° of that turn folds, 1.4% of the samples
from 30° to 40° fold, and no sample farther away folds (`fold-probe-turns-389.txt`).
