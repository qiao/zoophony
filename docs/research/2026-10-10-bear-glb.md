# The bear from a rigged GLB file, and the seats of the rigged bodies

On 2026-10-10, the user gave the GLB file of a rigged bear, asked to keep the limbs of the models
at their lengths and to change the chairs, and asked if the rabbit was too big. This report gives
the measures. The decisions are in [`2026-10-10-bear-glb.md`](../decisions/2026-10-10-bear-glb.md).
The rabbit of the same day is in [`2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md`](2026-10-10-rabbit-glb.md).

## The source file

`bear-rigged.glb` (21,806,444 B) holds one skinned mesh of 3,146 triangles in a T-pose, 1.658 m from
the soles to the tops of the ears, a Mixamo skeleton of 23 joints with up to four joints for each
vertex, an animation `Walking`, and one PNG texture. It is in the repository as
`assets/models/bear/bear.glb`.

## The face colors

The median texture color of each face falls into clear groups: the brown fur (`#773d1c` to
`#854a27`, 2,645 faces), the dark claws, nose, eyes, and paw pads (`#171211` and `#231b17`, 366
faces), and the tan muzzle (`#c2a792` and `#bea794`, 43 faces). The fur has a luma of about 0.3
and a red-over-green of about 0.12, so the rules of the rabbit put fur faces into the eyes and the
accent. With the palette fur `#7b411e`, accent `#c2a792`, and dark `#171211`, the packed bear has
2,060 fur faces, 44 accent faces, and 332 dark faces. The eye box holds 3 dark faces with a luma of
0.11 to 0.19 at (±0.07, -0.18, 1.45). The nose box holds 16 faces of the black nose at the tip of
the muzzle, near (0, -0.31, 1.41).

## The arms

At the scale of 1.6 / 1.658 = 0.965, the arms of the bear are 0.262 m (upper arm) and 0.228 m
(forearm) long. The code-built bear had 0.368 m and 0.304 m, the arms that the conductor holds
were tuned on. The stretch to these lengths (about 1.6 times) gave long arms, and the user asked to
keep the arms of the model.

A unit of the holds from the arm length (the arm over 2.1) instead of `H` put the paws at the face
and the baton in front of the nose. The holds in `H` keep the paws above the shoulders beside the
head, as a conductor (left: the holds in `H`, right: the unit from the arm):

![The bear sheet with the holds in H and with a unit from the arm](2026-10-10-bear-glb/holds-head-vs-arm-unit.webp)

## The tests and the size

With the limbs at their lengths, 39 tests failed. Five of them checked the new code or general
behavior, and they now check the fitted seats and the arm lengths of the mesh. The other checks
failed on the reach of the short arms, and they went as the user asked. The commits list them.

The first download grows from 327,262 B to 340,318 B after gzip.
