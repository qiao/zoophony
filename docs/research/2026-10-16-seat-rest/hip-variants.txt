The variants of a reclined trunk of the seated red fox (user, 2026-10-16 13:11: "the angle
between the torso and the upper leg is too unnatural"). trunk-recline.patch of this folder holds
the code of the try: the rule studio.trunk of the rig config (pnpm rig-mesh), which turns the
trunk bones of the seated bind back by their shares of an angle around a point `drop` units below
the hip joints, after the sit of the upright bind, and lifts the vertices of the haunch that go
below the seat top of the upright body onto it. The export writes the turns of the trunk into the
standing pose (StudioStanding.trunkTurns), and the app turns the trunk upright for a standing
soloist. The branch does not keep the code.

Measures of main and the branch with no recline: hip-main.txt and hip-branch.txt (hip-probe.ts).
The joint angle of the fox is 88.0 degrees in the seated bind and 87.3 degrees in the rest and the
play poses, on main and on the branch. The stops of the manager: more faces that turn over in the
bind than on main (3: chest 2, hips 1), the standing body more than 1 mm from the source, the trunk
more than 1 cm into the top rail of the chair back.

Commands: pnpm rig-mesh fox --check --roles fox-violinist --step 1 --set 'studio.trunk.turn=<t>'
(rig-check-fox-trunk.log, the shares of the second try), and for the other shares
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/blender/rig_mesh.py -- <config>
<out.glb> --obj <obj>, then the standing error of tools/rig-mesh-check.ts for the GLB and the OBJ.
