`dump.ts`, `faces.py`, `flap.ts`, and `with-glb.ts` are in

## 1. The question

The user said: "the bear has deformed meshes under the arm", then "note that many animals are
having this issue now". The audit found flat shards at both shoulders of the dog cellist from the
back. The goal: a clean arm root and inner arm at each moment of `ROLE_VIEWS.cellist`, from six
views, at the heights 20° and 40°, with no worse folds and at most 3.5 cm of upper arm in the body.

## 2. The cause and the fixes

Each arm of the source joins the trunk down to 0.80 (left) and 0.81 (right) of the upper arm
(`node tools/arm-gap.ts`), and the raised cello arms stretch the seam faces at the back of each
shoulder into flat shards. The last round found the fix (d4: a round patch on each inner arm in a
copy, and the cut 0.64), but stopped on three problems. `variants.txt` gives each variant with its
log.

- **Dark corner.** `faces.py` at rest from the back shows the inner back faces of the right arm
  through a narrow pocket between the arm and the trunk, below the cut top. A patch that pushes to
  the round form from near its top (`fade` 0.05 to 0.35, `scale` 1.0) fills the pocket.
- **Depth.** A later fade or a smaller patch radius keeps 5.5 cm, and the cut with no patch gives
  4.5 cm, so the patch form does not set the depth. `probes/inside.py` on a `dump.ts` of
  boulanger-matin at 248.38 s puts the deepest points at the front of the right upper arm, across
  the chest. On the base, these vertices had the chest weight 0.21, and the seam faces stood in
  front of them. After the cut, bone heat gives the free arm only arm weights. The new rule
  `weights.armRoot` gives the inner root a chest share of 0.25 (lift 4.8 to 2.7 cm). A share of 0.4
  folded 99.5% of the play.
- **Folds.** Face 342 at the top of the right shoulder folds in the lift. After the cut, the shape
  of the raised left arm of the play moves no vertex, so its slot took a shape of faces 342 and 172
  (`pnpm raise-fit cellist --faces 342,172 --side R --sets lift,play`): 3.6% / 12.4% folds. The
  zoomed strip from the right at 247 s then showed a pointed sheet at the top of the right
  shoulder, which d4 does not have (`shape-spike-247.webp`): the shape moves six vertices up to
  8 cm. The round removes the shape (`43e56d9f`).
