## 2. The longer shins

The studio makes each part from a measure of the sheet, in units from the soles to the ear tops.
The trunk and the head read the widths of the masks of the sheet at their heights
(`trunk_table()` and `head_table()` of `lowpoly-studio/studio/field.py`). A new field
`field.lift` moves the row profiles of the masks up by the lift (`lifted_views()`), so the parts
above the knees can stand higher than on the sheet and keep the widths of the sheet.
`shin_warp.py` moves each height of the config above the knees up by the lift: the trunk, the
head, the arms, the hips, the knees, the rig, the weight rules, the paint, the light center, and
the facet warps. The ankles, the feet, and the toes stay.
