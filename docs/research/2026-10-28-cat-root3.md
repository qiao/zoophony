## 1. The question

The user said "the cat's arm has lumps" on 2026-10-27 (`close-views play --at cat --time 30
--azimuth -72 --elevation 28 --zoom 3.5` on `boulanger-matin`), and on 2026-10-28: "always take

are introduced". Open after `sixth-cat-root2`: a soft fold at the front of the left root in the
play and a notch at rest, the crease and the 14 thin faces of the right root, and the points at
the ends of each armpit line from az ±150.

## 3. The causes

- **Left root in the play.** The front of the root keeps the trunk weights, and the shape of the
  play takes it half of the way with the arm. The skin between the chest and the arm stays a
  valley: edge 908 to 1364 has 74° in the bind and 76° in the play (`crease-L-main.txt`).
- **Left root at rest.** The copy has a fin at the front end of the left line: the socket and the
  first shoulder ring lie on the plane of the seam, and the trunk in front of them curves in by
  0.6 units-cm. Its edges have 80° to 107° (`sharp-main.txt`), and it shows as a tooth and a dark
  notch (`fin-copy.webp`, `fin-copy-labels.webp`).
- **Right root.** The trunk weights keep the root of the bow arm with the chest, and no shape
  acts on it. Faces 772, 775, 689, 690, and 822 fold in each play sample (`fold.txt`).
- **Line ends.** The band near each end keeps edges of 133° to 178° (`sharp-main.txt`).
