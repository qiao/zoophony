## 1. The wrist measure

`wrist.ts <role> [<changes>|-] [<demo>...]` poses the studio skin of each seated player of a bowed
role in the sample sets of its sweep. For each arm, it gives the angle between the axis of the
forearm (elbow to wrist) and the axis of the paw (wrist to finger joint), the normal of the palm in
the axes of the hips (in, up, forward), the elbow angle, and the elbow place from the shoulder joint
and from the side of the trunk. The trunk is each vertex of the hips, the spine, and the chest, moved
by its main bone. The play splits by the place of the bow on the hair (`bowStateAt()`): the frog and
the tip are the outer 15% of the played hair. `SUMMARY=1` prints one line for each species.

## 2. The rest

The grip of `main` lay 0.4 H out of the right knee. The arm factor 1.35 does not reach it, so the
arm was straight, and the paw kept the turn of the upright bow: the wrist bent 66°. The dog has no
`bowGrip`, so the stick crosses the paw square, and an upright bow needs a level forearm for a
straight wrist. A grip higher over the knee bends the elbow, and a tip that leans out and forward fits
the forearm that slopes down (`scan-rest-right.txt`). The rest depths and folds are in
`hold-fit-cellist-base-head.txt`, `hold-fit-rest-left.txt`, and `hold-fit-rest-tip.txt`.
