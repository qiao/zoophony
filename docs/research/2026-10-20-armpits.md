## 1. The question

The user said: "pig and rabbit's armpit are deformed. also pig's right hand is clipping with the
instrument". The goals: no spikes or folds at the armpits of the rabbit violinist and the pig
hornist in the play, the lift, and the rest, and no point of the right paw or forearm of the pig
through the metal of the bell. The whole arm gate decides (`pnpm arm-check`), with crop pairs.

## 2. The causes

```text
 hanging arm of the source           raised arm in the app
   trunk | arm                         trunk      arm
         |\                                 \____/  <- corner of the trunk with 0.43 of
         | \  seam faces                    /         the arm weight goes halfway out:
         |  \                              /          long faces make a ridge (spike)
```

- **Rabbit.** The inner side of each hanging upper arm of the source joins the side of the trunk
  down to the middle of the upper arm. A trunk corner there (vertex 74 of the module) had 0.43 of
  the upper arm weight. The raised bow arm dragged it halfway out, and the long faces from it to
  the belly (faces 95, 286, 289, and 290) made a ridge. These faces turn only 17° to 30° from their
  skin normal, so `pnpm fold-probe` does not count them, and `pnpm arm-shape` sees no spike.
- **Pig armpit.** The faces at the back of the right armpit (437, 438, 479, 222, and others) have
  corners with mixed arm and trunk weights. They turn 55° to 78° in each play sample, short of a
  fold at 90°, and show as a dark crumpled band.
- **Pig paw.** The pose turns the paw along the line from the shoulder to the palm grip
  (`handToward()` of `pose/winds.ts`). That line lies 86° from the axis of the bell, and the
  shoulder lies behind the closed side of the bell (110° from the opening). With the palm 6 cm
  inside the opening, the wrist went through the bell wall in each sample.

`armpit-probe.ts` counts the faces of each armpit with a fold angle over 45°. `bell-probe.ts`
walks each skin edge of the paw and the forearm at 5 mm steps, and finds each crossing of the wall
of the hollow of the bell behind its opening, with the 12 facets of the bell (`FACET=1`).
`bell-check.ts` shows that the bell vertices of the mesh lie on the profile of the probe.
