# The bow arm of the string players: the code round

Research of 2026-10-17, round `bow-arm`, base commit `9bdd6cee`. The round makes the bow arm of
the violin, the viola, the cello, and the contrabass follow the reference
[`docs/reference/string-technique.md`](../reference/string-technique.md): the motion spec B1 to B8
of section 5.3 of the report [`2026-10-17-string-hands.md`](2026-10-17-string-hands.md). The
decisions are in [`2026-10-17-bow-arm.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-bow-arm.md). The probes and
their output are in [`2026-10-17-bow-arm/`](2026-10-17-bow-arm/).

## 1. The motion

```text
 Score (notes of one player, beats of the score)
    | preparePlayer() -> noteLayerOf()                         (pose/plan.ts)
    v
 buildStrokes() --- planHand()                                (pose/fingering.ts)
    |           --- slurSizes(): the slurs of one beat        (pose/strokes.ts)
    |           --- crossStrings(): the mean string of a fast crossing run
    |           --- balanceShift(): a fast bow moves to the balance point
    |           --- followLoudness(): the loudness at the start of each stroke
    v
 Stroke { slur: BowSlur, crossMean, loudness, loudnessFrom, ... }
    | bowStateAt(): the bow in its slur, the crossing with its lead
    v
 BowState { bow, string, crossMean, loudness, ... }
    | poseBowed()                                             (pose/bowed.ts)
    v
 soundingPointAt(): the contact moves along the strings with the loudness
 bowAt(): the string plane (stringTurn) and the tilt of the stick (bowTilt)
 crossPole(): the right elbow keeps the mean string of a fast crossing run
```

```typescript
/** The motion of one bow: a note, or the notes of a slur in one bow direction. */
export interface BowSlur {
  /** The note start of the first note, in song seconds. */
  readonly start: number;
  /** The note start of the last note: one speed until it, then the bow slows to a stop. */
  readonly lastStart: number;
  /** The end of the sound of the last note, in song seconds. */
  readonly end: number;
  /** The place of the strings on the hair at the start and at the end, in meters from the frog. */
  readonly from: number;
  readonly to: number;
  /** The number of notes in the bow: 1 for a separate bow. */
  readonly notes: number;
}

/** The new fields of a stroke and of the bow state (pose/strokes.ts). */
interface StrokeBowFields {
  /** The bow of the stroke: its own bow, or the bow of its slur. */
  readonly slur: BowSlur;
  /** The string that the right elbow follows: the mean string of a fast crossing run. */
  readonly crossMean: number;
  /** The loudness of the stroke, from 0 to 1, and the loudness that the sounding point follows at its start. */
  readonly loudness: number;
  readonly loudnessFrom: number;
}

/** The new fields of a bowed hold (pose/bowed-rig.ts). */
interface BowedHoldBowFields {
  /** The angle between two neighbor string planes. */
  readonly stringTurn: number;
  /** The turn of the bow around its hair, so that the stick leans toward the fingerboard. */
  readonly bowTilt: number;
  /** The sounding point in piano and in forte, as a share of the string length from the bridge. */
  readonly soundingPoint: readonly [piano: number, forte: number];
}
```

A crossing, a slur, and a fast bow on a time line:

```text
 note A (string 2)          | note B (string 1)          | note C (string 1)
 ---------------------------+----------------------------+---------------------->
              crossing: at most 0.08 s and 0.3 of A
              [=========]  ends 0.01 s before B
 bow of a slur of A, B, C: one direction, one speed until C, then it slows to a stop
 bow place  ----------------------------------------------\_____ stop at the end of C
```

## 2. The bow plan

`bow-plan.ts` runs `buildStrokes()` and `bowStateAt()` of the tree on the part of the first player
of each bowed seat of the 14 demos, with the loudness of the score and a bow range of 0.03 m to
0.6 m. The command takes 4 s in each tree. `bow-plan-main.txt` and `bow-plan-branch.txt` hold the
output. A crossing is the time from the first move of the string of the bow to its arrival on the
string of the next note, sampled at each 1 ms. The sounding point is in mm from the bridge on a real
string of full size (328 mm, 370 mm, 690 mm, and 1,060 mm), at the middle of each note.

| Instrument | Turn | Tilt | Notes | Bows for each second of sound | Notes in a slur | Mean crossing, ms | Lead before the note, ms | Fast crossings in a run on two strings | Sounding point on `main`, mm | Sounding point, mm: loudness < 0.3 / 0.3 to 0.6 / > 0.6 |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Violin | 9° → 10° | 0° → 30° | 29038 | 4.39 → 3.37 | 37.9% | 88.3 → 62.5 | 0.0 → 9.8 | 48.6% of 3494 | 20.5 | 36.1 / 29.4 / 25.2 |
| Viola | 9° → 11° | 0° → 30° | 12114 | 4.05 → 3.40 | 27.0% | 90.2 → 65.3 | 0.0 → 9.8 | 41.1% of 1320 | 23.1 | 40.7 / 33.7 / 28.1 |
| Cello | 9° → 17° | 0° → 20° | 8851 | 3.31 → 2.85 | 23.0% | 94.7 → 70.9 | 0.0 → 9.9 | 44.9% of 1106 | 43.1 | 70.2 / 61.1 / 55.3 |
| Contrabass | 7° → 25° | 0° → 10° | 5715 | 3.53 → 3.10 | 20.9% | 93.4 → 69.9 | 0.0 → 9.9 | 43.5% of 843 | 66.3 | 107.6 / 92.8 / 85.4 |

The turn and the tilt are those of the first species of each section: the rabbit, the cat, the dog,
and the hippo. The red fox turns 9° and leans the stick 15° (section 3.1). The bow plan does not
depend on the species.

- **Bows.** On `main`, each normal note is a bow. On the branch, 37.9% of the violin notes and 21%
  to 27% of the other notes are in a slur, so the bow changes 3.37 times each second of sound on
  the violin in place of 4.39 times.
- **Crossings.** The crossing takes 62 ms to 71 ms on the mean (88 ms to 95 ms on `main`), and it
  ends 9.8 ms to 9.9 ms before the note (0 ms on `main`).
- **Fast crossings.** 41% to 49% of the crossings in which the next note starts within 0.25 s are
  in a run across two neighbor strings, where the right elbow keeps the mean string. The first
  rule gave the mean string to each stroke of such a run, also to the strokes that stay on one
  string, and 58% to 66% of the crossings took it. Then the second violins of the default demo
  held the elbow between two strings from the first note, and the right forearm of the red fox
  went 2.9 cm into the body in the lift (1.2 cm on `main`).
- **Sounding point.** On `main`, the bow plays at 1/16 of the string from the bridge at each
  loudness. On the branch, the violin plays at 36.1 mm on the mean in soft notes (loudness below
  0.3) and at 25.2 mm in loud notes (above 0.6). The two violinists of reference 2.4 played at
  42 mm in piano and at 18 mm to 20 mm in forte. The loudness of the demos rarely reaches 0 or 1,
  so the means stay between these values.

## 3. The arm

### 3.1 The string planes, the reach, and the folds

`bow-range.ts` prints the bow range of each species with the turn and the tilt of its hold, and
with other turns and tilts (`bow-range.txt`). The full hair is the hair less the free hair at each
end (`BowedHold.freeHair`), in the steps of 5 mm of the search.

| Species | Instrument | Turn, tilt | Bow range | Full hair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rabbit (chair and stand) | Violin | 10°, 30° (also 17°) | 0.060 m to 0.425 m | Yes, as on `main` |
| Red fox (chair and stand) | Violin | 9°, 15° (also 17°, 30°) | 0.120 m to 0.385 m | Yes, as on `main` |
| Cat (chair and stand) | Viola | 11°, 30° | 0.030 m to 0.475 m | Yes, as on `main` |
| Cat | Viola | 12°, 30° | 0.030 m to 0.470 m | No |
| Cat | Viola | 12°, 10° | 0.030 m to 0.475 m | Yes |
| Dog | Cello | 17°, 20° (up to 25°) | 0.030 m to 0.525 m | Yes, as on `main` |
| Hippo | Contrabass | 25°, 10° | 0.030 m to 0.525 m | Yes, as on `main` |

The hippo at 9° reached the hair from 0.035 m only. The turn of `main` (7°) and each turn from 12°
on keep the full hair.

The reach allows 17° on the rabbit and the red fox, but the skin of their mesh rigs folds. `pnpm
hold-fit <role> --sets play,lift --grid` gives the share of the play samples with a folded face,
at a step of 1 s on the demos of `FIT_DEMOS` (`fold-rabbit-grid.txt`, `fold-fox-grid.txt`,
`fold-fox-tilt.txt`). The first column of each species is `main`.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rabbit, stick 30° | 1.7% | 1.8% | 2.5% | 3.9% | 5.3% | 6.3% | 11.1% |
| Red fox, stick 30° | 5.4% | 7.0% | 8.0% | 10.0% | 12.9% | 16.7% | 21.7% |
| Red fox, stick 15° | 5.4% | 6.1% | | | | | |
| Red fox, no tilt | 5.4% | 5.4% | 6.0% | | | | |

The goal of the round is at most 1 point above `main`. The rabbit takes 10°: at 11°, its bow paw
also went 2.0 cm into the violin at the frog on the E string in the first note of the default demo
(1.7 cm at 10°, 1.4 cm on `main`, `depth-rabbit-turns.txt`). The red fox keeps 9° and leans the
stick 15°. The tilt also adds folds to the red fox. The fingers turn the stick by the tilt, and the
hand keeps its turn (`heldBowAt()`, `tilt-hand.ts`), but the grip of the tilted bow moves the
wrist. With the hand turned with the stick, as in the first commit of the tilt, the bow of the
rabbit went 1.7 cm into the violin in the lowering of `brahms-2` (0.0 cm on `main`,
`depth-violinist-split.txt`), and 0.4 cm with the turn in the fingers (`depth-rabbit-turns.txt`).

### 3.2 The arm levels (B2)

`arm-levels.ts` places the bow in the middle of the bow range on each string, in the playing
frames, and solves the right arm with the elbow of the hold. It prints the height of the right
elbow and of the right wrist above the right shoulder joint, in cm, with the turn and the tilt of
`main` and of the branch (`arm-levels.txt`).

| Species | Hold | Elbow / wrist cm, string 0 (lowest) | String 1 | String 2 | String 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rabbit violin | main | -12.6 / -0.6 | -13.2 / -2.6 | -13.6 / -4.3 | -13.9 / -5.8 |
| rabbit violin | branch | -12.4 / -0.4 | -13.1 / -2.7 | -13.5 / -4.6 | -13.9 / -6.3 |
| fox-red violin | main | -10.5 / -2.7 | -10.6 / -4.5 | -10.5 / -6.3 | -10.5 / -7.9 |
| fox-red violin | branch | -10.5 / -2.7 | -10.5 / -4.6 | -10.5 / -6.4 | -10.5 / -8.0 |
| cat viola | main | 1.8 / -2.1 | 1.8 / -4.4 | 1.8 / -6.4 | 1.6 / -8.2 |
| cat viola | branch | 1.7 / -1.6 | 1.6 / -4.5 | 1.5 / -7.0 | 1.1 / -9.1 |
| dog cello | main | 15.4 / -4.5 | 16.8 / -0.3 | 17.7 / 3.9 | 18.1 / 5.8 |
| dog cello | branch | 13.2 / -7.7 | 16.2 / -2.4 | 18.0 / 5.2 | 18.0 / 6.9 |
| hippo contrabass | main | 12.6 / -19.5 | 13.1 / -18.6 | 13.6 / -17.4 | 14.1 / -16.0 |
| hippo contrabass | branch | 11.5 / -21.8 | 12.8 / -19.5 | 14.5 / -15.3 | 15.2 / -10.2 |

The geometry gives the arm levels of reference 2.2. On the violin and the viola, the wrist and the
elbow are higher on the lowest string. On the cello and the contrabass, they are higher on the
highest string, where the bow reaches away from the body. The wider string planes make the range
larger: the elbow of the dog is 4.8 cm higher on the A string than on the C string (2.7 cm on
`main`), and the elbow of the hippo 3.7 cm higher on the G string than on the E string (1.5 cm).
The wrist of the cat moves 7.5 cm from the C string to the A string (6.1 cm on `main`). The rabbit
(10°) and the red fox (9°) stay near the levels of `main`.

### 3.3 The upper arm in a fast crossing (B3)

`elbow-travel.ts` poses the rabbit violinist on the part of the first violins of each demo, in the
strokes of the fast runs across two strings, at each 10 ms, in three passes: with the rule, with
the elbow on the string of the bow (no rule), and with the bow on the mean string (no crossing). It
adds the angle between the local rotations of the right upper arm and of the right forearm in two
samples in a row (`elbow-travel.txt`).

| Pass | Upper arm, °/s | Forearm, °/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| With the rule | 43.4 | 45.5 |
| No rule | 44.3 | 43.8 |
| No crossing | 43.0 | 31.1 |

The runs take 317.5 s of the parts. The crossings add 1.3°/s to the turn of the upper arm with no
rule, and 0.4°/s with the rule, so the rule removes about 70% of it, and the forearm takes it. Most
of the turn of a crossing is in the forearm also with no rule, because the hand follows the bow and
the elbow of the hold does not follow the string.

## 4. The fit of the bowed roles

The goals of the brief: each depth of the sweeps at most 0.5 cm above `main`, the folds of
`pnpm hold-fit` at most 1 point above `main`, and `bowRange` as on `main` or longer. Each probe ran
on the last code commit of the round.

`pnpm pose-depth <role> --base main` over the 14 demos (`pose-depth-violinist-base-main.txt`,
`pose-depth-violist-base-main.txt`). Only the rows that change by at least 0.1 cm:

| Species | Set | Pair | `main` cm | Branch cm | Change |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rabbit | lift | paws in violin | 1.4 | 1.7 | +0.3 |
| Cat | play | paws in body | 0.2 | 0.1 | −0.1 |
| Cat | play | upper arms in body | 0.6 | 1.1 | +0.5 |
| Cat | lower | forearms in body | 0.3 | 0.4 | +0.1 |
| Cat | lower | upper arms in body | 3.1 | 3.1 | 0.0 |

Each row stays within the goal. The upper arm of the cat is at the limit of the goal. The sounding
point causes it: with the contact point of the model, it went 0.5 cm into the body in `brahms-2`
(`depth-cat-split.txt`). The note target of the bow is 0.00 mm for each violinist. For the violas
of `boulanger-matin`, it is 22.6 mm at 33.6 s on the branch, and 25.0 mm at 34.0 s on `main` with
the old measure (`target-violist-main.txt`), so the error is older than the round.

`pnpm hold-fit <role> --base main` on the demos of `FIT_DEMOS` (`hold-fit-<role>-base-main.txt`):

| Role | Play folds, `main` | Play folds, branch | Lift folds, `main` | Lift folds, branch | `bowRange` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `violinist` (rabbit) | 1.7% | 2.5% | 9.7% | 9.7% | 0.060 m to 0.425 m, as on `main` |
| `fox-violinist` | 5.4% | 6.1% | 21.8% | 22.6% | 0.120 m to 0.385 m, as on `main` |
| `violist` (cat) | 19.4% | 18.3% | 58.9% | 59.7% | 0.030 m to 0.475 m, as on `main` |

`pnpm body-probe --base main` (`body-probe.txt`): the 95 bodies and the 23 players stay. 841 of the
1,492 pose sets stay, and each pose set that changes belongs to a violin, a viola, a cello, or a
contrabass player. The paused shot test of `src/shots/prepare.test.ts` passes on both trees
(`shots-paused.txt`).

The cello and the contrabass have no sweep role, so no probe measures their clearance. The dog and
the hippo show no new contact in the close views (`views-species-*.webp`).

## 5. The images

- `bow-place.webp`: the bow of the first player of the first violins of `boulanger-matin` and of
  `bach-bwv1048` from 0 s to 20 s, on `main` (red) and on the branch (blue): the place of the
  strings on the hair as a share of the bow range, and the turn of the bow around the strings from
  the middle of the strings. `bow-plan.ts --series` writes the data, and `plot.py` draws it. In
  `boulanger-matin`, the short notes of the branch move from the frog to the middle of the bow in
  about 2 s, and stay between 0.38 and 0.5 of the bow range (0.12 to 0.23 on `main`). In
  `bach-bwv1048`, the slurs of four notes move the bow in one direction over the whole bow range
  of the passage, and the bow changes direction less often. The turn of the bow uses the hold of
  the rabbit: on the G string, 15° from the middle of the strings (13.5° on `main`).
- `views-*.webp`: one `pnpm close-views batch scratch/views --species fox-red,rabbit,cat,dog,hippo
  --moments play --views front35,side --zoom 1 --zoom 2.5 --role violinist --role violist
  --camera violins --base main`. Each grid puts the row of `main` above the row of the branch.
  `views-violinist.webp` and `views-violist.webp` show the roles at the frog, the tip, a string
  crossing, the rest, the lift, the lowering, and the standing soloist. `views-species-play.webp`
  and `views-species-play-zoom2.5.webp` show each species at the time 0 of the test notes of the
  gallery, and `views-play-violins.webp` the play frame of the camera `violins` at 6 s of the
  default demo. At 6 s, the right paws of the rabbits and the red foxes of the branch are further
  out on the bow, at the middle of the bow, and on `main` they are near the frog.
