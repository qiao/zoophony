## The grip

`stickGrip()` of `tools/bow-grip.ts` measures the gap from the middle of the closed right paw to
the axis of the stick, and the depth of the axis in the paw. `grip-probe.ts` runs it over the
sample sets of the violinist sweep. `grip-eval.ts` gives the largest gap of each set for a list of
grips, which `grip-set.ts` sets in memory, so that a scan needs no edit of
`src/models/species.ts`. `preload.ts` does the same for the tools of the repository, such as
`pnpm hold-fit`.

```typescript
/** The grip of the bow in the right paw of a species (`SpeciesParams.bowGrip`). */
export interface BowGrip {
  /** The place of the stick at the grip, from the middle of the right palm, in head heights. */
  readonly at: Vec3;
  /** The turn of the stick around the normal of the palm, in radians, toward the wrist. */
  readonly turn: number;
}
```

A grip has three free values for the gap: the turn, the offset of the line in the plane of the
palm, and the offset along the palm normal. The place along the stick does not change the gap,
but it moves the wrist, and so the arm.

```text
   hand axes of the player (palm side -x, arm up +y, thumb side -z)

             wrist (+y)
               |
         turn  |   the stick lies along the line nearest to the
          \    |   middles of the turned paw (o), in the plane of
           \   |   the palm (y, z)
     o  o   \  |
        o  o \ |
   -z <-------\+
               \  the place along the stick sets the wrist,
                \ and the arm, but not the gap
```

The rabbit: on `main`, the stick lay 3.7 cm to 5.3 cm from the middle of the paw
(`grip-main.txt`). `stick-line.ts` fitted a line of 50.75° to the middles of `main`, and a second
fit on the tree with that line gave 39.5° (`stick-line.txt`): the new grip turns the paw, and the
middles move. Each turn from 30° to 50° with its best offset keeps the gap within 1 cm
(`grip-eval-rabbit.txt`).

The place along the stick changed the folds of the right upper arm of the rabbit
(`fold-scan-rabbit.txt`, all demos, a step of 1 s): 3.4% of the play samples at the middle of the
line fit, 2.3% at the place of `BOW_GRIP`, and 1.3% 2.3 cm nearer to the tip (0.7% on `main`). The
folds came in a repeated passage of `brahms-2` with the upper arm out, down, and forward
(`fold-where.ts`): the arm kept its direction, and the forearm turned more down, so the twist of
the upper arm changed. The elbow poles of the tip and of pizzicato did not change these folds.
The rabbit grip is the turn of 40° at that place.

The red fox: on `main`, the stick lay 4.0 cm to 5.3 cm from the middle of the paw. No line kept
the paw within 1 cm. `paw-polar.ts` gave the middles in the hand axes at radii of 3.1 cm to 8.7 cm
from the wrist (rabbit 2.7 cm to 3.9 cm), and `paw-rigid.ts` showed the cause: in the bind axes of
the studio bone `hand_R`, the middle of the seated paw moves by up to 3.8 cm (2.1 cm with the new
grip). The skin of the long wrist of the fox moves the middle, and the correctives do not. A grid of
57 grips (`eval-fox-grid.txt`) gave at best 2.1 cm. A turn of 60° folded face 184 of the right arm
in 43% of the play samples (`fold-scan-fox.txt`), so the red fox has the turn of 50°.

`gap-seq.ts` showed the gaps above 2 cm of the seated red fox in each pizzicato passage of
`boulanger-matin`, and at the start of the lowering after it. The plucking paw bends the wrist. The
bow tip of pizzicato of the fox points up and back, (0.3, 0.5, 1), and the seated paw keeps the
stick within 1.2 cm.

## The rest, the lift, and the lowering

The stick through the paw moves the hand about 4 cm on the bow, so each rest grip moves out:

```text
 species   rest grip (out from the knee or the thigh)   other change
 rabbit    0.55 H -> 0.65 H                              right elbow at the frog (1.5, -1.5, -1)
 red fox   0.30 H -> 0.50 H                              bow via 0.2 H out, 0.2 H up, 0.4 H front
```

With the old rest grip, face 372 of the right upper arm of the rabbit turned over in each rest
sample, and the right upper arm of the red fox went 5.0 cm into the body while the arms lowered
(`hf-fox-rest.txt`). The right paw of the rabbit rolls around the stick so that its back points
to the elbow, and with the old elbow of the frog the paw went 2.1 cm into the violin at the first
note after the lift (`hf-rabbit-lift.txt`).

layout of each demo with violins moves (`layout-dump.ts`): the violins up to 7.2 cm and the other

and `pnpm body-probe --base main` gives changed pose sets of the cat, the dog, the hippo, and the
other sections (`body-probe.txt`). The bodies and the holds of the other species stay. The paused
shot test of `src/shots/prepare.test.ts`, which reacts to moves of the footprints, passes on both
trees (`shots-paused.txt`).
