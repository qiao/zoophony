# The left hand of the string players: the code round

Research of 2026-10-17, round `left-hand`, base commit `1c44adcd`. The round makes the left hand
of the violin, the viola, the cello, and the contrabass follow the reference
[`docs/reference/string-technique.md`](../reference/string-technique.md): the motion spec L1 to L5
of section 5.2 of the report [`2026-10-17-string-hands.md`](2026-10-17-string-hands.md). The
decisions are in [`2026-10-17-left-hand.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-left-hand.md). The probes and
their output are in [`2026-10-17-left-hand/`](2026-10-17-left-hand/).

## 1. The motion

```text
 Score (notes of one player)
    | preparePlayer() -> noteLayerOf()                       (pose/plan.ts)
    v
 buildStrokes() --- planHand(), HAND_FRAMES, FINGERING_COSTS (pose/fingering.ts)
    |                vibratoRate(), vibratoWidth()          (pose/strokes.ts)
    v
 Stroke { string, hand: HandStep, vibrato, vibratoHz, ... }
    | bowStateAt() -> leftHandAt(): the shift, the finger, the press, the vibrato
    v
 BowState { fingerboard, reach, stopString, vibrato, press, ... }
    | poseBowed()                                            (pose/bowed.ts)
    v
 stopHandAt(place + vibrato, finger: reach and string) and leftElbow(hold, state)
```

```typescript
/** The plan of the left hand for one stroke (pose/fingering.ts). */
export interface HandStep {
  /** The position: the semitones of the first finger (or the thumb) above the open string. */
  readonly position: number;
  /** The middle of the hand frame on the target `fingerboard`: 0 at the nut, 1 at the end. */
  readonly place: number;
  /** The reach of the finger of the high note from the middle of the frame, -1 to 1. */
  readonly reach: number;
  /** The string under the finger of the high note, from 0 to 3. */
  readonly stopString: number;
  /** The finger of the high note: 0 for an open string or the thumb, 1 to 4. */
  readonly finger: number;
  /** True when the high note is an open string. */
  readonly open: boolean;
  /** The semitones of the high note above its open string. */
  readonly semitones: number;
}
```

The shift of the hand between two positions, and the finger of the next note:

```text
 note before (position A)                           | next note (position B)
 ---------------------------------------------------+------------------------>
 start        half of the note       landing        | note start
   |               |                    |  0.03 s   |
   |  vibrato      |<-- shift: at most 0.35 s, quintic ease -->|
   |  (stops 0.1 s before the shift)    | press of the next finger lands
```

## 2. The moves of the hand

`hand-moves.ts` runs `buildStrokes()` of the tree on the part of the first player of each bowed
seat of the 14 demos, as the probe of the report of `string-hands` does. A move is a change of the
place of the hand of more than 0.1 mm from one stroke to the next, in mm on a real string of full
size. `hand-moves-main.txt` holds the run on `main`, and `hand-moves-branch.txt` the run on the
branch. The vibrato column counts the strokes with a width above 0 (each loudness 0 in the probe).

| Instrument | Strokes | Moves on `main` | Moves on the branch | Prototype of `string-hands` | Mean move, branch | Moves for each second, branch | Strokes with vibrato |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Violin | 29,220 | 65.4% | 1.9% (559) | 1.9% (559) | 44.0 mm | 0.08 | 12.6% |
| Viola | 12,198 | 55.1% | 0.5% (61) | 0.5% (61) | 38.0 mm | 0.02 | 14.2% |
| Cello | 9,014 | 72.8% | 4.6% (419) | 4.6% (419) | 83.2 mm | 0.15 | 20.8% |
| Contrabass | 5,819 | 70.1% | 10.0% (579) | 10.0% (579) | 158.2 mm | 0.35 | 16.3% |

The app plans the same shifts as the prototype: `planHand()` takes the frames, the weights, and the
order of the search of `planner.ts`, so each part gives the same moves. The string changes of the
bow are 21.6% (violin), 21.3% (viola), 38.2% (cello), and 43.1% (contrabass) of the strokes, as in
the prototype.

The plan costs time when the app prepares a score (`prepare-time.ts`, `prepare-time.txt`): the best
of three runs of `preparePoses()` on the development Mac.

| Demo | `main` | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| `boulanger-matin` (the default demo) | 23 ms | 83 ms |
| `beethoven-9` | 27 ms | 105 ms |
| `brahms-2` | 40 ms | 191 ms |
| `bach-bwv1048` | 34 ms | 165 ms |

The load average was 12 to 22 during the run, so the times change by about 20% from run to run. The
search with typed arrays for the candidates of the stroke before saved about 25%. A search of the
candidates before in the order of their totals, which stops at the first total above the best
value, was not faster: the sort of each stroke takes the time that the stop saves.

## 3. The images

- `hand-place.webp`: the place of the left hand of the first player of the first violins, the
  violas, and the cellos, in mm from the nut on a real string, for 30 s of `boulanger-matin` and of
  `bach-bwv1048`, on `main` (red) and on the branch (blue). `hand-moves.ts --series` writes the
  data, and `plot.py` draws it. On `main`, the hand of the violas of `bach-bwv1048` jumps between
  20 mm and 93 mm on most notes, and on the branch it stays at 76 mm with one short vibrato. The
  cellos of `boulanger-matin` shift up and down on the A string between 13 s and 20 s, as the
  passage asks.
- `views-*.webp`: one `pnpm close-views batch scratch/views --species
  fox-red,rabbit,cat,dog,hippo --moments play,lift50 --views front35,left --zoom 1 --zoom 2.5
  --role violinist --role violist --camera violins --base main`. Each grid puts the row of `main`
  above the row of the branch. The batch of the brief, with the dog and the hippo added, so that one
  build of each tree also gives the cello and the contrabass. The grids `views-violinist.webp` and
  `views-violist.webp` show the roles at the frog, the tip, a string crossing, the rest, the lift,
  the lowering, and the standing soloist. `views-species-*.webp` show each species at the time 0 of
  the test notes of the gallery and in the middle of the lift, and `views-play-violins.webp` the
  play frame of the camera `violins`.
- `paws-cello-bass.webp`: the rows of the dog and the hippo of `views-species-play.webp`. On
  `main`, the left paw of the dog is near the scroll, at the place of the note near the nut. On the
  branch, it is at the middle of the frame of the first position, lower on the neck, and the left
  elbow is higher for the lower string of the note.

## 4. The fit of the bowed roles

The goals of the brief: each depth of the sweeps at most 0.5 cm above `main`, the folds of
`pnpm hold-fit` at most 1 point above `main`, and `stopRange` and `bowRange` as on `main`.

`pnpm pose-depth <role> --base main` over the 14 demos (`pose-depth-violinist-base-main.txt`,
`pose-depth-violist-base-main.txt`). Only the rows that change by at least 0.1 cm:

| Species | Set | Pair | `main` cm | Branch cm | Change |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Rabbit | play | paws in violin | 2.0 | 2.1 | +0.1 |
| Rabbit | lower | forearms in violin | 2.3 | 2.1 | −0.2 |
| Red fox | play | forearms in violin | 1.9 | 2.2 | +0.3 |
| Red fox | lift | forearms in violin | 1.6 | 1.7 | +0.1 |
| Red fox | lift | upper arms in violin | 2.0 | 2.1 | +0.1 |
| Red fox | lower | paws in violin | 2.3 | 1.3 | −1.0 |
| Red fox | lower | upper arms in violin | 2.1 | 2.3 | +0.2 |
| Cat | play | paws in viola | 2.7 | 2.4 | −0.3 |
| Cat | play | upper arms in body | 0.3 | 0.6 | +0.3 |
| Cat | lift | forearms in viola | 2.6 | 0.0 | −2.6 |
| Cat | lower | forearms in viola | 0.3 | 2.7 | +2.4 |

The last row misses the goal. The violas of `mozart-k191` end with F4, A♯5, and A♯4, and the plan
takes the three notes in one position, 8 semitones above the open string (the fifth position, the
first finger on E♭4 of the G string, `tail-strokes.ts`). On `main`, the last note A♯4 put the hand
near the nut. In this position, the left forearm of the cat goes 2.6 cm to 2.7 cm into the viola
near the wrist, also in the play of this passage, and the play of `main` reaches 2.7 cm in its
high notes too. The lowering after the last note starts from this place. Neither a smaller turn of
the left elbow (`depth-cat-elbow.txt`) nor a later move of the hand to the rest place in the
lowering changed the depth (2.7 cm). The roll and the turn of the finger block do not cause it:
with no roll, 2.6 cm, and with no turn, 2.6 cm (`depth-cat-lower.txt`).

`pnpm hold-fit <role> --base main` on the demos of `FIT_DEMOS` (`hold-fit-<role>-base-main.txt`):

| Role | Play folds, `main` | Play folds, branch | Lift folds, `main` | Lift folds, branch | `stopRange` | `bowRange` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `violinist` (rabbit) | 2.2% | 1.7% | 9.7% | 9.7% | 0.00 to 1.00, as on `main` | 0.060 m to 0.425 m, as on `main` |
| `fox-violinist` | 4.7% | 5.4% | 21.8% | 21.8% | 0.00 to 1.00, as on `main` | 0.120 m to 0.385 m, as on `main` |
| `violist` (cat) | 19.8% | 19.4% | 59.7% | 58.9% | 0.00 to 1.00, as on `main` | 0.030 m to 0.475 m, as on `main` |

`pnpm body-probe --base main` (`body-probe.txt`): the 95 bodies and the 23 players stay. 841 of the
1,492 pose sets stay, and each pose set that changes belongs to a violin, a viola, a cello, or a
contrabass player. The paused shot test of `src/shots/prepare.test.ts` passes on both trees
(`shots-paused.txt`).

The cello and the contrabass have no sweep role, so no probe measures their clearance. The dog and
the hippo show no new contact in the close views (`views-species-*.webp`).
