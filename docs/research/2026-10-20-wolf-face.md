# The head of the wolf at the size of the fox, and a happy face

Date 2026-10-20. Round `wolf-face`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-20-wolf-face.md`](../decisions/2026-10-20-wolf-face.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-20-wolf-face/`](2026-10-20-wolf-face/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree, and the base is `0e7130db`.

## 1. The question

The user said: "make the wolf's head the same size as the fox. reduce its body if needed", and
"the wolf look sad now. make it look happier". The ears of the two species differ, so the size of
the head is the skull: the mean of the skull height (skull top to chin) and the skull width.

## 2. The head size

**Rule.** `proportions.head` of a rig config scales the kept head on each axis around the top of
the blend zone (`proportioned()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`). With no factor, the wolf rig
keeps its bytes. The manager chose to keep the standing skull top at 1.620 m, so the body below
the neck takes one factor on each axis. `size_config.py` maps each place of the config of
`0e7130db` back to the source and then through the new factors.

**Size.** `head-probe.ts` measures the standing body of the app (`head-probe.txt`; the skull top
window takes the head factor, so that it finds the same vertex):

|---|---|---|---|
| Skull height (skull top to chin) | 0.382 m | 0.350 m | 0.420 m |
| Skull width | 0.547 m | 0.412 m | 0.509 m |
| Mean of the two | 0.465 m | 0.381 m | 0.464 m |
| Ear span | 0.509 m | 0.348 m | 0.430 m |
| Ear height | 0.200 m | 0.162 m | 0.201 m |

The mean factor of the skull, 1.2095, gave a mean of 0.455 m, 9.8 mm short, because the chin lies
in the blend zone and grows less than the head. The factor 1.235 gives 0.464 m, 0.3 mm from the
fox. The body factor 0.9422 then keeps the skull top at 1.620 m. The seated wolf is 1.467 m high
(1.470 m at the base, `check-final.log`).

## 3. The face

`face-dump.ts` writes the head faces of the module in the units of the config, and `lmap.py` draws
their Oklab lightness from the front and from the side (`lightness-front.webp`,
`lightness-side.webp`, on the rig of the first try, before the face rules). The three causes of the manager:

- **Brows.** The dark gray stripe of the forehead (lightness 0.41 to 0.55) came down between the
  eyes to the bridge of the nose at y 0.74 u, and the gray over the eyes ended at about 0.775 u.
  The slanted edges of the stripe read as raised inner brows. `colors.patch` with a flat ellipse
  gives the 8 faces of the stripe below 0.775 u the nearest light fur (`check-face1.log`).
- **Eyes.** Each pea eye faces out from the middle of the head box, and the large ruff behind the
  eyes turned them up: 9.7° and 11.0° at the base, 14.3° and 15.6° with the larger head
  (`head-probe.txt`). The fox eyes face -2.7° and 2.0°. `eyeLevel` of `ANIMALS` turns them level.
- **Mouth.** The mesh has no dark mouth line. From the front, the black nose and the shadow under
  it show a wide dark shape with low ends. A smile line in the dark of the paw spots gives the
  mouth a line with the ends up (`colors.smile` with `color`).

The variants (`variants.webp`, columns: no face rule, then the brows and the level eyes with the
wide line and with the thin line; rows: rest from the front, at 60°, and from the side):

| Variant | Faces | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Brows and eyes, no line (`check-face1.log`) | 8 | calmer, but the gray line of the patch dark does not show |
| Wide line: the faces that point up too (`smileD1.json`) | 8, 10 | a dark area under the nose |
| Thin line (`smileD2.json`, chosen) | 8, 5 | a closed smile with the ends 3.0 cm up, no dark block from the side |

## 5. The result

The skull of the wolf has the size of the skull of the red fox (mean 0.464 m against 0.465 m), the
standing skull top stays at 1.620 m, and the body below the neck is 0.9422 times as large. The
face has level brows, level eyes, and a thin closed smile. The right paw reaches its grip with
0.9 cm to spare, against 5.1 cm at the base.
