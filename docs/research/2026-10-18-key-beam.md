# Key light and beam

Date 2026-10-18. Round "do your recommendation" (user, 2026-10-18). Decisions:
[`2026-10-18-key-beam.md`](../decisions/2026-10-18-key-beam.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-18-key-beam/`](2026-10-18-key-beam/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user asked if the stage needs more light sources, if the shadow angle must change, and why the
beam is a rectangle where `orchestra-latest.png` has a fan. The user wants a cinematic feel. The
measures that decide: the direction and the length of the floor shadows, the shape of the beam in
the default view, and the pool of the key light.

## 2. The measures

### 2.1 The professional setup

A stage light from the front stands about 45° high, from two sides about 90° apart (the McCandless
method), with back and top light for separation
([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_McCandless),
[illuminated integration](https://illuminated-integration.com/blog/mccandless-method-stage-lighting/)).
Concert films of orchestras use cross keys, back light from trusses or chandeliers, and haze
([BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/uk-regional/article/Andr-Rieu-And-The-Johann-Strauss-Orchestra-Light-Up-With-Martin-Lighting-20190424),
[Intensity Advisors](https://intensityadvisors.com/projects/homecoming-the-kanas-city-symphony-presents-joyce-didonato/),
[Méliès](https://melies.co/cinematic-techniques/lighting/cross-lighting)). Film light is
motivated: each light has a visible source
([MasterClass](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/what-is-a-key-light-learn-how-key-light-creates-different-cinematography-effects)).
The app already has a back light (the key light) and two cross keys (the side lights). The visible
beam came from no light: its tip stood 1.2 S above the stage center, and the key light 0.7 S
behind it.

### 2.2 The shadows

The shadows of the reference are parallel and point to the front left on both sides: the podium,
the stands of the cellos, and the stands of the violins. The near key light on the stage axis gave
shadows that fanned out from the middle, and on the right side they pointed to the front right.
With the temporary override of
[`2026-10-18-light-color/`](2026-10-18-light-color/) and `KEY_PLACE` added to it, `variants.json`
moves the key light. A light 2.45 S from the stage center, 18° to 22° right of the stage axis and
60° to 66° high, gives parallel shadows to the front left (`compare.webp`, bottom row).

### 2.3 The beam

A wider haze alone (`BEAM_LOOK.column` 0.45 to 0.5) gave a broad haze with no edges. A haze cone up
to the far key light showed only a long, almost parallel part in the default view. A tip on the
line to the key light, low in the frame, gives the fan.

| ----------------------------- | -------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Before (`m0`)                 | 17 / 27        | A column with parallel edges. |
| Tip at the key light (`n2`)   | 12 / 18        | A soft glow with no edges.    |
| Tip 1.2 S, foot 0.25 S (`p1`) | 12 / 19        | A narrow fan.                 |

After the merge, the user said: "the bottom of the beam is still not fanned out like the reference
image". `beam_width.py` measures the width of the beam at rows of the default view, as a part of
the stage width (`beam-width.txt`). The rows below the heads of the players hold the players, so
only the top rows are clean.

## 3. The result

- The key light stands 2.45 S from the stage center, 18° right and 66° high, with a cone of 19°
  and the aim at 0.3. Its shadows are parallel and point to the front left, as in the reference.
  The side edges stay dark, and the back row keeps half of the light of the pool center.
- The beam comes from the key light, with its tip 1.0 S high on the line to the light and a foot
  of 0.3 S. It opens as a fan. Toward the true key light, the fan leaned to the right, so the tip
  takes the key light turned onto the stage axis, and the fan stands upright (variant `up`, hall
  p50 / p90 12 / 19). Its bottom did not spread out, so the tip moved down to 0.55 S and the foot
  out to 0.5 S, with soft edges (`u1`). The shot planner keeps its upright cone, because a leaning cone
  dropped the choir shots of the Ode to Joy (`src/shots/prepare.test.ts`).
- No new light: the setup is already the back light and the cross keys of a concert film. A cool
  fill (`#282c34`) makes the shade of the white fur a little grayer, and it is a variant only.
- The stage median grew from 124 to 130 against 107, because the far key light falls off less
  with the distance than the near one.
