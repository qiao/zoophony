# The left arm of the cat violist in high positions

Research of 2026-10-17, round `cat-hand`, base commit `9bdd6cee`, rebased onto `215674bf` (the
round `bow-arm`) for the fit against `main` (section 3). The round `left-hand` left the
left forearm of the cat 2.7 cm in the viola near the wrist in the fifth position at the end of
`mozart-k191`, also while the arms lowered after the last note
([`2026-10-17-left-hand.md`](2026-10-17-left-hand.md), section 4). The decisions are in
[`2026-10-17-cat-hand.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-cat-hand.md). The probes and their output are in
[`2026-10-17-cat-hand/`](2026-10-17-cat-hand/).

## 1. The fault

`arm-probe.ts` gives each point in the axes of the viola: along the target `fingerboard` from the
nut (L = 0.24 m), across the strings (+ to the bass side), and up from the strings. The body of
the viola starts 0.6 L from the nut, between 4.4 cm and 9.9 cm below the strings, and its upper
bout is 10 cm wide at 0.65 L and 16 cm wide at 0.8 L (`SLICES=1`).

```text
             nut                         0.6 L: upper bout        bridge side
  strings    |=================================+-------------------------
  -4.4 cm                                      |  top plate
                               wrist o         |    body of the viola
  -9.9 cm                              \       |  back
                                        \  forearm (into the bout)
                                         o elbow, 1.0 L, 29 cm below
```

On `main`, the wrist of the cat hangs 6.3 cm to 6.7 cm below the strings and 3.6 cm to the side of
the higher strings (`arm-probe-no-tilt.txt`). From the third position, the wrist is near the start
of the body, and the forearm leaves the wrist toward the shoulder, under the viola, so it enters the
upper bout near the wrist:

| Demo, time | Position | Fingers | Wrist | Deepest point | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bach-bwv1048`, 265.5 s | third | 0.49 L | 0.51 L, 6.7 cm below | 0.63 L, 7.3 cm below | 0.9 cm |
| `boulanger-matin`, 59.13 s | fourth | 0.59 L | 0.58 L, 6.7 cm below | 0.71 L, 7.3 cm below | 2.6 cm |
| `mozart-k191`, 223.3 s | fifth | 0.64 L | 0.64 L, 6.3 cm below | 0.76 L, 7.2 cm below | 2.7 cm |

The elbow cannot change the slope of the forearm near the wrist. The arm is bent: the wrist is
0.35 m from the shoulder joint, and the upper arm and the forearm are each 0.25 m long. The elbow
then lies on a circle around the line from the shoulder to the wrist, and that line runs under the
viola. A turn of the elbow pole moves the elbow on the circle, and the forearm keeps an angle of
about 45° to the line. The variants of the elbow left the forearm 2.4 cm to 2.8 cm in the viola
(`depth-elbow.txt`), and a pitch of the paw and a pole toward the elbow of a straight wrist 2.5 cm
to 2.8 cm (`depth-paw-variants.txt`).

## 2. The tilt over the upper bout

A tilt of the paw around the finger block moves the wrist up and to the side of the higher strings,
out of the body of the viola. A real hand goes up and over the shoulder of the instrument from the
third position to the sixth position (`docs/reference/string-technique.md`, section 1.5). With that
range and 50°, `boulanger-matin` kept 2.0 cm in the fourth position. The ranges from the second
position to the fourth, the fifth, or the sixth position gave the same depths, and the commit takes
the second position to the fifth position (`depth-paw-variants.txt`).

| Tilt | Play, forearms in the viola | Lower, forearms in the viola | Probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0° (`main`) | 2.7 cm | 2.7 cm | `depth-paw-variants.txt` |
| 40° | 1.0 cm | 0.4 cm | `depth-paw-variants.txt`, 4 demos |
| 45° (the commit) | 0.4 cm | 0.4 cm | `pose-depth-left-base-main.txt`, the left forearm on 5 demos |
| 50° | 0.4 cm | 0.4 cm | `depth-paw-variants.txt`, 4 demos |

The tilted paw bends the wrist more (`arm-probe.txt`):

| Demo, time | Wrist bend, `main` | Wrist bend, branch | Wrist, branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bach-bwv1048`, 265.5 s | 29.0° | 43.9° | 5.3 cm below, 5.4 cm to the side |

The violas play above the second position in 5 of 14 demos: 275 strokes of 928 in

`mozart-k191` (`positions.txt`).

## 3. The fit against `main`

The fit runs on the branch after its rebase onto `main` at `215674bf`, with the bow arm of the
round `bow-arm`. `pnpm pose-depth violist --base main` over the 14 demos
(`pose-depth-violist-base-main.txt`). The rows that change:

| Set | Pair | `main` cm | Branch cm |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| play | paws in viola | 2.4 | 1.4 |
| play | forearms in viola | 2.7 | 0.6 |
| lower | forearms in viola | 2.7 | 0.4 |

The deepest forearm of the play is now the right forearm, 0.6 cm into the viola near the wrist, as
on `main`. Each other row keeps its depth of `main`, also the play upper arms in the body (1.1 cm,
the right upper arm). The left arm alone on the five demos that change
(`pose-depth-left-base-main.txt`): the left forearm goes 0.4 cm into the viola in the play
(`bach-bwv1048`, 265.5 s) and 0.4 cm in the lowering after `mozart-k191` (2.7 cm and 2.7 cm on
`main`), and the left upper arm does not enter the viola.

`pnpm hold-fit violist --base main` on the demos of `FIT_DEMOS` (`hold-fit-violist-base-main.txt`):
the play folds 17.9% (18.3% on `main`), the lift folds 60.5% (59.7%), the rest faces 11 (11),
`stopRange` 0.00 to 1.00 and `bowRange` 0.030 m to 0.475 m, as on `main`.

`pnpm body-probe --base main` (`body-probe.txt`): the 95 bodies and the 23 players stay. 1,472 of
the 1,492 pose sets stay, and the 20 pose sets that change belong to the cat violas of
`bach-bwv1048`, `beethoven-9`, `boulanger-matin`, `brahms-2`, and `mozart-k191`.
