# The pea eyes of the studio bodies

Date: 2026-10-16. Branch `pea-eyes`, from `main` e6a5ed65. The decisions are in
[`2026-10-15-pea-eyes.md`](../decisions/2026-10-15-pea-eyes.md). The folder
[`2026-10-16-pea-eyes/`](2026-10-16-pea-eyes/) holds the probes, the logs, and the images. Each
command runs from the root of the repository.

## The old eyes

`old-eyes.ts` reads each studio GLB file (`old-eyes.log`). The studio eyes of the bear and the
polar bear are 260 gloss faces for each eye: nested shells of a white, two dark colors, and a
light. The fur under them is closed (0 open edges). The rig of the rabbit has a gloss fan of 11
faces over each eye, and the rig of the fox a fan of 12 faces. The box of the old left eye, width
by height in the head height of the eyes:

| Body | Old eye, m | Old eye, H | Pea eye, m | Pea eye, H |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | 0.046 × 0.047 | 0.146 × 0.149 | 0.041 × 0.056 | 0.13 × 0.18 |
| Polar bear | 0.045 × 0.046 | 0.125 × 0.127 | 0.047 × 0.065 | 0.13 × 0.18 |
| Rabbit | 0.039 × 0.059 | 0.120 × 0.182 | 0.042 × 0.058 | 0.13 × 0.18 |
| Fox | 0.074 × 0.054 | 0.193 × 0.141 | 0.050 × 0.069 | 0.13 × 0.18 |

The meters are the units of `old-eyes.log` times the scale of each module. The head height of the
eyes is `StudioEyes.headHeight` (`studio-body.log`): 0.313 m, 0.361 m, 0.323 m, and 0.386 m. The
fox has H 0.323 m for its holds and a head that the rig grows by 1.2.

## The size

`size-variants.webp` shows each body at 0.8, 1.0, and 1.2 times the oval of `addEye()`, beside the
cat. The camera of `faces.ts` stands at 2.2 head heights of the eyes in front of the eyes, so each
face fills the same part of its cell. At 1.0 the eye fills about the same part of the head as the
eye of the cat. The manager chose 1.0.

## The direction

The mean normal of the fur faces within half the eye height of the eye place faces 39° up on the
bear, 13° on the polar bear, 19° on the rabbit, and 11° on the left fox eye (`eye-tilt.ts`,
`eye-tilt-170d28f8-rim.log`). `direction-normal-radial.webp` shows main, the eyes along the normal,
and the eyes that face out from the middle of the head. Along the normal, the muzzle of the bear
hid the inner half of each eye in the front view. The light lies 0.035 H above the middle in the
world, so on an eye that faces 39° up, it lies 0.022 H further out along the axis. Its middle was
then 0.024 H in front of the oval, by the oval of `addEye()` at that height. The direction out
from the middle of the head turns the bear eyes by 44°, and the other eyes by 3° to 16° (the
directions of 5a35f342 and 170d28f8).

## The move out of the head

`eye-depth.ts` counts the corners of each oval more than a quarter of its half depth in front of
its middle that lie inside the head, by the sign of the nearest face of the body. On 170d28f8,
before `eyePush()`, 3 of the 12 corners of each bear eye lay up to 0.047 H inside the head
(`eye-depth-170d28f8.log`). They lie at the lower inner part of the oval, under the rise of the
muzzle. `eye-tilt.ts` gives the move along the axis for tilts of the axis from the direction out
of the head (0) to the normal (1):

| Rule | Bear move | Bear rim float | Polar bear move | Fox left move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Each front corner out (`ZMIN=0`) | 0.082 H | 0.107 H | 0.020 H | 0.024 H |
| Each corner of the front face out (`ZMIN=0.015`) | 0.046 H | 0.076 H | 0.010 H | 0.012 H |

The rim float is the largest distance of a corner of the rim out of the head. The logs are
`eye-tilt-170d28f8-rim.log` and `eye-tilt-170d28f8-front.log`. `bear-move-variants.webp` shows
the bear with no move, the move of the front face at the tilt 0, and the moves at the tilts 0.25
and 0.5. At the tilt 0 the whole bead shows from the front and from 35°. At the tilt 0.5 the
light sits on the upper edge. `eyePush()` takes the front face at the tilt 0, in steps of
0.001 H: the bear eyes move 0.046 H, the polar bear eyes 0.009 H, the left fox eye 0.010 H, and
the rabbit eyes and the right fox eye 0 (`studio-body.log`). After the move, the corners of the rim
in front of a quarter of the half depth can still lie in the head: 1 corner of each bear eye, at
most 0.022 H (`eye-depth-tip.log`).

## The colors of the old eyes

`eye-rim.ts` and `draw.py` draw the faces near each old eye of the fox, seen along the normal of
its fan (`fox-old-eye-faces.webp`, the rig of 02550fb3 above the rig of the branch). The dark
faces under the fan had the mean color of their neighbors. The white of the almond eye kept its
color, because the dark rule does not catch it: an Oklab chroma of 0.002 to 0.015 and a lightness
of 0.81 to 0.90. These faces made the cream rim around each pea eye. After the palette, the rig
now gives the faces of each old eye the color with the largest area in the ring of faces around
them. That color covers 54% and 59% of the area of the ring of the fox eyes, and 63% and 53% of
the rabbit eyes (`rig-mesh-check-fox.log`, `rig-mesh-check-rabbit.log`). The old eye holds 39 and
40 faces of the fox and 36 and 38 faces of the rabbit. `faces-main-branch.webp` shows the faces of
the five players, main beside the branch, from the front and from 35°. The polar bear and the bear
show no rim, because their studio eyes sat on closed fur.
