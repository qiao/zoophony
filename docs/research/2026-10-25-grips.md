# The grips of the winds and the triangle

Date 2026-10-25. Round `grips`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-25-grips.md`](../decisions/2026-10-25-grips.md). The probes and their outputs are in
[`2026-10-25-grips/`](2026-10-25-grips/). Each command runs from the root of the tree. `main` is

## 1. The question

The user said: "the animals are still not holding the instruments correctly even though their
arm look better". The manager listed seven faults from the close views: the triangle floats below
the paw, the bassoon lies near level, the trombone slide points down to the lap, the trumpet paws
cover the face, the tuba hides both paws, and the mouse and the wolf paws are open or round fists.
A grip is good when a person sees a hand that holds the tube.

## 2. The causes

1. **The palm side.** A curl of the `fingers` bone moves the finger tips of the skin toward +x of
   the left hand of the player and toward -x of the right hand (`curl-side.ts` of `probes/`).
   `palmToTube()` of `pose/winds.ts` took the other signs, so it turned the back of each paw to
   the tube, and the fingers curled away from it.
2. **The finger weights.** The rule `weights.fingers` 0.5 gave half of the finger weights to the
   paw. On the beaver, the badger, the panda, the monkey, and the rhino, no paw vertex followed
   the fingers more than the paw, so a curl moved little skin (`finger-weights.ts`,
   `glb_fingers.py`).
3. **The curl.** Each wind paw that only holds curled 35°, and an open key 15°.
4. **The holds.** The play turn of the shared bassoon hold added 20° to the lean of its bind pose
   (54° from the vertical, `bassoon-dir.ts`). The trombone slide pointed 35° below the level. The
   trumpet paws reached 5 cm to 6 cm above the mouthpiece. The valve branch of the tuba hid the
   right paw. The triangle hung on a cord of 1.5 mm from the heel of the paw.
5. **The measure.** `pnpm wind-grip` passed a paw with the tube through its open middle.

## 3. The fixes

- A paw grasps when its finger tips are on the instrument (0.6 cm), its palm faces the tube
  (60°), and it is on the side of the tube that the technique names (60°). The tool measures the
  bassoon, the brass, and the triangle clip too, on the tube that the instrument names for each
  palm (`InstrumentGrips.tubeL`).
- The palm faces the tube (`WindHold.faceTube`), and a roll near 180° turns around the long axis
  of the hand. Each hold gives the fingers a grip curl (`WindHold.gripCurl`).
- The five rigs keep the whole finger weights of bone heat. Each base rig of `pnpm rig-mesh
  --check` gave the bytes of its file in `assets/`, so the change of the rule is the only change.
- The holds and the clip of the decision file.
