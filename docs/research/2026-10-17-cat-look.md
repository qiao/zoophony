# The thick arms and the rest of the cat violist

Date: 2026-10-17. Round `cat-look`, base `main` at `58d47e37`. The decisions are in
[`2026-10-17-cat-look.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-cat-look.md). The folder
[`2026-10-17-cat-look/`](2026-10-17-cat-look/) holds the probes, the logs, and the images.

## 1. The question

On 2026-10-17 at 14:17, the user said: "the cat's initial pose is weird. also when playing, its arm
seem too muscular". The first screenshot shows the rest: the right arm of the seated cat hangs almost
straight, down and out to the side, with the paw far from the body. The second shows the play from
the front left: the arms look thick and blocky, with a bulge at the elbow.

## 2. The cross-sections of the arms

`arm-cuts.ts` cuts the skin of a studio body with the plane across each arm bone: at 0.6 of the upper
arm (at 0.5, the cut of the cat merges with the trunk at the armpit), at the elbow (the plane of the
bisector), and at the middle of the forearm. Each cut gives the width seen from the front and the
depth seen from the side in the standing bind, and the largest and the smallest caliper width in the
play (the median of `boulanger-matin` at 20, 40, 60, and 80 s). The values are in head heights H.
Usage: `node docs/research/2026-10-17-cat-look/arm-cuts.ts <root of a tree> cat:viola`. The trees of
the older commits were `git archive` copies of `src/`, `demos/`, and `tools/`.

```text
 skin of the arm        plane across the bone
      ___                    |
  ___/   \___  ---------->   |  loop around the axis point
 /           \               |  -> front width, side depth,
 \___     ___/               |     caliper widths, area
     \___/                   |
```

The sheet front widths come from the runs of the arms below the armpits of the front figure of

0.357 H: the shape of the bent left elbow adds 0.054 H.

## 4. The rest

`rest-arm.ts` measures both arms in the rest of one sample (`bach-bwv1050`, wall 21.1 s): the elbow
angle, the upper arm from the vertical, the elbow out from the side of the trunk, the direction of the
forearm, and the place of the wrist against the axis of its thigh. Usage:
`node docs/research/2026-10-17-cat-look/rest-arm.ts '<changes>'...`.

The grids `rest-grid.log` and `rest-grid2.log` (1,984 grips and elbows) show that the arm of the cat
reaches the top of the right thigh only nearly straight. A grip with the elbow bent to 110° or less
kept the wrist 13.8 cm or more above the axis of the thigh, and a grip on the top of the thigh put the
right forearm 4.0 cm to 8.2 cm into the belly (`fit-rest4.log`). `rest-gridL.log` and `fit-rest3.log`
give the left place and the left elbow. `fit-rest5.log` and `fit-rest6.log` give the grips nearer
to the body with the slim arms: the chosen grip, 0.6 H out, 0.3 H down, and 0.1 H back at 0.5 of the
way to the knee, puts the deepest point of the lift, the rest, and the lowering at 1.6 cm.
`rest-candidates.webp` shows three candidates with the arms of `main` from the front, at 35° to the
right, and from the side. `rest-candidates-2.webp` shows `main`, the grip 0.7 H out at 0.55 of the
way (the elbow bends to 143°, and the forearm goes out to the side), and the grip 0.55 H out at 0.5
of the way (the elbow bends to 100°) with the slim arms. The chosen grip lies between the last two.
`rest-main.webp` shows the rest of `main`.

## 5. The fit against `main`

`pnpm hold-fit violist --base main` (`hold-fit-violist-base-main.txt`): the deepest point of the
lift, the rest, and the lowering is the left forearm in the viola, 1.6 cm (1.4 cm, 1.3 cm, and
1.5 cm on `main`). The play keeps 2.5 cm of the left forearm in the viola (2.4 cm). `pnpm body-probe
--base main` (`body-probe.txt`) changes only the cat bodies, its player, and the rest, lift, and
lowering sets of the 41 seated cat violists. The paused shot test fails on neither tree
(`shots-base-main.txt`).
