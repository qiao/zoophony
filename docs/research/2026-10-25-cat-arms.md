# The rebuilt arms of the cat violist

Date 2026-10-25. Decisions: [`2026-10-25-cat-arms.md`](../decisions/2026-10-25-cat-arms.md). The
outputs and the images are in [`2026-10-25-cat-arms/`](2026-10-25-cat-arms/). Each command runs
from the root of the tree. "main" is `a3a44898`.

## 1. The question

The user allowed loop edges, retopology, and extra bones for the arm and wrist twist on
2026-10-24. The rabbit got rebuilt arms that day, but the route failed on the cat: its upper arm
joins the side of the trunk down to the elbow, so a cut at a right angle to the bone found no
closed loop, and a cut at 0.8 of the upper arm folded 12 to 16 faces at each elbow. The manager
asked for a cut along the join, a rebuild of the free arm, or new faces under the arm, and for the
open faults of the cat violist: the viola angle and the head in it, the arms in the viola, the
twisted wrist band, and the folds.

## 2. The route

`tools/blender/cat_arms.py` replays the session headless, with the plain Blender tools. A run
gives `cat-arms.glb` and `cat-weights.json` byte for byte. `rebuild.webp` shows the steps.

```text
 cat.glb (user) --> Merge by Distance
   --> Bisect along the seam (plane |x| = 0.08 u at 0.6 u high, leaning out 0.1 u per unit down)
   --> Bisect at the wrist --> Delete Faces of the arm
   --> armpit line across the hole at 0.6 u --> Fill (Beauty): the side wall of the trunk
   --> quad tube, 16 rings x 16: 2 rings blend from the socket, 14 at a right angle to the bones
   --> flat inner side on the seam plane, 0.004 u inside the wall --> Shrinkwrap (limit 0.012 u)
   --> Bridge Edge Loops: socket (top arc + armpit line) and paw --> Beautify Faces
   --> UV, bake, normals, length x 1.1496, section x 0.85, Smooth Vertices on the flat edges
   --> skeleton + 4 + 4 segments + helpers --> automatic weights --> Weight Paint fixes
```

```text
         shoulder top (socket top arc, joined)
        _______________________
       /   tube    |  trunk    \
      |  (arm)     |  side     |      the tube and the side wall meet
      |  flat side | wall      |      only at the armpit line, 0.6 u high
      |  on the    |  (new)    |
      |  wall      |           |
       \___________|___________/  <-- old join at the elbow, now open
```

The Weight Paint fixes (`step_paint()`): the trunk, the side walls, the socket, and the first
ring of each shoulder lose the arm and clavicle groups, so `pnpm rig-mesh` gives them the rule
weights of the user mesh. The other rings and the paw lose the trunk groups. The tube near the
side walls takes a chest share of 0.2 (Vertex Weight Proximity and Vertex Weight Mix). Smooth runs
10 times on the tube.

- The left forearm goes 2.5 cm into the viola in the fifth position, and the arms 2.9 cm in the
  moves.
- The viola points 74° to the left, because each turn toward 64° costs the nut.
- The viola goes 2.6 cm into the chest (limit 2.0, main 2.4).
