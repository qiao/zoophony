# The wolf mesh of the user as the clarinetist

Date 2026-10-20. Round `mesh-wolf`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-20-mesh-wolf.md`](../decisions/2026-10-20-mesh-wolf.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-20-wolf-mesh/`](2026-10-20-wolf-mesh/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree, and the base is `3268680f`, the commit before the round.

## 1. The question

The user gave a textured mesh of a wolf with no skeleton (`assets/models/wolf/wolf.glb`), and
slept. The clarinet wolves get it by route 3. The measures that decide are the size against the
dog cellists of the orchestra images, the reach of the clarinet grips at the angle of a real
clarinetist, the clearance of the sweep, the folds, and the arm gate.

## 3. The result

The wolf mesh sits on a chair that fits its legs, at 1.10 times the seated height of the dog
cellist, as in the orchestra images, with arms of the reach of the parametric wolf. It holds the
clarinet at 45° with the mouthpiece under the muzzle and the elbows down, and it rests the
clarinet over the lap. The bushy tail hangs behind the seat. No sample folds, each part stays
within the contact depth, and the arm gate passes.
