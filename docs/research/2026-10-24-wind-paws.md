# The wind paws of the wolf, the mouse, and the tan fox

Date 2026-10-24. The user asked: "fix how the wolf is holding the instrument", "fix how the tan fox
is holding the instrument", and "also how the mouse if holding the instrument". The decisions are
in [`2026-10-24-wind-paws.md`](../decisions/2026-10-24-wind-paws.md). The images and the gate tables
are in [`2026-10-24-wind-paws/`](2026-10-24-wind-paws/).

## The fault

The wolf and the mouse have long muzzles, so the mouthpiece sits far in front of the chest. With
the clarinet 45° and the oboe 40° from the vertical, the bell came far in front of the knees, and
the paws held the instrument at arm's length with level forearms (`wolf-paws-main.webp`). Each paw
also pointed along the line from its shoulder to its grip, and with the tube far in front, that
line runs along the tube: the fingers pointed past the tube in place of around it.

## The fix

The new share `WindHold.aimAcross` takes that share of the part of the aim along the tube away in
the playing pose, so that the paw crosses the tube. Only a paw that rolls its palm to the tube
takes it, and the default 0 changes no hold. The clarinet comes to 30° from the vertical (45°
before), and the oboe keeps the 30° of the app (40° before). The bells come near the knees, and
the forearms angle up to the grips (`wolf-final.webp`, `mouse.webp`, the hold of `main` in the top
row). The mouthpiece of the wolf no longer goes into the head: 0.0 cm, 1.6 cm before
(`wolf-depth.txt`).

The tan fox flute lies 8° down to the right, as a real flute, and its 30° head turn makes it look
steeper from the front. Its right paw now aims across the flute. The change is small
(`fox-tan.webp`): no larger fault showed from the front or the sides (`fox-tan-paws-main.webp`).

Each variant passes each limit of the arm gate (`gate-variants.txt`).
