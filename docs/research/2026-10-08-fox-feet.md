# The feet of the seated red fox

Date: 2026-10-08. Branch: `fox-feet`, from `main` at `e75338a`. The decision file
[`2026-10-08-fox-feet.md`](../decisions/2026-10-08-fox-feet.md) gives the decisions.

## 1. Summary

The rigged red fox (`fox-red`, the second violins) floated on its chair. Now each foot rests on
the floor. `buildRigged()` stretches the lower legs of the mesh down to the floor, and the GLB
of the model does not change.

| Measure | `main` | This branch |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest point of the right foot, default demo, 4 s, 8 s, and 50 s of play | 0.0725 m | 0.0000 m |
| Lowest point of the left foot, same moments | 0.0882 m | 0.0000 m |
| Lowest points of the feet, sheet page `views.html?sheet=fox` | 0.0882 m, 0.0725 m | 0.0000 m, 0.0000 m |
| Seated species that fail the new feet test | red fox | none |
| First download (`pnpm size`) | 482,825 B | 483,074 B (+249 B) |

The heights are the heights of the lowest posed corner of the shin and the foot of each side
above the floor, with the skin of four bones of `pose/test-skin.ts`. All four red foxes of the
default demo have the same heights, because the legs do not move in the poses.

## 2. The cause

The model sheet `fox-views.png` lets the feet hang. In the front view, the lowest pixel of the
feet in the matte is at row 1080, and a foot on the floor 0.3 m in front of the seat center is at
row 1148 with the camera of the sheet. The side view shows the same gap, and a shadow under each
foot. The pipeline of `tools/spike/fox-gated/` carves the legs with the matte of the front and
the side view, so the GLB follows the sheet. Its lowest foot corners are 0.0725 m (right) and
0.0882 m (left) above the floor.

`buildRigged()` put the mesh on the seat with no change, because the seat of the sheet and the
chair of the app both have a height of 0.35 m. The other candidates do not cause the gap:

| Candidate | Result |
|---|---|
| Rest skeleton of the legs | The legs have no bind frames. The rest joints are the joints of the rig, and the pose code turns no leg bone |
| Seat height | The chair, the sheet, and `SHEET_SEAT` of the packer all have 0.35 m |
| Root offset of the pose | 0 at each sampled moment of the rest pose. During play, the feet have the heights of the bind mesh |
| Bind mesh | The bind mesh already has the gap: 0.0725 m and 0.0882 m before the skin |

The design wants the feet on the floor: design 4.5 (chair) puts the feet on the floor, and the
foxes of `orchestra-latest.png` have their feet on the floor. Design 1 says that
`orchestra-latest.png` wins where it differs from the other references.

## 3. The fix

A change of the model does not pass the silhouette gates. Feet on the floor add model pixels
under the legs where the matte has background. An estimate from `fox-matte.png` gives
13,000 px in the front view and 8,500 px in the side view. The IoU then falls to about 0.90 in
each view, under the gates of 0.94 (front) and 0.93 (side).

Thus `feetToFloor()` of `models/rigged-body.ts` fits the decoded mesh to the floor, before
`buildRigged()` adds it:

```text
height (m)
 0.45 ┤ thigh on the seat ───────────── knee        stays
 0.35 ┤ seat top
 0.303┤ ─ ─ ─ top: lowest corner of the rest of the body (hips) ─ ─ ─
      │        shin  │                 │
      │              │  scale along y  │  right leg x 1.31, left leg x 1.41
      │        foot  │                 v
 0.07 ┤ sole (main) ─┘
 0.00 ┤ floor ─────────────────────── sole (this branch)
```

1. A corner of a lower leg is a corner whose main bone is its shin or its foot.
2. The top of the stretch is the lowest corner of all other corners: 0.303 m, the bottom of the
   hips on the seat. Thus no other corner is under the top, and the mesh has no crack.
3. Under the top, each corner of a lower leg moves along y so that the lowest corner of its leg
   comes to the floor: `y' = top - (top - y) × top / (top - lowest)`.
4. The ankle joints (`footL`, `footR`) move by the same rule. The knee joints (0.42 m) are above
   the top, and they stay.

The skin binds each leg bone at its rest joint, so the GPU skin and the stretch agree. The
footprint of the layout uses only x and z, so the stage does not change.

## 4. The tests

| Test | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `pose/seated-feet.test.ts` | Each species on the seat of each instrument of its section (the chair, the stool, the high stool, and the bench), in the rest pose at four wall times: the lowest corner of the shin and the foot of each side is at most 0.02 m above its rest (the floor, or the foot ring of the high stool) and not below it |
| `models/rigged-body.test.ts` | The lowest corner of each lower leg of the fox is on the floor of the chair, and on the foot ring of the high stool. Each corner of the rest of the body keeps its place. Each ankle moves down. The other joints keep the joints of the rig |

The new feet test failed on `main` with two lines, and with no other species:

```text
fox-red violin (chair): the left foot is 0.0882 m above its rest
fox-red violin (chair): the right foot is 0.0725 m above its rest
```

The rabbit, the cat, the dog, the hippo (on the foot ring), the deer, the polar bear, the tan
fox, the mouse, the wolf, the beaver, the pig, the monkey, the panda, and the rhino pass it. The
rabbit, the polar bear, the tan fox, the pig, and the panda have a lowest point a little under
the floor from the rounding of the skin (it prints as -0.0000 m), so the test accepts 1e-6 m
under the rest.

The three tests of the fit in `models/rigged-body.test.ts` and the feet test fail with
`feetToFloor()` taken out of `buildRigged()`.

## 7. What remains

- The lower legs of the fox are now 31% (right) and 41% (left) longer under the hips than in
  the sheet. The stretch is along y only, so the facets of the shins and the paws are taller.
  A new model with longer shins needs a matte or gates that accept feet on the floor, because
  the sheet lets them hang.
- The two legs stretch by different amounts, because the left foot of the model hangs 0.016 m
  higher than the right foot.
