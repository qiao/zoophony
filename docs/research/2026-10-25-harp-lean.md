# The lean of the harp of the deer

Date 2026-10-25. Round `harp-lean`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-25-harp-lean.md`](../decisions/2026-10-25-harp-lean.md). The sources are in
[`harp-technique.md`](../reference/harp-technique.md). The probes are in
[`2026-10-25-harp-lean/probes/`](2026-10-25-harp-lean/probes/), the outputs beside them, and the
images in `images/`. Each command runs from the root of the tree, and the base is `main`
(55c4cc17).

## 1. The question

The deer held its harp upright in front of its chest. A harpist leans the harp back, so that the
top of the soundbox rests on the right shoulder, and the knees hold a part of it (sources 1 to 3
of the reference). The round leans the harp back onto the right shoulder of the deer with no
longer arms. The measures that decide are the reach of the 350 note targets of
`boulanger-matin`, the depths of the arms, the depth of the soundbox and the strings in the body,
the gap of the head to the harp, and the arm gate.

## 2. The measures

**The body.** `front-surface.ts` maps the front of the trunk. The deer has a deep chest: at the
collarbone, the front lies 13 to 16 cm in front of the shoulder joints, and the play lean of 8°
moves it 5 cm more. The knees lie 12 cm in front of the hips, behind the front of the belly
(`harp-body-main.txt`, rest joints). Thus no straight soundbox touches both the shoulder and the
knees.

**The place.** `solve.ts` places the harp so that the top of the soundbox axis is at a contact
point, for a scale, a tilt, and a turn. Each `grid.sh` row is one `pnpm pose-depth harpist --gaps
--every 6` variant (`place-scan-*.txt`):

| Scan | Result |
|---|---|
| scale 0.78, contact at the collarbone, hold of main | turn 15° to 30°: the left paw misses the far bass strings by 8 to 126 mm. Turn 45°: each target, but the neck of the harp at the muzzle (gap 0 cm) |
| scale 0.6 to 0.7, contact at the right shoulder, elbows of main | the upper arms 5.7 to 8.1 cm in the body, because the left paw reaches across to the strings |
| scale 0.6 to 0.7, elbows out | upper arms 2.7 to 4.2 cm, each target at turn 25° and scale 0.6 or 0.65 |

A long muzzle sets the head gap: the snout reaches 31 cm in front of the head joint
(`harp-geo.ts`). A larger turn sends the neck across the muzzle, and a smaller turn moves the far
bass strings out of reach. A higher contact point brings the neck to the muzzle.

**The elbows** (`arm-scan.txt`, `elbow-scan-3.txt`). With the left elbow fully out, the left flat
p5 falls to 0.77 (0.83 on main, margin 0.05), a `FAIL` on the margin. The left elbow at
[-0.85, -1, 0] passes, with the upper arms at 3.1 cm. The right move twist max stays at 106° to
110° for five right elbows, against 100° on main.

**The footprint and the layout.** The smaller harp has a smaller footprint (`foot.ts`), so the
layout moves the seats of `boulanger-matin` (`layout-moves.txt`): 4 to 6 cm for most seats, and
8 to 30 cm for the deer, the piano, three violins, and the three oboes. The other demos have no
harp and keep their poses (`body-probe.txt`).

## 3. The result

The harp is 0.6 of the concert harp of the app (0.84 m), leans 12° back, and turns 25°. The top
of its soundbox rests on the front of the right shoulder, and it stands on a box of 0.23 m. The
spine leans 3° forward, and the elbows point out and down.

**The harp in the body** (`harp-geo.ts` with each fourth play sample, `harp-body.txt`,
`harp-body-main.txt`):

| Pose | Soundbox in body, main | Soundbox in body, branch | Strings in body | Head gap, main and branch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| play | 4.6 cm, at the chest | 2.2 cm, at the right shoulder | 0.0 cm | 6.8 cm, 6.6 cm |
| rest | 1.5 cm | 0.2 cm | 0.0 cm | 15.4 cm, 10.4 cm |

**The depths of the arms** (`pnpm pose-depth harpist --gaps --base main`, `pose-depth.txt`):
each pair is lower than on main, and none grows.

| Set | Pair | main | branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| play | upper arms in body | 4.9 cm | 3.2 cm |
| play | forearms in body | 2.7 cm | 0.0 cm |
| play | forearms in harp | 3.6 cm | 1.8 cm |
| lift | upper arms in body | 3.7 cm | 2.7 cm |
| rest | upper arms in body | 3.7 cm | 2.4 cm |
| lower | upper arms in body | 4.5 cm | 2.9 cm |

`pnpm pose-depth harpist --targets` (`targets.txt`): each of the 350 targets within 5 mm, the worst
0.00 mm (1.28 mm on main).

**The arm gate** (`pnpm arm-check harpist --base main`, `arm-check.txt`, main in parentheses):

| Arm | Folds | Twist | Flat p5 | Paw twist | Paw swing | Grip turn | Move twist max | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L | 2 (1) | 37 (30) | 0.79 (0.83) | 75 (69) | 62 (54) | 13 (9) | 68 (66) | PASS |
| R | 7 (7) | 50 (55) | 0.81 (0.84) | 108 (102) | 29 (29) | 2 (2) | 108 (100) | FAIL |

The right `FAIL` is on the margin of 3° only, within the limit of 176°. The leaned harp puts the
treble strings near the right shoulder, so the right paw reaches back to them. The right twist p95
falls from 55° (a `FAIL` on main) to 50°. The median paw twists give the entry
`['harpist', { L: -55, R: -99 }]` against `{ L: -55, R: -101 }`, so `ACCEPTED_GRIPS` keeps its
entry. `pnpm arm-check --all --base main` (`arm-check-all.txt`) gives no other change.
`pnpm clip-probe --base main --jobs 4` (`clip-probe.txt`) passes each demo, equal to main.
`pnpm size --base main` (`size.txt`): +193 B.

**The look.** `pnpm close-views harpist <dir> --moments chord,pluck,rest --views
front,left,right,back,35,-35 --zoom 1.2 --base main --pairs front,left,right,back` gives
`views-*.webp` and `views-grid.webp`. From each side, the harp leans back onto the shoulder on its
box. The 2x paw pairs (`paws-*.webp`, `--zoom 2 --scale 2`) show the left paw on the bass strings
from the left side with the fingers up along them, and the right palm on the treble strings from
the right side. `stage-default.webp` and `stage-at-deer.webp` show the stage of main and of the
branch: the composition keeps its rows, and the red foxes hide most of the deer from the
audience.

**What differs from the sources.** The knees of the deer lie under its belly, so the base stands
in front of the knees, between them only in the front view. The forearms rise about 30° from the
elbow to the wrist, where a harpist holds them level. The left palm is 0.14 m above the middle of
the strings and the right palm at the middle, where a harpist holds both hands at almost one
level. From the front, the crown of the column stands just below the muzzle.
