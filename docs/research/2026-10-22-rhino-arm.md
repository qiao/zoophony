# The round inner side of the rhino arms

Date 2026-10-22. Round `rhino-arm`, base `63e23947`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-22-rhino-arm.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-rhino-arm.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-22-rhino-arm/`](2026-10-22-rhino-arm/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree. Copy a probe to `scratch/` before you run it. `with-glb.ts` and `flap.ts` are in
[`2026-10-22-panda-armpit/`](2026-10-22-panda-armpit/).

## 1. The question

The user said: "the panda and rhino's arms also got deformed". From az-150, the left forearm of
the rhino tubist was flat and angular under the tuba. Each thick arm of the source joins the
trunk from the paw up to the shoulder, so the cut closed the inner side of each arm with one flat
cap of 22 (left) and 23 (right) faces. The goal: round arms with no flat sheet, dent, lip, or
flap at each moment of `ROLE_VIEWS.tubist`, and the limits of `pnpm arm-look`.

## 2. The cause of the crease share

`crease-map.ts` counts each edge of the arm region of `pnpm arm-look` that creases in the play.
The tuba hold is almost still, so each edge creases in more than 90% of the samples or in less
than 10%. On the base, 73 of 436 left edges crease in each sample (`crease-map-base.txt`):

- 24 edges of the lower forearm near the wrist, which bone heat gives 0.05 to 0.18 of upper arm
  weight. Face 2693 there turned over in each sample, as a black facet under the forearm.
- 8 edges of the lips of the trunk beside the forearm, with 0.14 to 0.21 of upper arm weight.
  The arm at the tuba drags them, as dark slits on the side of the trunk.
- The edges of the armpit, the clavicle, and the shoulder top.

## 3. The edit and the rules

`rhino_arms.py` (the rabbit elbow script, as `arm_patch.py` of the panda round) writes
`assets/models/rhino/rhino-arms.glb`. It removes the 48 and 49 faces that join each arm to the
trunk below `cut.top`, and closes each arm with a round patch of 20 and 19 new vertices
(`patch.txt`). The rig then needs these rules (`tools/mesh-rigs/rhino.json`):

- `weights.armIslands`: the free arm takes each patch.
- `cut.low` 0.18: the cut closes the hole of the trunk (down to y 0.189), above the open edges
  of the tail (`holes.py`).
- `weights.armBelow` [0.4, 0.5]: the trunk beside the forearm gives its arm weight back.
- `weights.rigid`: one thin face of the left inner forearm, three of the right wrist, and the
  long thin face at the back of each arm root (faces 38 and 2413 of the base) move rigidly.
