# The twisted and flat arms of the hippo and the elephant

Date 2026-10-19. Round `arm-skin`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-19-arm-skin.md`](../decisions/2026-10-19-arm-skin.md). The outputs of the probes are in
[`2026-10-19-arm-skin/`](2026-10-19-arm-skin/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

After four arm rounds, the user said that the arms of the hippo and the elephant are still twisted
and deformed in the play. The fold count of `pnpm fold-probe` had gone down to 13.6% and 0.4%. A
skin that twists around the arm, or an arm that goes flat, turns no face over, so a fold count
does not see it. The round needed a measure of the twist and of the collapse, the cause, and a
choice of remedy.

## 2. The measure

`pnpm arm-shape <role>` cuts the arm skin with four planes normal to the bone: at the shoulder
(0.15 of the upper arm), the middle of the upper arm, the elbow (0.85), and the middle of the
forearm. A ring is the set of the crossings of the mesh edges with a plane. The meshes have only 1
to 9 vertices near each plane, so rings of vertices gave noise.

| Value | Definition |
|---|---|
| twist | the largest step of the roll of the rings, from the chest to the shoulder ring and along the upper arm, in degrees |
| flat | the narrow axis of the ring as a share of the bind (1 is round) |
| girth | the mean distance of the ring from the axis as a share of the bind |
| spikes | the faces of the shoulder whose longest edge grows more than 1.8 times |

The girth did not separate the arms (hippo 0.94). The flat share did. The arms that look right
give a play twist of 36° and a flat share of 0.80 (rabbit) and 0.85 (red fox).

## 3. The cause

The plane of the arm sets the roll of the one upper arm bone. The hippo bow arm, with its elbow
out and level, rolls 86°. The elephant arms, with the elbows out at the height of the shoulders,
roll 83° and 84°. The shoulder skin is about half on the upper arm, so the linear blend turns the
shoulder ring 56° in 15% of the arm and squeezes it flat (hippo flat 0.54). The faces at the
clavicle and the neck of the hippo stretch 2.1 to 2.7 times. No face of the elephant stretches
1.8 times: its torn look is the arm cut, which opens when the elbows rise.

## 4. The variants

A is a dual quaternion blend, B a twist bone of the upper arm with half of the roll
(`upperTwist` 0.5), and C the new holds: the hippo right elbow down (`[1,-1.5,-0.5]`), and the
elephant elbows down and near the body (`[∓0.7,-1,0.2]`). The values are for the play, at the worst
arm. The hippo values are for the right arm. The elephant values are for the left and the right
arm.

| Variant | hippo twist | hippo flat | hippo spikes | hippo folds | elephant twist | elephant flat | elephant folds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| base | 56 | 0.54 | 5.1 | 13.6% | 57 / 43 | 0.96 / 0.74 | 0.4% |
| A | 61 | 0.69 | 7.1 | 100% | 56 / 42 | 0.99 / 0.82 | 0.7% |
| B | 45 | 0.99 | 4.9 | 98.9% | 39 / 39 | 0.97 / 0.97 | 0.3% |
| C | 43 | 0.81 | 7.0 | 100% | 36 / 25 | 0.90 / 0.76 | 100% |
| B + C | 27 | 1.00 | 7.0 | 100% | 23 / 22 | 0.88 / 0.91 | 98.1% |
| A + C | 49 | 0.89 | 7.1 | 100% | 34 / 25 | 0.94 / 0.83 | 100% |

`variants.txt` holds each table, and `depths-c.txt` the sweep of C. The elephant C keeps its depths
within 0.3 cm and each target. The hippo C puts the right forearm 8.2 cm into the body, so its pole
needs a scan. The corrective shapes and the rigid weights of both bodies fit the old holds and the
linear blend, so each variant folds most samples until a refit. A gives the hippo shoulder a bulge
of 1.28 and a shader cost.

## 5. The result

The user chose B + C. The twist bone is a rule of the config of the mesh rig, and no body uses it
before the refit rounds of the hippo and the elephant.

## The refit of the elephant

Round `refit-elephant`. The outputs are in
[`2026-10-19-arm-skin/elephant/`](2026-10-19-arm-skin/elephant/). `shapes-v<n>.json` holds the
list `raise` of each variant of `rig-shapes.txt`. The round turned on B (`upperTwist.share` 0.5)
and C (`poleL` `[-0.7,-1,0.2]`).

The cause of the folds of B alone: with the twist bone, the pose of the old rule of the bent left
elbow keeps face 837 turned after 60 rounds of relaxing (`rig-b.txt`). Its shape then folds that
face in 37% of the play. A rule of the bent elbow that also bends the paw as in its samples
(`turns.hand`) relaxes in its pose (v1 against v2).

| Shapes on the rig with B and C | play | lift | rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| the old shapes (`rig-no-shapes.txt` has none: 100%) | 93.8% | 67.7% | 0% |
| v1: the four fits of `pnpm raise-fit` on the rig with no shapes | 39.4% | 23.4% | 0% |
| v2: the right arm, the bent elbow with the paw turn, the left arm | 16.1% | 10.5% | 0% |
| v5: v2 and a bent wrist fit on the rig of v2 | 0.2% | 2.4% | 0% |
| v5 with no rigid face (chosen) | 0.2% | 0.8% | 0% |
| v5 with no rigid face, share 0.4 / 0.6 (`rig-shares.txt`) | 0.2% / 1.0% | 0.8% / 2.4% | 0% |

| Measure (the play, L / R) | base | round |

| twist (`arm-shape.txt`) | 57° / 43° | 22° / 22° |
| flat share | 0.96 / 0.74 | 0.91 / 0.91 |

The shares 0.4 and 0.6 give a twist of 24° (`arm-shape-shares.txt`), so 0.5 stays. The upper arms
point about 57° below the level in the play (`fold-no-shapes.txt`).
