## The grip of the bow

`stickGrip()` of `tools/bow-grip.ts` measures the gap from the middle of the closed right paw to
the axis of the stick, and the depth of the axis in the paw. The middle of the paw is the middle of
the volume of the skin of the bones `handR` and `fingersR`, with a cap on the cut to the forearm.
`grip-probe.ts` runs it over the sample sets of the violinist and violist sweeps.

```text
          hand axes of the player (palm side -x, arm up +y)

    main: stick 4.3 cm out on the palm side        branch: stick through the paw
            +-------+                                     +-------+
            | paw   |                                     | paw   |
            |   o   |        * stick                      |   o*  |  (o middle, * stick)
            +-------+                                     +-------+
```

On `main`, each species holds the stick at `BOW_GRIP`, 0.127 H from the middle of the palm on the
palm side, so the stick lies under the paw in each moment (`grip-main.txt`). For the cat, a stick
square across the palm at the middle of the paw still missed the turned paw: the studio paw turns
in the plane of its palm toward the forearm by 0° to 70° (`PAW_TURN`), and its middle moves on an
arc around the wrist (`stick-line.ts` fits the line to the middles of the paw in the axes of the hand). The stick of the cat
now lies along the line nearest to that arc, 50° from square toward the wrist (`stick-line.ts`,
`stick-line.txt`), so that the paw slides along the stick and keeps it in each turn.

```typescript
/** The grip of the bow in the right paw of a species (`SpeciesParams.bowGrip`). */
export interface BowGrip {
  /** The place of the stick at the grip, from the middle of the right palm, in head heights. */
  readonly at: Vec3;
  /** The turn of the stick around the normal of the palm, in radians, toward the wrist. */
  readonly turn: number;
}
```

`paw-grip.webp` shows the posed right paw and the bow in the axes of the hand, from the palm side,
the side of the stick, and the fingers: the cat of `main`, the cat of the branch, and the red fox
and the rabbit, which do not change.

upright on the left thigh, and the bow upright at the right side, with the frog in the paw at the
outer side of the right thigh. The right grip came from the scans of `rest-scan.txt`: the goals of

near 0.57 of the way to the knee. The left paw at rest goes 1.3 cm into the viola, and each tried

## The wrist

On `main`, faces 2116 and 2007 of the left wrist of the cat folded in each play sample, and the turn
of the paw from the forearm stayed within 10° of one turn (`wrist-turn.txt`). The cat rig now has
the corrective shape of a bent wrist (`raise` with the bone `hand`), and the app reads its weight
from the turn of the paw from the forearm. The Blender pose of the rule at the mean turn of 153.7°
turned over 1 face and left face 2116 folded in 30% of the play samples, so the rule turns the paw
to 165° on the same axis (`rig-mesh-check-wrist.log`). The folds of the right forearm and of the
right clavicle stay. The new grip moves the hand about 4 cm out on the bow, and face 235 of the
right forearm folds in more samples. The right elbow of the play points a little further out
(`poleR` of `CAT_HOLD`), so that the right upper arm stays within 0.5 cm of `main`.
