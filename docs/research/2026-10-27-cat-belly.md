## 1. The question

The user said "make the cat's belly more oval and smooth". In `cat-views.png`, the trunk is an
even egg from the front and one convex arc from the side, and the light patch is a long oval
(`reference-outlines.txt`). The measure: cuts across the line from the hips joint to the neck
joint, from 0.1 to 0.8 of it (`f`), in steps of 1.3 cm in the app. Each outline gives the gap to
its convex hull (a dent or a pinch), the dents over 2 mm, and the largest turn between cuts (a
corner or a lump). The patch takes its soft width: the light share of each face (Oklab 0.55 to
0.88) along the cut. The facets take the angles between neighbor light faces.
