# Front key light

Date 2026-10-27. Round `sixth-key-front`, on `main` at `6b58f6f2`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-27-key-front.md`](../decisions/2026-10-27-key-front.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-27-key-front/`](2026-10-27-key-front/), and `commands.txt` gives each command. Each
command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user looked at two wide views from the front right (`user/user-45.webp`, `user-46.webp`): the
beam came down from the back, and the shadows fell toward the camera. The user said: "make the key
light go from front to back, instead of back to front, and let the shadows react accordginly".

The plan of the manager: the key light stands in front of the stage and high, lights the faces,
and casts the shadows toward the back. It stays the only light with shadows. Haze scatters most
light forward, so a beam shows when it shines toward the camera: each visible beam comes from a
rim light behind the stage, with no shadow. The fill stays low. A normal concert has even light
from above and a front light from the hall. A concert for television adds back and top light from
trusses, and haze for visible beams.

The measures that decide: the side of the shadows, the white fur and the lit floor against
`orchestra-latest.png` and `main`, the back rows against the front rows, and the frame time.

## 2. The light

```text
             rim light: 2.45 S behind, 66° high, 5 lux, no shadow
                       │  (visible beam in the haze)
   back    ┌───────────v───────────┐
           │  rims on the heads    │   shadows: to the back right

   front   └───────────────────────┘
               ^
               │ key light: 2.45 S, 35° left of the front axis, 60° high,
               │ 6.5 lux at the stage center, the only shadow
            audience and default camera (the user camera: front right)
```

## 3. The direct light

`pnpm light-probe` gives the light on the front of each player at the chest, as a part of the
first row (`light-probe-main.txt`, `light-probe-round.txt`):

| Demo | `main`: first row, back row, choir | Round: first row, back row, choir |
|---|---|---|
| `beethoven-9` | 2.54 lux, 0.65, 0.91 | 2.74 lux, 0.91, 1.20 |
| `brahms-2` | 2.46 lux, 0.74 | 2.68 lux, 1.03 |
| `boulanger-matin` | 2.48 lux, 0.67 | 2.70 lux, 0.93 |

The key light gives 0.6 to 0.96 of the light of each row, and the back rows get almost the light
of the first row. On the floor behind the back row, the back-row lights give 18% of the key light
(34% on `main`), and the rim light 64%. The back-row lights spill nothing on the first row.

`pool-probe.ts` gives the key light on the stage top as a part of the stage center (`pool.txt`).
With the cone of 19° and the aim at the stage center, the side edges keep 0.62 to 0.76, the front
corners 0.15 to 0.24, and the back corners 0.20 to 0.38. The aim at 0.3 of the way to the podium
gave the back middle 0.45.

## 4. The variants

Batch 1 moved the key light to the front with the old side lights: the white fur of the front
left rabbit had a luma of 214 to 222 against 150 on `main` and 163 in the reference. A front light
lights the fronts by the cosine of its elevation and the floor by its sine. Batches 2 and 3
lowered the side lights, raised the key light, and gave the floor a part of its light from the
rim light. With the key light 20° from the axis, the fronts were flat, and the players hid their
shadows. With 35°, each body has a lit side and a shade side. Batch 4 compared the rim light and
the beams. Batches 1 to 3 had the key light at the right. The user looks from the front right, so
the final light stands at the left, where the shadows show in that view too.

`variants.webp` shows the final set (`variants/final.md`). The columns: the default view, a high
view from the front right as `user-45`, and a lower one as `user-46`.

| Variant | Fur, rabbit | Fur, default p50/p90 | Lit floor L/R | Back/front fur | Back floor | Hall p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reference | 163 | 185/246 | 191/158 | 0.98 | 41 | 49 |
| `main` | 150 | 177/218 | 118/131 | 0.93 | 63 | 34 |
| b: one beam (chosen) | 193 | 200/226 | 115/111 | 0.90 | 63 | 34 |
| a: no beam, no rim light | 192 | 196/222 | 100/96 | 0.85 | 18 | 21 |

The `main` row is `variants/look-main.md`. The fur of b is brighter than on `main`, but it stays
below the p90 of the reference and does not clip. The high key gives the fur of `main` and the
reference, but its shadows are short, and the camera sees few of them. The low key gives long
shadows across the stage and the brightest fronts.

## 5. The result

The code takes b: the key light 35° left and 60° high with 6.5 lux, the side lights 0.5 lux, the
back-row lights 1.5 lux, and one rim light of 5 lux with its beam on the stage axis. It is the
nearest to the one beam of each reference image, and the shadows fall to the back right, beside
the players in the default view and in the view of the user. The skirt of the stage moves from a
luma of 35 to 37 (`skirt.py`), so its baked light stays.
