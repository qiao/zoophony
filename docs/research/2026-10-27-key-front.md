# Front key light

Date 2026-10-27. Round `sixth-key-front`, on `main` at `6b58f6f2`, rebased onto `93700a1e` and
`65b3aad8`.
Decisions: [`2026-10-27-key-front.md`](../decisions/2026-10-27-key-front.md). The probes and their
outputs are in [`2026-10-27-key-front/`](2026-10-27-key-front/), and `commands.txt` gives each
command. Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user looked at two wide views from the front right (`user/user-45.webp`, `user-46.webp`): the
beam came down from the back, and the shadows fell toward the camera. The user said: "make the key
light go from front to back, instead of back to front, and let the shadows react accordginly".

The plan of the manager: a high front key that lights the faces, casts the shadows toward the
back, and stays the only light with shadows, and beams from rim lights behind the stage with no
shadow, because haze scatters most light forward. A concert for television adds such back light
and haze to the front light of a hall.

The measures that decide: the side of the shadows, the white fur and the lit floor against
`orchestra-latest.png` and `main`, the back rows against the front rows, and the frame time.

## 2. The light

```text
             rim light: 2.45 S behind, 66° high, 5 lux, no shadow
                       │  (visible beam in the haze)
   back    ┌───────────v───────────┐
           │  rims on the heads    │   shadows: to the back right
           │  side 0.5 ◄──► 0.5    │   back-row lights 1.5 lux
   front   └───────────────────────┘
               ^
               │ key light: 2.45 S, 35° left of the front axis, 60° high,
               │ 6.5 lux at the stage center, the only shadow
            audience and default camera (the user camera: front right)
```

## 3. The direct light

`pnpm light-probe` gives the light on the front of each player at the chest, as a part of the
first row (`light-probe-main.txt`, `light-probe-round.txt`):

| Demo | `main`: first row, back row, choir | Round: first row, back row, choir |
|---|---|---|
| `beethoven-9` | 2.54 lux, 0.65, 0.91 | 2.00 lux, 1.26, 1.65 |
| `brahms-2` | 2.46 lux, 0.74 | 2.05 lux, 1.35 |
| `boulanger-matin` | 2.48 lux, 0.67 | 2.03 lux, 1.23 |

The key light gives most of the light of each row. On the floor behind the back row, the back-row
lights give 18% of the key light (34% on `main`), and the rim light 64%. The back-row lights spill
nothing on the first row. The look lab gives the back rows 0.93 of the front rows
(below).

`pool-probe.ts` gives the key light on the stage top as a part of the stage center (`pool.txt`).
With the cone of 19° and the aim at the stage center, the side edges keep 0.62 to 0.76, the front
corners 0.15 to 0.24, and the back corners 0.20 to 0.38.

## 5. The hot spot

The hot spot multiplies the key light by up to 1.85 around the podium (`LIGHT_POOL`). From the
front, it lit the faces of the front rows: in the rabbit close shot, a channel of 15% of the light
fur pixels was at 255 (2.4% on `main`). A weaker shot key changed this only to 13.7%. With no hot
spot on the players, the back of the conductor fell to a luma of 24 (43 on `main`, 52 in the
reference). The players now take the hot spot with 0.3 of its radii (`variants/hot-spot.md`): the
conductor keeps 36, and the front rows lose the boost (`clip.txt`):

## 6. The frame time

`pnpm frame-compare` measures `perf-60.mid` (`frame/`). Each `*-table.md` gives the median of 2
runs, and `diagnose-*` one run of each item:

| Item | GPU p50 | Main thread p50 | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| `main` (`93700a1e`, `65b3aad8`) | 7.91 to 7.96 ms | 3.76 to 3.95 ms | all |
| round, rim light as a spotlight | 8.18, 8.25 ms | 3.92 ms | `spotlight.md` |
| no rim light, no beam | 7.53 ms | 3.88 ms | `spotlight.md` |
| three rim spotlights and beams | 12.39 ms | 3.95 ms | `spotlight.md` |
| round, rim term for each pixel | 8.16 ms | 3.94 ms | `diffuse-table.md` |
| the same, with the key place of `main` | 8.19 ms | 3.70 ms | `diagnose-3` |
| the same, with no rim on the stage | 8.05 ms | 3.70 ms | `diagnose-5` |
| the same, with no rim on the players | 8.10 ms | 4.56 ms | `diagnose-5` |
| round, rim term of the vertices (final) | 7.79 ms | 3.79 ms | `vertex-table.md` |
| three beams, rim terms of the vertices | 8.13 ms | 3.79 ms | `vertex-table.md` |

The commits of the round (`diagnose-4`) put the cost on the commit of the rim light, at 0 lux.
The key place costs nothing. The rim light cost 0.25 ms as a spotlight of three.js, and also as
a term for each pixel: 0.18 ms on the stage and 0.13 ms on the players. A far light falls off
slowly over a face, so the vertex shaders now calculate it, and the round is 0.17 ms below `main`.
Each beam costs its haze: about 0.4 ms for one (no beam: 7.53 ms), and three beams 0.34 ms more
than one.

## 7. The frozen shots

`pnpm frozen --base main` (`frozen.txt`, `images/frozen.webp`): each of the four shots changes,
because the key light, its shadows, and the light on the fronts change. The part of the pixels
above 16 levels: `play` 18%, `conductor` 13%, `perf-wide` 20%, and `perf-section` 34%, the close
shot of the pianist, where the floor around the piano gets less light from the front.

## 8. The images

`images/` holds `main` above the round: the default view and the two views from the front right
for two demos (`wide-*.webp`), the default view at 390 × 844 (`phone-*.webp`), and the close
shots of the rabbit, the dog, and the hippo (`shot-*.webp`). In each one, the shadows fall to the
back right, and the key light lights the faces from the front left. The right cello dog of
`shot-dog.webp` blinks in the frame of the round: three more renders of the same moment show open
eyes with each gloss of the lacquer.

## 9. The result

The code takes b with the narrow hot spot: the key light 35° left and 60° high with 6.5 lux, the
side lights 0.5 lux, the back-row lights 1.5 lux, and one rim light of 5 lux with its beam on the
stage axis, as a diffuse term of the vertex shaders. It is the nearest to the one beam of each reference image. The white fur is near the
reference, and the back rows keep 0.93 of the front rows, as on `main`. The skirt of the stage
moves from a luma of 35 to 37 (`skirt.py`), so its baked light stays.
