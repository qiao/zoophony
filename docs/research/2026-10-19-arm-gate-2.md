## 1. The question

The first gate failed the phase 2 paws of the elephant only against a base, and it failed the
mouse oboist on a stretch that never changes. The manager asked for a paw measure that fails a
turned paw with no base, a stretch against the rest, views with no hidden arm, and a check of the
paw of the bear conductor at the start of its lift.

The rest paws of the mesh bodies lie 70° to 180° from the bind, so the bind paw is no neutral
wrist. A good violin left paw turns 133° to 156° from the bind and 110° to 151° from its rest.
The phase 2 paw turns 136° from the bind and 0° from its rest. Thus no anatomy limit of 90°, from
the bind or from the rest, fails phase 2 and passes the good paws.

The gate now reads the grip turn: the turn of the median play twist of each paw from its accepted
grip (`ACCEPTED_GRIPS` of `tools/arm-check.ts`, the play medians of `eefece23`), with the limit
45°. A round that changes a grip on purpose gives its new median with the crops that the user
accepted.
