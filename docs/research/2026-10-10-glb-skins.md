# The skins of the rigged rabbit and bear

On 2026-10-10, the user asked to make the rigged bear and rabbit look more like the reference
images and their GLB models, and asked why the app looked so different from the GLB files. This
report gives the causes, the measures of the bake, and the results. The decisions are in
[`2026-10-10-glb-skins.md`](../decisions/2026-10-10-glb-skins.md).

## The causes of the difference

The app kept the mesh of each file, so the silhouettes, the limb lengths, and the ears were the
same. The colors were different:

1. The texture did not ship. `rig_body.py` gave each face the region of the nearest color of a
   palette (fur, accent, dark), and the app painted each region in one flat color. All detail
   inside a face went: the white of the eyes, the iris rings, the lid lines, the round nose, the
   mouth line, and the tones of the facets.
2. The species colors took the place of the colors of the files. The fur of the rabbit file is
   gray (`#c1bcb9`), and the app painted it `#ece6dc`. The fur of the bear file is `#7b411e`, and
   the app painted it `#765646`.
3. Code plates covered the eyes: a dark brown disc, a black pupil, and a white light. The packer
   measured each eye from the corners of the few dark faces in the box of the eyes. The two eyes
   of the bear got the radii 0.039 m and 0.024 m.
4. The black nose of the bear texture is smaller than its faces. The faces that were mostly dark
   made a block, and the collapse turned it into a parallelogram.

## The textures of the files

Each file has one PNG texture of 4096 × 4096 texels: 21.5 MB for the bear and 17.1 MB for the
rabbit. The textures are atlases of many small islands with black gaps, as an image-to-3D tool
makes them. An eye of the bear is about 30 texels wide in the atlas. A copy of 512 × 512 texels
is 20 kB to 28 kB as WebP, but each eye is then about 4 texels wide, and the black gaps bleed
into the islands at the small mipmap levels. Thus the packer bakes a new texture with a layout of
its own.

## The bake

`tools/blender/rig_body.py` makes the skin in these steps:

- Smart UV project (66°) on the collapsed mesh, the islands of the face at two times the scale,
  and a pack with a margin of 6 texels around a pinned white square of 8 texels at (0, 1).
- An emission bake of the base color with Cycles: 4 samples, a cage of 0.01 m, rays of at most
  0.04 m, and a margin of 6 texels. A bake of the diffuse color gave black, because a glTF
  material with no metal value is a metal.
- The texels outside the islands get the fur color, so that the small mipmap levels show no dark
  seams.
- A bake takes 9 s to 10 s for one body on the CPU of the development machine, with the two
  passes of the eyes.

The skins are 1024 × 1024 texels, WebP at quality 90: 46,404 B for the bear and 33,810 B for the
rabbit.

## The eyes

The packer samples the texture of each face in the box of the eyes at 153 points (16 steps along
each edge). The dark core is the samples with a luma below `eyeLuma`. The eye is the samples
within 3 RMS radii of the core whose color is more than 0.15 from the fur color. The half width
and the half height are two standard deviations of the eye samples in the plane of the eye.

| Body | Middle of the eye (file units) | Half width | Half height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear, left | 0.0772, -0.1814, 1.4627 | 0.0231 | 0.0157 |
| Bear, right | -0.0774, -0.1817, 1.4656 | 0.0225 | 0.0166 |
| Rabbit, left | 0.0744, -0.0925, 0.8690 | 0.0245 | 0.0323 |
| Rabbit, right | -0.0742, -0.0925, 0.8694 | 0.0248 | 0.0318 |

The two eyes of each file differ by 3 mm at most, so the packer uses their mirrored mean.

The first plates were flat. On the curved head of the rabbit, a flat plate took another light
than the facets around it and showed a disc. Each plate now has an inner and an outer ring of 12
corners on the surface of the head, 1.5 mm in front of it: 60 triangles for each eye.

The skin under a plate must show fur when the eye closes. A paint of the fur color after the bake
left thin marks of the eyes at the edges of the triangles, from the margin of the bake. The packer
now paints the fur color around each eye onto a copy of the GLB texture (1.3 times the measured
eye), bakes the body from the copy, and bakes only the plates from the texture. The faces with
no plates:

## The colors

The first move of the palette colors added the difference of each palette color in sRGB, with a
weight from the distance. The light chest of the bear is far from the fur color, so it moved less
and stayed orange. The move in Oklab keeps the relation of the colors: the same change of the
lightness, the same ratio of the chroma, and the same turn of the hue, with the weight
`exp(-(d² / 0.15²)²)` for a distance `d` in Oklab. Now the chest stays a lighter brown, as in
`bear-views.png`.

`pnpm sheet-check bear` gives the color medians of the sheet and of the page in the front view:
107, 68, 49 and 118, 77, 53. Before the skin, the page gave 110, 70, 48.

## The tail of the bear

The back of the bear file is deepest at a height of 0.6 m in the units of the file
(y = 0.322). In the back view of `bear-views.png`, the tail is at 0.32 of the height of the bear,
about 0.53 m, with a diameter of about 0.13 m. The ball has a radius of 0.065 m at
(0, 0.32, 0.53), 80 triangles, and the bake gives it the fur of the hips behind it.

The silhouette IoU of the bear page against the sheet is 0.744, 0.677, and 0.749 (front, side,
back). Before, it was 0.744, 0.683, and 0.748: the mesh is the same, and the tail sticks out a
little more than the tail of the sheet in the side view.

## The budgets and the size

| Body | Collapsed mesh | Eye plates | Tail | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rabbit | 2,396 | 120 | 0 | 2,516 |
| Bear | 2,376 | 120 | 80 | 2,576 |

A vertex on a seam of the UV layout becomes two vertices, and each vertex gets its skin
coordinates. The modules grow from about 16 KB to 29,728 B (rabbit) and 30,209 B (bear) after
gzip. With the skins, the first download is 448,953 B after gzip.
