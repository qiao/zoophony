# Blender stage S1: the dog family, the tan fox, and the wolf

Date: 2026-10-07. Branch: `blender-s1`, from `main` at `d70405d`. Stage S1 of
[`2026-10-07-blender-models-plan.md`](2026-10-07-blender-models-plan.md), section 7.

## 1. Summary

- **One recipe for the dog family.** `tools/blender/canine.py` builds the foxes and the wolf. A
  species file gives its measures in H (`canine.Measures`), and the rig gives the colors and the
  accent parts of the species.
- **The red fox follows `fox-views.png`.** The user added this reference during the stage, and it
  wins for the fox. The fox has a round head, a short muzzle, large ears with dark tips, large
  eyes with a white, dark gloves, round haunches, and a larger tail.
- **The tan fox and the wolf have a sculpt.** All three species pass every unit test and the
  speed tests with no bound changed.
- **A test over all sculpts** checks each sculpt against its rig, its colors, its budget, its
  skin, and its mesh (closed shells that face out).
- **`pnpm sculpt --all`** builds each species with a recipe. A second build gives the same bytes.
- **Size.** The first download is 357,000 B after gzip (limit 1,000,000 B). Each sculpt adds
  about 17 KB.

Gaps that stay:

- **Head size.** The heads of `fox-views.png` are larger against the body than our heads. The
  head height H and the bones fix the proportion, so the sculpt does not change it.
- **Tail.** The tail of `fox-views.png` lies on the seat beside the body. Our tail hangs behind
  the chair and keeps the depth of the parametric tail. A tail 0.12 H less deep made the stage of
  Boulanger 0.012 m smaller, and `layout-scene.test.ts` pins the stage side to 0.005 m.
- **Shins.** The shins are long, because the knee is 0.30 m above the hock on the chair.
- **Mouth.** The mouth point of the parametric body is 0.43 H below the head center. The chin of
  the shorter muzzle is at about 0.38 H, so the flute and the clarinet touch the front of the
  chin.

## 5. Notes for stage S2

- **The recipe of a family.** Copy the form of `canine.py`: a frozen `Measures` dataclass with
  the values of one species as the defaults, a `Recipe` class, and a short species file that
  gives `regions`, `build_body`, and `build_legs`. `kit.py` has the shared steps, the chair of
  the previews (`kit.show_boxes()`), and the views of the references.
- **Rigid limbs.** Each limb segment is a closed rigid part on one bone: the upper arm, the
  forearm, the thigh, and the shin. One shell across a joint fails the volume test of each bone.
- **Tests that read the sculpt.** `species.test.ts` checks the top of the head (2.3 H to 2.37 H
  above the seat), the colors of 4.5, the budget, and the volume of each bone. `sculpt.test.ts`
  checks the rig, the colors, the budget, the skin, and the closed shells. The layout, the rest
  and play clearances, the hidden heads, and the shots read the mesh too.
- **The depth of the body sets the layout.** Keep the front of the head and the back of the tail
  near the parametric values, or the packed rows move.
- **The budget.** The cuts of the trunk double its triangles. Set the decimate of the trunk to
  about 540 to 640, and count the parts of the face. The rabbit sheet of `rabbit-views.png` has
  about 1,150 triangles, so a rabbit can have fewer.
- **The dog.** The dog of `dog-views.png` is in the dog family, but it needs a new ear form (soft
  hanging ears), a feathered tail, and one golden color with no cap. The color is a change of
  `SPECIES_COLORS.dog` and of design 4.5, and the lights of the stage were measured on the dogs.
  S1 leaves the dog for S2.
- **The previews.** `pnpm sculpt <species> --render <dir>` writes the front, the right side, and
  the back on a chair, as the three-view references. The colors of Blender are darker than the
  app. Judge the look in the app.
