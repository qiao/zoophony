# The thin hanging tail of the dog cellist

Date 2026-10-17. Round `dog-tail`, on `main`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-17-dog-tail.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-dog-tail.md). The probes, the configs of the
variants, and the logs are in [`2026-10-17-dog-tail/`](2026-10-17-dog-tail/). Each command runs
from the root of the tree.

## 1. The cause

The tail of the dog mesh is a wedge: 0.18 units high at its root, where it joins the back of the
haunch, and 0.33 units long. Its root bone already goes 53° below the level in the source, so the
hanging tail of the round `dog-arms` moved the root little, and the wedge hung behind the chair as
thick as a thigh.

## 2. The variants

`cfg/` holds the changes of each variant against the config of `70d10fb9`. `rig/` holds the log
lines of each rig. `tail-dump.ts` and `tailapp.py` measure the tail in the bind pose of the player,
and `tail-measures-variants.txt` holds the output for each variant. `pose-depth-tail-*.txt` sweeps
the tail bones against the body and the cello, and `fold-probe-*.txt` counts the folds.

The column "Contacts in the app" covers the seat, the back posts, the rail, the body, and the

edge of the trunk part, as in `docs/research/2026-10-17-dog-arms/tailwhere.py`. The sweep of the
closed body gives 0.0 cm for each variant.

## 3. The final tail

The user chose V1. `rig-check-blend.txt` and `rig-check-blend2.txt` compare the blend of the root
(`pnpm rig-mesh dog --check --set`). The blend from 0.3 to 0.8 gives the smallest gap of the
haunch, 0.52 cm, with the chair 0.8 cm deeper than before. The blend of V1, from 0.1 to 0.7, moved
the root back, and the chair grew 2.2 cm. `tail-measures-final.txt`,
`pose-depth-tail-final.txt`, and `fold-probe-final.txt` measure the final rig: no contact, the
lowest point 14.5 cm above the floor, and no tail face folds.
