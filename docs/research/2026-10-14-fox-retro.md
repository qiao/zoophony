# The retro tools of the fox mesh round (2026-10-14)

The round of the fox mesh merged on 2026-10-14 ([`2026-10-14-fox-rig.md`](2026-10-14-fox-rig.md)).
It lost about 65 minutes in loops of hand probes. Its agent named the causes, and the manager
chose five items. This report gives the items, the time of each new tool, the break command of
each new pure function, and one fault of `pnpm pose-depth` that the new tools found. The logs are
in [`2026-10-14-fox-retro/`](2026-10-14-fox-retro/).

## 1. The items

| 15 min | Variants of the rig config by hand scripts | 3 | `pnpm rig-mesh <animal> --check --set <path>=<value>` |
| 8 min | A tail tip "down behind the chair" that the seat does not permit | 4 | The seat and the floor lines of `--check` |
| 12 min | A commit checked only with the lint and the typecheck | 5 | A rule in "Commits" of `AGENTS.md` |

The new tools replace the probes of the fox round in
[`2026-10-14-fox-rig/`](2026-10-14-fox-rig/). `fold-faces.ts` became `--faces`. `fold-set.ts`
and the runs of `pnpm pose-depth --set` and `pnpm hold-reach --set` became `pnpm hold-fit`.
`run-variant.sh` and `seat-tail-variants.sh` became `--check --set`.

## 3. A fault of `pnpm pose-depth`

The first run of `hold-fit` gave a play depth of 1.9 cm for the red fox: the right forearm in the
body at 162.55 s of `bach-bwv1050` (`hold-fit-all.txt`). The final sweep of the fox round gave 0
for each play pair of the red fox
([`pose-depth-final.txt`](2026-10-14-fox-rig/pose-depth-final.txt)). `pnpm pose-depth violinist
--demo bach-bwv1050` on `main` also gave 0 for the red fox `violinist 4`
(`pose-depth-1050-before.txt`).

The cause was in `sweepDemos()` of `tools/pose-depth.ts`. It took the sample sets of a demo one
time and gave them to each player. The play set of a role is a generator (`playSamples()` of
`pose/test-sweep-roles.ts`), so the first player used it up, and each later player got no play
sample. The violinist sweep takes the first seated rabbit first, so the play depths of the red
fox and of each soloist were 0. Commit `9fd0100d` gives each player its own sets, as
`describeRoleClearance()` of `pose/test-role-clearance.ts` does. After it, `pnpm pose-depth
violinist --demo bach-bwv1050` gives the depth of 1.9 cm for the red fox
(`pose-depth-1050-after.txt`).

The decision row of [`2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md`](../decisions/2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md) on the sweep
of the red fox says that the play has no depth. That claim came from the fault. The hold of the
red fox does not change in this round (the brief keeps each body, pose, and hold).

## 4. Decisions of the round

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| `hold-fit` has no role table of its own. It takes each role of `FOLD_ROLES`, and finds the role of `SWEEP_ROLES` whose players in a scene are bodies of the role (`fitSweep()`). It sweeps only these bodies | The violinist sweep holds the rabbits and the red foxes. A new role of both tables, such as the flutist of round 3, then needs no entry |
| The depth cell of a sample set gives the pair with the largest depth less its limit | A pair over its limit always shows. With one limit for each pair, it is the deepest pair |
| A mark `OVER` follows play folds over 1% of the samples | The goal of the play folds of section 7 of `docs/briefs/studio-body.md` |
| The default demos of `hold-fit` are `FIT_DEMOS`, and `--demo all` takes all demos | One variant over the 14 demos takes 173 s (section 2) |
| The seat and the floor lines are in `tools/rig-mesh-check.ts`, from the seated body and the standing body of the export. `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py` does not change | The lines are in the meters of the app, a test covers them, and the rig keeps its bytes |
| `--check` prints the SHA-256 of the rig | The check of the bytes of a rig then needs no write of the GLB file |
| A variant of `--check --set` that fails gives its message in its row, and the next variant runs. The exit code is then 1 | One bad variant does not stop a long run |
| With `--preview`, each variant of `--set` writes into `<dir>/base`, `<dir>/variant-1`, and so on | The images of two variants do not write over each other |
| `pnpm fold-probe --json <file>` writes the counts for the table of `--check` | `runTool()` of `tools/glb-swap.ts` gives the output of the child to the terminal. The JSON file gives the counts with no parse of the text |
