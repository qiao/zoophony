# The measures of the fox mesh of the user (2026-10-14)

On 2026-10-14, the user said: "now replace the fox by @~/Downloads/fox.glb". The user then chose
"All foxes, violins first": the red violin foxes (`fox-red`) get the mesh first, by the route of
the rabbit, and then the flute foxes (`fox-tan`) get the same mesh in tan colors. This first round
measures the mesh, writes the first rig config `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json`, and changes no species.

The folder [`2026-10-14-fox-mesh/`](2026-10-14-fox-mesh/) holds each probe and each log of this
report. All lengths in units (u) are in the units of the studio: 1 u from the soles to the ear
tips, the soles at y = 0, +z forward, and +x the left side of the fox. All meters are at the scale
of the first config, 0.9296 m for each unit, unless a row gives another scale.

## The pipeline

`pnpm rig-mesh fox --check` reads the mesh with no change of a tool. The check rigs into a
temporary folder and writes no file into the repository.

```text
 assets/models/fox/fox.glb (mesh, texture, no skeleton)
      | sections.py, arm_axis.py, facts.py, eyes.py, colors.py (Blender, read only)
      v
 joints, rules, colors --> tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json (armFactor 1, check.thin)
      | pnpm rig-mesh fox --check [--preview <dir>]
      v
 first line: shoulder joint, reach, factor --> triangles --> stops above 3,100
      | the same check with the budget at 3,200 in a temporary edit
      v
 module size, H, seat, chair, standing body, thin faces (rig-mesh-check-budget.log)
```

The tail hangs down behind the legs, and it touches the backs of the thighs at y 0.25. The studio
format has one `tail` bone, and the app retarget moves it as the bone `tail1` of the player
(`STUDIO_TO_BODY` of `src/models/studio-rig.ts`). The player has two tail bones, `tail1` and
`tail2`, with an idle sway about the vertical axis (`src/pose/pose-at.ts`). The first config puts
each tail vertex on the one tail bone with the rule of the tail ball: a ball of radius 0.24 u about
(0, 0.2, -0.17), behind the plane z = -0.07. `preview-bones-standing.webp` shows the whole tail on
one bone.

One bone and a weight rule are not enough. The bind pose does not turn the tail, so in the seated
bind the tail goes down through the seat. The chair of the rig takes the lowest point of the body
over the seat, and the tail puts it at 0.0623 u (0.049 m in the app,
`rig-mesh-check-budget.log`). With a chair that only reaches back to z -0.05, out of the reach of
the tail, the seat top is 0.2249 u at the back of the haunch (`seat-probe.log`: the
config with `chair.back` -0.05, rigged by `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`).

The chair of the app is open below its top rail: the rail is 0.1 m high at the top of a back of
0.5 m above the seat, and the back posts are 0.42 m apart at their outer faces (`CHAIR` and
`chairBack()` of `src/models/props.ts`). A tail of 0.19 m width goes through the opening.

right, with 49 gloss faces with the nose). The budget of a studio body is 3,100
(`tools/studio-budgets.ts`), so `pnpm rig-mesh fox --check` stops after the triangle line
(`rig-mesh-check-preview.log`). With a full left fan of 12 faces, the rig has 3,135 triangles.

`scene-triangles.ts` counts the scene of `perf-60.mid` (`scene-triangles.txt`): 166,806 of the
limit of 200,000, with 6 red foxes (violins) and 3 tan foxes (flutes). The body of a parametric
fox has 2,372 triangles: the 2,824 of the red fox violinist less the violin of 452 triangles. The
violin is the rabbit violinist (3,552) less the rabbit body of 3,100 triangles.

The largest colors of the palette are four black browns (#190c05, #1d0e05, #221106, #130a06, 970
faces), six oranges (#ef862c, #ea8229, #e47f27, #f28c31, #dd7a25, #d57523, 1,317 faces), and two
cream whites (#fef7de, #fdf3d8, 348 faces). The rule of 2026-10-14 skips the dark samples of a face
that is not dark, so a gap of the atlas does not darken a face. The 101 faces at the edge of the
black fur take their orange or white samples, so the edge of each black part follows whole faces.

The tan variant can come from a color map of the same faces. The texture has no tan, so a second
rig with other color rules would need the same map. The map moves the Oklab color of each face by
the step from the color of `fox-red` to the color of `fox-tan` of `src/models/colors.ts` (the fur,
the belly, and the accent), blended by the nearness of the face to each red color. The hue step of
the fur shrinks with the chroma of the face, so no pale face turns cool (8 faces turned cool with
no such scale). The eyes and the nose keep their colors.

| Class | Faces | Mean color of the mesh | Mean tan color |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fur | 1,513 | #e78531 | #c3945e |
| Belly | 555 | #faeed2 | #ead3a7 |
| Accent | 968 | #1c0e06 | #654523 |

`colors-red-tan.webp` shows the red faces (top) and the tan faces (bottom). The tan fox has brown
feet, lower arms, and ear backs, as the accent of `fox-tan` on the ears and the feet. The white
tail tip shows less against the tan fur.

The plan of the tan variant: one rig and one GLB file. The packed mesh of a studio body holds a
palette, so the export can write a second palette of the same 48 colors with the map, and the
species `fox-tan` selects it. The cost is the map, a field of the format, a field of the species,
and a test, with less than 1 KB more in the module. A second module of the same mesh adds about
26.6 KB after gzip to the first download.

## The flute hold of a studio body

The retarget drives the arms of a studio body by the joints of the pose of the player, so it needs
no change for a flute (`poseStudioBones()` of `src/models/studio-rig.ts`). The other parts are new:

- The holds: the flute hold is in head heights of the parametric fox (H 0.380 m). The fox mesh has
  H 0.194 m at the shoulder landmark, so each hold place moves. A flute player with a studio body
  needs a hold of its own for the species, as `SPECIES_HOLDS` of `src/pose/bowed-rig.ts` gives the
  violin hold of the rabbit, with the lip plate at the mouth under the long muzzle.
- The size: the flute of the app has one size. The arms of the mesh reach 0.274 m with no factor,
  so the arm factor that the user chooses for the violin also sets the reach of the flute. A
  check of the reach, as `pnpm hold-reach` does for a violin, is new.
- The sweep: a role `flutist` in `SWEEP_ROLES` of `src/pose/test-sweep-roles.ts` (the arms, the
  paws, and the head against the body and the flute), its shard files (paused by the pattern of
  `PAUSED_TESTS`), an entry in `FOLD_ROLES` of `tools/fold-probe.ts`, and an entry in
  `ROLE_VIEWS` of `tools/close-views.ts`.

The rabbit violinist took five agent rounds. With the tools of the rabbit, the flute takes about
three rounds: the role entries and the sweep, the fit of the hold with the folds, and a review
with its fix.
