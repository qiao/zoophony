## 1. The question

The user said "the cat's arm has lumps" on 2026-10-27, with an image of the viola cat in the play
from its left front (`close-views play --at cat --time 30 --azimuth -72 --elevation 28 --zoom
3.5` on `boulanger-matin`). Two faults of the image stayed after the round `sixth-cat-wall`: a
dark crease at the front of the left arm root, and face 1594 at the front of the left armpit,
which folds in each play sample. The target: a round front of each arm root with no dark crease,
and no new folded face against `95a99a39d` (the main before the wall round).

## 2. The measures

- `probes/root-crease.ts` takes the edges of the skin in front of the root of an upper arm (within
  0.10 m of its line, from -0.1 to 0.75 of its length, within 100° of the front) in the bind and
  in 11 samples. A valley is the angle between the normals of the two faces of an edge, where the
  surface turns in. The crease sum adds the length in cm times the valley over 20°. The deepening
  is the valley of the sample less the valley of the bind: it leaves out the sharp armpit line,
  which the bind already has.
- `arm-rings.ts` of `2026-10-28-cat-wall/probes/` gives the area radius of cuts at a right angle to
  the upper arm (`u0.45`: 0.45 of the length from the shoulder joint).
- `probes/vert-info.ts` gives the place and the weights of a vertex, and `root-weights.ts` of the
  wall round the weights around the root (`root-weights-L.txt`).

## 3. The cause

The front of the left root at 0.4 to 0.5 of the upper arm hangs on the trunk: vertex 1574 has
spine1 0.61, chest 0.25, and the arm segments 0.14, and vertex 1608 has spine1 0.96. These are
the rule weights of the trunk, which the socket ring and the Smooth of `step_paint()` give the
first rings of the tube. In the play, the upper arm turns 33.7° from the bind and the clavicle 20°
(`raise-fit.txt`). The root front stays with the chest, and the arm moves over it, so the skin
between them folds into a valley: the dark crease of the image. Face 1594 (vertices 895, 894,
896) lies in this valley: its corner 895 hangs 0.77 on the clavicle, which turns forward, and its
corner 894 0.72 on spine1, which stays. At rest, the upper arm hangs 75.6° down from the bind
(`raise-fit-rest.txt`), past the same root front, which folds into a notch: the deepening of the
rest is 3,643 cm·°, the largest of all samples, and 5 faces there fold in each rest sample.
