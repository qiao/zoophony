## 1. The question

The user said on 2026-10-28: "also the hippo's arm is messed up. fix it", with a close image of
the bow arm from the right (`user-56.webp`). The manager read a bulge of the right forearm, and a

## 2. The angle and the fault

The stage camera of `cams56.json` (`probes/cams56.py`, `node probes/place.ts`) shows the same frame
at 30 s of boulanger-matin: the chair behind the paw, the floor, and the paw on the bow. It shows a
pale cuff around the bow wrist (`u56-main-round.webp`, left). The gallery views show the same band
as a black ring from the right, az150, and the back, at the frog, the tip, the crossing, and in the
moves (`sheet-e20-*.webp`, top rows). The colors probe shows no dark color there, and the normals
probe shows a ring of faces that face back toward the elbow (`probe-wrist.webp`).

The fold probe does not see the ring: the faces of the band turn at most 26° in the play
(`spike-main.txt`). The form of the band is the fault:

- **The source.** A section of `hippo-arms.glb` in the plane of the wrist turn shows a slit
  about 0.04 units deep on the inner side of the wrist (`section-wrist.webp`: the user mesh,
  `main`, and the round, in the plane of the turn and at 90° to it, `probes/section.py`,
  `probes/plot_sec.py`). The mesh of the user has no slit there.
- **The bind.** 47.6% (right) and 55.3% (left) of the area of the bridge faces between the last
  ring of the tube and the paw face back toward the elbow (`probes/band-back.ts`, `band-back.txt`).
- **The cause.** The last ring stands at the wrist joint at a right angle to the line halfway
  between the forearm and the paw. The wrist cut stood at a right angle to the paw, 0.2 of the
  paw from the joint. The paw turns 35° from the forearm, and the wrist is about 0.06 units
  thick, so on the inner side of the turn the cut lay 0.007 units behind the ring. The bridge
  then went back toward the elbow (the trap of a thick arm in `rig-blender.md`).

`pnpm close-views bassist --base ac9f9cb7f` (the end of the rebuild of 2026-10-25) shows the ring
too, so the rebuild of the arms made it. The bow hold of 2026-10-26 (`3b3824971`) and the key
light of 2026-10-27 changed the pose and the light, but the slit is older
(`regress-ac9f-right.webp`).

## 3. The fixes

| Step | Try | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Wrist cut | parallel to the last ring, at 0.15, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3 of the paw | the slit goes. 0.15, 0.2, and 0.3 leave a thin face of a paw or a socket that folds in each play sample (`rig-variants.txt`) |
| Wrist cut | 0.25 alone | a pale spike under the bow wrist at the tip (`spike-tip.webp`) |
| Smooth | 4 rounds of 0.5 on the last ring and the cut loop | with the parallel cut, no slit and no spike. With the old cut, a step stays on the paw top (`source-wrists.webp`) |
| Elbow pole | 8 poles of the bow elbow | the paw swing of the gate moves only from 25 to 31 (`pole-scan.txt`) |

The round keeps the parallel cut at 0.25 and the smooth (`cut_planes()` and
`step_smooth_wrist()` of `tools/blender/hippo_arms.py`). The replay of the script also left out
the belly step of the cat, which stopped it with a KeyError, and it gives the files byte for byte.

## 6. The result

The bow wrist of the hippo is an even band from the forearm to the paw in each view, with no
lip, no slit, and no spike, and the left wrist with it. The arm gate, the sweep, and the poses of
the other players do not change.

Open: in the play, the paw swings 25° to 31° off the forearm (the gate, p95), so the heel of the
paw and the end of the forearm still show as a lump under the bow paw from the right and from az120
(`u56-main-round.webp`). The pole scan does not change the swing (`pole-scan.txt`). The replays of
`fox_arms.py` and `deer_arms.py` stop at the belly step of the cat, as the replay of the hippo did.
