# The fox look: a feature checklist of `fox-views.png`

Date: 2026-10-08. Branch: `worktree-agent-a761a560a6e0e78b2`, from `main` at `7d6552e` (after
stage P1 of the proportions and the rabbit sculpt).

## 1. Summary

The red fox of stage P1 had the skeleton of `fox-views.png`, but it did not look like the same
character: a dark brown fur, round button eyes, thin ears, a narrow bib, long dark gloves, and
long dark lower legs. This stage changes only the forms and the colors of the fox sculpts. No
bone moves, and the snapshot hash of the skeleton stays.

- **Colors.** The fur is `#ac6236`, the white `#eee2e0`, and the dark brown `#603a2a`.
- **Face.** Large white eyes with a brown iris, a lid, and a light. A flat crown, high cheek
  tufts, a V lower face, and a white cheek patch that goes down the throat.
- **Ears.** Large ears with a base that runs down the side of the head, dark tips, and dark
  backs.
- **Body.** A pointed bib, short gloves, and short socks.
- **Budget.** The red fox has 2,526 triangles in each stance (limit 2,600). The tan fox has
  2,378.
- **Not matched.** The value of the fur on the views page (113 against 160), the pose of the
  views page, the place of the tail, and the width of the torso. Section 4 gives the numbers.

## 2. Method

All lengths are in H, the head height of the fox (0.327 m). The sheet has 737 px for each meter:
its chair seat is 258 px above the floor in the front view, and the seat is 0.35 m. Thus H is 241
px of the sheet. The crown of the side view is 0.51 H above the head center that this scale
gives, and the skeleton puts it 0.5 H above. A height or a depth in the table is from the head
center, a negative z is in front of it, and a sample is the mean of a box of 9 × 9 px.

```text
 fox-views.png ----------------------------+
   crops, grids, color samples             |
   (sample.py, grid.py)                    v
                                    +-------------+   measures in H   +-------------------+
                                    |  checklist  |------------------>| fox_red.py        |
                                    +-------------+                   | canine.py         |
                                           ^                          +---------+---------+
                                           |                                    | pnpm sculpt
   overlay at 737 px/m                     |                                    v
   (fox_preview.py, compare.py, zoom.py) <-+---- Blender preview <--- src/models/sculpts/
   views page and default view           <-+---- the app (capture.mjs)      fox-*.ts
```

The scripts are in [`scripts/fox-look/`](scripts/fox-look/). `fox_preview.py` renders the
sculpt in orthographic views at the scale of the sheet, so that `compare.py` and `zoom.py` can
lay the sheet over the preview. `capture.mjs` takes the views page at 3 times its size and the
default view after 6 s of play.

## 4. What does not match, and why

### 4.1 The value of the fur on the views page

The views page uses `NeutralToneMapping` with an exposure of 0.8 and the studio light
`VIEW_LIGHT` (design 7.1). The default view uses AgX. The same base color gives these values:

| Fur | Views page, lit | Default view, lit head | Default view, side |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#915125` (before) | 94 | 131 | 88 |
| `#a65e36` | 106 | 137 | 107 |
| `#ac6236` (this stage) | 113 | 143 | 101 to 112 |
| `#b26638` | 115 | 145 | 115 |
| `#b5602d` | 113 | 152 | 108 |
| sheet / `orchestra-latest.png` | 160 | 104 to 153 | 75 to 90 |

In linear values, the fur of the sheet has 0.65 of the luminance of its white. Our fur has 0.23 of
the luminance of our white (0.14 before this stage). AgX compresses this ratio in the default view,
and the neutral tone mapping of the views page keeps it. With the fur `#915125` and an exposure of
1.5 on the views page, the fur reached only 116, and the white clipped at 230. Thus no base color
gives 160 on the views page and keeps the default view in the range of `orchestra-latest.png`. A
change of the light or the tone mapping of the views page (not a fox file) can close the gap. With
the fur `#915125`, AgX, and an exposure of 1.0 on the views page, the fur gave 118 with a
saturation of 0.55 and the white 185.

### 4.2 The pose of the views page

The views page takes the playing pose of the Beethoven 9 demo (`src/gallery/view-poses.ts`). In this
pose, the violin is in front of the chest, below the chin, and the head bends down to it. In the
sheet, the violin is on the left shoulder under the chin, the left hand holds the scroll at the
height of the head, and the head is up. The pose module owns this pose, so this stage does not
change it.

### 4.3 The tail

The tail stays behind the chair, at most 0.333 m behind the seat center (decision of stage P1).
A brush on the seat (0.58 m high) moved the harp row of Boulanger and gave the flute solos
cameras above 40°.

### 4.4 The torso

The rest pose test (5.2) keeps the arms 0.01 H from the torso. With the new head forms, a pear
0.84 wide left 0.006 H between the torso and the right upper arm of the standing violin
soloist. A pear 0.8 wide leaves 0.011 H. Thus the torso is a little narrower than the sheet.

## 6. What the dog family can reuse

The wolf and the dog of stage P2 use `canine.py`. Each new measure has a default that keeps the
wolf sculpt the same to the byte.

- **Eyes with a white:** `eye_offset`, `iris`, `pupil`, `eye_light`, `lid`, `eye_depth`. The
  parts point their poles to the front, so a white of 12 sides and 2 rings has a smooth outline
  for 24 triangles.
- **Ears:** `ear_heights` (a base down the side of the head), `ear_turn`, `ear_ahead`, and
  `ear_back_dark`. The dog of `dog-views.png` needs a new ear form (soft hanging ears).
- **Head:** `crown` (a flat top) and `cheek_chin` (a V lower face).
- **Face colors:** `cheek_back`, a white cheek patch with a border that leans back.
- **Bib:** `bib_v`, a pointed bib on the front only.
- **Legs:** `sock` and `shin_rings`. **Tail:** `tail_triangles`.
- **Budget:** the cut of a color border adds about 2 triangles for each triangle that it crosses.
  Count the trunk after the cuts.
- **Clearance:** the arm gaps of the rest pose read the trunk mesh. Run
  `src/pose/rest-clearance.test.ts` after each change of the head or the torso.
- **Tools:** `scripts/fox-look/` renders a sculpt at the scale of a sheet and lays the sheet over
  it. Change the scale and the floor rows for another sheet.
