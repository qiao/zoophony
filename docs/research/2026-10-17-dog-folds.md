# The corrective shapes of the dog cellist

Date 2026-10-17. Round `dog-folds`, on `main` from `914da25c`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-17-dog-folds.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-dog-folds.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-17-dog-folds/`](2026-10-17-dog-folds/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The probes

- `keys-dump.ts <out.json> [--step <s>] [--set <changes>]` writes the arm turns of each sample of
  `pnpm fold-probe cellist` in the axes of the studio, with the folded faces and the weights.
- `keys-read.ts <keys.json> <faces>` gives the mean turns of the samples in which the faces fold,
  the folds by the angles from these turns, and with `--fade` the reach of a shape.
- `keys-glb.ts` runs `keys-dump.ts` with the body of another rig (`withGlbBody()`).
- `rig-var.sh <name> <raise.json> [<path>=<json>]...` rigs a variant of the config with Blender
  and writes its keys and the face table of `pnpm fold-probe --glb`. `rig-only.sh` writes only the
  rig log, and `rig-hold.sh` adds a hold change. `rules.txt` holds the log lines and the fold rows
  of each trial.
- `glb-run.ts <rig.glb> <probe.ts>` runs a probe of the folder with the body of another rig, such
  as `weight-try.ts`, which gives some vertices one skin in memory and counts the folds.
- `ear.ts <demo> [<changes>]...` gives the smallest gap of the head part to the closed cello in
  each sample set, with the nearest triangle and the count of the samples below 0.5 cm.
- `cello-sets.sh` turns a place of the cello into hold changes (`cello-place.ts` of
  `2026-10-17-dog-mesh/`). `reach.ts` of `2026-10-17-dog-rest/` gives the reached hair.

## 3. The rules

`rules.txt` holds the 37 trials. The rig log names the faces that turn over in the pose of each
rule (`86250709`), and this showed which rule can relax its faces:

- The bent right elbow (`bone: forearm`, fade 5° to 10°) relaxes face 316 in 2 rounds.
- The raised left arm of the play relaxes faces 1733 and 1169 in 1 round with `below` 0. With
  `below` 0.3, the relaxing ran 60 rounds, moved forearm vertices up to 45 mm, and face 1800 of the
  paw folded in 5.1% of the play samples (`v1`).
- In the pose of the left rest with the arm turns alone, only face 1757 turns over. Face 1803 also
  needs the turn of the paw, so `turns.hand` came to the rules (`7de1fea3`). With it and one ring of
  neighbors (`grow` 1, `below` 0), both relax in 4 rounds (`h2`). With no ring, three faces of the
  paw stayed turned over (`h4`).
- A shape at the mean turns of the lift folds of faces 1803, 1800, and 1756 has its weight in most
  play samples too, because the left arm of the lift and of the play are near. It lowered the lift
  folds from 30.9% to 28.1% (`v12`, `v15`).
- Face 331 did not relax with any `below` from -0.3 to 0.7, with rings, or with the turn of the paw
  (`q1` to `q10`). In memory, the mean skin of its three corners removed its folds and changed no
  play fold (`weight-try.txt`). `weights.rigid` does this in the rig
  (`0ae975f4`), and the lift folds fall to 15.9% (`v18`).
- The right shoulder rule (faces 171, 172) never relaxed: face 172 stays turned over after 60
  rounds with each `below` and `grow` (`r1a` to `r1d`), and its moves fold face 235 of the right
  forearm (`v6`, `v14`). A wider sliver rule folded each sample (`s1`, `s3`).
