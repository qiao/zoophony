# The shorter arms with the full violins, and the rest of the red fox (2026-10-14)

This report gives the measures and the probes of the branch `arms-rest`. The decisions are in
[`2026-10-14-arms-rest.md`](../decisions/2026-10-14-arms-rest.md). The probes, the logs, and the
images are in [`2026-10-14-arms-rest/`](2026-10-14-arms-rest/).

## The requests of the user

On 2026-10-14, the user looked at the app and said:

1. "change the rest pose of the fox to be like this", with a screenshot of two cat viola players
   at rest: the viola upright on the lap in the middle between the knees, the scroll up, the left
   paw at the neck, and the bow across the lap, about level, in the right paw, with the tip to the
   left.
2. "also, reduce the arm length of the fox and rabbit without changing the violin size"
