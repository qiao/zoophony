# The round shoulders of the rhino

Date 2026-10-22. Round `rhino-arm-3`, base `502f7a75`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-22-rhino-arm-3.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-rhino-arm-3.md). The probes and their
outputs are in [`2026-10-22-rhino-arm-3/`](2026-10-22-rhino-arm-3/). Each command runs from the
root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user reported the rhino arms four times on 2026-10-22, the last time "the rhino's arm is
deformed again", then "you take screenshot for the rhino". The goal: each arm starts at a round
shoulder that blends into the trunk and the back, with no gash, slot, or color patch at full size
from the stage.

## 2. The defects of `502f7a75`

`pnpm close-views play <dir> --demo brahms-2 --time 283 --at rhino --azimuth <az> --elevation
20|40` and the probes `colors` and `noshadow` of `pnpm close-views tubist` show three defects.
az-90 shows the left side.

| Defect | Cause | State |

| D2: no shoulder; each tube leaves the side of the chest at mid height, under a flat trunk | The tube ends in a dome inside the trunk, and the trunk keeps the flat side of the source above it. | fixed |
| D3: dark gray and light beige patches with jagged edges on the fill | Each fill face copies the UVs of one trunk face, and its neighbors copy faces of other colors. | fixed |

## 3. The edit

`rhino_shoulders.py` writes `assets/models/rhino/rhino-shoulders.glb` from the file of the user,
which stays byte for byte. For each side, it removes the arm and the trunk within 0.16 u of the
shoulder joint, below 0.665 u. It fills the hole with a grid of 0.022 u, and fairs the fill and
two rings of the trunk around it. The fill then takes the smooth union of the faired trunk and a
round cone from the ball down to the side of the trunk. The arm starts at a ball of 0.08 u around
the shoulder joint, and its rings take the smooth max of the ball and of the source arm. A turn of
the upper arm turns the ball around its middle, so the shoulder stays in its place in each pose.
Each new face takes one fur texel of a smooth color: the harmonic mean of the trunk colors around
the fill, and the smoothed nearest source colors on the arm.
