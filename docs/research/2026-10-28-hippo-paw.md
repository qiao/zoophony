Date 2026-10-28. Round `sixth-hippo-paw`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-28-hippo-paw.md`](../decisions/2026-10-28-hippo-paw.md). The probes, their outputs, and

## 1. The question

The user said on 2026-10-28: "also the hippo's arm is messed up. fix it", with a close image of
the bow arm from the right (`user-56.webp`). After the wrist round
([`2026-10-28-hippo-arm.md`](2026-10-28-hippo-arm.md)), a lump stayed under the bow paw from the
right and from az120. That round read the paw swing of the arm gate (25° to 31°, p95) as a bent
wrist, and the brief asked for a bow hold with a straighter wrist.

## 2. The cause

`probes/paw-swing.ts` splits the turn of the hand of the player off its forearm, before the
retarget, into the bend toward the palm and the turn in the plane of the palm, and gives the turn
of `PAW_TURN` (`swing-main.txt`):

| Value (play, 12,844 samples) | p5 | p50 | p95 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hand off the forearm, in the plane of the palm | 27.6° | 45.6° | 71.3° | 109.8° |
| Hand off the forearm, toward the palm | -0.8° | -0.8° | -0.8° | 10.3° |
| Turn of the retarget (`PAW_TURN`) | 27.6° | 45.6° | 70.0° | 70.0° |
| Turn in the plane that stays after it | 0° | 0° | 1.3° | 39.8° |
| Paw swing of the arm gate | 25.0° | 25.0° | 26.3° | 65.7° |

- The roll of the hand (`rollToward()`) keeps the forearm in the plane of the palm, and the
  retarget takes away the turn in that plane. So the seen paw lies on the line of the forearm, and
  a grip place or roll changes only the hand of the player, which the viewer does not see.
- The swing of 25° is the bind. `probes/bind-bends.ts` (`bind-bends.txt`): the seated bind bends
  each paw of each studio body 24° to 26° off its forearm, in the plane of the palm (`bindArms`
  of the rig configs). On the hippo, the paw of the mesh also lies 25° to 30° off the forearm
  (`probes/mesh-paw.ts`, `mesh-paw.txt`), and the joints of the source give 24.5°.
- So the retarget bent the skin of the thick wrist 25° off its form. `probes/wrist-strain.ts`
  gives the length of each edge of the wrist faces over its bind length: up to 1.94 times
  (p95), and down to 0.72 times on the other side (`strain-main.txt`). That is the lump.

## 3. The fix

The retarget now keeps a share of the bind bend of the right paw (`StudioArm.bindBend`,
`pawKeepR` of the pose), and the hippo hold keeps all of it (`HIPPO_HOLD.pawKeepR` 1). The pose
of the player does not change, so the grip of the bow stays (`bowGrip`).

The scan of the share gives these values (`keep-scan.txt`, `probes/stick-grip.ts`,
`probes/wrist-strain.ts`, 2 s steps). `turn-scan.webp` (`probes/turn-sheet.py`) shows an added
turn of -30°, 0°, 15°, and 30°, a share of -1.2 to 1.2, at 30 s:

| Share | Wrist edges, longest p95 | Wrist edges, shortest p5 | Stick gap p50 / max | Stick depth min |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 (`main`) | 1.94 | 0.72 | 1.50 / 1.75 cm | 1.30 cm |
| 0.6 | 1.54 | 0.76 | 1.21 / 1.79 cm | 1.28 cm |
| 1 (round) | 1.30 | 0.78 | 1.08 / 2.43 cm | 1.40 cm |
| 1.4 | 1.23 | 0.81 | 0.86 / 3.15 cm | 1.56 cm |

The share 1 keeps the paw of the bind, and the stick stays deep in the closed paw. With 1.4, the
stick runs up to 3.2 cm from the middle of the paw.

A refit of the grip place (`probes/stick-line.ts`) gave a largest gap of 2.1 cm, but larger
middle gaps. The forearm turns about 45° against the bow over a stroke, and the paw slides on the
stick with it. The grip turn changes only the hand of the player.

## 7. Open

- The stick runs up to 2.4 cm from the middle of the paw in the lowering (1.75 cm on `main`),
  because the paw slides on the stick over a stroke. A fixed grip needs an own turn of the bow
  paw, and then the wrist bends about ±22° around the bind over a stroke.
- In about 5% of the play, the hand turns more than 70° in the plane of the palm, past the limit
  of `PAW_TURN`, and the swing reaches 41.6°.
