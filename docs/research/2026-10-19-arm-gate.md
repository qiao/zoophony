# The arm gate

Date 2026-10-19. Round `arm-gate`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-19-arm-gate.md`](../decisions/2026-10-19-arm-gate.md). The probes and their outputs are in
[`2026-10-19-arm-gate/`](2026-10-19-arm-gate/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

Five rounds in two days met their own arm measure and shipped or nearly shipped a twisted arm:
the paw flips of the hippo bow arm, a twist band at the hippo shoulder, a paw turned about 170° on
the elephant forearm, flat shares that left out the twist bone, and the paw turned 133° to 139° of
the elephant pose phase 2. The measures existed in four tools, and no round ran all of them. The
gate `pnpm arm-check` runs all of them on each arm with one table of limits and a base compare.
It must separate the known faults from the arms that look good.

## 2. The measures

For each arm, over the rest, play, and lift samples of `pnpm fold-probe`, the gate reads eight
values (`measureArm()` of `tools/arm-check.ts`). The value of the gate is the worst sample set.

| Measure | Value | Limit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| folds | p95 of the folded faces of the arm | 8 faces | good arms up to 6 (red fox) and 8 (tan fox) in the rest and the lift |
| twist | p95 of the largest roll step from the chest along the upper arm | 55° | good upper arms up to 51° (rabbit bow arm), the hippo band 60° |
| forearm twist | p95 of the roll steps from the elbow ring to the wrist ring | 80° | good forearms up to 77° (rabbit left arm, lift) |
| flat | p5 of the narrow axis of the flattest of five rings, as a share of the bind | 0.55 | good arms 0.56 and more (red fox, polar bear), the hippo band 0.53 |
| paw twist | p95 of the twist of the paw on its forearm from the bind | 170° | good paws up to 168° (red fox left paw) |
| paw swing | p95 of the bend of the paw with no twist | 70° | anatomy: a wrist bends about 70°, good paws up to 64° |
| paw jumps | share of the play samples whose paw twist changes by more than 90° in one step | 0.5% | good paws have none |
| stretch | p95 of the largest edge growth of the forearm and paw faces, in upper arm lengths | 0.35 | good arms up to 0.32 (dog cellist) |

`steps.ts` gave the values of each ring step and the edge growth of each role on `a3755083`
(`steps-a3755083.txt`). Three choices came from these numbers:

- The forearm twist has its own limit, because a supinated paw twists the forearm skin of a good
  arm: the rabbit left arm reaches 77° from the forearm ring to the wrist ring. A band at the
  shoulder of the hippo reaches 60°.
- The paw twist cannot take the anatomy limit of 80° to 90°. The bind paw of a studio body is no
  neutral wrist: the left paws of the rabbit, the red fox, the dog, and the mouse reach 152° to
  168°. Thus the base compare catches a turned paw, with a margin of 30°.
- The relative edge growth is noisy: short thin faces of good arms grow 4 to 5 times (the rabbit
  bow arm at rest, the red fox). The stretch counts the growth in upper arm lengths, on the faces
  of the forearm and the paw.

it with no base. The phase 2 state also runs against `35a27c5f`, the merge case. Each output is
`state-<hash>.txt`.

Each known fault fails. The phase 2 state fails with no base only on the stretch, by 0.02, so the
base compare is the main guard of a turned paw. The open items of the violist, the bassist, and
the bassoonist are known. The stretch of the right forearm of the mouse oboist (0.60) is a new
finding.

`pnpm arm-check timpanist --base 35a27c5f --views <dir>` in the worktree of `a3c48f55` rendered
the worst paw twist of each arm, a strike, and a rest (`views-a3c48f55.txt`). At the strike, the
paws of phase 2 hold the mallets along the forearms and turn on the wrists.

## 4. The run time

`pnpm arm-check --all` runs four roles at a time, each in a child process. The machine had a load
average of 20 to 30 from other work.

| Run | Time |
|---|---|
| all roles, no base (`a3755083`) | 152 s to 181 s |
| all roles, base `35a27c5f`, cold cache (`time-cold.txt`) | 307 s |
| all roles, base `35a27c5f`, warm cache (`time-warm.txt`) | 155 s |
| one role, base in the cache (`state-a3c48f55-base.txt`) | 52 s |
| `--views` of one role: four frames of two builds each | about 5 minutes |

The warm run is within the aim of 5 minutes, so the merge runs all roles.

## 5. The result

The gate separates each known fault from the good arms. It runs in the merge of each branch that
changes `src/pose/`, `src/models/`, or `tools/blender/`, and in each round that moves an arm.
