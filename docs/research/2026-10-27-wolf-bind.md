Date 2026-10-27. Round `sixth-wolf-bind`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-27-wolf-bind.md`](../decisions/2026-10-27-wolf-bind.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-27-wolf-bind/`](2026-10-27-wolf-bind/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree. "Main" is `c6602974`, with the clarinet rest on the right knee of the round
`sixth-wolf-knee` (`2026-10-27-wolf-knee.md`). `876ba389` is the commit before that rest.

## 1. The question

The rest on the knee keeps both paws on their grips of the play, and the arm gate failed its
margins against `876ba389` with each value inside its limit: the left paw twist 152° (89°), the
left upper arm twist 37 (24), the left paw swing 57 (40), and the right flat share 0.75 (0.82).
The goal: `pnpm arm-check clarinetist --base 876ba389` and `--base main` with no `FAIL`, with the
same rest place, grips, and grasp, and no depth that grows against `main`.

## 2. The causes

`probes/bind-scan.ts` of `2026-10-27-wolf-knee/` gives the paw twist on the forearm and the roll
of the upper arm from the chest in each sample (`bind-scan-*.txt` of both folders):

| Arm | Paw twist rest, play | Upper arm roll rest, play |
|---|---|---|
| L, `main` | -152°, -68° | 98°, 34° |
| R, `main` | 118°, 77° | -118°, -48° |
| L, round | -28°, 41° | 48°, -11° |
| R, round | 32°, -23° | -82°, -3° |

The forearm of the app turns with its elbow hinge, so the bind arms set the zero of both values.
The bind paw is pronated, and the rest pronates the left paw further. The bind elbow bends up, and
at rest each upper arm rolls about 100° from it. The arm segments share the roll, so the skin steps
from the chest along the upper arm (the twist of the gate) and the right shoulder ring goes flat
(3.37 cm against 4.50 cm in the bind, `explain-flat.txt`).
