# The arm roots of the bear, the deer, the dog, and the panda

Date 2026-10-22. Round `arm-roots`, base `e7ef4f3a`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-22-arm-roots.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-arm-roots.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-22-arm-roots/`](2026-10-22-arm-roots/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree. Copy a probe to `scratch/` before you run it. `with-glb.ts` of
[`2026-10-22-bear-armpit/`](2026-10-22-bear-armpit/) runs a probe on a kept rig
(`SPECIES=<species>`). The shell scripts hold the paths of the round.

## 1. The question

The user said: "the bear is not fixed. there's still visible dent there in the arms. Make sure
that you're taking screenshots for both rest pose and playing pose, and from multiple angles.
note that many animals are having this issue now". The goal: no dent, flap, shard, or fin at the
arm root of the bear conductor, the deer harpist, the dog cellist, and the panda trombonist, at
each moment, from six views at the heights 20° and 40°, by a local edit of a copy of the source.

## 2. The tool

`tools/blender/arm_root.py` reads an edit file of `tools/arm-roots/`, welds the source as the rig
does, applies a list of edits, and writes the copy with its SHA-256. The methods are `flip`,
`fair`, `smooth`, `hull`, `push`, and `split` (the TSDoc of the script). `dump.py` gives the
welded indices. `--edits` gives a variant with no new file, and `pnpm rig-mesh <animal> --check
--set source=scratch/glb/<copy>.glb --keep <dir>` rigs it (the copy must lie in the tree).

## 5. The deer and the dog

The deer shards are rig faces 980, 585, 263, and 275: they join the trunk (x 0.08 u, arm weight
0.14) to the arm (x 0.11 u, 0.83 to 0.88) at the back of the arm root, y 0.46 to 0.56. The arm
reaches forward to the harp, and these faces turn down and dark. The deer has no cut, and the
fold probe gives 100% of its samples on the base (`check-deer-D.txt`). A `smooth` of the 8 and
10 vertices of the web (d1) makes the left shard smaller, but it moves them 3.4 cm. The outward
smooth (d2, 1.7 cm) does not change the look (`deer-d1-d2-back.webp`). The dog got no variant in
the time box.
