# The rig of the fox mesh and the red violin foxes (2026-10-14)

On 2026-10-14, the user gave the fox mesh `assets/models/fox/fox.glb` and chose "All foxes, violins
first". The first round measured the mesh ([`2026-10-14-fox-mesh.md`](2026-10-14-fox-mesh.md)).
Then the user chose "1.08x, rabbit reach" for the arms. This second round rigs the mesh and gives
it to the red foxes of the second violins (`fox-red`). The tan flute foxes keep the parametric body.
The decisions are in [`2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md`](../decisions/2026-10-14-mesh-fox.md).

The folder [`2026-10-14-fox-rig/`](2026-10-14-fox-rig/) holds each probe, log, and image of this
report. A length in units (u) is in the units of the studio (1 u from the soles to the ear tips).
The meters are at the scale of the rig, 1.19264 m for each unit.

## The pipeline

```text
 assets/models/fox/fox.glb + tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json (scale, arms, seat, tail, rules)
      | pnpm rig-mesh fox (tools/blender/rig_mesh.py, Blender)
      v
 assets/models/fox/fox.rig.glb: 31 body bones (tail + tail_tip), seated bind, 4 shapes, chair
      | pnpm studio-body fox (ANIMALS: shoulders 0.7454 m, seated, soloist, crease 35)
      v
 src/models/studio/fox.ts --> STUDIO_BODIES['studio-fox'] --> SPECIES['fox-red'].body
      |                                     |
 retarget: tail -> tail1, tail_tip -> tail2  SPECIES_HOLDS['fox-red'].violin (FOX_HOLD)
      v
 pnpm fold-probe fox-violinist, pnpm pose-depth violinist, pnpm hold-reach fox-red,
 pnpm close-views fox-violinist, pnpm body-probe --base main, pnpm snapshot
```

### The seat of a long thigh

The seat of the studio rabbit turns the thigh 57°. The thigh of the fox is long and wide, so with
that seat and the haunch ramp of the first config (0.11 to 0.185 u), the upper half of the thigh
stayed on the hips and stood upright. The new seat turns the thigh 81° onto the seat, 15° below the
level and 8° out (`legturn.ts`, `legturn.txt`), and the shin, the foot, and the toes keep the turn
of the source. Two haunch ramps of the variants of the rig:

| Haunch ramp (u) | Thigh turn | Faces that turn over in the bind after the relax | Seat top over the floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 to 0.27 | 86° | 11 (9 at the hip crease, 2 under the chin) | 0.092 u |
| 0.18 to 0.29 | 86° | 6 | 0.093 u |
| 0.2 to 0.3 | 81° | 3 | 0.098 u |
| 0.16 to 0.27 (kept) | 81° | 2 (under the chin) | 0.106 u |

`seat-tail-variants.sh` rigs each variant with the final config otherwise (`seat-tail-variants.txt`,
the rows `seat-a` to `seat-kept`). A debug print of the rig script in `scratch/` of the worktree
gave the places of the turned faces. The seat top over the floor is the seat top of the log less the
lowest point of the body. `preview-knees.webp` shows the thighs on the seat.

## The hold

The hold of the fox starts as the hold of the rabbit, because the head heights are near (0.249 m
and 0.269 m). `fold-set.ts` counts the folds with changes of the hold in memory, at a play step of
1 s, with the rules of `tools/hold-sets.ts`. `fold-set-final.txt` gives the final hold and its
changes:

| Hold | Play | Lift |
|---|---|---|
| Final: slant -12°, shoulder turn 25°, elbow at the tip (1, -0.85, -1), violin z -0.08 H | 2.3% | 20.3% |
| The bow of the rabbit: slant -5°, turn 30°, elbow (1, -1, -1) | 15.7% | 19.8% |
| Slant -25° | 1.6% | 20.3% |
| Slant -20° | 1.7% | 20.3% |
| Shoulder turn 30° | 12.6% | 19.8% |
| Shoulder turn 20° | 9.0% | 22.8% |
| Elbow at the tip (1, -1, -1) | 3.4% | 20.3% |
| Violin at z -0.02 H | 2.6% | 19.8% |

A larger slant gave fewer folds, but the right forearm then went into the chest at the end of the
lift of the violin. `pnpm pose-depth violinist --demo beethoven-8` with changes of the hold
(`pose-depth-lift-grid.txt`, from the hold with the slant of -25° and the violin at z -0.02 H):

| Slant | Violin z | Right forearm in the body, lift |
|---|---|---|
| -25° | -0.02 H | 2.5 cm |
| -15° | -0.05 H | 1.7 cm |
| -15° | -0.08 H | 1.3 cm |
| -20° | -0.08 H | 1.6 cm |
| -10° | -0.05 H | 1.3 cm |
| -20° | -0.11 H | 1.2 cm |

With the violin of 0.43, the slant of -15° gave 1.6 cm, and the slant of -12° 1.3 cm
(`pose-depth-lift-final.txt`). The rest of the chin hold on the lap put the right upper arm 3.3 cm
into the body (`pose-depth-lap-rest.txt`), so the fox keeps the rest at the side of the rabbit.

`pnpm hold-reach fox-red` gives the rule of a violin shop for each hold. With the hold of the
rabbit and the violin of the app, the scroll was 0.630 m from the left shoulder joint, and the rule
gave 0.489 (`hold-reach-scale-1.txt`). Each move of the violin forward moves the scroll away, so
the final hold gives 0.433, and `violinScale` is 0.43: a violin of 0.218 m. The left paw reaches the
whole fingerboard, and the bow paw 0.030 to 0.185 m of the hair (`hold-reach-final.txt`).
