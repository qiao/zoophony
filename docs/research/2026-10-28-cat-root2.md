## 1. The question

The user said "the cat's arm has lumps" on 2026-10-27 (the play from the left front, `close-views
play --at cat --time 30 --azimuth -72 --elevation 28 --zoom 3.5` on `boulanger-matin`), and on
2026-10-28: "always take screenshots from all angles and validate them to ensure things are fixed
and no new regressions are introduced". The round `sixth-cat-root` left a soft fold at the front
of the left arm root in the play, a notch at rest, the crease of `main` between the rest and the
play, and thin faces at the front end of the left armpit line, where vertices 1607, 1609, and
1610 lie on one line. The target: no fold or notch at the front of either root in any step, and
no new folded face against `main`.

## 2. The measures

- `root-crease.ts` of `2026-10-28-cat-root/probes/` gives the valleys in front of a root, with
  the samples of section 5 (the lift samples 3 to 7 and the lowering samples 12 to 15 are the
  steps between the rest and the play).
- `probes/pit_probe.py` runs the steps of `cat_arms.py` and lists the faces with a corner under
  12° within 0.03 units of each end of each armpit line (`pit-main.txt`, `pit.txt`), with the part
  code, ring, and spoke of each corner. `probes/pit_dist.py` gives the distance of the shoulder
  ring vertices to the line, and `probes/pit_plot.py` draws the band.
- `probes/raise-path.ts` gives, for each sample, the angle of the upper arm from the turn of each
  shape of `raise` and its weight (`raise-path.txt`).
- `probes/pdiff.py` compares the weights of `cat-weights.json` with `main` at the same places,
  and `probes/wdiff.py` the weights of the body module by place (`probes/dump-w.ts`).

## 3. The causes

- **The band.** The first shoulder ring lies 0.15 of the way from the socket to the first ring
  of the tube. On the flat inner side of the arm this way is short, and `step_shape()` makes it
  shorter: the ring lies 0.66 units-cm from the line before the shape and 0.14 to 0.52 after
  (`pit_dist.py`). Bridge Edge Loops joined the 10 vertices of the line to it with thin
  triangles. Face 2809 of `main` (vertices 1609, 1607, 1610) has the corners 2.5°, 3.4°, and
  174°. In the bind, its edge 1607 to 1610 is a valley of 167°, the largest in front of the root
  in the bind and in most samples (`crease-L-main.txt`). Near the line ends, 27 faces have a
  corner under 12°: 13 at the left line, 14 at the right line.
- **The step between the rest and the play.** The two shapes are 43.6° apart. On the lift and
  the lowering, the upper arm passes 30° to 45° from both (lift samples 3 to 6, lowering samples
  12 to 15, `raise-path-main.txt`), so neither has weight there.
- **The smoothing.** A smoothing of the skin in the pose of a shape (`probes/fill.patch`, as
  `fill_skin.py` of the root round) shrinks the round front of the root. After the clean of the
  band, 2 rounds still pulled it into a dark pocket, and with 4 rounds a face of the band stayed
  turned over after the relax (`tries/crease-L-c4-f*.txt`, `tries/fill-72.webp`).
