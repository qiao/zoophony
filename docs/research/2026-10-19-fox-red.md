# The redder fur of the red fox

Date 2026-10-19. Round `fox-red`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-19-fox-red.md`](../decisions/2026-10-19-fox-red.md). The probes and their outputs are in
[`2026-10-19-fox-red/`](2026-10-19-fox-red/). Each command runs from the root of the tree.

## 1. The question

The user said: "make the red fox's color more red, like the reference image's". The measure is the
Oklch median of the orange fur of four fox heads in the play frame, against four fox heads of
`docs/reference/orchestra-latest.png` (`measure.py`, the boxes of the manager). The light, the
grade, and the stage colors stay.

## 2. The measures

The orange faces of the texture have the lightness 0.64 to 0.76, the chroma 0.14 to 0.16, and
the hue 55° to 56°. The faces between the orange and the white fur have the chroma 0.06 to 0.13.
The rule `colors.shift` of `tools/mesh-rigs/fox.json` multiplies the lightness and the chroma of
each orange face and turns its hue. A step is a share of the move from the base frame to the
reference.

| Variant | Factors (L, C) | Turn | L p50 | C p50 | Hue p50 | Oklab distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| reference | | | 0.651 | 0.139 | 43.4° | 0 |
| base `fecc1605` | 1, 1 | 0° | 0.844 | 0.107 | 63.8° | 0.200 |
| half step | 0.8725, 1.105 | -6° | 0.769 | 0.125 | 50.6° | 0.120 |
| full step (chosen) | 0.745, 1.21 | -12° | 0.655 | 0.125 | 43.1° | 0.015 |
| past the reference | 0.643, 1.294 | -16.8° | 0.563 | 0.125 | 38.2° | 0.090 |
| lighter, redder | 0.79, 1.3 | -16° | 0.697 | 0.135 | 40.8° | 0.047 |
| snapshot `10459ade` | 0.745, 1.21 | -12° | 0.660 | 0.127 | 43.7° | 0.015 |

Each variant ran `pnpm rig-mesh fox`, `pnpm studio-body fox`, and
`pnpm close-views play <dir> --time 33.5 --camera default` (the demo, the time, and the camera of
`pnpm snapshot`). Then `python3 docs/research/2026-10-19-fox-red/measure.py <label>=<frame>`
measured the frame. The files of the folder:

- `variants.txt`: the measures of each variant and of the probes 1 to 6 before the variants.
- `variants.webp`: the play frame and the front close view of each variant, and the reference.
- `lineup.webp`: the lineup of `pnpm close-views batch` with the base above the round.
- `body-probe.txt`, `size.txt`, and `rig-mesh-fox.log`: the outputs of the checks.

The probes show three facts:

- The warm stage light makes the hue of the fur yellower. The texture hue 56° shows as 64°.
- The darker orange leaves the sRGB gamut. A clip of each channel (probes 1 to 4) turned the hue
  by up to 12° on its own, and a chroma factor above 1.21 gave no more chroma. The rule now keeps
  the hue and the lightness, and lowers the chroma to the gamut edge (probes 5 and 6).
- The gamut limits the chroma of each step to 0.125. The lighter, redder variant gets 0.135, with
  a lightness of 0.697.

## 3. The result

The lightness and the hue of the reference lie inside the range of the variants. The chroma of the
reference, 0.139, lies above it. The full step is nearest to the reference, with an Oklab distance
of 0.015 against 0.047 for the lighter, redder variant. In the play frame and in the close views,
it reads as red-orange, with no brown and no pink, so it stays. The snapshot of the last commit
measures the same.

The white belly, the muzzle, the black legs, the ear backs, the eyes, and the nose keep their
colors. `pnpm body-probe --base fecc1605` shows only the colors of the red fox: the tan fox and each
other species keep each byte, and each pose stays. The first download is 30 B smaller.
