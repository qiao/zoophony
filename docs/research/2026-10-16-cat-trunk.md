## 1. The rule of the trunk

`trunk_moves()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py` follows the neck factor: the trunk bones (`hips`,
`spine`, `spine1`, `chest`) shorten by the factor along the line from the hips joint to the neck
joint (`segment_stretch()`). Each other chain moves as a whole with the point of that line at its
first joint after the trunk: the neck and the head at the neck joint, each arm at its clavicle
joint, each leg at its hip joint, and the tail at its root.

```text
   ears, head, neck  --- move by the drop of the neck joint
         |
   neck joint  ------+
   clavicle joint ---|-- the arm moves by the drop at its clavicle joint
                     |   the trunk: each point moves by (factor - 1) x its place along the line
   tail root  -------|-- the tail moves by the drop at its root
   hips joint  ------+   no move below it: the hip joints, the legs, the haunch
```

- The head, the arms, the legs, and the tail keep their sizes. A vertex with weights on the trunk
  and on an arm blends two moves of nearly the same size: at the factor 0.85768, the clavicle
  joint lies 0.435 units along the line of 0.45 units, so the arm moves 0.062 units and the neck
  joint 0.064 units (the line "trunk:" of `rig-mesh-check-final.log`).
- The trunk keeps its width and its depth. The faces take their colors from the texture before the
  move, so the cream belly keeps its width and becomes 14% shorter along the trunk.
- The legs: the trunk lowers the ear tips by (1 - factor) × 0.45 units (the height of the neck
  joint above the hips joint), and the leg factor raises them by (factor - 1) × 0.145 units (the
  height of the hip joint above the ankle). The leg factor 1.5 + (1 - trunkFactor) × 0.45 / 0.145
  keeps the ear tips at 1.0725 units, so the scale and the arm factor stay.
- `pnpm rig-mesh` reads the factor (`trunkDrop()` in the height of the shoulder joint of
  `tools/rig-mesh.ts`), and a variant of `--set` can add it.
- With no factor, the cat, fox, and rabbit rigs keep their bytes (`ea085ee6`, `19ade726`, and
  `2ab1d663`, the hashes of the GLB files of `main`).

## 3. The seat and the tail

The sit drops the haunch to a fixed seat top (`sit.top`), the seat top of the hanging tail. The
longer legs raise it: the rig of each variant with the hanging tail of `main` before the curl
(`studio.tailBind`, no `sit.top`) gives the seat top in the line of the trunk turn
(`seat-tops.txt`): 0.2157 units (V0), 0.2571 units (V1), and 0.2746 units (V2).

The trunk factor moves the root of the tail (0.09 units above the hips joint) 0.0128 units down
along the trunk. The seat top rises 0.0589 units, 0.0051 units less than the rise of the legs
(0.0640 units, `rig-mesh-check-variants.log`). So the root comes about 0.008 units (1.3 cm)
closer to the seat top. The curve of the curled tail stays. A lift of its points changes the
gap of the haunch (`tail-lift.txt`):

| Lift of the points (units) | Side view of the haunch above the seat | Tail into the seat top |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (chosen) | 0.13 cm | 0.43 cm |
| 0.0003 | 0.13 cm | 0.38 cm |
| 0.0006 | 0.11 cm | 0.34 cm |
| 0.0008 | 1.93 cm | 0.30 cm |
| 0.0012 | 1.95 cm | 0.23 cm |
| 0.0023 (V2 of the mid-report) | 1.99 cm | 0.08 cm |

From a lift of 0.0008 units, the curl raises root vertices of the tail that the sit counts as the
haunch. The curve of `main` keeps the gap far from that step.
`node docs/research/2026-10-16-cat-seat/tail-sway.ts mozart-k525 bach-bwv1050` (`tail-sway.txt`)
measures the tail in the app: 0.0 cm into the seat, 0.0 cm into the back posts, 11.9 cm outside
the side edge of the seat, and the lowest point 24.0 cm above the floor (seat 25.3 cm). With
`SHARE=0.95`, the tail goes 0.3 cm into the body, as on `main`.

`node docs/research/2026-10-16-cat-mesh/seat-probe.ts` (`seat-probe.txt`): the back of the
haunch is 1.2 cm in front of the back posts, the thighs over the front 10 cm of the seat are
0.1 cm above the seat top, and the front edge of the seat is 15.9 cm behind the back of the knees
(9.9 cm on `main`). The ankles are 9.0 cm in front of the knees.

## 5. The larger stages

The rig check gives the stage side of each demo against `main` (`rig-mesh-check-final.log`): 11
of the 14 demos grow by 1.4% to 4.1%, the most in the Boulanger demo (11.381 m to 11.853 m).
`stage-probe.ts` prints the places of the players (`stage-probe-main.txt`,
`stage-probe-branch.txt`): the first row moves from 3.482 m to 3.653 m from the podium.

`reach-probe.ts` (`reach-probe.txt`) measures the footprint of the seated cat: the boxes below
0.3 m reach 0.510 m in front of the seat center (0.437 m on `main`, 0.545 m for the red fox), and
the whole footprint keeps its front (0.552 m, the viola) and its width. The arc between two
neighbors grows until the players and the stands keep the gap (`spacing()` of
`models/layout-scene.ts`), and a row that leaves its span moves out (`fitRow()`).

The check of the cause (`stage-low-boxes.txt`): two copies of the source of `main`
(`git archive main src`), one with the cat module of the branch, each with a filter in
`playerFootprint()` that keeps only the boxes whose top is above `LOW` meters:

| `LOW` | `main` | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 11.381 m | 11.853 m |
| 0.2 m | 11.361 m | 11.833 m |
| 0.3 m | 11.561 m | 11.561 m |

With the boxes below 0.3 m left out, both trees give the same stage, so the cause is the lower
shins and the feet of the longer legs.
