# The trial rig of the T-pose pig mesh

Date 2026-10-24. The user gave a new pig mesh in a T-pose and said: "after all these, replace the
pig model with [...]". The trial rig did not reach the look of the pig of `main`, so the pig of
`main` stays in the app. The probes and the images are in
[`2026-10-24-pig-tpose/`](2026-10-24-pig-tpose/). The probe scripts are those of
[`2026-10-23-rabbit-apose/`](2026-10-23-rabbit-apose/).

## The mesh

The mesh has 3,135 triangles and 1,566 vertices after the weld, in one part, and 255 faces with a
corner angle below 10° (`assets/models/pig/README.md`). Each arm goes out level at y 0.59 and a
little back, free of the trunk beyond x 0.17, to the hoof at x 0.41 (`sections-x.txt`,
`arm-axis.txt`). The big head starts above the narrow neck at y 0.67, the floppy ears stand out to
the sides from y 0.88, and a curly tail sits behind the rump (`source-views.webp`). The eyes are 5
and 6 dark faces (`head-colors.txt`).

## The trial

`make_config.py` writes the trial config `pig-tpose-config.json` from the pig config of `main`:
the chair, the sit rule, the size (1.2 m for each unit), and the reach of 0.3845 m (arm factor
1.3692 on this mesh). The leg turns come from `leg_turns.py` of `2026-10-16-hip-open/` with the
seat of design 5.2. The seated bind sits well on its chair (`seated-bind.webp`).

## The arms

The arm gate fails with each palm of the scan (`gate-variants.txt`): 9 to 14 folded faces (limit
8), and a flat upper arm (0.39 to 0.54, limit 0.55). The palm down and forward, (0, -0.7, 0.7),
gives the least twist. In the views, the level arm of the T-pose turns far down to the horn: the
left upper arm reaches across the chest as a flat slab, and a flat flap shows at the right
shoulder in the play (`views.webp`, the pig of `main` in the odd rows, and `arm-zoom.webp`). The
polar bear mesh of 2026-10-24 has the same fault at rest, and no weight rule or corrective shape
of that round removed it (`2026-10-24-polar-bear-tpose.md`). A ring at each shoulder may split the
long faces that cross it, which needs the user.
