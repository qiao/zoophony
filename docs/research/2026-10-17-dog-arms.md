# The arms, the cello size, and the tail of the dog

Date 2026-10-17. Round `dog-arms`, on `main` from `037606d8`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-17-dog-arms.md`](../decisions/2026-10-17-dog-arms.md). The probes, their outputs, and the
images are in [`2026-10-17-dog-arms/`](2026-10-17-dog-arms/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree.

The user said that the arms of the dog cellist were "way too long and unnatural", and that the tail
seemed unnatural. The round had three steps:

```text
 phase 1: variants (arms x cello size, 3 tails) --> user: 1.35 + 1/2 cello, T3
 phase 2: cello size, rig, refit of the hold    --> forearm in the cello at the frog
          --> user: "Keep 1.35, less bow at frog" --> BowedHold.frogHair, final refit
```

## 2. The cello size

A real 1/2 cello is 0.87 of the length of a 4/4 cello (section 2.7 of the string reference). The
dog takes 0.86 (`SpeciesParams.celloScale`). The end pin grows by the body length times the missing
share (`endPinLength()`), so that the top of the body stays at the chest. The bow scales with the
cello.

## 3. The rig

The arm factor is 1.35. The tail bones hang 50° and 80.5° below the level. The old tail rule then
lifted the back of the haunch 3.30 cm above the seat, and 10 faces of the root turned over.
`sit-grid.txt` lists 23 variants of the rule. The tail rule behind z = -0.09 units, `sit.root` 0.8
to 0.98, and `sit.top` give 0.39 cm and no turned face (`rig-mesh-check-1.log`). In the app, the
tail goes into no seat, post, body, or cello, and its lowest point is 14.0 cm above the floor
(`tail-final.txt`, `pose-depth-tail.txt`).

## 4. The refit

The hold of the arm factor 1.75 folded 92% of the play samples on the new arms, and the right
forearm went 5.0 cm into the cello. `arm-at.ts` shows the cause. At the frog, the arm is 0.9
extended, and the line from the shoulder to the frog passes through the right rim of the cello.

| Hold (all demos, play step 1 s) | Play folds | Right arm into the cello | Bow, m of 0.479 |
|---|---|---|---|
| hold of 1.75 | 92.0% | 5.0 cm | 0.095 to 0.345 |
| C: lean 3°, elbow out and down, pluck up the fingerboard (`fit-all-1.txt`) | 11.0% | 4.1 cm | 0.050 to 0.395 |
| final: C, `frogHair` 0.12, string planes 6°, tilt 55°, tip elbow down (`fit-final.txt`) | 10.3% | 1.9 cm | 0.120 to 0.445 |

The elbow directions, the shoulder turn, the bow slant, and the cello place did not move the
forearm out of the rim (`fit-trials.txt`). A frog margin alone left the paw in the rim on
the C string (`fit-frog-hair.txt`). Together with string planes 6° apart, it gave 2.2 cm
(`fit-frog-a.txt`, `fit-frog-b.txt`). A new right raise shape at the turn of the folds lowered the
folds only from 13.7% to 12.9% (`raise-trials.log`), so the rig keeps its shape.
