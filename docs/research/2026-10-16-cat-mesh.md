# The cat mesh of the user as the viola cat

Date 2026-10-16. Branch `cat-mesh`. Decisions: [`2026-10-16-mesh-cat.md`](../decisions/2026-10-16-mesh-cat.md).
The probes, their outputs, and the images are in [`2026-10-16-cat-mesh/`](2026-10-16-cat-mesh/).
Each command runs from the root of the worktree.

The user gave a sheet of a gray low-poly cat (`docs/reference/cat-views.png`) and a textured mesh
with no skeleton (`assets/models/cat/cat.glb`). The viola cats get that mesh by route 3 of
`AGENTS.md`: `pnpm rig-mesh cat` rigs it, and `pnpm studio-body cat` writes the module
`src/models/studio/cat.ts`. The work had two phases. The first phase wrote the rig config with the
arm factor 1 and gave a mid-report. The user then chose the size, the arms, and the legs, and the
second phase made the body of the app, the tools of the role, the viola hold, the images, and the
docs.

```text
 cat.glb (user) --tools/mesh-rigs/cat.json--> pnpm rig-mesh cat --> cat.rig.glb
                                                                       | pnpm studio-body cat
                                                                       v
 SPECIES.cat.body = 'studio-cat' <-- STUDIO_BODIES <-- src/models/studio/cat.ts
        |
        v
 CAT_HOLD (pose/bowed-rig.ts) --> pnpm hold-fit violist, pnpm fold-probe violist,
                                  pnpm pose-depth violist, pnpm close-views violist
```

0.868 m high to its ear tips, the parametric cat 1.160 m, the red fox 1.758 m, and the rabbit
1.622 m (`landmark-probe.txt`). The reach with no arm factor was 0.2538 m. The image of the

- "Fox reach (Recommended)": the reach of the red fox, 0.5452 m.

## 5. The dark line under the chin

In the app, the cat had a thin dark line under the chin from the front, and a black band under the
head from the sides and the back (`stand-sheet-before-smooth.webp`, `chin-before-after.webp`).

- The face colors under the chin are the cream of the throat (linear luminance 0.52 to 0.56), as
  the faces of the front of the neck (0.56 to 0.65). The faces of the underside of the chin face
  down (normal y -0.89).
- A Blender render of the rig with a flat light and the face colors shows no line. With a studio
  light, the underside of the head is dark (`chin-blender.webp`, left: flat light, middle: studio
  light and face colors, right: studio light and one gray;
  `blender -b --factory-startup --python docs/research/2026-10-16-cat-mesh/flat_render_chin.py -- assets/models/cat/cat.rig.glb <prefix> 0.74 0.1 0 0.3`).
- So the cause is the geometry under the light of the app: the big head hangs over the thin neck,
  and its underside faces down into the shadow of the head. No rule of the rig darkens it.
- The rule `smooth` of the rig, which removed the dark band at the back of the neck of the fox,
  removes it: a box from the back of the neck to z 0.12 units, behind the chin, 34 vertices, at most
  0.031 units. A box to z 0.25 units also smoothed the chin and changed H from 0.339 m to 0.352 m
  (`rig-mesh-check-cat-smooth-1.log`). The box of the back half of the fox held 14 vertices of the
  cat (`rig-mesh-check-cat-smooth-2.log`). H, the head center of the holds, the seat, and the
  footprint stay (`chin-before-after.webp`, `stand-sheet.webp`).
