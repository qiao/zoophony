# The side walls under the cat arms

Date 2026-10-28. Round `sixth-cat-wall`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-28-cat-wall.md`](../decisions/2026-10-28-cat-wall.md). The probes and their outputs are
in [`2026-10-28-cat-wall/`](2026-10-28-cat-wall/), the tries of the root weights in `tries/`.
Each command runs from the root of the tree. Copy a probe of `probes/` to `scratch/` before you
run it. "Main" is `95a99a39`, with the cat of `8b5a37ea`.

## 1. The question

The user said "the cat's arm has lumps" on 2026-10-27, with an image of the viola cat in the play
from its left front (`close-views play --at cat --time 30 --azimuth -72 --elevation 28 --zoom
3.5` on `boulanger-matin`). The round `sixth-cat-arm` made the left elbow and forearm even. Two
faults of the image stayed: a light patch with a crease under the arm, and a dark crease at the
front of the left arm root.

## 2. The side wall

`probes/wall_probe.py` runs the steps of `tools/blender/cat_arms.py` up to the copy, and measures
each side wall and the trunk faces in three rings around it (`wall-probe.txt`).
`probes/wall_cuts2.py` cuts the copy at heights of 0.49 to 0.61 units, and `probes/cut_turns.py`
gives the largest turn of the trunk outline near the plane of the seam (`cut-turns.txt`,
`cuts-L.webp`, `cuts-R.webp`: main above the round, the arm blue, the wall red).

The cause: the fill of `step_wall()` is a fan of 27 long thin faces on the plane of the seam,
with no vertex inside. The Bisect of the seam left lips at the lower run of the wall: trunk faces
that turn back under the wall, with normals that point into the trunk. In the play, the smooth
normals of the wall and of the lips showed as a light patch, and the fold of the lips as its
crease. The right wall has the same lips.

## 3. The fix

| Step | Fix | Commit |
|---|---|---|
| Copy | `step_round_wall()` of `cat_arms.py`, after `step_belly()`: delete the lips, fill the hole again with a 2D Delaunay triangulation of its outline and of a grid of points of 0.016 units, and smooth the wall and two trunk rings within 0.03 units along the normal of the plane, 6 rounds | `b97f576c` |
| Copy | A lip lies at most 0.02 units over the armpit line, and a vertex of the outline more than 0.002 units over the line merges into its nearer neighbor on the outline. | Replays | `step_belly()` skips an animal with no `belly` key. All 11 arm replays give their files byte for byte (`replays.sh` of `2026-10-28-hippo-paw/probes/`, `replays.txt`); on main, the fox and deer replays stopped with a `KeyError` | `53fa6727` |

The round walls had moved the bone heat of the arm roots, and the root rings went 0.04 to 0.07 flatter in the play: the arm gate failed the right arm (flat p5 0.77, 0.84 on main) | `0002091d`, `bf6ad2fa` |
| Rig | `cat-arms.glb`, `cat-weights.json`, `cat.rig.glb`, `src/models/studio/cat.ts` | `15adc513`, `fc356678` |

The wall may sink 0.006 units under the plane (`deep`). With 0.002, the fill took the room of
the lips, and the left upper arm went 1.7 cm into the wall in the play, the lift, and the
lowering of three demos (`tries/pose-depth-deep*.txt`, 1.0 to 1.2 cm on main). With 0.01, the
right wall folded at 175°. More trunk rings, with no reach limit, took long faces down to the
hips, moved them 3 cm, and drew a V of hard edges under the wall in the play. A fair in all
directions moved wall vertices up to 10 cm along the plane, and Smooth Vertices in all
directions sank the wall into a pocket. The sculpt tool Fair Positions of Blender stops a
background Blender. The live Blender of MCP was the session of another process, so the round used
the headless replay.

## 4. The arm root

`probes/root-weights.ts` gives the weights of the app around each upper arm root.
`probes/ring_share.py` gives the share of the lower bones of `paintedLower` on each ring of the
tube in Blender, which the rig replaces with the rule weights of the trunk. On main, the Smooth of
all deform groups in `step_paint()` averages the first rings with the socket ring, which hangs on
the trunk bones only: the second ring takes 0.60 and the third 0.28. In the app, the front of the
left root at 0.4 to 0.5 of the upper arm holds 0.77 to 0.87 of the trunk, so it stays with the
chest when the arm rises.
