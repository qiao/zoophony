Gate rows of the variants of the Blender session, in the order of the round. Each variant changes FOX of tools/blender/fox_arms.py in memory (a driver that sets the keys from JSON), then pnpm rig-mesh fox --check --set "source=<variant>/fox-arms.glb;paintedWeights=<variant>/fox-weights.json" --keep <dir> and pnpm arm-check <role> --glb <dir>/variant-<n>.glb. The rows give: folds p95, twist, forearm twist, flat, paw twist, paw swing, paw jumps, stretch, grip turn, move twist, move turn, verdict.
