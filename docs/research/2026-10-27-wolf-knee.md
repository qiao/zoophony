## 1. The wolf: the question

The user said: "the wolf is not holding the instrument properly in rest mode". The target is the
rest of a real clarinetist: the bell on the chair or the knee, or on the seat between the legs,
and the right thumb on the thumb rest carries it (`docs/reference/wind-technique.md` 3). Each paw
wraps the tube, and the move from the rest to the play keeps the hold.

The source knee is no wider and no deeper than the leg around it. In the bind, the knee is not

leg on the left knee and 0.027 units (4.4 cm) on the right knee (`side-cuts.webp`). The linear
blend puts each vertex with the weights of both the thigh and the shin on the chord between
their two places: vertex 1088 at the knee middle (thigh 0.41, shin 0.56) lies 0.053 units from
the joint, against 0.064 to 0.071 for its neighbors (`front-weights.txt`). The edges of the bevel
turn up to 78.7°, over the crease angle of 40°, so the app shades them as sharp lines. The rig
makes the block, and the source has none.

## 8. The polar bear knee: the fix and the tries

`tries-knee.txt` gives each try. A dual quaternion blend of the knee front kept the radius of the
middle vertex, but pushed ring vertex 2108 out to 0.090 units: the largest edge turned 97.7°.
A fair along the normals turned ring faces over with 24 rounds. A floor of the target distance at
the round of the leg (0.06 to 0.065 units) widened the knee and gave 3 to 7 hard edges. The fix
has three parts:

- Each vertex of the knee front moves across the leg bones toward the mean distance of its
  neighbors from them (`knee_front()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`, 0.1 units, 12 rounds, at
  most 1.60 cm). Alone, it left 4 hard edges.
- Two more front rings, 0.02 units above and 0.025 units below the knee plane, give the corner
  more vertices. Alone, they left 8 hard edges.
- The 436 faces in front of the knees take the skin normals (`rootNormals.kneeFront`).

The polar bear has 4,772 triangles in place of 4,550. The rounding turns no face over. With the rings, 4 faces of the hips turn over in the bind (3 on `main`), and the fold probe gives the folds of `main` in each pose set.
