[`2026-10-27-wolf-knee.md`](../decisions/2026-10-27-wolf-knee.md). The probes and their outputs

## 1. The question

The user said: "the wolf is not holding the instrument properly in rest mode". The target is the
rest of a real clarinetist: the bell on the chair or the knee, or on the seat between the legs,
and the right thumb on the thumb rest carries it (`docs/reference/wind-technique.md` 3). Each paw
wraps the tube, and the move from the rest to the play keeps the hold.

## 2. The cause

`probes/wolf-rest.ts` measures the bell against the seat and the knees, each paw against the tube
(the true palm from the thumb of the mesh, as `palm-probe.ts` of the round `wind-palms`), and the
turn of the clarinet in each paw over each move (`stepsCarry()` of `tools/carry-check.ts`).
`pnpm wind-grip clarinetist` gives the grip measures.

| Measure | Main | Round |
|---|---|---|
| Bell end | 12.3 cm in front of the seat front, between the knees, 14.0 cm from the skin | against the inner right knee, rim 0.1 cm from the skin |
| Clarinet from the vertical | 20° forward | 30° forward, 10° to the left |
| Left paw along the tube | 4.6 cm, its top 0.4 cm above the mouthpiece end | 24.3 cm (20.7 cm in the play) |
| Palm to the tube, left / right (`wind-grip`) | 51° / 50° | 39° / 39° |
| Slide of each grip along the tube in a move | 15.7 cm / 15.1 cm | 0 |
| Turn of the clarinet in the left / right paw over a move: net, tilt of the axis | 22°, 5° / 9°, 5° | 38°, 11° / 12°, 12° |
| Largest angle between the turn axes of the clarinet and a paw | 33° / 50° | 20° / 18° |
| Deepest arm in the body at rest (`hold-fit`) | 1.7 cm, over the limit of 1.5 | 1.1 cm |

`rest-main.txt` and `rest-round.txt` give the values. Main holds the clarinet like a cane: the left
paw caps its top, the bell hangs in the air in front of the seat, and each paw slides along the
tube in each lift. The net turn of the round is mostly a roll around the tube in the closed paw:
the axis tilts at most 12° in each paw.
