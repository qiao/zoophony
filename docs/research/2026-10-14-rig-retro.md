## The union merge of `docs/design.md`

Each round adds the line of its decision file to `docs/design.md`, and two rounds that each add a
line conflicted at the merge. `.gitattributes` now merges the file with the union driver of git.
`union-merge.sh` checks the driver in a temporary repository with a copy of `docs/design.md`
(`union-merge.txt`):

| Case | With the driver | With no driver |
|---|---|---|
| Merge of two branches that each add a line | no conflict, both lines | conflict |
| Rebase of one branch onto the other | no conflict, both lines | conflict |
| Merge of two branches that change one line | no conflict, the line two times | not run |

The last case is the risk of the driver: a line that both sides change stays two times.
