# The seated cat and the standing bow

Date 2026-10-16. Branch `cat-seat`. Decisions: [`2026-10-16-cat-seat.md`](../decisions/2026-10-16-cat-seat.md).
The probes, their outputs, and the images are in [`2026-10-16-cat-seat/`](2026-10-16-cat-seat/).
Each command runs from the root of the worktree. The Python and shell probes ran from `scratch/`
of the worktree, and they read and write `scratch/variants/`: copy them there to run them again. `color-variants.txt` names the front
renders of each color variant, and `rig.sh` gives the name of each variant of the other tables.

After the merge of the cat mesh, the user chose a gray fur on the stage, a tail that curls on the
seat, and a standing bow that hangs down. The manager also listed the gap under the haunch and the
folds of the wrists. The round had two phases: variants of the tail, the gap, and the colors with a
mid-report, and then the choice of the user (the tail T1 on the left and the half step of the
gray) in the config, the standing bow of the rabbit and the red fox, the folds, and the docs.

```text
 tools/mesh-rigs/cat.json
   sit.root + sit.top ............ the haunch on the seat (G)
   studio.tailCurl (T1) .......... the tail curled on the left of the seat (T)
   colors.tone (half step) ....... the gray fur under the stage light (C)
        | pnpm rig-mesh cat, pnpm studio-body cat
        v
 src/models/studio/cat.ts --> SPECIES.cat.seatedTail 'curl' --> PlayerPlan.tailSway 0 (no sway)
 pose/bowed-rig.ts: HANGING_BOW --> the standing bow of the rabbit, the red fox, and the cat
```

## 1. The variant loop

`rig.sh <name> <path>=<json>...` writes a variant of the config with `patch.py` and rigs it with
a copy of the rig script that `make_debug.py` makes: the copy prints the side profile of the
underside of the haunch in the final bind, and saves the bind, the weights, the triangles, the
source, the bind before the curl, and the tail joints as numpy files. One rig takes 6 s. The
copy of a variant with no change gave the bytes of the GLB file of `main`
(`1d4316e3...`). The tools of the round were committed first, and the rig of the final config
gives the bytes of the committed GLB file (`ea085ee6...`). The fox rig (`19ade726...`) and the
rabbit rig (`2ab1d663...`) keep their bytes.

`haunch.py` printed the vertices at the back of the haunch: each had 0.45
to 0.51 of its weight on the tail. They are the root of the tail, where it meets the buttocks.
The sit counted them as free, moved them by half of its drop, and left their faces out of its
gap. The rule `sit.root` counts the tail share of a vertex only by its smoothstep over two
shares. `pnpm rig-mesh cat --check` with the committed values (`rig-mesh-check-gap.log`): the
seat 1 mm lower, the chair 2 mm deeper, the standing body within 0.248 mm of the source, and the
same folds.

## 3. The tail

The root bone of the tail is 0.223 units (0.37 m) long, and the tip bone 0.144 units (0.24 m).
In the seated bind, the root is 0.157 units above the seat top. The haunch is 0.11 to 0.13 units
wide on each side of the middle over the seat (`outline.py`), and the seat
of the app 0.128 units. So each straight direction of the root bone goes into the body, into the
seat, or off the seat. The rule `studio.tailCurl` bends the tail along a curve
(section 4.4 of the design). `topview.py` plots the variants from above.

The mid-report gave three variants (`tail-TL.webp`, `tail-TR.webp`, and `tail-T3.webp`, the
measures of `tailprobe.py` in `tail-variants.txt`):

| Variant | Outside the seat cm | Into the posts cm | Into the body cm | Lowest above the floor cm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `main` (hangs behind the chair) | 43.5 | 0.0 | 0.0 | 11.7 |
| T1, curl on the left | 9.3 | 1.1 | 3.2 | 21.1 |
| T2, curl on the right | 9.4 | 0.0 | 3.2 | 20.8 |
| T3, two straight bones, left | 12.3 | 6.0 | 0.7 | 17.3 |

The user chose T1. Its curve then had three faults:

- The seat top rose 0.8 to 1.2 cm. The root of the hanging tail had pulled a few vertices of the
  haunch down, and the sit dropped the haunch to them. The curled tail does not pull them. The
  rule `sit.top` keeps the seat top of the hanging tail (`seattop.py`).
- The root of the tail followed the curve by its full tail share and lifted the back of the
  haunch. The value `blend` of `studio.tailCurl` moves a vertex by the smoothstep of its tail
  share from 0.5 to 0.95.
- The tail went 3.2 cm into the body and 1.1 cm into the left back post.

`curlopt.py` is a model of the curl in numpy: the tail of the final bind is the curl of the bind
before the curl, moved by the mean move of the later steps of a Blender run (spread 0.4 cm).
`curlfit.py` fits the five points of the curve to the contacts with a small change of its look.
The model held for the contacts (body 3.35 cm against 2.8 cm in Blender), but not for the gap
(`curlfit2.py`). So the last steps changed the first two points in Blender runs (`TLp1` to `TLr4`
in `tail-variants.txt`). The committed curve is `TLr4`:

| Measure | Rig | App |
|---|---|---|
| Into the seat | 0.0 cm | 0.0 cm |
| Into the body | 0.5 cm | 0.3 cm |
| Into the back posts | 0.1 cm | 0.0 cm |
| Outside the seat from above | 11.8 cm | 11.9 cm |
| Lowest point above the floor | 19.9 cm | 19.9 cm |
| Side view of the haunch above the seat | 0.16 cm | |

The app column comes from `tail-sway.ts` (`tail-sway.txt`): the seated cat of K. 525 and of
BWV 1050, over one period of the idle sway in the rest, and in the play, the lift, and the lowering
of the sweep. It measures the corners with a tail share above 0.95. The corners at the root of
the tail (share 0.5 to 0.95) go 1.8 cm to 1.9 cm into the closed body with a cap on the cut of the tail.
`pnpm pose-depth violist --bones tail1,tail2 --into viola` (`pose-depth-tail.log`) and the bow of
the sweep find no contact of the tail with the viola or the bow.

Before the stop of the sway (`tail-sway-before.txt`, all corners of the tail), the front of the
curled tail moved 12.3 cm across and 5.5 cm along, went 5.3 cm into the left thigh, and 1.1 cm
into the left back post. The brief allowed a change of `pose-at.ts` above 1 cm, so a seated tail
that lies on the seat does not sway (`OwnBodyParams.seatedTail`, `PlayerPlan.tailSway`).

The root of the curled tail lies further back than the haunch of the hanging tail, so the chair
of the app grows from 0.307 m to 0.316 m, and the seated cat moves 9.7 mm forward. The stages of
13 demos shrink by 0.1% to 2.9% (`rig-mesh-check-final.log`).

A third tail bone would need a new studio bone that follows `tail2`, changes of the rig script,
of the export, and of the format, and about 2 to 3 hours. The curl does not need it.

## 5. The wrist folds

`pnpm fold-probe violist --base main --faces 8` (`fold-probe-violist-base-main.log`, all demos
at a step of 0.25 s): each rest, play, and lift sample folds, as on `main`. Faces 2118, 2007,
2008, and 2009 of the left wrist fold in 100% of the play samples, faces 235 and 332 of the right
forearm in 87% and 78%, and face 204 of the right shoulder in 58%.

`hand-dir.ts` (`hand-dir.txt`) gives the mean directions of the arms of the seated cat in the
play of K. 525. The left paw bends 85° from the forearm, and it points 99° from the bind paw of the
config. Four bind paws turned a quarter, a half, three quarters, and all of the way to the paw of
the play bend (`bind-paw-folds.txt`, mozart-k525 at a step of 1 s): the turns removed faces 2008
and 2118, and halved the folded face samples of the left wrist (1,116 to 581), but each play
sample kept a fold. The bind paw is the bind paw of both sides, so its turn changes both paws in
each pose (`w2-compare.webp`: the close views of the final rig above the turn by a half). The
bind paw stays. A corrective shape of the bent left wrist is the next variant.

## 7. Checks

- `pnpm hold-fit violist --base main` (`hold-fit-violist-base-main.log`): no depth grows by
  more than 0.1 cm. The holds of the cat stay. The seated rest keeps the viola on the left thigh
  and the bow along the right thigh, and the curled tail lies under the viola side of the seat
  with no contact (`pose-depth-tail.log`).
- `pnpm rig-mesh cat --check` (`rig-mesh-check-final.log`): 3,157 triangles, the seat 0.202 m,
  the chair 0.316 m, the standing body within 0.240 mm of the source, and the same folds.
- `pnpm body-probe --base main` (`body-probe-base-main.log`): 90 of 95 bodies and 22 of 23
  players stay. Each differing body is a body of the cat. 989 of 1,492 pose sets stay: the
  footprints of the cat change the layout.
- `pnpm pose-depth violinist --base main` (`pose-depth-violinist-base-main.log`, 353 s): each
  depth of the seated rabbits and red foxes is the depth of `main`. The demos have no standing
  violin soloist, so the hanging bow is in `stand-bow.txt` only.
- `pnpm build && pnpm size --base main` (`size.txt`): 433,256 B on `main`, 434,078 B on the
  branch, +822 B. The packed mesh of the cat grows from 26,676 B to 27,342 B after gzip, within
  its budget of 32,000 B (`rig-mesh-check-final.log`).
