Date: 2026-10-10. Branch: `fox-reference`, from `main` at `d4bad946`. The decision file
[`2026-10-10-fox-body.md`](../decisions/2026-10-10-fox-body.md) gives the decisions.

## 1. Summary

The user set the goal: "make the fox model look like the reference image", and permitted a
change of rules and tests where the goal needs it. The red fox and the tan fox now have a body of
their own, built in code in `src/models/fox/`, on the bones of design 4.7. The parametric body of
the other species does not change.

## 2. Method

`pnpm sheet-check fox <dir>` (a new saved tool) builds the site, captures the sheet page of the
views page (`views.html?sheet=fox`, design 7.1) at the size of the sheet, and captures the
silhouette of the body (`?sheet=fox&matte`). It stacks the sheet above the page, and compares the
silhouette with the matte `tools/sheet-mattes/fox.png` view by view. The matte comes from
`tools/spike/fox-gated/sheet_matte.py` of the fox spike of 2026-10-08.

```text
 fox-views.png --> sheet_matte.py --> tools/sheet-mattes/fox.png --+
                                                                   v
 src/models/fox/*.ts --> pnpm sheet-check --> page + silhouette --> IoU and difference image
                               ^                    |
                               +-- edit, 16 rounds -+--> grids in meters, color samples
```

Each round changed the model, ran the tool, and compared three images of each view: the sheet,
the page, and the difference. Grids in meters over the same box of the sheet and the page gave
the measures. The scale is 737 px for each meter of the sheet: its chair seat of 0.35 m is 258 px
high.

## 4. The body

`buildFoxBody()` of `models/fox/fox-body.ts` builds the body from parts on the bones of 4.7, as
the parametric body does. The parts are lofts through rings of free corners (`ringLoft()` of
`models/primitives.ts`). Each quad splits on its shorter diagonal, and each corner has a small
fixed offset, so the facets look cut and uneven, as on the sheet. Each triangle gets its own color
from its centroid and its normal, so the white bib, the face mask, the gloves, and the socks have
borders along the edges of the triangles.

```text
 buildBiped(species, stance)
     |
     +-- species.body === 'fox' --> buildFoxBody()            models/fox/fox-body.ts
     |                                 +-- torso (spine, chest), neck, tail (tail1, tail2)
     |                                 +-- addFoxHead()       models/fox/fox-head.ts
     |                                 |     skull and muzzle, nose, eyes, ears
     |                                 +-- addFoxArms(), addFoxLegs()   models/fox/fox-limbs.ts
     |
     +-- other species --> trunk, tails, arms, legs (the parametric body)
```

- **Head.** One loft from the back of the skull to the nose, 16 corners for each ring. The upper
  half of each ring is an oval, the cheek tufts are a radial bump 18° below the side, and the
  muzzle tapers to the nose below the eyes.
- **Eyes.** Thin plates on the face, 0.018 H out of it, so that the forehead does not hide the top
  of the white. The iris and the pupil end at the lids.
- **Torso.** The two parts overlap past the chest bone with a ring at 0.97 of the size. With 0.9,
  a turn of the chest in the playing pose showed the top face of the lower part as a bright line.
  With no overlap, it showed the cap.
- **Tail.** The brush lies on the seat in front of the back post of the chair, and its radius is
  0.3 H where it passes the post, so that it stays out of the post.

## 5. What does not match

![The silhouette difference: gray both, red the sheet only, blue the model only](2026-10-10-fox-body/silhouette-diff.webp)

- **The pose of the sheet.** The head of the sheet turns about 15° to its left and leans toward
  the violin, and the left elbow is farther out. The sheet page shows the head to the front (design
  7.1), so the front IoU stays near 0.75. This is not a fault of the model.
- **The tail in the back view.** The back view of the sheet shows the tail behind the chair, down
  to 0.2 m, and the front view shows it on the seat, at 0.38 m to 0.58 m. The sheet does not agree
  with itself. The body follows the front view.
- **The chest of the tan fox.** The chest of the sheet is about 0.03 m deeper in the side view.
  With that depth, the left upper arm of the tan fox went 0.057 H into the chest while it played
  the flute (limit 0.02 H). The upper chest is less deep, and the torso leans forward 0.08 H at
  the neck in place of 0.1 H. The flute arm then goes 0.016 H into the chest.
- **The paws.** The feet of the sheet hang about 0.05 m above the floor. The body keeps the paws on
  the floor, as the test of the seated feet asks.
- **The standing fox.** The sheet shows no standing fox. A standing fox with the legs of the
  seated fox put its head 0.13 m higher than the parametric fox, and the solo flute of K. 315 hid
  3% and 5% of the head of a first violin. The standing base is 1.05 H, and the standing thigh
  0.55 H.

## 6. Test changes

- `models/proportions.test.ts` builds the parametric wolf in place of the red fox, because the red
  fox has no parametric body now. The test checks the height map of the parametric torso.
- `tools/pack-rigged.test.ts` reads the fur color of the red fox from `SPECIES_COLORS` in place of a
  copy of the old color.
- `app/seat-text.test.ts` takes the new seat color of the red fox.
- `models/fox/fox-body.test.ts` checks the behavior of the new body: the skeleton, the parts of
  the eyes, the ears, the bib, the gloves and the socks, the white tail tip, the tail out of the
  chair back, the paws on the floor and on the foot ring, and the overlap of the torso parts.
- No limit of a behavior test changed. The flute, the bow hold, the layout, and the budget tests
  pass with the new body.
