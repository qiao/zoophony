# The cat and the hippo: stage P4a of the proportions

Date: 2026-10-08. Branches: `p4a-cat` (the cat) and `p4a-hippo` after it (the hippo), from
`main` at `cdf40cb` (after the dog family, the bears, and the views page). This report gives the
measures and the feature checklists of the first part of stage P4 of
[`2026-10-07-proportions.md`](2026-10-07-proportions.md): the cat of the violas and the hippo of
the contrabasses. It uses the method of the fox look
([`2026-10-08-fox-look.md`](2026-10-08-fox-look.md)).

## 1. Summary

- **Cat.** A Blender sculpt from the cats of `orchestra-side.png`, `orchestra-latest.png`, and
  `lowpoly-animals.png`, with a recipe family of its own (`tools/blender/feline.py`) that uses
  the arms, the hands, the legs, the ears, and the eyes of the dog family. The skeleton stays
  (the report measured the cat within 10% of the scenes). The colors of stage P2 stay.
- **Hippo.** A Blender sculpt with the recipe of the bears (`tools/blender/hippo.py` on
  `ursine.py`), an option A row with wider joints and a longer upper arm, and a lighter and
  bluer fur. The hippo keeps the high stool.
- **Limit of the width.** The scenes show a hippo 1.0 to 1.3 m wide with the arms. The seat arc
  of 0.95 m (design 4.5) limits each seated body, and the clearances of the contrabass limit
  the shoulders. The hippo stays 0.82 m wide in the bind pose, with wider joints.
- **Tests.** No bound became looser. Three pins moved, each with its cause: the hash of the
  hippo skeleton, the start of the fingerboard of the hippo, and the stage side of perf-60.

## 2. Method

- **Units.** H is the head height of the skeleton. Hv is the visible head from the chin to the
  crown (`measure.ts` takes the lowest corner of `head` or `jaw` within 0.2 H of the middle).
  A value of a scene is in Hv, with an error of about 15% (the proportions report, section 4).
- **Crops.** Each scene is cropped at 3 times its size (`grid.py` draws a grid with the pixels of
  the original file).
- **Colors.** `lit.py` gives the lit color of a box: the mean of the pixels from the 70th to the
  95th percentile of the value. Thus the shade and the white parts count less. The default view
  is the first screen of the Boulanger demo at 1440 × 900 with 2 device pixels for each CSS
  pixel.
- **Views page.** The views page shows no cat and no hippo. `views-patch.py` puts them on the
  rows of the rabbits and the foxes for a capture only. The patch is not in a commit.
- **Side-by-side images.** Each image shows the scene crops, the Blender preview of the sculpt in
  its bind pose, the views page on `main` and on this branch, and the default view on `main` and
  on this branch.
- The scripts are in [`scripts/cat-hippo/`](scripts/cat-hippo/). Section 7 gives the steps.

```text
 scene crops (grid.py, lit.py) --> measures in Hv --> SPECIES.hippo.proportions (skeleton)
                                                  \-> tools/blender/cat.py, hippo.py (forms in H)
                                                              | pnpm sculpt <species>
                                                              v
                                               src/models/sculpts/<species>.ts
                                                              |
  compose.py <-- preview.sh (Blender), capture.mjs (views page, default view) <--+
  probes: probe-hippo.ts (head and neck), probe-hidden.ts (0.5%), footprint.ts
```

### 4.3 Tests and measures

- **Bow reach.** With the shoulders 0.6 H from the middle and the upper arm of 0.7 H, the bow arm
  reached the hair only from 0.04 m (`bowed.test.ts`, limit 0.03 m). With 0.76 H, it reaches it
  from 0.03 m, and the left hand reaches the fingerboard from 0.06 of its length (0.13 before).
- **Head and neck of the contrabass.** The test measures the head bones, and the trunk under the
  jaw follows `head`. The first sculpt touched the neck (0 m). `probe-hippo.ts` found the
  contact in a band 0.25 H to 0.65 H below the head center: the shoulders under the jaw. A
  narrower head and narrower jowls did not help. A low narrow chest form (0.82 H above the base of the
  torso, 0.94 H wide) and a narrow neck (0.56 H) give 0.038 m (limit 0.03 m).
- **Rest pose.** The arms of option B (`plumpModel()`) scale the forearm of the sculpt by 1.25 at
  the wrist. A wrist of 0.13 H put the right forearm 0.012 H into the legs. A wrist of 0.12 H and
  a belly 1.24 H wide keep 0.01 H.
- **Bow in the paw.** The stick must lie 0.015 H to 0.03 H from the finger ball. A finger ball
  0.11 H across gave 0.032 H, and 0.13 H gave 0.014 H. It is 0.12 H.
- **Floor.** On a chair (`player.test.ts`, each species with each instrument), the nails of the
  hanging paws went 0.009 m below the floor. The paws have no nails.
- **Layout.** The first sculpt reached 0.4 m behind the seat center above the seat (the
  parametric torso 0.27 m), and Beethoven 9 grew from 11.77 m to 11.85 m. With the back at 0.33
  m, Beethoven 9 keeps 11.77 m, and perf-60 is 13.55 m (13.57 m before). The other stage sides
  keep their values to 5 mm.
- **Hidden heads.** A copy of the test with each species in front and a limit of 0.5%
  (`probe-hidden.ts`) finds one pair in the six demos, the choir test file, and the four
  chamber groups, before and after: a monkey hides 3.94% of the head of an elephant in the choir
  test file. The hippo hides no head.
- **High shots (N4).** 55 of 85 section and solo shots are low (limit 51, no change), and 11 are
  high (10 before, limit 12). The highest camera is 50°.
- **Seat arc.** The hippo is 0.82 m wide in the bind pose (limit 0.95 m).

### 4.5 Standing or seated

The scenes show the hippos standing. An experiment set the support of the contrabass to `stand`
and ran `pnpm test`: 12 of 2,180 tests failed. Four were pins (the stance of `player.test.ts`,
the footprint, the snapshot hash, and the fingerboard map). Eight were faults of the holds: the
head touched the neck of the contrabass (0 m, limit 0.03 m), the bow went 0.013 m into the right
forearm at 103.3 s, the rest bow went into the hips, the left fingers left the strings at rest
(0.036 m), the rest elbows were straight (179.8°, limit 150°), the bow hand turned faster than
1,000° a second in the lift, and two rest-clearance tests failed. The layout pins passed. A
standing hippo needs a new hold of the contrabass (design 4.6, 5.3), so the hippo keeps the
high stool, as decision D4 of the anatomy plan.

## 6. For the rest of stage P4

- **The elephant and the penguin.** The recipe of the bears takes a new species with a species
  file that subclasses `ursine.Recipe` (`hippo.py`): it adds parts, repaints parts, and adds
  regions with no change to `ursine.py`. `feline.py` does the same with `canine.py`.
- **Blob forms grow.** The fields of `kit.blob()` add up, so overlapping forms make a surface far
  outside their radii. A head of forms of 0.42 H was 1.43 H wide. Measure the built mesh
  (`low.ts`, `measure.ts`) after each change.
- **The trunk under the jaw follows `head`.** The skin field leans to the front (`TILT`), so the
  upper chest under the chin is part of the head in the clearance tests. A chest form above the
  shoulder joints puts the shoulders into `HEAD_BONES`.
- **Clearances to check first.** The seat arc of 0.95 m in the bind pose (`species.test.ts`), the
  head and the neck of a floor instrument, the rest pose with the arms of option B
  (`rest-clearance.test.ts`), the bow in the paw (`bow-hold.test.ts`), and each vertex above the
  floor with each instrument on a chair (`player.test.ts`).
- **The back of a sculpt moves the rows behind it.** Keep the back of the torso near the depth of
  the parametric torso above the seat (`footprint.ts` prints the boxes). The elephant of the
  timpani stands in front of the risers.
- **Eyes on a blob head.** The eyes of the bears lie on the head surface (`kit.surface()`). A knob
  form under each eye gives the raised eyes of the hippo.
- **The views page** shows no species of P4. `views-patch.py` puts two species on it for a
  capture only.
- **N4** has a margin of one shot for the high shots (11 of 85, limit 12) and four shots for the
  low shots (55 of 85, limit 51).

## 7. How to make the images and the measures again

1. Run `pnpm install`, and start `pnpm dev` (or `npx vite --port <port>` after one `pnpm dev`).
2. Blender preview: `zsh docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/preview.sh <species> <dir>/p<species>`.
3. Views page: `python3 docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/views-patch.py src/gallery/views.ts`, then
   `node docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/capture.mjs http://localhost:<port>/ <dir>/cap/final all
   boulanger-matin`, then `git checkout -- src/gallery/views.ts`. For `main`, check out
   `src/models/species.ts` and `src/models/colors.ts` of `main` before the capture, with the
   prefix `<dir>/cap/main`.
4. `python3 docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/compose.py <species> <out.jpg> <dir>/`.
5. Measures: `node docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/measure.ts <species> <instrument>`, `low.ts`,
   and `footprint.ts`.
6. Colors: `python3 docs/research/scripts/cat-hippo/lit.py <image> <name>:<x0>,<y0>,<x1>,<y1>`.
7. Probes: copy `probe-hippo.ts` to `src/pose/probe-hippo.test.ts` and `probe-hidden.ts` to
   `src/scene/probe-hidden.test.ts`, run them with `pnpm vitest run --project unit <file>`, read
   the file of `P4A_LOG`, and remove the copies. They have no `.test.ts` name here, so that
   `pnpm test` does not run them from this folder.
8. The counts of N4 come from a line in the N4 test of `src/shots/prepare.test.ts` that writes
   `low`, `high`, and the count of the elevations. The line is not in the repository.
