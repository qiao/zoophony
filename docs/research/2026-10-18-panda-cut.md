# A cut of the panda mesh at the arm seam

Date 2026-10-18. Round `panda-cut`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-18-panda-marks.md`](../decisions/2026-10-18-panda-marks.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-18-panda-cut/`](2026-10-18-panda-cut/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree.

## 1. The question

The user chose "Cut the mesh (Recommended)" for the gray streaks under the arms (marks 3 and 4 of
[`2026-10-18-panda-marks.md`](2026-10-18-panda-marks.md)). Short faces join each hanging forearm of
the source to the belly ([`2026-10-18-panda-seam.md`](2026-10-18-panda-seam.md)). The goal: a rig
step that removes those faces and closes both holes, with a gray streak share of at most about 5%,
a black share on the white belly at most the value of `main`, and the folds and the depths of
`main`.

## 2. The measures

The rule `cut` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py` removes each face below the height `top` with a
corner in the free arm and a corner in the body (`cut_seam()`). The loop of the hole has a run of
the arm and a run of the body. A cap closes each run (an ear clip with the largest smallest angle,
then edge flips), and a quad joins the two runs at the top. No vertex moves, so the standing check
passes with no exception (0.234 mm, `rig-mesh-check.txt`). The caps of the arm take the colors
across their edges, and the caps of the body and the quads the white of the nearest light belly
face. `rig-log.txt` gives the faces and the runs.

| `top` (u) | Faces cut, L / R | Gray streaks | Folds rest / play / lift |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `main` | none | 16.4% | 0% / 0% / 2.2% |
| 0.56 | 18 / 15 | 7.9% | 0% / 0% / 5.4% |
| 0.58 | 20 / 17 | 3.7% | 0% / 0% / 1.1% |
| 0.60 | 20 / 18 | 3.3% | 100% / 0% / 15.1% |

`folds.txt` gives the folded faces of each height. At 0.58, face 86 of the right forearm folds in
one lift sample. The visibility probe of `2026-10-18-panda-seam/hidden/` finds it in 0 pixels in
the lift and lowering frames, 3 pixels in a rest frame, and 40 pixels in a play frame, where it
does not fold (`visibility-counts.txt`).

The first rig of 0.58 gave the caps of the belly the black of the band, and left six original belly
faces at the old seam black with two or three white neighbors. The manager saw these new black
marks. `black.py` measures the share of the pixels darker than the Oklab lightness 0.3 in boxes
of the white belly under each arm (`black-shares.txt`):

| View | `main` | Round |
|---|---:|---:|
| rest, left / q-left | 4.00% / 0.22% | 3.34% / 0.21% |
| rest, right / q-right | 6.57% / 16.85% | 4.41% / 8.14% |
| play, left / q-left / front | 0.66% / 0.00% / 0.00% | 0.58% / 0.00% / 0.00% |
| play, right / q-right | 17.18% / 33.27% | 13.30% / 23.79% |
| stage close shot | 52.54% | 48.08% |

The option `teeth` of `colors.border` gives each dark face of the trunk box with at least two
light neighbors the white: 6 faces. The depths of the sweep stay (`pose-depth.txt`), the triangles
stay 3146, and the packed mesh goes from 29,716 B to 29,604 B after gzip.

## 3. The result

The cut at 0.58 removes the gray streaks (16.4% to 3.7%), halves the lift folds (2.2% to 1.1%),
and lowers the black share in each view. `before-after.webp` shows `main` and the round in the
views of `streak-variants.webp` and in the stage close shot. The black that stays in the right
boxes is the black band of the texture and the shadow of the arm.
