## The fit

- The rest columns of `main` measured the bow across the lap. The new columns measure the upright
  rest (`tools/lap-rest.ts`). The sweep measures a part by the corners of its triangles, so the
  middle of the stick went through the body of the red fox with no depth. The columns take a point
  at each 1 cm along the bow.
- The round belly of the rabbit covers the top of its thighs with triangles of the spine
  (`lap-top.ts`, `lap-top.txt`). So the gaps of the lower end and of the frog are heights above the
  top of the lap, as `lapPoint()` finds it.
- A direct mix of the upright rest bow and the playing bow turned the tip through the head. The bow
  went 12 cm to 15 cm into the body of the red fox while the arms lowered, with each of 8 sides of
  the hair (`fox-direct-mix.txt`, `grid-fox-hair.txt`). The via of the bow turns the tip through
  the front (`BowVia`, `liftVia()`).
- The grids of the places and of the right elbow (`grid-*.txt`) ran in the rest mode
  (`--sets lift,rest,lower`) on the source of each step of the fit, so a later grid starts from
  the values of the earlier one.
- The long right arm of the red fox reaches its thigh only nearly straight. Each place of the grip
  over the top of the thigh put the right upper arm 3.6 cm to 9.9 cm into the body (`grid-g2`,
  `grid-g4`, `grid-g5`). The chosen grip is at the outer side of the thigh, with 3.2 cm.
