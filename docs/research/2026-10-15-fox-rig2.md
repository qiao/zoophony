Date: 2026-10-15. Branch `fox-rig2`, from `main` at c071bf2e. The decisions are in

## 1. The neck band and the change of the layout

The smoothing box of the round of 2026-10-14 (`fox-smooth-config.json` of
`2026-10-14-fox-rabbit-fix/`) cleans the back of the neck, but it grew the stages of four demos.
`layout-dump.ts` writes the footprint boxes of the fox players, one box for each bone and band of
0.2 m as `playerFootprint()` makes them, and the stage side and the place of each player of each
demo. `layout-compare.py` compares two dumps (`layout-compare.txt`):

| Module of the fox | Stages that change | Players with another place |
|---|---|---|
| smoothed mesh, moved head center | bach-bwv1047 and bach-bwv1048 +2.2%, mozart-k191 +0.2%, mozart-k525 +4.6% | 152 |
| smoothed mesh, head center of `main` | none | 0 |
| mesh of `main`, moved head center | the same four as the first row | 152 |
| smoothed mesh with `head_shift()` | none | 0 |

```text
fur spikes at the back of the head
        | smooth_box()
        v
box of the head fur loses 5.3 cm at the back
        | the export: head center = middle of that box
        v
head center 1.71 cm forward (z -0.190085 -> -0.207156 m)
        | holdInstrument(): violin at the head center + offset
        v
violin box 1.71 cm forward --> arcs of the violins grow --> stage grows
```

The footprint boxes of the shoulders and of the chest also change, by up to 10.6 cm, because the
main bone of some corners at the base of the neck changes with the new weights. These boxes
move no stage (second row). The cause is the head center of the holds. The moved violin also
put the nut out of reach of the left paw: `stopRange` 0.08 to 1.00, against 0.00 to 1.00 on
`main` (`neck-moved-center-fit-fox-violinist-base-main.txt`).

The rig now writes the move of the head center back to the head before the smoothing into the
file: 0.0110 units, 1.71 cm (`head_shift()` of `tools/blender/rig_mesh.py`), and the export adds
it (`headShiftOf()` of `tools/studio-body.ts`). The module keeps the head center of `main`,
[0, 1.282174, -0.190085]. With it, `pnpm body-probe --base main` gives each of the 1,492 pose sets
equal, and only the meshes of the fox bodies and players differ
(`neck-body-probe-base-main.txt`). The folds and the depths of both foxes are those of `main`
(`neck-fold-*-base-main.txt`, `neck-fit-*-base-main.txt`).

The two tests of `src/shots/trace.test.ts` that failed with the moved head center took the last
candidate with tries as the chosen one. At the cut of K. 525 at 229.09 s, the low strings found
their camera in band 3 of the second pass, after two solo shots failed in bands 1 and 2 of the
first pass (`shot-trace-k525-moved-center.txt`). The tests now take the one candidate with a
found try.

## 2. The corrective shape of the raised bow arm

On `main`, faces 389 and 390 at the front of the right shoulder of the red fox fold in 3.7% and
11.0% of the play samples (`pnpm fold-probe fox-violinist --faces 12`). The bow arm is out to the
side and down: the right upper arm is 20° to 30° below the level and 40° to 60° out from the
front. `arm-dirs.ts` writes the turn of each studio bone of the right arm in the axes of the chest
and the folded faces of each sample, and `raise-turn.py` reads them (`raise-turn-main.txt`):

| Angle from the mean turn of the folded samples | By the direction of the upper arm | By the whole turn of the upper arm |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0° to 5° | 41.5% with a fold | 100.0% with a fold |
| 5° to 10° | 24.0% | 81.4% |
| 10° to 15° | 3.6% | 18.4% |
| from 15° | 0.0% | 0.0% |

The whole turn, with the twist of the upper arm, separates the folds, so the weight of the shape
comes from it. The weight of the arm-down shape of the right arm is 0.21 to 0.59 in the folded
samples, and with that weight forced to 0 the face 390 still folds in 9.3% of the samples at a
step of 1 s, so the fold comes from the skin itself.

```text
 config raise: clavicle turn, upper arm turn, fade, below
        | raise_shape() of tools/blender/rig_mesh.py
        v
 bind mesh + arm-down shape at its weight --skin of the pose--> posed mesh
        | relax the faces with a cosine below `below` (only the vertices of the arm)
        v
 relaxed mesh --inverse of the skin--> move in the bind pose = arm_raise_R
        | pnpm studio-body: raiseOf(), the shape after the arm-down shapes
        v
 poseStudioBones(): weight = raiseWeight(turn of the upper arm in the chest axes)
```
