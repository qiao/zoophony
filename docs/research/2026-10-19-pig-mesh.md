# The pig mesh of the user as the hornist

Date 2026-10-19. Round `mesh-pig`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-19-mesh-pig.md`](../decisions/2026-10-19-mesh-pig.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-19-pig-mesh/`](2026-10-19-pig-mesh/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree, and the base is `9a4613b4`, the commit before the round.

## 1. The question

The user gave a textured mesh of a pig with no skeleton (`assets/models/pig/pig.glb`). The horn
pigs get it by route 3, at the size of the parametric pig, with the arms that the user chose from
the mid-report. The measures that decide are the reach of the horn grips, the clearance of the
sweep, and the play folds.

## 3. The result

The pig plays the horn of the app with the arms 1.5 that the user chose. The hold turns the horn
back toward the body, so the left paw holds the valves at the middle of the body with 8.0 cm to
spare, and the bell sits at the right of the torso. No play sample folds, and each part stays
within the contact depth. Open: two faces of the right hoof fold at rest, and the horn rests on
the belly over the lap.
