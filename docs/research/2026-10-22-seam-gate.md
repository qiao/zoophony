# A gate of the arm seams

Date 2026-10-22. Round `seam-gate`, base `c5fae22c`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-22-seam-gate.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-seam-gate.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-22-seam-gate/`](2026-10-22-seam-gate/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree. The probes import `../tools/` and `../src/`, so copy a probe to `scratch/` before you run
it. A bad state at an old commit runs in a tree of `git archive <commit>` with the probe in its
`scratch/` (`run-explore.sh`).

## 1. The question

The user found flat flaps, shards, and dents at the seam of the arm and the trunk from behind, and
asked: "how to systematically / holistic fix these and prevent it from happening in the future".
Each one passed `pnpm arm-check`, which reads the rings of the arm. The goal: one measure of
`GATE_SCRIPTS` that fails each bad state and passes each good role, an intake check, and an audit
sheet.

The bad states: the bear conductor at `5593e92b` (flaps) and at `c5fae22c` (a dent at the back of
each upper arm, the second report of the user), the panda trombonist, the deer harpist, and the
dog cellist at `c5fae22c`, and the rabbit violinist at `7c1747ed` (the flat cap at the elbow).

## 2. The measures

Four ideas, each over the rest, play, and lift samples of `probeFolds()` at a step of 2 s
(`table.md`, `node scratch/table.ts`). "Seam faces" have corners with mixed arm and trunk weights,
or are caps of a cut.

1. The deformation of the seam faces (`seam-explore.ts`): the area of faces whose normal turns
   more than 60° from the mean turn of their bones, the area stretched or sheared past 2 or 3
   times, from the bind and from the rest, and the down area of `flap.ts` of the bear armpit
   round (`flap/`).
2. The creases of the seam edges (`seam-explore.ts`): the length of the edges with a convex or a
   concave fold above 60° and 90°, and of the edges whose fold changes by 60° from the bind.
3. The visible seam (`seam-view.ts`): a z-buffer of the posed body from the back, az150, and
   az-150 at 20° and 40°, with the visible area of each class of idea 1 and of the visible back
   faces (`view.txt`, four states only).
4. The form of the trunk near the root of the arm (`seam-ray.ts`): the area of trunk faces whose
   ray inward meets the trunk within 0.05 to 0.3 of the upper arm (a fin), or whose ray outward
   meets it (a notch).

## 3. The cause

`flap-faces-5593e92b.txt` (`faces-at.ts`) gives the 15 flap faces of the bear in brahms-2 at
718.38 s. Each face keeps its bind area within 11% and its bind normal within about 20°. The
flap is the form of the source: the back strip of the arm root sticks out of the trunk in the
bind, the hanging arm hides it, and a raised arm shows it. The dent of the bear is the notch of
the source below the caps. The deer and the dog have no cut, and their arm skin goes into the
trunk down to 0.8 to 0.94 of the upper arm. Each measure of the skin in the pose reads only a
change from the bind or the rest, so it cannot see a form that does not change. The good image
meshes have many thin faces with sharp folds, so an absolute measure of the form fires on them
too.
