# The beat fold of a fast notated beat

Date 2026-10-21. Round `beat-fold`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-21-beat-fold.md`](../decisions/2026-10-21-beat-fold.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-21-beat-fold/`](2026-10-21-beat-fold/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree. A probe script of the folder runs from `scratch/` (`node scratch/<name>.ts`).

## 1. The question

The user wrote on 2026-10-21: "the animals are wiggling too frequently while playing
Disney_Themes_-_Whole_New_World.mid". The file is a file of the user, and the repository does not
hold it. The measure that decides is the time of one side of the sway, and the conducted beat,
over the whole song, on this file and on each demo (`pnpm beat-rates`).

## 3. The cause

- `barPattern()` of `src/score/beats.ts` gave each bar of 4/x the pattern 4. The rule of the
  table of design 5.4 that folds a fast bar existed for 2/x, 3/x, and 6/x only. Thus the file got
  4 conducted beats each second (0.249 s each).
- `beatSway()` of `src/pose/pose-at.ts` changed the side of the sway on each conducted beat
  (`swayOf()` read `plan.beats`). At 241 BPM, the spine changed its side each 0.25 s.
- Each other motion that reads the beats followed: the count-in beat (0.249 s), the baton, and the
  mirror of the left paw. The cues, the bow changes, the strikes, and the beaks follow the notes.

## 4. The rules of real players

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A sideways sway of the body locks to the bar (four beats) more often than to the beat, at 89 BPM and 104 BPM. Up and down motion locks to the beat. | Burger, Thompson, Luck, Saarikallio, Toiviainen, "Hunting for the beat in the body: on period and phase locking in music-induced movement", Frontiers in Human Neuroscience 8:903, 2014 (PMC4224089) |
| The mediolateral sway follows the four-beat level, at four tempi from 92 BPM to 138 BPM in 4/4 | Toiviainen, Luck, Thompson, "Embodied meter: hierarchical eigenmodes in music-induced movement", Music Perception 28(1), 2010, as Burger 2014 cites it (the agent did not read the full text) |
| The preferred tempo of a listener is about 120 BPM. Listeners move to a lower metrical level when the music is much faster | Moelants 2002, as Burger et al. 2013 (Frontiers in Psychology 4:183) and Burger 2014 cite it |
| A fast 4/4 or a cut time is felt and conducted in 2, with the half note as the beat | Songtive, "2/2 Time Signature vs 2/4: Which Is Cut Time?" (a glossary page) |
| The tempo list puts Presto at 168 BPM to 200 BPM | Wikipedia, "Tempo" |

No source that the agent found gives a tempo at which a conductor changes from 4 to 2. The
round keeps the limit 168 of the app for 2/x and 3/x, and applies it to 4/x, 9/8, and 12/8.

## 6. The result

- **Conductor.** A bar of 4/x gets 2 conducted beats from a beat tempo of 168 and 1 beat from
  336. A bar of 9/8 or 12/8 gets 1 or 2 beats from a dotted tempo of 168. The file is now
  conducted in 2 at 120.5 BPM. No demo has such a bar.
- **Felt pulse.** The sway changes its side on the felt pulse (`markPulses()`): the conducted beat
  when the median conducted beat of the song is at most 120 BPM, else 2 or 4 beats. The song has
  one pulse length in note values, so the ritardando of the file slows the sway, also where the
  conductor goes from 2 to 4 beats. The sway of the file changes its side each 1.0 s, at the felt
  beat of about 60 BPM.
- **Demos that change.** `beethoven-9`, `boulanger-matin`, `brahms-2`, and `mozart-k525` have a
  median conducted beat of 132 BPM to 144 BPM, above the preferred tempo, so the sway takes 2
  beats a side, as in the measured sideways sway. `beethoven-1` keeps the bar pulse of its bars in
  1 in its few bars in 2 (a side of 0.34 s before). `bach-bwv1048` keeps a side of the quarter in
  its last bar of 7/8 and its first bar of 1/8 (a side of 0.3 s before).
