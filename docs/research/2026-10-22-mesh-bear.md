# The bear mesh of the user as the conductor

Date 2026-10-22. Round `mesh-bear`. Decisions:
[`2026-10-22-mesh-bear.md`](../decisions/2026-10-22-mesh-bear.md). The probes and their outputs
are in [`2026-10-22-mesh-bear/`](2026-10-22-mesh-bear/). Each command runs from the root of the
tree, and the base is `7c1747ed`, the commit before the round.

## 1. The question

The user gave a textured bear mesh with no skeleton and asked for it in place of the bear, with
the motion checked from the front, the back, and each side, and no clipping, twisted arm, or wrong
hold of the baton. The measures that decide are the size against the studio bear, the balance on
the podium, the arm gate, the clearance sweep with the stand, the hold of the baton, the face, and
the colors.

## 3. The result

The mesh stands on the podium near the size of the studio bear, with its own arms, a cut of each
arm seam, the brown of the reference image, and a smile. The baton lies along the index finger
and points forward, a little up, and in. The arm gate passes for all roles, and the conductor
keeps clear of its body and its stand.
