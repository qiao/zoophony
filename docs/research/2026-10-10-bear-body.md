## 2. Method

The work used the saved tool of the fox body, `pnpm sheet-check <sheet> <dir>`, with a new sheet
page and a new matte.

```text
 bear-views.png --> tools/sheet-mattes/bear_matte.py --> tools/sheet-mattes/bear.png --+
                                                                                      v
 src/models/bear/*.ts --> pnpm sheet-check bear --> page + silhouette --> IoU and diff image
 src/pose/conductor.ts         ^                         |
                               +---- edit, 16 rounds ----+--> row widths, color samples
```

- `views.html?sheet=bear` shows the bear on the podium of the stage, with no conductor stand, in
  the ready gesture of a count-in from the song start: `poseAt()` at the song time -1 s, 1 s
  before the first beat of the test notes.
- `bear_matte.py` marks the brown and the dark pixels above the top of the podium as bear. It
  ignores five lines of the batons and the top face of the podium, where the paws stand.
- Each round compared the sheet and the page in three ways: the stacked images, the widths of the
  silhouettes at each row of each view, and crops of the faces and the profiles at the same size.
- A script in the scratchpad set the holds of the conductor in memory and printed the posed
  elbows and wrists in `H` from the shoulder joints, for the ready gesture and two beats. Each
  set of values then ran the tests of `src/pose/`.

## 4. The body

The body uses the parts of the fox body: lofts of free corners with small fixed offsets
(`ringLoft()`), the color of each triangle from its centroid (`addPainted()`), limb tubes, and
eye plates. A refactor moved these parts out of `models/fox/`.

| Round | Change | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The new body: a small head, a pear-shaped torso, thick arms, short legs | IoU 0.865, 0.801, 0.864 |
| 2 | Colors from samples, and a vertical offset of each corner of the torso | rows of facets broken |
| 3 | The torso as one part on `spine` | no dark line at the waist |
| 4 | The legs as one part on `thigh`, a boxy muzzle, and the tan only on the muzzle | no ring at the knee |
| 5 | A back that is less deep, shins that slant back, and the skull top forward | IoU 0.899, 0.898, 0.886 |
| 6 | The top of the torso rises to the widest part of the head | no shelf behind the head |
| 7 | The throat forward under the chin, and lighter ear hollows | no dark band under the chin |

The occlusion bake (design 4.5) explains two of the rounds:

- With a split of the torso at the chest bone, the corners of the split kept 0.4 of their light,
  and the other corners of the back kept 1.0. At the split, a corner sees the triangles of the
  other bone on one side only, and the back of the bear is a little hollow there. The bake then
  counts these triangles as a fold. A torso of one part keeps at least 0.87 of the light on the
  back. The breath of the idle motion also turned the chest 1° against the spine, which moved
  the back of the wide torso 4 mm at the split.
- The shin on its own bone drew the same kind of dark ring at the knee.

The chest of the conductor turns at most 15° against the spine (half of a cue turn of 30°). The
shoulder joints then move about 7 cm along the side of the torso, and the shoulder balls of
0.36 H cover the move. The shoulder test of `pose/arm-joints.test.ts` reads the spine part for a
torso of one part.

## 6. The pose

The holds of the conductor are in head heights. The new `H` is 0.62 of the old one, but the arm
keeps its length in meters (0.67 m against 0.69 m), so the places first scaled by 1.6. With these
values, all tests passed with the new body, except two tests that pinned the old values. The
ready gesture then moved to the sheet:

| Wrist from the shoulder joint, in H (out, up, forward) | Right | Left |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet | 0.81, 0.5 to 0.6, 1.5 | about the mirror |
| Old ready gesture, scaled | 1.12, 1.13, 0.97 | 0.56, 1.11, 1.20 |
| This branch | 0.84, 0.63, 1.16 | 0.81, 0.59, 1.41 |

Four tests limit the values:

- The beat plane stays square to the front (`beatRight.x` above 0.9), so the baton at beat 1
  leans inward by at most about 25°.
- The arm keeps at least 3% of its reach at each beat, so the beat point cannot go much further
  forward.
- The paws stay outside the outline of the head from behind (`pose/conductor-view.test.ts`).
  With the wrist 0.55 H out at beat 1, beat 2 of a pattern of 4 took the paw over the head in
  the default view, so the wrist stays 0.75 H out.
- The wrist at each beat is at least 0.3 H above the shoulder joint.

The ready point above the last beat of the bar put the right paw 0.2 H further out than the left
paw, so the ready point is now above the point of beat 1. A ready lift of 0.4 units put the right
paw 0.17 H higher than the left paw, so the lift is 0.2 units. The left paw is a loose fist (a
curl of 50°) in the ready gesture. The elbows point out, down, and a little forward, as in the
sheet.

## 7. Known differences

- The sheet has a baton in each paw. The user decided on 2026-10-10 that the conductor holds one
  baton, so the left paw is empty.
- The podium of the stage is smaller than the podium of the sheet, in relation to the bear.
- In the side view, the right paw of the ready gesture is about 0.35 H less forward than in the
  sheet, and it is near the muzzle. A beat point further forward passed 97% of the reach of the
  arm in the loud beats.
- The key light of the sheet page casts the shadow of the right arm on the chest. A thin shadow
  line of a claw of the left paw lies on the left upper arm in the back view.
- The feet are flat wedges with claws. The feet of the sheet have round toes.
- The baton of the sheet bends a little at the tip. The baton of the app is straight.

## 8. Second round

A second round on the same day worked on the three largest differences of section 7: the paws
of the side view, the feet, and the podium.

| Measure | First round | Second round |
|---|---|---|
| Silhouette IoU, front view | 0.900 | 0.898 |
| Silhouette IoU, side view | 0.904 | 0.903 |
| Silhouette IoU, back view | 0.887 | 0.892 |
| Triangles of the body | 2,300 | 2,460 |
| Podium, width and height | 0.9 m, 0.25 m | 1.05 m, 0.35 m |

- **Side paws.** The paw in front of the muzzle in the side view was the left paw. Its wrist was
  1.41 H forward of the shoulder, and the right wrist 1.16 H. In the sheet, both paws are equally
  far forward, so the far paw hides behind the near paw. The left paw now has 0.75 of the reach
  of the arm in the ready gesture (`readyReach`), in place of the reach of a cue (0.85). In the
  side view, the paw now covers rows 250 to 320 and reaches column 905. The paw of the sheet
  covers rows 260 to 310 and reaches column 910.
- **A rejected step.** Longer arms (1.3 H and 1.1 H) with a beat point further forward put the
  paw 48 px past the paw of the sheet, and the IoU fell to 0.882, 0.877, and 0.868.
- **Height of the paws.** The sheet does not agree with itself: its paws are about 25 px higher
  in the front view than in the side view. The ready paws are now at a height between the two:
  the right wrist 0.57 H and the left wrist 0.55 H above the shoulder joints.
- **Feet.** Each foot has four round toes of five corners, with the claws at their front.
- **Podium.** The podium of the stage now has the size of the podium of the sheet in relation to
  the bear. Only five tests changed, all because they pinned the old size. The tests of the
  layout, the hidden heads, and the conductor stand passed with no change.
