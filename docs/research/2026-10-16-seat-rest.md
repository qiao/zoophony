## Sources

- OSHA, eTool "Computer Workstations", "Good Working Positions": "Feet are fully supported by the
  floor", "Thighs and hips are supported and generally parallel to the floor", and "Knees are about
  the same height as the hips with the feet slightly forward".
  <https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/positions>
- Gerald Elias, "Tales of an orchestral violinist: sitting correctly", The Strad, 2021-03-10: "Sit
  all the way back, (not slouching), with your back and posterior against the back of the chair",
  and "Out in front, straight up and down, with the fiddle resting on your knee".
  <https://www.thestrad.com/playing-hub/tales-of-an-orchestral-violinist-sitting-correctly/11628.article>
- violinspiration.com, "Violin posture", the source of the seated rest of 2026-10-14: "the violin
  resting on your left leg with the neck in your left hand". The user chose the rest of the cat
  violas on 2026-10-14, so that rest wins.

## The hip angle

The joint angle of the hips stays at 88.0° (red fox) and 86.4° (rabbit). The trunks of the meshes
lean forward over the level thighs, and the haunch of the red fox reaches 0.16 units behind its hip
joints. `hip-variants.txt` gives the three kinds of a reclined trunk that the round tried in the
rig (`trunk-recline.patch`). A turn of the pelvis put the haunch up to 6.5 cm into the seat, and
the lift onto the seat top turned 10 to 12 faces over (3 on `main`). A turn of the spine chain kept
the faces, but the standing body missed its source by 1.8 mm to 15 mm, over the limit of 1 mm. The
round did not find the cause of that error within its time box.

## The rest pose

`rest-probe.ts` gives the lower end of the violin, the bow, and the elbows at four wall times of
the rest. `hold-fit-*-final.txt` and `fold-*-final.txt` compare the rest with `main`. The play folds
and the play depths stay. The rest depths are 1.2 cm (rabbit) and 1.1 cm (red fox). The lowering of
the red fox puts the upper arms 4.0 cm into the body (2.6 cm on `main`), and one face of the front
of the left shoulder of the rabbit turns over at rest (none on `main`).
